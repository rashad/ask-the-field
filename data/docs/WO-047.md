# Work order: Safety valve test, W-34

## Scope of work

Subsurface safety valve function and leak test on W-34.

1. Isolate the well and bleed down the tubing head per the site isolation plan.
2. Function test the subsurface safety valve and record opening and closing response.
3. Perform the leak test and record the result against the acceptance criterion.
4. Restore the well to service and confirm stable flow before handover.

## Safety and permits

- Work permit issued and reviewed at the pre-job meeting.
- Well isolation confirmed by the Lead Operator before the start.
- Gas detection in use at the wellhead throughout the job.
- HSE Advisor informed of the planned shut-in.

## Materials and equipment

- Hydraulic test pump and calibrated gauges
- Wellhead control panel access
- Standard consumables (seals, test fittings)

## Execution notes and close-out remarks

- The well was shut in for the test on 2026-02-21 and returned to production the same day.
- Total downtime was 3 hours.
- The valve function test and leak test were completed and the valve was left in service. No repairs were needed.
- No leaks were seen at the wellhead during restart.
