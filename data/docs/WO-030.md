## Scope of work
Routine inspection of ESP surface equipment and check of VSD parameters on W-19.
1. Inspect the VSD cabinet, cooling fans, filters and terminations.
2. Check the junction box and the step-up transformer for signs of heat or moisture.
3. Record VSD frequency, motor current, voltage and drive fault history, and compare with the commissioning set points.
4. Inspect the wellhead power cable penetrator and surface cable glands.

## Safety and permits
- Work permit and electrical isolation (lock-out/tag-out) in place before the cabinet was opened.
- Well shut down in a controlled manner for the isolation, then restarted after the checks.
- Toolbox talk held with the electrical technician and the Lead Operator.

## Materials
- Replacement cabinet filters (held in stock, not required).
- Contact cleaner and thermal imaging camera.

## Execution notes and close-out
- Inspection completed on 2025-10-22. The well was down for 4 hours in total.
- No defects found on the surface equipment. Parameters were within the expected range and no drive faults were logged.
- Well restarted and returned to normal operation with no alarms.
- Production, 7 days before the work (to 2025-10-21): oil 1272.6 bbl/d, gas 1262.1 Mscf/d, water cut 34%, GOR 992 scf/bbl.
- Production, 6 days after restart (to 2025-10-29): oil 1283.7 bbl/d, gas 1285.3 Mscf/d, water cut 34.5%, GOR 1001 scf/bbl.
- Rates before and after are essentially unchanged, so no performance impact from the work.
- No related events in the previous 30 days. No follow-up actions raised.

Work order closed by the Production Engineer. Next routine inspection to follow the standard maintenance schedule.
