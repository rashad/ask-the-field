## Scope of work
- Grease the wellhead valves.
- Function test the wellhead valves after greasing.
- Record valve condition and any leakage or stiff operation.

## Safety and permits
- Work permit issued by the Production Supervisor for the shut-in period.
- Well isolated and pressure checked before work. Toolbox talk held with the crew.
- HSE Advisor reminded the crew about sand and hot surfaces at the wellhead. Standard PPE required.

## Materials
- Valve grease (approved grade) and grease gun.
- Basic hand tools and a calibrated pressure gauge.

## Execution and close-out remarks
- The well was shut in on 2025-10-04 for the work. Total downtime was 4 hours.
- All valves were greased and function tested, and each operated through its full travel. No leaks were seen at the wellhead.
- The well was restarted the same day and returned to normal operation.
- Production before the job (14 days to 2025-10-03): 1397.1 bbl/d oil, 1608.7 Mscf/d gas, 24.4 % water cut, GOR 1151 scf/bbl.
- Production after restart (6 days to 2025-10-11): 1533.7 bbl/d oil, 1784.9 Mscf/d gas, 25.1 % water cut, GOR 1164 scf/bbl.
- Earlier ESP trip on underload (EV-0033, 2025-09-20 to 2025-09-21) was closed before this job.
- A choke bean replacement (EV-0044) is planned from 2025-10-22. It is not part of this work order.

**Work order closed.** Completed by the Field Engineer and accepted by the Lead Operator.
