# Work order: Scale squeeze treatment, W-17

**WO number:** WO-051
**Event reference:** EV-0140
**Well:** W-17 (Wadi Field, vertical, ESP)
**Category:** Maintenance (preventive)
**Priority:** Routine
**Raised:** 2026-07-21
**Planned start:** 2026-07-23
**Completion date:** 2026-07-24
**Status:** Closed
**Applicable procedure:** SOP-OPS-008 Scale Inhibitor Squeeze Treatment

## Scope of work
Preventive scale inhibitor squeeze on W-17 to protect the near-wellbore area and the ESP from scale as water cut rises in Wadi Field. Shut in the well, pump the inhibitor and overflush, hold the shut-in soak, then restart.

## Safety and permits
- Work permit and pumping permit issued before mobilisation.
- Pre-job safety meeting held with the pumping crew and operations.
- Chemical handling controls and spill containment in place at the wellsite.
- Pressure test of surface lines completed before pumping.

## Materials
- Scale inhibitor and overflush fluids, as per the SOP-OPS-008 programme.
- Pumping unit and chemical transfer equipment from the service contractor.

## Execution notes
The well was shut in on 2026-07-23 and the treatment was pumped as planned. After the soak period the well was returned to production on 2026-07-24. Total downtime was 26 hours.

## Close-out remarks
- Treatment completed with no safety or environmental incidents.
- Production before the job (14 days to 2026-07-22): oil 1114 bbl/d, gas 874.5 Mscf/d, water cut 36.9 %, GOR 785 scf/bbl.
- Production after restart (6 days to 2026-07-31): oil 1115.8 bbl/d, gas 870.6 Mscf/d, water cut 37.2 %, GOR 780 scf/bbl.
- Rates are in line with the pre-job baseline. No related events in the previous 30 days.
- The Production Engineer will schedule scale inhibitor residual sampling from produced water as per SOP-OPS-008.

Work order closed.
