# Work order: Flowline pigging, W-08

**WO number:** WO-051
**Event reference:** EV-0137
**Well:** W-08 (Field A, vertical, ESP)
**Category:** Maintenance
**Priority:** Routine
**Raised:** 2026-07-06
**Planned start / actual start:** 2026-07-08 / 2026-07-08
**Completion date:** 2026-07-08
**Status:** Closed

## Scope of work
Pig the W-08 flowline to CPF-1 to remove wax and sand deposits. Launch pig at the wellsite launcher, receive at the CPF-1 receiver, and record the debris recovered.

## Safety and permits
- Hot work not required. Cold work permit and line-breaking permit issued.
- Flowline isolated and depressured before launcher opening.
- Toolbox talk held covering trapped pressure, pyrophoric scale and manual handling at the receiver.
- HSE Advisor informed of the activity.

## Materials
- 1 foam pig (spare held), 1 set of launcher and receiver door seals
- Debris drums and absorbent for the receiver

## Execution notes
- W-08 shut in at start of the job and the ESP stopped in line with normal practice.
- Pig launched and received at CPF-1 with no sticking. Receiver recovered wax and sand deposits as expected.
- Launcher and receiver closed, leak-tested and returned to service. Well restarted the same day.
- Total downtime: 7 hours.

## Close-out remarks
Production before the job (7 days to 2026-07-07): oil 1113.8 bbl/d, gas 839.6 Mscf/d, water cut 27.9 percent, GOR 754 scf/bbl.

Production after restart (6 days to 2026-07-15): oil 1055.2 bbl/d, gas 790.8 Mscf/d, water cut 28.2 percent, GOR 749 scf/bbl.

Rates after restart are below the pre-job average, with water cut and GOR broadly unchanged. The Production Engineer to keep watching W-08 trends. No defects found and no follow-up work raised. Work order closed.
