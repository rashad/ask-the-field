# Work order: ESP inspection, W-08

**WO number:** WO-046
**Well:** W-08 (Dune Field, vertical, ESP)
**Event reference:** EV-0082
**Category:** Maintenance, ESP inspection
**Priority:** Routine
**Raised:** 2026-02-10
**Planned start:** 2026-02-12
**Actual start / completion:** 2026-02-12 / 2026-02-12
**Status:** Closed

## Scope of work
Routine inspection of ESP surface equipment and check of VSD parameters.
- Inspect VSD cabinet, junction box and wellhead power cable entry.
- Check VSD frequency, current, and trip settings against the well file.
- Check cooling fans and filters, terminal tightness and signs of heat damage.
- Record readings before shutdown and after restart.

## Safety and permits
- Electrical isolation and lock-out/tag-out applied by the maintenance electrician.
- Work permit issued by the Production Supervisor.
- Toolbox talk held before work; HSE Advisor informed.
- Well shut in for the duration of the isolation, then restarted under Lead Operator supervision.

## Materials
- Replacement cabinet air filter (1 off) held in stock, not required.
- No spares consumed.

## Close-out remarks
- Inspection completed on 2026-02-12 with no defects found on surface equipment.
- VSD parameters were confirmed in line with the well file and no changes were made.
- Well was down for 4 hours in total for the isolation and checks, then restarted without issues.
- Production check, 14 days before the work (to 2026-02-11): oil 1255.4 bbl/d, gas 918.8 Mscf/d, water cut 25.5 %, GOR 732 scf/bbl.
- Production check, 6 days after restart (to 2026-02-19): oil 1218.9 bbl/d, gas 896.4 Mscf/d, water cut 25.6 %, GOR 735 scf/bbl.
- Rates are being watched by the Production Engineer through normal surveillance. No follow-up work is raised.

**Work order closed.**
