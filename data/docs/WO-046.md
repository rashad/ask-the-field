# Work order: Safety valve test, W-38

**WO number:** WO-046
**Event reference:** EV-0075
**Well:** W-38 (Bayda, deviated, gas lift)
**Category:** Maintenance
**Priority:** Routine (scheduled integrity test)
**Date raised:** 2026-01-17
**Planned start:** 2026-01-19
**Actual start:** 2026-01-19
**Completion date:** 2026-01-19
**Status:** Closed

## Scope of work
Function test and leak test of the subsurface safety valve on W-38. Confirm the valve closes on command and holds pressure within the acceptance criteria. Return the well to production after the test.

## Safety and permits
- Work permit and isolation certificate issued before mobilisation.
- Toolbox talk held with the slickline crew and the Lead Operator.
- Wellhead area barricaded. Gas detection checked before work.
- Well shut in and the flowline isolated at the wellhead for the duration of the test.

## Materials and equipment
- Slickline unit and pressure control equipment
- Test pump and calibrated gauges
- Standard seal kit (not required, held in reserve)

## Execution notes
- The well was shut in on the morning of 2026-01-19 and the pressure was stabilised.
- The safety valve was function tested and then leak tested. The valve closed on command and held pressure during the test period.
- No defects were found and no parts were replaced.
- The well was reopened and returned to production the same day.

## Close-out remarks
- Total downtime: 4 hours.
- Production in the 7 days before the test (to 2026-01-18): 1406 bbl/d oil, 2108.5 Mscf/d gas, water cut 27%, GOR 1500 scf/bbl.
- Production in the 6 days after restart (to 2026-01-26): 1397.4 bbl/d oil, 2111.2 Mscf/d gas, water cut 27.5%, GOR 1511 scf/bbl.
- Rates are essentially unchanged, so the test had no lasting effect on well performance.
- Next test is due at the normal scheduled interval.
- A flowline flange inspection on W-38 is expected in February (EV-0086, planned) and is tracked separately.

**Closed by:** Production Engineer, with sign-off from the Lead Operator
