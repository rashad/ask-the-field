# Well test: W-31, 2025-11-10

**Document ID:** WT-007
**Well:** W-31 (Salt Flat Field, vertical, natural flow)
**Test date:** 2025-11-10
**Procedure:** SOP-OPS-004 Well Test Procedure with the Mobile Test Separator

## Test conditions
- Test duration: 12 hours
- Choke: 24/64 in
- Flowing wellhead pressure: 467 psi
- Well status: producing

## Measured results (3-day period ending 2025-11-10)
- Oil: 1775.8 bbl/d
- Gas: 3404.3 Mscf/d
- Water cut: 15.3 %
- GOR: 1917 scf/bbl
- Sand: 19 ppm

## Comparison with previous baseline (7-day period ending 2025-09-11)
| Parameter | Baseline | Current |
|---|---|---|
| Oil (bbl/d) | 1935.9 | 1775.8 |
| Gas (Mscf/d) | 3677.5 | 3404.3 |
| Water cut (%) | 12.5 | 15.3 |
| GOR (scf/bbl) | 1900 | 1917 |

Oil is down about 160 bbl/d and water cut is up 2.8 points. GOR is essentially unchanged.

## Observations
- A sand monitor alarm (EV-0049, medium severity) occurred 2025-11-03 to 2025-11-06 after a rate increase. The choke was reduced and sand settled after three days. The event is closed, with 32 hours of downtime.
- Sand at 19 ppm during this test is the reading at the reduced choke. The separator was checked for sand carry-over after the test.
- The lower oil rate is consistent with the restricted choke following the alarm.

## Recommendation
- Keep the choke at 24/64 in and continue monitoring sand at the sand monitor.
- Repeat the well test in the next scheduled cycle to confirm the water cut trend.
- Increase the choke only in small steps, with sand checked after each step.
