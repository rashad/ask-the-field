# Work order: Safety valve test, W-34

**WO number:** WO-050
**Event reference:** EV-0122
**Well:** W-34 (Bayda, horizontal, gas lift)
**Category:** Maintenance
**Priority:** Routine
**Raised:** 2026-05-30
**Planned start:** 2026-06-01
**Actual start:** 2026-06-01
**Completion date:** 2026-06-01
**Status:** Closed

## Scope of work
Subsurface safety valve function and leak test on W-34.
1. Shut in the well in a controlled sequence and isolate the flowline.
2. Bleed down above the valve and confirm the valve closes on command.
3. Hold the leak test and record pressure behaviour over the test period.
4. Reopen the valve, confirm full opening, and restart the well.

## Safety and permits
- Work permit issued by the Production Supervisor.
- Toolbox talk held with the operators and the slickline crew before work started.
- Pressure test limits agreed and posted at the wellhead.
- Gas lift supply isolated and locked out during the test.
- HSE Advisor reviewed the permit, no extra controls required.

## Materials
- Calibrated pressure gauges and test pump
- Control line fluid, standard consumables
- No spare parts used

## Execution notes and close-out remarks
- The valve closed on command and held the leak test within the acceptance criteria.
- The valve was reopened and the well returned to production the same day.
- Downtime for the job was 4 hours.
- Production before the job (7 days to 2026-05-31): oil 1275.8 bbl/d, gas 2269.8 Mscf/d, water cut 28.7%, GOR 1779 scf/bbl.
- Production after restart (6 days to 2026-06-08): oil 1255.4 bbl/d, gas 2293.3 Mscf/d, water cut 28.3%, GOR 1827 scf/bbl.
- Rates are in line with the pre-job period and no abnormal behaviour was seen after restart.
- No defects found, no follow-up work required.

**Closed out by:** Production Engineer, with confirmation from the Lead Operator.
