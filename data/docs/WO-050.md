# Work order: ESP inspection, W-09

**WO number:** WO-050
**Related event:** EV-0136
**Well:** W-09 (Dune Field, vertical, ESP)
**Category:** Maintenance
**Priority:** Routine
**Raised:** 2026-07-16
**Planned start / actual start:** 2026-07-18 / 2026-07-18
**Completion date:** 2026-07-18
**Status:** Closed

## Scope of work
Routine inspection of ESP surface equipment and check of VSD parameters.
1. Visual check of the VSD cabinet, junction box, cable entry and wellhead power penetrator.
2. Check of VSD parameters against the last recorded setpoints (frequency, current limits, under/overload trips).
3. Inspection of the transformer and cable connections for heat marks and loose terminals.
4. Record of readings and restart of the well.

## Safety and permits
- General work permit and electrical isolation (lock-out/tag-out) for the VSD work.
- Toolbox talk held before start.
- Gas detector check done at the wellhead before entry, given the earlier H2S alarm on this well (EV-0129, 2026-06-23, closed, leak on a needle valve repaired).

## Materials
No spares used. Standard electrical tools and thermal camera.

## Execution and close-out remarks
- Well shut down for the work and restarted the same day. Downtime: 2 hours.
- Surface equipment found in good condition, with no loose terminals or heat marks.
- VSD parameters checked and left at existing setpoints, with no changes made.
- No leaks or gas readings at the wellhead during the job.

**Production check:**
- Before (14 days to 2026-07-17): oil 1289.4 bbl/d, gas 854 Mscf/d, water cut 14.7 %, GOR 662 scf/bbl.
- After restart (6 days to 2026-07-25): oil 1248.1 bbl/d, gas 795.2 Mscf/d, water cut 14.9 %, GOR 637 scf/bbl.

Production Engineer to keep watching the well trend in the routine review. Work order closed.
