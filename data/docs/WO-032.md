# Work order: Scale squeeze treatment, W-24

**WO number:** WO-032
**Event reference:** EV-0048
**Well:** W-24 (Field B, vertical, ESP)
**Category:** Maintenance (preventive)
**Priority:** Routine
**Date raised:** 2025-10-26
**Planned start:** 2025-10-28
**Actual completion:** 2025-10-29
**Status:** Closed
**Applicable procedure:** SOP-OPS-008 Scale Inhibitor Squeeze Treatment

## Scope of work
Preventive scale inhibitor squeeze on W-24. Shut in the well, bullhead inhibitor and overflush per the program, hold the shut-in soak period, then restart the ESP and return the well to CPF-1 routing.

## Safety and permits
- Hot work not required. Work permit and chemical handling permit issued before mobilisation.
- Pre-job safety meeting held with the pumping crew and operations. HSE Advisor reviewed the chemical data sheet and spill containment.
- Pressure test of surface lines completed before pumping.
- Wellhead isolation and lock-out applied to the ESP drive for the duration of the job.

## Materials
- Scale inhibitor and overflush fluid as specified in the treatment program
- Pumping unit and temporary surface lines
- Spill kit and containment bunding

## Execution notes and close-out remarks
- Work started 2025-10-28 and finished 2025-10-29. Total downtime was 34 hours.
- Treatment pumped as planned with no pressure excursions or leaks. Site was cleaned and containment inspected before demobilisation.
- Pre-job production (7 days to 2025-10-27): oil 685.1 bbl/d, gas 346.6 Mscf/d, water cut 25.5%, GOR 506 scf/bbl.
- After restart (6 days to 2025-11-05): oil 677.6 bbl/d, gas 337.2 Mscf/d, water cut 25.8%, GOR 498 scf/bbl.
- Rates and water cut are essentially unchanged, which is expected for a preventive treatment. No related events in the following 30 days.
- Production Engineer to include W-24 in the scale monitoring routine and review produced water samples at the next scheduled round.

**Close-out:** Work complete, well returned to production. Work order closed.
