# Work order: Safety valve test, W-22

**WO number:** WO-032
**Event reference:** EV-0040
**Well:** W-22 (Wadi Field, deviated, ESP)
**Category:** Maintenance
**Priority:** Routine
**Raised:** 2025-10-16
**Planned start:** 2025-10-18
**Actual start:** 2025-10-18
**Completion:** 2025-10-18
**Status:** Closed

## Scope of work
Subsurface safety valve function and leak test on W-22.
1. Shut in the well and isolate it from the flowline.
2. Bleed down tubing pressure above the valve to the test pressure.
3. Function test the valve and record closing and opening response.
4. Hold the leak test and record any pressure build-up.
5. Return the well to production once the valve is confirmed open and the wellhead is reinstated.

## Safety and permits
- Work permit and isolation certificate issued before the shut-in.
- Toolbox talk held with the operator and the slickline crew.
- Pressure test barriers checked and gas detection confirmed working.
- HSE Advisor informed of the planned shut-in.

## Materials
- Test pump and calibrated gauges
- Hoses and fittings
- Standard seal kit held as a spare, not used

## Close-out remarks
- The valve was function tested and leak tested on 2025-10-18 and the test was accepted.
- No leak-off outside the acceptance limit was observed, and no repair was needed.
- Downtime for the job was 3 hours.
- The well was restarted the same day and the isolation was removed.
- Production before the job (14 days to 2025-10-17): oil 538.5 bbl/d, gas 491.1 Mscf/d, water cut 29%, GOR 912 scf/bbl.
- Production after restart (6 days to 2025-10-25): oil 527 bbl/d, gas 481.3 Mscf/d, water cut 29.6%, GOR 913 scf/bbl.
- Rates after restart are close to the pre-job values, and no abnormal behaviour was seen.
- Test result recorded in the well integrity file. Work order closed by the Production Engineer.
