# Incident note: Power outage on W-29

**Document ID:** IN-016
**Event ID:** EV-0044
**Note date:** 2025-10-18
**Well:** W-29 (Field B, vertical, ESP)
**Severity:** Low
**Status at note date:** Closed
**Applicable procedure:** None identified for this event type

## What happened
A field power dip on 2025-10-18 tripped W-29. The ESP was restarted once power was restored. Downtime for the day was 9 hours (uptime 15 hours).

## Immediate actions
- The Lead Operator confirmed the trip was caused by the field-wide power dip and not by an ESP fault.
- Power was verified stable at the wellsite before restart.
- The ESP was restarted and the well was returned to flow.
- Drive current, intake pressure and wellhead pressure were checked after restart and looked normal.

## Findings
No equipment damage was found. The trip was a consequence of the power supply disturbance and was not linked to a downhole problem.

## Production impact
| Item | Before (7 days to 2025-10-17) | On 2025-10-18 |
|---|---|---|
| Oil (bbl/d) | 1007.8 | 604.8 |
| Gas (Mscf/d) | 666.8 | 389.3 |
| Water cut (%) | 36.4 | 34.7 |
| GOR (scf/bbl) | 662 | n/a |

Oil on the day was roughly 403 bbl below the prior 7-day average, which is the deferred volume attributed to the outage. The water cut on the day was slightly lower than the baseline.

## Related history
A preventive scale inhibitor squeeze (EV-0037) was carried out on W-29 from 2025-09-29 to 2025-09-30, with 43 hours of downtime. That job was closed and is not considered related to this trip. The power dip was external to the well.

## Status and next actions
The incident is closed. The well is back on production.
- The Production Engineer will monitor W-29 rates over the next few days to confirm they return to the pre-event level.
- The Production Supervisor will raise the field power dip with the power utilities team so the cause of the disturbance can be reviewed.
