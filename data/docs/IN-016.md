# Incident note: Wellhead leak on W-01

**Document ID:** IN-016
**Event ID:** EV-0061
**Note date:** 2025-12-10
**Well:** W-01 (Dune Field, vertical, ESP)
**Category:** Incident
**Severity:** Low
**Status at note date:** Closed

## What happened
On 2025-12-10 a leak was found on the wellhead valve stem packing of W-01. The leak was small and contained to the wellhead cellar area. The well was shut in so the packing could be replaced safely.

## Immediate actions
- The well was shut in and the wellhead area isolated and barricaded.
- Pressure was bled off and the area checked for residual hydrocarbons before work started.
- The Production Supervisor and the Lead Operator were informed.
- The valve stem packing was replaced by the maintenance crew under a permit to work.
- The valve was function tested and leak tested after the repair, with no seepage observed.
- The well was returned to service the same day.

## Findings so far
The leak came from worn valve stem packing only. No damage to the valve body, the stem or other wellhead components was found. No fluid reached the environment beyond the cellar area, and no injuries occurred.

## Impact
- Downtime: 7 hours on 2025-12-10 (uptime 17 hours).
- Production on the day: 516.2 bbl of oil and 329.4 Mscf of gas, with water cut at 28.9%.
- Baseline for the previous 7 days to 2025-12-09: 764.7 bbl/d oil, 479.7 Mscf/d gas, water cut 29.7%, GOR 627 scf/bbl.
- The shortfall on the day is consistent with the shut-in period.

## Status
Closed. The packing has been replaced and the well is producing normally. Today the well status remains producing.

## Next actions
- The Lead Operator to include the W-01 wellhead valves in the routine walk-round checks for the next few rounds.
- The Maintenance Planner to review the packing condition on the other wellhead valves at the next planned inspection.

## Procedure reference
No specific procedure applies to this event.
