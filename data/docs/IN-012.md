# Incident note: High separator level trip on W-21

**Document ID:** IN-012
**Note date:** 2025-09-24
**Event ID:** EV-0033
**Well:** W-21 (Field B, horizontal, ESP)
**Severity:** Low
**Status at note date:** Closed

## What happened
On 2025-09-24 the test separator high-level trip activated and shut in W-21. The well was down for 3 hours before it was returned to service the same day.

## Immediate actions
- The Lead Operator confirmed the trip on the CPF-1 control panel and checked that the separator was not carrying over liquid to the gas line.
- The well was kept shut in while the level instrument was checked locally.
- The Production Engineer was informed.

## Findings
- The test separator level controller had drifted, giving a reading that did not match the local gauge glass.
- The level controller was recalibrated by the instrument technician and checked against the gauge glass at several levels.
- No leaks, no damage to the ESP and no abnormal motor readings were seen on restart.

## Impact
- Downtime: 3 hours (uptime 21 hours on 2025-09-24).
- Production on the day: oil 1022.5 bbl, gas 778.7 Mscf, water cut 36.6%.
- Baseline for the 7 days to 2025-09-23: oil 1177.4 bbl/d, gas 874.5 Mscf/d, water cut 35.5%, GOR 743 scf/bbl.
- Oil on the day was about 155 bbl below the baseline rate, consistent with the lost hours. Water cut is slightly above baseline, within normal variation.

## Status
Closed. W-21 is producing and the separator level reads in line with the local gauge. No related events were recorded in the previous 30 days.

## Next actions
- Operators to compare the level controller reading with the gauge glass at each shift check for the coming week.
- Instrument technician to add the separator level controller to the next calibration round.
- No specific procedure applies to this event type.
