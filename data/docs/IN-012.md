# Incident note: Sand production alarm on W-31

**Document ID:** IN-012
**Note date:** 2025-11-06
**Event ID:** EV-0049
**Well:** W-31 (Salt Flat Field, vertical, natural flow)
**Severity:** medium
**Status at note date:** closed
**Applicable procedure:** SOP-OPS-002 Sand Management and Sand Production Alarms

## What happened
On 2025-11-03 the sand monitor on W-31 went into alarm after a rate increase on the well. The Lead Operator was informed and the well was brought back on choke.

## Immediate actions
- Choke reduced to bring the well back inside the sand-free rate window.
- Sand monitor readings watched at each round until the signal returned to baseline.
- Production Engineer informed and the alarm logged against EV-0049.

## Findings
Sand production started when the rate was raised on a sand-prone well in Salt Flat Field. After the choke reduction the sand settled over three days, with no sustained alarm after 2025-11-06. No surface leak or equipment damage was reported during the event.

## Impact
- Downtime: 32 hours as of the note date.
- Rates before the event (7 days to 2025-11-02): oil 1862.8 bbl/d, gas 3580.6 Mscf/d, water cut 13.4 percent, GOR 1922 scf/bbl.
- On 2025-11-03: oil 1258.1 bbl, gas 2297.2 Mscf, water cut 14.6 percent, uptime 16 hours.
- The shortfall against the prior rate is the deferred oil from the partial day and the reduced choke.

## Status
The alarm cleared and the incident is closed. W-31 is producing.

## Next actions
- Keep the rate within the limit set after the choke reduction, and increase it only in small steps with sand monitoring, as set out in SOP-OPS-002.
- A choke bean replacement (EV-0056, eroded choke bean) is planned from 2025-11-29. The work has not started.
- Production Engineer to review the sand trend at the next well review.
