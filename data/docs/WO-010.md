# Work order: Wellhead maintenance, W-21

**WO number:** WO-010
**Event reference:** EV-0016
**Well:** W-21 (Field B, horizontal, ESP)
**Category:** Maintenance, wellhead
**Priority:** Routine
**Raised:** 2025-07-01
**Planned start:** 2025-07-03
**Planned completion:** 2025-07-03
**Actual start / completion:** 2025-07-03 / 2025-07-03
**Status:** Closed

## Scope of work
- Grease all wellhead valves (master, wing and swab) at the grease fittings.
- Function test each valve through its full travel and check for smooth operation.
- Inspect the wellhead for leaks, corrosion and loose fittings.

## Safety and permits
- Work permit issued for non-intrusive wellhead maintenance.
- Well shut in and isolated for the job, with the wellhead depressured to a safe state before greasing.
- Toolbox talk held before starting. Standard PPE and gas detector required.
- Lock-out of the ESP power supply confirmed by the Lead Operator.

## Materials
- Valve grease (approved grade) and grease gun.
- Replacement grease fitting caps (spares, none needed).
- Leak detection fluid.

## Execution notes and close-out
- Work carried out on 2025-07-03 and completed the same day. The well was down for 4 hours in total.
- All valves greased and function tested with no sticking found. No leaks seen on the wellhead after the test.
- Well restarted without issues and returned to normal operation. No follow-up repair was raised.
- Production before the job (7 days to 2025-07-02): oil 1283.4 bbl/d, gas 954.9 Mscf/d, water cut 32.2 %, GOR 744 scf/bbl.
- Production after restart (6 days to 2025-07-10): oil 1281.6 bbl/d, gas 946.8 Mscf/d, water cut 32.5 %, GOR 739 scf/bbl.
- Rates are essentially unchanged, so no impact on performance from the work.

## Related work
- Flowline pigging on W-21 (EV-0019) is planned from 2025-07-27 to remove wax and sand deposits. It is not part of this work order.

**Close-out:** Work complete, work order closed. Production Supervisor informed.
