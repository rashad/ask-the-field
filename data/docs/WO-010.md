## Scope of work

Flowline pigging to remove wax and sand deposits. Launch the pig at the wellsite launcher, receive it at the CPF-1 receiver, and record the debris recovered. The well is shut in for the duration of the job and restarted after the receiver is isolated and drained.

## Safety and permits

- Hot work not required. Cold work permit and line-breaking permit issued for the launcher and receiver.
- Pressure bleed-down verified before opening any closure.
- Hydrocarbon and H2S monitors worn by the crew at the receiver.
- HSE Advisor informed of the job. Toolbox talk held before start.

## Materials

- Foam-bodied pig and spare cup pig
- Launcher and receiver closure seals
- Waste containers for wax and sand

## Close-out remarks

- Work executed on 2025-07-13 and completed the same day.
- Total downtime: 8 hours.
- Pig received at CPF-1 with wax and sand deposits recovered, no sticking or stalling reported.
- Launcher and receiver closed, leak-tested and returned to service. Well restarted without issues.
- Previous pigging on this line: 2025-06-29 (EV-0010, 9 hours downtime).
