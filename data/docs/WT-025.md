# Well test: W-23, 2026-07-15

**Document id:** WT-025
**Well:** W-23 (Field B, vertical, ESP)
**Test date:** 2026-07-15
**Procedure:** SOP-OPS-004 Well Test Procedure with the Mobile Test Separator

## Test conditions
- Duration: 12 hours through the mobile test separator
- Choke: 32/64 in
- Flowing wellhead pressure: 512 psi
- Well status today: producing

|---|---|

## Comparison with baseline
The previous baseline covers the 7 days to 2026-05-16: oil 929.2 bbl/d, gas 508.2 Mscf/d, water cut 33.7 %, GOR 547 scf/bbl.

- Oil is down 230.8 bbl/d against the baseline.
- Gas is down 129.1 Mscf/d, in line with the oil decline.
- Water cut is up 15.4 points.
- GOR is essentially unchanged (543 against 547 scf/bbl), so the gas decline follows liquid loss, not a change in gas behaviour.

## Observations
- Sand is negligible at 2 ppm.
- The water cut rise is consistent with event EV-0126 (water breakthrough, 2026-06-14 to 2026-06-15, closed, 8 hours downtime). That event was attributed to injector water breakthrough from the north flank, and this test shows the higher water cut persisting.
- The separator readings were stable during the test.

## Recommendation
- Adopt this test as the new baseline for W-23.
- Repeat the test in 30 days to track the water cut trend.
- Production Engineer to review the ESP operating point against the higher water cut and to discuss injection allocation on the north flank with the reservoir team.
