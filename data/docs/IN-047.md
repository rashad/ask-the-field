# Incident note: Wellhead leak on W-08

**Document ID:** IN-047
**Event ID:** EV-0119
**Note date:** 2026-05-27
**Well:** W-08 (Field A, vertical, ESP)
**Category:** Incident
**Severity:** Medium
**Status at note date:** Closed
**Author:** Production Engineer

## What happened
On 2026-05-27 a leak was found on the wellhead valve stem packing of W-08. The well was taken offline to make the repair.

## Immediate actions
- The well was shut in and the wellhead area isolated and barricaded.
- The Lead Operator and HSE Advisor were informed, and the area was checked for gas before work started.
- The maintenance crew replaced the valve stem packing.
- After the repair, the valve was leak tested at working pressure with no leakage observed, and the well was returned to service.

## Findings
The leak came from worn packing on the valve stem. No other wellhead components showed signs of leakage. No injuries and no reportable release were recorded.

## Impact
- Downtime: 10 hours on 2026-05-27 (uptime 14 hours).
- Production on the day: 651 bbl oil and 463.9 Mscf gas, water cut 26.9%.
- Baseline for the previous 7 days to 2026-05-26: 1,178.5 bbl/d oil, 852.4 Mscf/d gas, water cut 26.6%, GOR 723 scf/bbl.
- Deferred oil is therefore roughly 528 bbl against the baseline rate. Water cut is unchanged within normal variation.

## Status
Closed as of 2026-05-27. The well is producing and no restrictions remain. No related events were logged on this well in the previous 30 days.

## Next actions
- Include W-08 in the next routine wellhead valve inspection round and check the packing condition.
- Production Engineer to confirm rates have returned to baseline over the next few days.
- Review whether similar packing on other Field A wellheads should be checked during routine rounds.

## Applicable procedure
No specific procedure applies to this event.
