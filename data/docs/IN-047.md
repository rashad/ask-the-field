# Incident note: Power outage on W-28

**Document ID:** IN-047
**Event ID:** EV-0121
**Note date:** 2026-05-27
**Well:** W-28 (Wadi Field, deviated, gas lift)
**Severity:** Low
**Status at note date:** Closed
**Prepared by:** Production Engineer

## What happened
A field power dip on 2026-05-27 tripped W-28. The well went offline and was restarted once power was restored.

## Immediate actions
- The Lead Operator confirmed the power supply was stable before any restart was attempted.
- Wellhead and flowline pressures were checked, with no abnormal readings or leaks found.
- The gas lift supply was verified and the well was brought back online in a controlled manner.
- The Production Supervisor was informed of the trip and the restart.

## Findings
The trip was caused by the external power dip. No fault was found on the well or on the gas lift equipment. After restart the well returned to stable flow.

## Impact
- Downtime: 5 hours (uptime on the day was 19 hours).
- Production on 2026-05-27: 389.7 bbl of oil and 302.5 Mscf of gas, with water cut at 39.6%.
- Baseline for the 7 days to 2026-05-26: 500.1 bbl/d oil, 409.5 Mscf/d gas, water cut 40.9%, GOR 819 scf/bbl.
- Oil lost against baseline on the day was about 110 bbl, caused by the outage hours. Water cut and GOR stayed in line with the baseline.

## Status
Closed. W-28 is producing normally. No related events were recorded on this well in the previous 30 days.

## Next actions
- The Lead Operator will keep watching W-28 rates on the next two shifts to confirm they match the baseline.
- No corrective work is needed on the well.

## Applicable procedure
No specific procedure applies to this event.
