# Incident note: Flowline leak on W-16

**Document ID:** IN-003
**Event ID:** EV-0004
**Note date:** 2026-06-06 

**Well:** W-16 (Field A, deviated, ESP)
**Severity:** Medium
**Status at note date:** Closed
**Applicable procedure:** SOP-HSE-006 Flowline Leak Response

## What happened
On 2025-06-06 a small leak was found on a flowline flange on W-16. The well was taken off line so the flowline could be isolated and the flange repaired.

## Immediate actions
- Well shut down and the flowline isolated and depressurised.
- Area barricaded and the Production Supervisor informed.
- Small amount of released fluid contained and cleaned up. No injuries and no reported release outside the pad.
- The HSE Advisor attended the site and confirmed the isolation before work started.

## Findings
The leak was confined to the flange joint. The flange was repaired and the line was checked before restart. No other leaks were seen on the flowline during the walkdown.

## Impact
- Downtime: 12 hours (uptime on the day was 12 hours).
- Production on 2025-06-06: 724.5 bbl oil, 664.5 Mscf gas, water cut 17.9%.
- Baseline for the previous 7 days (to 2025-06-05): 1509 bbl/d oil, 1376.6 Mscf/d gas, water cut 17.5%, GOR 912 scf/bbl.
- Deferred oil is about 785 bbl against the baseline rate (1509 minus 724.5).

## Status
Closed. The leak was isolated and repaired on 2025-06-06 and the well returned to production. There were no related events on W-16 in the previous 30 days.

## Next actions
- Production Engineer to confirm that rates return to the baseline range over the next few days.
- Lead Operator to include the repaired flange in the routine flowline rounds.
- Integrity Engineer to review flange condition on neighbouring Field A flowlines at the next scheduled inspection.

The response followed SOP-HSE-006 Flowline Leak Response.
