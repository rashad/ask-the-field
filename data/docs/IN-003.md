# Incident note: Sand production alarm on W-08

**Document ID:** IN-003
**Event ID:** EV-0012
**Note date:** 2025-07-11
**Author:** Production Engineer
**Well:** W-08 (Dune Field, vertical, ESP)
**Severity:** High
**Status:** Closed

## What happened
On 2025-07-11 the sand monitor on W-08 raised a sand production alarm. The Lead Operator confirmed the alarm on the local panel and reported it to the Production Engineer.

## Immediate actions
- The choke was reduced until the sand monitor readings returned to normal.
- The wellhead, flowline and sand monitor probe were inspected for leaks and signs of erosion. Nothing abnormal was found.
- The Production Supervisor and the CPF-1 control room were informed of the restriction.
- Once readings were stable, the well was brought back to production.

## Findings so far
- Sand readings fell back to normal after the choke reduction and stayed normal after the restart.
- Water cut on the day was 23.5%, in line with the 7-day average of 23.8% before the event. No change in water production was seen.
- No related events on W-08 in the previous 30 days.

## Impact
- Downtime: 6 hours (uptime 18 hours on 2025-07-11).
- Production on the day: 1008.9 bbl oil and 786.3 Mscf gas.
- Baseline for the 7 days to 2025-07-10: 1385.1 bbl/d oil, 1015.1 Mscf/d gas, GOR 733 scf/bbl.
- Deferred oil is about 376 bbl against the baseline rate.

## Status at note date
Closed. W-08 is producing and the alarm has cleared.

## Next actions
- The Lead Operator will monitor sand readings at each shift check for the next several days.
- The Production Engineer will review the choke setting against the sand trend before any increase in rate.
- The event is handled under SOP-OPS-002 Sand Management and Sand Production Alarms. Operators should follow it if the alarm returns.
