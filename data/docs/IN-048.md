# Incident note: Sand production alarm on W-06

**Document ID:** IN-048
**Event ID:** EV-0124
**Note date:** 2026-06-08
**Well:** W-06 (Field A, deviated, natural flow)
**Severity:** High
**Status at note date:** Closed
**Applicable procedure:** SOP-OPS-002 Sand Management and Sand Production Alarms

## What happened
On 2026-06-08 the sand monitor on the W-06 flowline went into alarm. The Lead Operator confirmed the alarm was not a spurious signal by checking the monitor trend and the local readout, then reduced the choke to bring sand readings down. The choke stayed restricted until readings returned to normal.

## Immediate actions
- Choke reduced in steps and readings observed after each step.
- Production Supervisor informed of the alarm and the restriction.
- Downstream separator and CPF-1 inlet checked for any sign of sand carryover; nothing abnormal reported.
- Well kept under observation until readings were stable.

## Findings
- The alarm cleared after the choke reduction and readings stayed normal afterwards.
- No equipment damage was reported.
- Water cut on the day was 17.4%, in line with the previous baseline of 18.4%.

## Impact
- Downtime: 9 hours (uptime 15 hours on 2026-06-08).
- Production on the day: 471 bbl oil and 397.5 Mscf gas.
- Prior 7-day average to 2026-06-07: 682.2 bbl/d oil, 580.7 Mscf/d gas, GOR 851 scf/bbl.
- Deferred oil is the shortfall against the prior average, roughly 211 bbl for the day.

## Status
The incident is closed. W-06 is producing today. No related events were recorded in the previous 30 days.

## Next actions
- Production Engineer to review the sand trend for W-06 over the coming days and confirm the restored choke setting is acceptable.
- Lead Operator to continue routine sand monitor checks per SOP-OPS-002.
- Any repeat alarm to be escalated to the Production Supervisor for a review of the allowable drawdown.
