# Incident note: Power outage on W-31

**Document ID:** IN-048
**Event ID:** EV-0122
**Note date:** 2026-05-30
**Author:** Production Engineer
**Well:** W-31 (Salt Flat Field, vertical, natural flow)
**Severity:** High
**Status at note date:** Closed

## What happened
On 2026-05-30 a field power dip tripped W-31. The well was restarted after power was restored to the site.

## Immediate actions
- The Lead Operator confirmed the trip was caused by the power dip and not by a well or flowline fault.
- Once power was stable, the well was restarted and the wellhead and flowline were checked for normal pressures.
- The Production Supervisor was informed of the trip and the restart.

## Findings
- The well tripped on loss of supply and returned to stable flow after the restart.
- No equipment damage was reported.
- Uptime on the day was 16 hours, so downtime was 8 hours.

## Impact
- Downtime: 8 hours on 2026-05-30.
- Production on the day: 1061.6 bbl oil, 2030.6 Mscf gas, water cut 17.4 %.
- Baseline for the 7 days to 2026-05-29: 1576.6 bbl/d oil, 2944 Mscf/d gas, water cut 16.8 %, GOR 1867 scf/bbl.
- Deferred oil against baseline: about 515 bbl for the day (1576.6 minus 1061.6 = 515.0).
- The water cut on the day was 0.6 points above the baseline, which is a small difference and is noted for monitoring only.

## Status
Closed as of 2026-05-30. The well is producing today. No related events were recorded in the previous 30 days.

## Next actions
- The Lead Operator will keep watching W-31 rates and water cut over the next few shifts to confirm they return to baseline.
- The Production Engineer will review with the Maintenance Planner whether the field power dip needs a follow-up check on the supply side.
- No specific procedure applies to this event.
