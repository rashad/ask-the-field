# Incident note: High separator level trip on W-03

**Document ID:** IN-052
**Note date:** 2026-06-24
**Event ID:** EV-0130
**Well:** W-03 (Dune Field, horizontal, ESP)
**Severity:** High
**Status at note date:** Closed

## What happened
On 2026-06-24 the test separator high-level trip activated and shut in W-03. The well was offline for 5 hours before it was restarted. The Lead Operator confirmed the trip was not caused by a real liquid carry-over problem, and the level controller was found to be reading out of calibration.

## Immediate actions
- Well shut in by the trip logic, wellhead and flowline isolated and visually checked for leaks (none found).
- Production Engineer and Production Supervisor informed.
- Level controller on the test separator recalibrated by the instrument technician and verified against the gauge glass.
- Well restarted after the level returned to normal and the trip was reset.

## Findings so far
- The trip was triggered by a faulty level controller calibration on the test separator.
- No damage to the separator or downstream equipment was observed.
- ESP parameters were normal after the restart.

## Impact
- Downtime: 5 hours (uptime on the day 19 hours).
- Production on 2026-06-24: oil 1319.9 bbl, gas 1296.6 Mscf, water cut 7.6%.
- Previous 7-day average to 2026-06-23: oil 1632 bbl/d, gas 1654.4 Mscf/d, water cut 9.7%, GOR 1014 scf/bbl.
- Deferred oil for the day is roughly 312 bbl against the 7-day average rate.

## Status
The incident is closed. W-03 is producing normally. No related events were recorded in the previous 30 days.

## Next actions
- Add the controller to the next routine instrument calibration round and check the drift trend.
- Operators to compare the controller reading against the gauge glass at each shift start for the next week.

## Procedure reference
No specific procedure applies to this event.
