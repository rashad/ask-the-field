# Incident note: Wellhead leak on W-03

**Document ID:** IN-052
**Note date:** 2026-07-10
**Event ID:** EV-0140
**Well:** W-03 (Field A, horizontal, ESP)
**Severity:** medium
**Status at note date:** closed
**Author:** Production Engineer

## What happened
A leak was found on the wellhead valve stem packing of W-03 on 2026-07-10. The well was taken offline so the leak could be isolated and repaired.

## Immediate actions
- The well was shut in and the wellhead area isolated and barricaded.
- The Production Supervisor and HSE Advisor were informed.
- Residual fluid was cleaned up and the area was checked for gas before work began.
- The valve stem packing was replaced and the valve was function-tested and leak-tested at working pressure.
- The well was returned to production the same day.

## Findings
The leak came from worn stem packing on the wellhead valve. No other leak points were found during the inspection of the wellhead. No injuries and no reportable release were recorded.

## Impact
- Downtime: 8 hours (uptime on the day was 16 hours).
- Production on 2026-07-10: oil 1016.3 bbl, gas 992.3 Mscf, water cut 10.5%.
- Baseline for the previous 7 days (to 2026-07-09): oil 1647.4 bbl/d, gas 1637.3 Mscf/d, water cut 8.4%, GOR 994 scf/bbl.
- The shortfall in oil against the baseline for the day was about 631 bbl. The higher water cut on the day is likely a restart effect and will be watched.

## Status
Closed. The repair is complete and the well is producing. No related events were logged in the previous 30 days.

## Next actions
- The Lead Operator will check the repaired packing at each round for the next several days.
- The Production Engineer will confirm that water cut returns toward the 8.4% baseline.
- The Maintenance Planner will add the stem packing condition on similar wellhead valves to the next inspection round.

## Procedure reference
No specific procedure applies to this event.
