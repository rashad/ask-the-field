# Incident note: Sand production alarm on W-24

**Document ID:** IN-018
**Note date:** 2025-12-15
**Event ID:** EV-0064
**Well:** W-24 (Wadi Field, deviated, ESP)
**Severity:** Low
**Status at note date:** Closed
**Applicable procedure:** SOP-OPS-002 Sand Management and Sand Production Alarms

## What happened
On 2025-12-15 the sand monitor on W-24 raised a sand production alarm. The Lead Operator reduced the choke until the sand readings returned to normal, in line with the procedure.

## Immediate actions
- Choke reduced on W-24 and sand monitor readings watched until they normalised.
- Separator and CPF-1 inlet checked for visible solids carry-over; nothing abnormal reported.
- Production Supervisor informed.

## Findings so far
- Water cut on the day was 26.3 percent, against a 7-day average of 24 percent to 2025-12-14.
- No other wells in Wadi Field reported sand alarms during the same period.
- Cause of the sand release is not established. The event was short and readings recovered after the choke reduction.

## Impact
- Downtime: 13 hours. Recorded uptime on the day was 11 hours.
- Production on 2025-12-15: oil 598.8 bbl, gas 325.1 Mscf, water cut 26.3 percent.
- Prior 7-day average to 2025-12-14: oil 1371.5 bbl/d, gas 727.1 Mscf/d, water cut 24 percent, GOR 530 scf/bbl.
- Deferred oil is the shortfall against that average, approximately 773 bbl for the day.

## Status
Closed as of 2025-12-15. W-24 is producing.

## Next actions
- Production Engineer to review sand trend and choke position at the next routine well review, per SOP-OPS-002.
- Operators to keep monitoring sand readings on W-24 on each shift.
- Another sand alarm on W-24 is planned or expected on 2026-01-11 (EV-0073, medium severity, not yet occurred). Handling will follow the same choke-reduction approach.
