# Incident note: Power outage on W-28

**Document ID:** IN-018
**Event ID:** EV-0055
**Note date:** 2025-12-03
**Author:** Production Engineer
**Well:** W-28 (Field B, vertical, ESP)
**Severity:** High
**Status at note date:** Closed

## What happened
A field power dip on 2025-12-03 tripped the ESP on W-28. The well was restarted after power was restored and returned to production the same day.

## Immediate actions
- Lead Operator confirmed the trip was caused by the supply dip and not by a fault on the well.
- Power restoration confirmed at the CPF-1 switchboard before any restart attempt.
- ESP restarted by the operator after the drive and wellhead readings were checked and found normal.
- Production Supervisor informed of the trip and restart.

## Findings
- The trip was caused by the field power dip. No equipment damage was found on the ESP drive or the wellhead.
- The well restarted without difficulty and was stable after the restart.

## Impact
- Downtime: 10 hours on 2025-12-03 (uptime 14 hours).
- Production on the day: oil 771.3 bbl, gas 768.5 Mscf, water cut 35.2%.
- Previous 7-day baseline to 2025-12-02: oil 1361.8 bbl/d, gas 1332.7 Mscf/d, water cut 33.7%, GOR 979 scf/bbl.
- Oil on the day was about 590.5 bbl below the baseline rate, which is the deferred oil from this event.
- Water cut was slightly higher than baseline (35.2% against 33.7%), which is consistent with a restart after a stop. It will be checked over the next days.

## Status
Closed. W-28 is producing. No related events were recorded in the previous 30 days.

## Next actions
1. Production Engineer to review W-28 rates and water cut over the next few days to confirm return to baseline.
2. Maintenance Planner to pass the outage record to the power supply team for review of the dip.
3. Lead Operator to include the event in the shift handover.

## Procedure reference
No specific procedure applies to this event.
