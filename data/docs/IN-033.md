# Incident note: Wellhead leak on W-03

**Document ID:** IN-033
**Event ID:** EV-0093
**Note date:** 2026-03-06
**Well:** W-03 (Dune Field, horizontal, ESP)
**Severity:** Medium
**Status at note date:** Closed
**Prepared by:** Production Engineer

## What happened
On 2026-03-06 a leak was found on the wellhead valve stem packing of W-03. The Lead Operator reported it during the routine round, and the well was taken off line to repair it.

## Immediate actions
- Well shut in and the wellhead area isolated and barricaded.
- Area checked for residual hydrocarbon and gas before work started.
- Maintenance crew replaced the valve stem packing.
- Leak test of the repaired valve carried out before restart, with no leakage observed.
- Well restarted and the wellhead inspected again after return to flow.

## Findings
The leak came from worn packing on the valve stem. No damage to the valve body or the wellhead was found. No injuries and no release outside the wellhead area were reported.

## Impact
- Downtime: 9 hours on 2026-03-06 (uptime 15 hours).
- Production on the day: 1,151.8 bbl oil and 1,166.5 Mscf gas, water cut 7.0%.
- Baseline for the 7 days to 2026-03-05: 1,683.8 bbl/d oil, 1,690.3 Mscf/d gas, water cut 7.1%, GOR 1,004 scf/bbl.
- Oil deferred on the day is about 532 bbl against the baseline rate.

## Related events
Flowline pigging on W-03 (EV-0091) was done on 2026-03-03 with 8 hours of downtime, to remove wax and sand deposits. It was a separate, closed activity, and this leak was found on the valve stem packing.

## Status and next actions
The incident is closed as of 2026-03-06. W-03 is producing.
- Include the valve in the next scheduled wellhead inspection round.
- Record the packing replacement in the maintenance history.

## Procedure reference
No specific procedure applies to this event.
