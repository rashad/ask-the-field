# Incident note: Sand production alarm on W-20

**Document id:** IN-033
**Note date:** 2026-03-09
**Event id:** EV-0091
**Well:** W-20 (Field B, deviated, gas lift)
**Severity:** high
**Status at note date:** closed
**Applicable procedure:** SOP-OPS-002 Sand Management and Sand Production Alarms

## What happened
On 2026-03-09 the sand monitor on W-20 went into alarm. The Lead Operator reduced the choke until the sand readings returned to normal. The event started and ended on the same day.

## Immediate actions
- Choke reduced in steps while the sand monitor trend was watched.
- Wellhead and flowline checked for leaks and abnormal noise, none found.
- Production Supervisor and Production Engineer informed.
- Readings held under observation after they normalised before the well was left on its adjusted setting.

## Findings so far
- Sand readings stabilised once the choke was reduced, which points to a rate-related sand response.
- The last wellhead work on W-20 was valve greasing and a function test on 2026-02-11 (EV-0084, 3 hours downtime). No link to the alarm has been identified.

## Impact
- Downtime: 14 hours as of this note.
- Production on 2026-03-09: oil 328.4 bbl, gas 316.8 Mscf, water cut 33 percent, uptime 10 hours.
- Baseline for the 7 days to 2026-03-08: oil 741.6 bbl/d, gas 739.8 Mscf/d, water cut 33.4 percent, GOR 998 scf/bbl.
- Water cut was essentially unchanged against the baseline, so the loss comes from reduced uptime and the lower choke.

## Status
Closed as of 2026-03-09. W-20 is producing today.

## Next actions
- Production Engineer to review the sand trend and the choke setting against the SOP-OPS-002 limits.
- Lead Operator to keep the sand monitor on routine checks each shift.
- Consider a sand sample at the next test to confirm the trend.
