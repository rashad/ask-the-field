# Work order: ESP inspection, W-01

**WO number:** WO-018
**Event reference:** EV-0028
**Well:** W-01 (Field A, vertical, ESP)
**Category:** Maintenance
**Priority:** Routine
**Date raised:** 2025-08-31
**Planned start / actual start:** 2025-09-02 / 2025-09-02
**Completion date:** 2025-09-02
**Status:** Closed

## Scope of work
Routine ESP surface equipment inspection and VSD parameter check.
- Inspect the VSD cabinet, cooling fans, filters and terminations.
- Check the junction box, wellhead power feedthrough and cable glands.
- Record VSD parameters (frequency, motor current, voltage, intake and discharge readings) and compare them with the last saved set.
- Confirm trip and alarm setpoints are unchanged.

## Safety and permits
- Electrical isolation and lock-out/tag-out applied before cabinet work.
- Work permit and isolation certificate issued by the Production Supervisor.
- Well shut in for the duration of the job, with the Lead Operator informed at start and finish.

## Materials
- Replacement cabinet filter (on hand, not required).
- Torque tools, thermal camera and calibrated multimeter.

## Close-out remarks
- Inspection completed on 2025-09-02 with no defects found on surface equipment.
- VSD parameters were within the expected range and setpoints matched the saved configuration.
- Well was off production for 6 hours and was restarted the same day without issues.
- No follow-up work required.
