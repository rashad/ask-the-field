## Scope of work

Subsurface safety valve function and leak test on W-16.

1. Shut in the well and isolate it from the flowline.
2. Function test the valve and record the closing and opening response.
3. Carry out the leak test and record the result against the acceptance criterion.
4. Restore the well to production and confirm stable flow.

## Safety and permits

- Work permit and isolation certificate issued before start.
- Toolbox talk held with the operations crew and the slickline unit crew.
- HSE Advisor informed of the shutdown. No hydrocarbon release expected.
- Wellhead area barricaded during testing.

## Materials

- Test pump and calibrated gauges
- Standard seals and fittings (no replacements used)

## Close-out remarks

The test was completed on 2025-08-26 and the well was returned to production the same day. Total downtime was 5 hours. The valve function and leak test were completed without any need for repair, and no follow-up work was raised. The Lead Operator confirmed normal wellhead readings after restart.
