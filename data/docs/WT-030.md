## Observations

- Separator readings stabilised during the last hours of the test, and the Lead Operator saw no slugging.
- Sand is low at 2 ppm.
- No events were recorded on the well in the last 45 days.

## Recommendation

- The oil loss with rising water cut points to water breakthrough rather than a mechanical problem. The Production Engineer should review the ESP performance data and the pump intake conditions.
- Repeat the test within the normal test interval to confirm the trend.
- Update the well's baseline in the production model after review.
