## Scope of work
Subsurface safety valve function and leak test on W-20. Close the well in, function the valve, run the leak test, record results and return the well to production.

## Safety and permits
- Work permit and isolation certificate issued before start.
- Toolbox talk held with the operations crew and the slickline unit crew.
- Wellhead area barricaded; gas detection checked before work.
- Production Supervisor informed of the planned shut-in.

## Materials
- Test pump and calibrated gauges
- Hydraulic control line fittings
- Test record sheet

## Execution notes and close-out remarks
- Well shut in on 2025-08-11 for the test. Total downtime was 4 hours.
- Valve function test completed and the valve held the leak test. No defects found and no repairs required.
- Control line and wellhead connections checked for leaks after the test, none observed.
- Well restarted the same day and ESP run-up was normal.
- Test results filed in the well file.
