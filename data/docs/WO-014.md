# Work order: Wellhead maintenance, W-38

**WO number:** WO-014
**Event reference:** EV-0020
**Well:** W-38 (Bayda, deviated, gas lift)
**Category:** Maintenance
**Priority:** Routine
**Date raised:** 2025-08-03
**Planned start:** 2025-08-05
**Actual start:** 2025-08-05
**Completion date:** 2025-08-05
**Status:** Closed

## Scope of work
- Grease all wellhead valves (master, wing and swab) at the designated grease points.
- Function test each valve through full travel and record any stiffness or leakage.
- Return the well to production after the test.

## Safety and permits
- Work permit issued by the Production Supervisor for the work area.
- Well shut in for the duration of the job, with wellhead isolation confirmed before work started.
- Toolbox talk held with the crew. Gas detection carried by the crew, and Bayda wells are treated as high GOR.

## Materials
- Valve grease (approved grade for the wellhead valves)
- Grease gun and fittings
- Torque wrench and standard hand tools

## Execution notes and close-out
- Work started and finished on 2025-08-05.
- All valves greased and function tested. Valves moved through full travel with no leaks observed.
- Downtime for the job: 2 hours. The well was restarted the same day.
- Production, 7-day average to 2025-08-04: oil 1551.3 bbl/d, gas 2300.3 Mscf/d, water cut 24.5%, GOR 1483 scf/bbl.
- Production, 6-day average after restart to 2025-08-12: oil 1522.7 bbl/d, gas 2329.1 Mscf/d, water cut 25%, GOR 1530 scf/bbl.
- Rates are broadly in line with the pre-job period. No further action is needed for this order.

## Follow-up
- A subsurface safety valve function and leak test on W-38 (EV-0022) is planned from 2025-08-11 under a separate work order.

**Close-out:** Work complete, order closed.
