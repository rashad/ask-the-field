# Incident note: Sand production alarm on W-21

**Document ID:** IN-025
**Note date:** 2026-01-12
**Event ID:** EV-0070
**Well:** W-21 (Field B, horizontal, ESP)
**Severity:** Low
**Status at note date:** Closed
**Applicable procedure:** SOP-OPS-002 Sand Management and Sand Production Alarms

## What happened
On 2026-01-12 the sand monitor on W-21 raised an alarm. The choke was reduced until the sand readings returned to normal.

## Immediate actions
- Operator acknowledged the alarm and confirmed the reading against the monitor trend.
- Choke was reduced in steps and the sand monitor was watched after each step.
- Production Supervisor was informed of the alarm and the restriction.
- Surface lines and the sand monitor probe area were checked for visible leaks or abnormal noise. Nothing abnormal was reported.

## Findings so far
- Readings returned to normal after the choke reduction, and no further alarms were recorded.
- Water cut on the day was 39.3%, in line with the 7-day average of 39.8% to 2026-01-11. No water breakthrough is indicated.
- No related events were logged on this well in the preceding 30 days.

## Impact
- Downtime: 12 hours on 2026-01-12 (uptime 12 hours).
- Production on the day: oil 521.4 bbl, gas 396.5 Mscf.
- Baseline for the 7 days to 2026-01-11: oil 1027.5 bbl/d, gas 760.7 Mscf/d, GOR 740 scf/bbl.
- Oil on the day was about half of the baseline daily rate, consistent with the 12 hours of downtime.

## Status
Closed as of 2026-01-12. W-21 is producing.

## Next actions
- Production Engineer to review the sand trend over the next few days as part of routine surveillance.
- Operators to keep the choke at the reduced setting until the engineer confirms the return to normal operation, following SOP-OPS-002.
- Re-open the incident if the alarm repeats.
