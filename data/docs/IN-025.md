# Incident note: Wellhead leak on W-02

**Document ID:** IN-025
**Note date:** 2026-02-06
**Event ID:** EV-0080
**Well:** W-02 (Dune Field, deviated, gas lift)
**Severity:** Medium
**Status at note date:** Closed
**Written by:** Production Engineer

## What happened
A leak was found on the wellhead valve stem packing at W-02 on 2026-02-06. The well was taken offline so the leak could be repaired safely.

## Immediate actions
- The well was shut in and the wellhead area isolated and barricaded.
- The Lead Operator informed the Production Supervisor and the HSE Advisor.
- The stem packing was replaced by the maintenance crew.
- After the repair, the valve and surrounding connections were checked for leaks before the well was returned to service.

## Findings
The leak came from worn stem packing on the wellhead valve. No other leak points were found during the inspection. No injuries were reported.

## Impact
- Downtime: 8 hours on 2026-02-06 (uptime 16 hours).
- Production on the day: 922.7 bbl oil, 444.5 Mscf gas, water cut 21.8 percent.
- Baseline for the 7 days to 2026-02-05: 1379.1 bbl/d oil, 686.4 Mscf/d gas, water cut 21.3 percent, GOR 498 scf/bbl.
- Deferred oil against the baseline: about 456 bbl.

## Status
Closed. The packing was replaced, the leak test passed and the well is producing. No related events were recorded in the previous 30 days.

## Next actions
- Production Engineer to confirm that rates return to the baseline in the next daily allocation.
- Maintenance Planner to add the wellhead valve stem packing on W-02 to the next routine inspection round.
- No specific procedure applies to this event.
