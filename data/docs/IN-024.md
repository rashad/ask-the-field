# Incident note: H2S alarm on W-23

**Document ID:** IN-024
**Note date:** 2026-01-10
**Event ID:** EV-0069
**Well:** W-23 (Field B, vertical, ESP)
**Severity:** Medium
**Status at note date:** Closed
**Applicable procedure:** SOP-HSE-003 H2S Alarm Response

## What happened
On 2026-01-10 the fixed H2S detector near the W-23 wellhead went into alarm. The alarm was acknowledged in the control room and the well pad was treated as a gas release area until proven otherwise.

## Immediate actions
- The area around the wellhead was cleared and access was barred.
- The Production Supervisor and the HSE Advisor were informed.
- The well was taken offline while the source was investigated.
- Re-entry was made by a two-person team with portable gas detectors and breathing apparatus, as required by SOP-HSE-003.

## Findings
The source was a leak on a needle valve at the wellhead. The valve was repaired and the joint was leak tested. Portable and fixed detector readings returned to normal before the area was reopened. No injuries were reported.

## Impact
- Downtime: 5 hours on 2026-01-10.
- W-23 uptime on the day was 19 hours.
- Production on the day: oil 828.5 bbl, gas 449.8 Mscf, water cut 30.7%.
- The 7-day average to 2026-01-09 was 1049.3 bbl/d oil, 556.9 Mscf/d gas, water cut 30.5%, GOR 531 scf/bbl. The deferred oil is the shortfall against that baseline.
- The water cut on the day is in line with the baseline.

## Related history
The scale inhibitor squeeze on W-23 (EV-0063, 2025-12-24 to 2025-12-25) was completed earlier and is closed. The leak is not linked to that treatment, but the Integrity Engineer will check the needle valve fittings when reviewing the squeeze work.

## Status and next actions
The incident is closed and W-23 is producing normally. Next actions:
1. Production Engineer to confirm the valve replacement is recorded in the maintenance system.
2. Integrity Engineer to include the other wellhead needle valves on the pad in the next routine leak inspection.
3. HSE Advisor to review the alarm response at the next toolbox talk.
