# Incident note: Power outage on W-41

**Document ID:** IN-024
**Event ID:** EV-0078
**Note date:** 2026-02-04
**Well:** W-41 (Salt Flat Field, vertical, natural flow)
**Category / severity:** Incident / medium
**Status at note date:** Closed
**Prepared by:** Production Engineer

## What happened
On 2026-02-04 a field power dip tripped W-41. The well went down on the trip and was restarted once power was restored. The event started and ended on the same day.

## Immediate actions
- Lead Operator confirmed the power dip on the field supply and that the trip was not caused by a well-side fault.
- After power was restored, the wellsite control panel and instrumentation were checked and the well was restarted.
- Flowline pressure and choke position were checked after restart and the well was returned to normal flow.

## Findings
- Cause: a field power dip. No equipment damage was reported on W-41.
- Summary: field power dip tripped the well; restarted after power restored.
- The well is natural flow, so there was no artificial lift equipment to recover after the restart.

## Impact
- Downtime: 11 hours as of the note date (uptime on the day: 13 hours).
- Production on 2026-02-04: oil 960.5 bbl, gas 1502.1 Mscf, water cut 18 percent.
- Baseline for the 7 days to 2026-02-03: oil 1729.6 bbl/d, gas 2636.7 Mscf/d, water cut 16.9 percent, GOR 1524 scf/bbl.
- Oil on the day was about 769 bbl below the baseline rate, which is the approximate deferred oil. The water cut was slightly above baseline on the day, which is consistent with a well settling after a restart.

## Status
Closed. W-41 is producing normally.

## Next actions
- Production Engineer to review water cut and rates over the next few days to confirm they return to baseline.
- Maintenance Planner to pass the power dip to the facility power team for follow-up on supply stability.
- No applicable procedure is on record for this event type.
