# Work order: Choke replacement, W-34

**WO number:** WO-004
**Well:** W-34 (Bayda, horizontal, gas lift)
**Category:** Maintenance
**Priority:** Medium
**Raised:** 2025-06-15
**Planned start:** 2025-06-17
**Planned completion:** 2025-06-17
**Actual start / completion:** 2025-06-17 / 2025-06-17
**Status:** Closed
**Reference event:** EV-0008

## Scope of work
Replace the eroded choke bean on the W-34 flowline choke. Isolate the well, depressurize the choke body, remove the worn bean, fit a new bean of the same size, pressure test and return the well to production.

## Safety and permits
- Hot work not required; cold work permit issued.
- Isolation and lock-out of the wellhead wing valve and flowline valve confirmed by the Lead Operator.
- Gas test of the choke area before breaking containment.
- Toolbox talk held with the crew before start. HSE Advisor informed.
- No applicable procedure is referenced for this job.

## Materials
- 1 x replacement choke bean
- 1 x seal and gasket set for the choke body
- Standard hand tools and torque wrench

## Execution notes
Well shut in, choke body depressurized and bled to the closed drain. The old bean showed clear erosion on the bore, consistent with the sand-prone production at Bayda. The new bean and seals were fitted and the choke body was pressure tested with no leaks observed. The well was opened up and flow stabilized.

## Close-out remarks
- Work completed on 2025-06-17. Downtime on W-34: 6 hours.
- Production, 7 days to 2025-06-16: oil 1538.8 bbl/d, gas 2780 Mscf/d, water cut 23.9%, GOR 1807 scf/bbl.
- Production after restart, 6 days to 2025-06-24: oil 1557.9 bbl/d, gas 2842.5 Mscf/d, water cut 23.2%, GOR 1825 scf/bbl.
- Rates are stable and slightly above the pre-job level. No related events in the following 30 days.
- Recommend the Production Engineer keep W-34 choke inspection on the routine sand-monitoring round.
