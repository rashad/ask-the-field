# Work order: Flowline pigging, W-15

**WO number:** WO-004
**Well:** W-15 (Dune Field, deviated, ESP)
**Related event:** EV-0006
**Category:** Maintenance
**Priority:** Medium
**Raised:** 2025-06-16
**Planned start / end:** 2025-06-18 / 2025-06-18
**Actual start / end:** 2025-06-18 / 2025-06-18
**Status:** Closed

## Scope of work
Flowline pigging to remove wax and sand deposits from the W-15 flowline to CPF-1. Launch the pig at the wellsite launcher, receive it at the CPF-1 receiver, and record the debris volume recovered.

## Safety and permits
- Hot work not required. Cold work permit and line-breaking permit issued.
- Flowline depressurised and isolated before opening the launcher. Pressure confirmed at zero before closure removal.
- HSE Advisor briefed the crew on pig handling, residual pressure and the handling of waxy, sand-laden debris.
- Gas detector in use at the receiver during the retrieval.

## Materials
- Foam-bodied cleaning pig, one spare on site
- Launcher and receiver closure seals
- Waste containers for wax and sand debris

## Execution notes
- The well was shut in for the job and restarted after the pig was received. Total downtime was 6 hours.
- The pig ran through without sticking. Wax and sand deposits were recovered at the receiver and bagged for disposal.
- Launcher and receiver closures were reinstated and leak-tested before the line was returned to service.

## Close-out remarks
- Production in the 14 days before the job (to 2025-06-17) averaged 1539.9 bbl/d oil, 984.6 Mscf/d gas, 11 % water cut, GOR 639 scf/bbl.
- For the 6 days after restart (to 2025-06-25) the averages were 1535.6 bbl/d oil, 977.7 Mscf/d gas, 10.6 % water cut, GOR 637 scf/bbl. Rates are essentially unchanged from before the job.
- Work complete and the work order is closed. The Production Engineer was informed.
- A preventive scale inhibitor squeeze on W-15 (EV-0009) is planned from 2025-06-28 and will be handled under a separate work order.
