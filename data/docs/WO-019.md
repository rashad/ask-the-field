# Work order: ESP inspection, W-10

**WO number:** WO-019
**Event reference:** EV-0029
**Well:** W-10 (Field A, vertical, ESP)
**Category:** Maintenance
**Priority:** Routine
**Raised:** 2025-08-31
**Planned start:** 2025-09-02
**Actual start:** 2025-09-02
**Completion:** 2025-09-02
**Status:** Closed

## Scope of work
Routine inspection of ESP surface equipment on W-10 and check of VSD parameters.
1. Shut down the well and isolate the ESP power supply.
2. Inspect the VSD cabinet, junction box, wellhead feed-through and cable terminations.
3. Check the VSD parameters against the commissioning setpoints (frequency, motor current, under-load and overload trips).
4. Restart the well and confirm stable operation.

## Safety and permits
- Electrical isolation and lock-out/tag-out applied before cabinet access.
- Work permit issued by the Production Supervisor.
- Standard PPE plus arc-rated gloves for cabinet work.
- No applicable procedure is referenced for this job.

## Materials
- Contact cleaner, torque tools, thermal camera.
- No spares consumed.

## Execution notes
Well shut down on 2025-09-02 for the inspection. Surface equipment was found in acceptable condition and the VSD parameters were checked and left within the expected settings. The well was restarted the same day.

## Close-out remarks
- Total downtime: 5 hours, all on 2025-09-02.
- Average production for the 7 days before the job (to 2025-09-01): 750.8 bbl/d oil, 536.8 Mscf/d gas, 23.6% water cut, GOR 715 scf/bbl.
- Average production for the 6 days after restart (to 2025-09-09): 737.7 bbl/d oil, 532.1 Mscf/d gas, 23.2% water cut, GOR 721 scf/bbl.
- Rates are broadly in line with the pre-job baseline, with no sign of a step change from the inspection.
- No follow-up actions raised. The Production Engineer is informed and the work order is closed.
