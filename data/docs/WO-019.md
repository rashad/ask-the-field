## Scope of work
Subsurface safety valve function and leak test on W-38. Close the valve, confirm it holds, record the leak rate against the acceptance limit, reopen and return the well to production.

## Safety and permits
- Work permit and isolation certificate issued by the Production Supervisor before start.
- Toolbox talk held with the slickline unit crew and the Lead Operator.
- Wellhead area barricaded. Gas detection checked before work.
- CPF-1 control room informed of the planned shut-in and restart.

## Materials
- Test pump and calibrated gauges
- Valve test log sheet
- Standard seals and fittings (none consumed)

## Execution notes
- Well shut in on 2025-08-30 for the test.
- Safety valve function test passed. The valve closed fully on command.
- Leak test passed with no pressure build-up above the acceptance limit.
- Well reopened and restarted the same day. Total downtime was 4 hours.
