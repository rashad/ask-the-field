# Incident note: Flowline leak on W-19

**Document ID:** IN-055
**Note date:** 2026-08-04
**Event ID:** EV-0148
**Well:** W-19 (Field B, horizontal, ESP)
**Severity:** Low
**Status at note date:** Closed
**Applicable procedure:** SOP-HSE-006 Flowline Leak Response

## What happened
On 2026-08-03 a small leak was found on a flowline flange at W-19. The well was isolated and the flange was repaired. The work was completed on 2026-08-04.

## Immediate actions
- Well shut in and the flowline isolated and depressurised.
- Area barricaded and the Production Supervisor and HSE Advisor informed.
- Spilled fluid contained and cleaned up. No fluid reached the surrounding ground beyond the contained area.
- Flange repaired and checked for tightness before the line was put back in service.

## Findings
The leak was limited to the flange joint. No injuries occurred and no other equipment was affected. Gasket condition and bolt torque were reviewed during the repair.

## Impact
- Downtime as of the note date: 28 hours.
- Production on 2026-08-03: 378.1 bbl oil and 385.1 Mscf gas, water cut 41.5 percent, with 10 hours of uptime.
- Prior 7-day baseline to 2026-08-02: 883.6 bbl/d oil, 885.6 Mscf/d gas, water cut 43.2 percent, GOR 1002 scf/bbl.
- Compared with the baseline, the oil lost on 2026-08-03 was roughly 505 bbl (883.6 minus 378.1).

## Status
Closed. W-19 is back in production. No related events were recorded within the previous 30 days.

## Next actions
- Production Engineer to confirm that W-19 rates return to baseline over the next few days.
- Lead Operator to include the repaired flange in routine rounds for the coming week.
- Integrity Engineer to note the flange in the inspection plan for Field B flowlines.

Response followed SOP-HSE-006 Flowline Leak Response.
