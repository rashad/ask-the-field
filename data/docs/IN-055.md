# Incident note: Flowline leak on W-32

**Document ID:** IN-055
**Note date:** 2026-08-03
**Event ID:** EV-0144
**Well:** W-32 (Salt Flat Field, vertical, ESP)
**Severity:** High
**Status at note date:** Closed
**Applicable procedure:** SOP-HSE-006 Flowline Leak Response

## What happened
On 2026-08-03 a small leak was found on a flowline flange on W-32. The well was shut in and the flowline isolated.

## Immediate actions
- Well shut in and flowline isolated and depressurised in line with SOP-HSE-006.
- Area barricaded and the Production Supervisor and HSE Advisor informed.
- Spilled fluid contained and cleaned up. No injuries reported.
- Maintenance repaired the flange and the joint was checked before the line was returned to service.

## Findings
The leak was small and limited to the flange connection. The repair was completed the same day. No related events on this well in the previous 30 days.

## Impact
- Downtime as of this note: 23 hours.
- Production on 2026-08-03: 21.3 bbl oil, 31.6 Mscf gas, water cut 32 percent, uptime 1 hour.
- Baseline for the 7 days to 2026-08-02: 558.2 bbl/d oil, 811.9 Mscf/d gas, water cut 29.7 percent, GOR 1454 scf/bbl.
- Deferred oil is roughly the difference between baseline and the 21.3 bbl produced, about 537 bbl for the day.

## Status
Closed. The well is producing today and the flowline is back in service.

## Next actions
- Production Engineer to confirm rates and water cut return to baseline over the next few days.
- Lead Operator to include the repaired flange in routine rounds for leak checks.
- Integrity Engineer to review the flange condition at the next scheduled inspection.
