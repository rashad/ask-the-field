# Incident note: Sand production alarm on W-05

**Document ID:** IN-037
**Note date:** 2026-03-29
**Event ID:** EV-0099
**Well:** W-05 (Field A, vertical, ESP)
**Severity:** High
**Status at note date:** Closed
**Author:** Production Engineer

## What happened
On 2026-03-29 the sand monitor on W-05 flowline went into alarm. The well was producing normally before the event, with a 7-day average to 2026-03-28 of 867.9 bbl/d oil, 789.1 Mscf/d gas, 26.5 % water cut and GOR of 909 scf/bbl.

## Immediate actions
- Lead Operator acknowledged the alarm and confirmed the reading at the local sand monitor panel.
- Choke was reduced until sand readings returned to normal.
- Production Supervisor was informed and the well was kept under observation by the operator rounds.
- Surface line and separator inlet were checked at CPF-1 for signs of carryover, with nothing abnormal reported.

## Findings so far
The alarm cleared after the choke reduction, which points to a drawdown-related sand response rather than an instrument fault. No equipment damage was reported.

## Impact
- Downtime: 19 hours as of the note date.
- Production on the day: 172.3 bbl oil and 147.6 Mscf gas, with 27.2 % water cut and 5 hours of uptime.
- Compared with the 7-day baseline of 867.9 bbl/d, the day's oil was well below the normal rate, so deferred oil is substantial for the day.

## Status
The event is closed as of 2026-03-29. W-05 is producing today. No related events were recorded on this asset in the previous 30 days.

## Next actions
1. Production Engineer to review the choke and drawdown history and agree a conservative operating envelope for W-05.
2. Operators to keep sand monitor readings in the shift log during the next rounds.
3. Any repeat alarm to be handled per SOP-OPS-002.

## Reference
SOP-OPS-002 Sand Management and Sand Production Alarms.
