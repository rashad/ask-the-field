# Incident note: Wellhead leak on W-01

**Document ID:** IN-037
**Event ID:** EV-0102
**Note date:** 2026-04-03
**Well:** W-01 (Dune Field, vertical, ESP)
**Severity:** Low
**Status at note date:** Closed
**Prepared by:** Production Engineer

## What happened
On 2026-04-03 the Lead Operator found a leak at the stem packing of a wellhead valve on W-01 during the routine round. The leak was small and contained at the wellhead cellar area.

## Immediate actions
- The well was shut in and the wellhead isolated.
- The area was barricaded and the HSE Advisor was informed.
- No hydrocarbon reached the ground beyond the wellhead pad, and no injuries were reported.

## Findings
The leak came from worn stem packing on the wellhead valve. The valve body, flanges and seals were inspected and showed no other leak paths. The packing was replaced, and the valve was function-tested and leak-tested before return to service.

## Impact
- Downtime: 14 hours as of this note.
- Production on 2026-04-03: 312.2 bbl oil, 188.8 Mscf gas, water cut 29.1 percent, uptime 10 hours.
- Baseline for the 7 days to 2026-04-02: 704.9 bbl/d oil, 430.8 Mscf/d gas, water cut 30.6 percent, GOR 611 scf/bbl.
- The shortfall on the day is consistent with the hours the well was down.

## Status
The well was restarted after the repair and is producing. The incident is closed. No related events were recorded on W-01 in the previous 30 days.

## Next actions
- The Lead Operator will check the valve stem area for seepage on each round over the coming week.
- The Maintenance Planner will add the wellhead valve packing to the next routine inspection list.
- No specific procedure applies to this incident, so the repair was done under the general work permit system.
