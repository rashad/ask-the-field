# Incident note: H2S alarm on W-38

**Document id:** IN-038
**Event id:** EV-0100
**Note date:** 2026-03-30
**Author:** Production Engineer, with HSE Advisor input
**Well:** W-38 (Bayda, deviated, gas lift)
**Severity:** medium
**Status at note date:** closed

## What happened
On 2026-03-30 the fixed H2S detector near the W-38 wellhead went into alarm. The Lead Operator confirmed the alarm at the local panel and in the CPF-1 control room.

## Immediate actions
- The area around the wellhead was cleared and access was barred.
- The well was shut in and the response followed SOP-HSE-003 H2S Alarm Response.
- Personnel in breathing apparatus, with portable gas detectors, inspected the wellhead and found a leak on a needle valve.
- The needle valve leak was repaired, the area was re-tested and readings returned to normal before the all-clear was given.
- The Production Supervisor and HSE Advisor were informed.

## Findings
The source was a leaking needle valve at the wellhead. No injuries were reported. No other leak points were found during the inspection.

## Impact
- Downtime: 4 hours (uptime on the day: 20 hours).
- Production on 2026-03-30: oil 1130.6 bbl, gas 1819.9 Mscf, water cut 28.4%.
- The previous 7-day average to 2026-03-29 was 1383.2 bbl/d oil, 2068.5 Mscf/d gas, water cut 28.1% and GOR 1495 scf/bbl. Oil on the day was about 252.6 bbl below that average, mostly due to the shutdown.
- No related events were recorded in the previous 30 days.

## Status
Closed. W-38 is back on production and the repair has been verified.

## Next actions
- Include the repaired needle valve and neighbouring fittings in the next routine leak check round.
- The HSE Advisor to review the alarm log and confirm the detector response was as expected.
- Reinforce the clearance and muster steps of SOP-HSE-003 at the next shift briefing.

**Applicable procedure:** SOP-HSE-003 H2S Alarm Response
