# Incident note: Flowline leak on W-38

**Document ID:** IN-038
**Note date:** 2026-04-11
**Event ID:** EV-0106
**Well:** W-38 (Salt Flat Field, deviated, gas lift)
**Category / severity:** Incident / medium
**Prepared by:** Production Engineer
**Status at note date:** Closed

## What happened
On 2026-04-10 a small leak was found on a flowline flange on W-38. The well was shut in and the flowline isolated. The leak was repaired and the well returned to production by 2026-04-11.

## Immediate actions
- The Lead Operator shut in the well and isolated the flowline at the wellhead and upstream of CPF-1.
- The area was barricaded and the HSE Advisor was informed.
- Spilled fluid was contained and cleaned up. No injuries were reported.
- The flange was repaired by the maintenance crew and the joint was pressure checked before restart.

## Findings
- The leak was small and confined to the flange joint.
- No other leaks were found on the flowline during the walk-down.
- No related events on this well in the previous 30 days.

## Impact
- Downtime as of the note date: 37 hours.
- Production on 2026-04-10: 305.4 bbl oil and 399.8 Mscf gas, water cut 17.6 %, uptime 5.5 hours.
- Baseline for the 7 days to 2026-04-09: 1251.1 bbl/d oil, 1819.7 Mscf/d gas, water cut 17.2 %, GOR 1454 scf/bbl.
- Deferred oil is the difference between the baseline rate and the volume actually produced over the downtime period. The Production Engineer will confirm the final figure in the monthly deferment review.

## Current status
Closed. W-38 is producing and the flowline is back in service with no leaks observed after restart.

## Next actions
- The Lead Operator will include the flange in the routine rounds for the next week as a precaution.
- The Integrity Engineer will review the flange and gasket condition at the next scheduled inspection.
- The response followed SOP-HSE-006 Flowline Leak Response. The HSE Advisor will file this note against that procedure.
