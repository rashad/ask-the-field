# Incident note: Gas lift valve failure on W-38

**Document ID:** IN-053
**Note date:** 2026-07-21
**Event ID:** EV-0143
**Well:** W-38 (Bayda, deviated, gas lift)
**Severity:** medium
**Status at note date:** closed
**Author:** Production Engineer

## What happened
On 2026-07-20 the unloading valve on W-38 stuck open, so lift gas was injecting at a shallow point in the string instead of the design depth. Rate fell by almost half. The well was down for part of the day and then returned to service.

## Immediate actions
- Lead Operator (K.M.) reviewed injection pressure and lift gas rate trends and confirmed the shallow injection signature.
- Injection was adjusted to hold the well stable while the slickline change-out was arranged.
- Production Supervisor informed and the slickline unit was scheduled.

## Findings
- The stuck-open unloading valve was the cause. No other well on the Bayda lift gas header showed a similar response.
- Production before the event (7 days to 2026-07-19): 1238.6 bbl/d oil, 1839.5 Mscf/d gas, 31.2 % water cut, GOR 1485 scf/bbl.
- Production on 2026-07-20: 398 bbl oil, 621.2 Mscf gas, 30.8 % water cut, 14 hours uptime.
- Water cut was essentially unchanged, which points to a lift problem and not a reservoir change.

## Impact
- Downtime as of the note date: 20 hours.
- Oil on 2026-07-20 was about 840 bbl below the pre-event average of 1238.6 bbl/d. Deferment on 2026-07-21 is not included in this figure.

## Status
The valve was changed out by slickline and the event ended on 2026-07-21. W-38 is producing. The incident is closed. No related events on this well in the previous 30 days.

## Next actions
- Production Engineer to confirm rate recovery to the pre-event baseline over the next week.
- Field Engineer to check the retrieved valve for the failure mode and record it.
- Review the lift gas surveillance frequency for Bayda wells.
