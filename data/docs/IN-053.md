# Incident note: High separator level trip on W-33

**Document ID:** IN-053
**Event ID:** EV-0137
**Note date:** 2026-07-18
**Author:** Production Engineer
**Well:** W-33 (Salt Flat Field, vertical, gas lift)
**Severity:** Low
**Status:** Closed

## What happened
On 2026-07-18 the test separator high-level trip shut in W-33. The well was offline for 3 hours before being returned to service the same day.

## Immediate actions
- The Lead Operator confirmed the trip was a high-level shutdown and that no relief or carry-over to the flare occurred.
- The level controller on the test separator was checked, found out of calibration, and recalibrated by the instrument technician.
- The level reading was compared against the gauge glass after recalibration and the two agreed.
- W-33 was restarted and monitored through the following hours with stable separator level.

## Findings
The trip was caused by the level controller reading out of calibration, which gave a false high-level signal. No damage to the well, flowline or separator was observed. No injuries, spills or releases.

## Impact
- Downtime: 3 hours (uptime on the day was 21 hours).
- Production on 2026-07-18: 949.9 bbl oil, 1245.8 Mscf gas, water cut 19.3 percent.
- Previous 7-day average to 2026-07-17: 1165.7 bbl/d oil, 1458.7 Mscf/d gas, water cut 20.2 percent, GOR 1251 scf/bbl.
- The shortfall against the 7-day average is mostly explained by the 3 hours of downtime. No lasting change in well behaviour is expected.

## Status at note date
Closed. W-33 is producing normally and the controller is operating within tolerance.

## Next actions
- Operators to watch the test separator level trend on each shift for the next few days.
- Instrument technician to add the controller to the next routine calibration check list.
- Wellhead valve greasing and function test on W-33 (EV-0142) is planned for 2026-07-25. It is not yet started.

## Applicable procedure
No specific procedure applies to this event.
