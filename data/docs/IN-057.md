# Incident note: H2S alarm on W-02

**Document id:** IN-057
**Event id:** EV-0155
**Note date:** 2026-08-20
**Well:** W-02 (Field A, deviated, gas lift)
**Severity:** medium
**Status at note date:** closed
**Prepared by:** HSE Advisor

## What happened
On 2026-08-20 the fixed H2S detector near the W-02 wellhead went into alarm. The area was cleared in line with the alarm response, and the well was taken offline while the source was investigated.

## Immediate actions
- Personnel mustered at the upwind point and a headcount was completed.
- Area isolated and access barricaded. Entry only with portable gas detectors and breathing apparatus.
- Control room and Production Supervisor informed.
- Well shut in at the wing valve from a safe position.

## Findings
A leak was found on a needle valve at the wellhead instrument connection. The valve was repaired and the connection leak-tested. Follow-up readings with portable detectors showed no H2S in the area, and the fixed detector returned to normal. No injuries or exposures were reported.

## Impact
- Downtime: 6 hours (uptime 18 hours on the day).
- Production on 2026-08-20: oil 945.3 bbl, gas 433.8 Mscf, water cut 25.1 percent.
- Previous 7-day average to 2026-08-19: oil 1285.2 bbl/d, gas 619.9 Mscf/d, water cut 25.4 percent, GOR 482 scf/bbl.
- Deferred oil for the day is roughly 340 bbl against that average.

## Status
Closed as of 2026-08-20. The well is back on production. There were no related events on W-02 in the previous 30 days.

## Next actions
- Production Engineer to confirm W-02 rates return to the pre-event average over the next few days.
- Maintenance Planner to include the other needle valves and instrument connections on the W-02 wellhead in the next routine leak check.
- Lead Operator to note the event in the shift handover log.
