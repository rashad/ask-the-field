# Incident note: ESP failure on W-27

**Document ID:** IN-057
**Note date:** 2026-08-22
**Event:** EV-0150
**Well:** W-27 (Wadi Field, horizontal, ESP)
**Severity:** High
**Status at note date:** Closed

## What happened
On 2026-08-18 the ESP on W-27 tripped on motor overtemperature. A restart attempt then tripped on ground fault. Insulation resistance measured on the motor was below the minimum acceptable value. The installed pump is a Varenta VL-538, pump serial 7Q-0419-086 (batch 7Q-0419), with a run life of 11 months. The well was shut in pending workover.

## Immediate actions
- The well was kept shut in after the ground fault trip, and no further restarts were attempted.
- The drive was isolated and the surface cable and connections were checked.
- The Production Supervisor and the Workover Supervisor were informed.

## Findings so far
- The overtemperature trip followed by a ground fault at restart, together with low motor insulation resistance, points to a motor electrical failure.
- The cause of the failure will be confirmed when the unit is pulled and inspected.

## Impact
- Downtime: 120 hours from 2026-08-18 to 2026-08-22.
- Production on 2026-08-18 was 0 bbl of oil and 0 Mscf of gas, with 0 hours of uptime.
- The 7-day average before the event (to 2026-08-17) was 558.2 bbl/d oil, 611 Mscf/d gas, water cut 41.1 percent and GOR 1095 scf/bbl. This is the rate deferred for each day of shut-in.

## Status
The incident record is closed as of 2026-08-22. The well remains shut in until the workover.

## Next actions
- An ESP replacement workover is planned to start on 2026-08-23 (EV-0156).
- The Production Engineer will review the teardown findings once the unit is out of the hole.
- Post-workover rates are to be compared with the baseline above.

No specific procedure is referenced for this incident.
