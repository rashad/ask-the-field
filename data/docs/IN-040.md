# Incident note: ESP trip on W-27

**Document ID:** IN-040
**Note date:** 2026-04-14
**Event ID:** EV-0110
**Well:** W-27 (Wadi Field, horizontal, ESP)
**Severity:** High
**Status at note date:** Closed
**Prepared by:** Production Engineer

## What happened
On 2026-04-14 the ESP on W-27 tripped on underload. The well was offline and was restarted after checks were completed.

## Immediate actions
- Operator confirmed the trip at the CPF-1 control room and the wellsite VSD panel.
- Lead Operator informed the Production Supervisor.
- Wellhead pressures, VSD fault log and motor current history were reviewed before any restart attempt.
- Insulation and electrical checks were done per the restart section of the procedure.
- The pump was restarted after the checks were satisfactory.

## Findings so far
- The trip was logged as underload. No other fault codes were recorded on the VSD.
- Following the restart the well returned to production at a rate consistent with the pre-event baseline water cut.

## Impact
- Downtime as of the note date: 20 hours.
- Uptime on 2026-04-14: 4 hours.
- Production on the day: 99.5 bbl oil, 111.6 Mscf gas, water cut 36.4 %.
- Prior 7 days to 2026-04-13: 631.4 bbl/d oil, 676 Mscf/d gas, water cut 37.5 %, GOR 1071 scf/bbl.
- The day's volume is well below the prior average, so the deferred oil is significant for the day.

## Status
Closed. The well is producing. No related events were logged on this well in the previous 30 days.

## Next actions
- Production Engineer to keep watching motor current and intake conditions on W-27 during routine surveillance.
- Lead Operator to record any further underload alarms in the shift report.

## Applicable procedure
SOP-OPS-001 ESP Trip Response and Restart
