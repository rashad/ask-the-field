# Incident note: H2S alarm on W-19

**Document ID:** IN-040
**Note date:** 2026-04-11
**Event ID:** EV-0102
**Well:** W-19 (Field B, horizontal, ESP)
**Severity:** medium
**Status at note date:** closed
**Author:** HSE Advisor, with input from the Production Engineer

## What happened
On 2026-04-11 the fixed H2S detector near the W-19 wellhead went into alarm. The alarm was treated as a real release until proven otherwise.

## Immediate actions
- The area around the wellhead was cleared and the muster point was confirmed.
- The well was shut in and the Lead Operator informed the Production Supervisor.
- Personnel with portable gas detectors and breathing apparatus approached from upwind to locate the source.
- Entry was allowed only after readings fell back to normal.

## Findings
The source was a leak on a needle valve at the wellhead. The valve was repaired and the joint was leak-tested before restart. Portable and fixed detector readings were normal after the repair. No injuries were reported.

## Impact
- Downtime: 5 hours on 2026-04-11 (uptime 19 hours).
- Production on the day: oil 810.9 bbl, gas 824.1 Mscf, water cut 39.9%.
- Baseline for the 7 days to 2026-04-10: oil 1043.8 bbl/d, gas 1059.2 Mscf/d, water cut 40.2%, GOR 1015 scf/bbl.
- The reduced volumes on the day are consistent with the 5 hours lost. Water cut did not change materially.

## Status
Closed as of 2026-04-11. W-19 is back online and producing.

## Next actions
- Include the repaired needle valve in the next routine wellhead leak check.
- Review the response times from this alarm at the next HSE meeting.
- Note: a field power dip affecting W-19 is planned or expected on 2026-05-02 (EV-0114). It is not part of this incident.

SOP-HSE-003 H2S Alarm Response
