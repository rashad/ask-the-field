# Incident note: Sand production alarm on W-31

**Document ID:** IN-017
**Event ID:** EV-0049
**Note date:** 2025-11-06
**Author:** Production Engineer
**Well:** W-31 (Bayda, deviated, gas lift)
**Severity:** Medium
**Status at note date:** Closed

## What happened
On 2025-11-03 the sand monitor on W-31 went into alarm shortly after a rate increase on the well. The alarm persisted until the choke was reduced, and sand levels settled over the following three days. The event ended on 2025-11-06.

## Immediate actions
- Choke on W-31 reduced to bring sand readings back below the alarm threshold.
- Lead Operator confirmed the sand monitor reading against the local probe and checked the CPF-1 separator for solids carry-over.
- Production Supervisor informed and the well kept under close observation through the following shifts.

## Findings
- The alarm was triggered by the higher drawdown after the rate increase. Bayda is sand-prone and the well responded as expected to the extra drawdown.
- No equipment damage was reported on the flowline or at the choke.
- Production in the 7 days to 2025-11-02 averaged 1211.3 bbl/d oil, 2032.7 Mscf/d gas, water cut 22.6 percent and GOR 1678 scf/bbl.
- On 2025-11-03 the well made 797.7 bbl oil and 1363.9 Mscf gas, with water cut 21.6 percent and 16 hours of uptime.

## Impact
- Downtime as of the note date: 32 hours.
- Deferred oil is visible mainly on 2025-11-03, when output was well below the previous 7-day average rate.
- No related events on any well in the previous 30 days.

## Status
Closed. Sand readings are stable at the reduced choke setting and W-31 is producing.

## Next actions
1. Keep the choke at the reduced setting and do not increase rate without engineering review.
2. Any future rate increase to be made in small steps, with sand monitor readings checked at each step.
3. Production Engineer to review the sand trend at the next well test.

## Applicable procedure
SOP-OPS-002 Sand Management and Sand Production Alarms.
