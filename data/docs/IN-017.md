# Incident note: H2S alarm on W-36

**Document ID:** IN-017
**Event ID:** EV-0062
**Note date:** 2025-12-10
**Author:** Production Engineer
**Well:** W-36 (Salt Flat Field, deviated, ESP)
**Severity:** Medium
**Status:** Closed

## What happened
On 2025-12-10 the fixed H2S detector near the W-36 wellhead went into alarm. The alarm and the response both took place on the same day.

## Immediate actions
- The area around the wellhead was cleared and access was restricted.
- The Lead Operator and HSE Advisor were informed and the response followed SOP-HSE-003 H2S Alarm Response.
- The well was taken offline while the source was located.

## Findings
The source was a leak on a needle valve at the wellhead. The valve was repaired. Area readings were checked before personnel were allowed back, and the detector was confirmed clear.

## Impact
- Downtime: 7 hours on 2025-12-10, giving 17 hours of uptime.
- Production on the day: 994.2 bbl oil, 1174.1 Mscf gas, water cut 26.2%.
- Baseline for the 7 days to 2025-12-09: 1474.8 bbl/d oil, 1693.9 Mscf/d gas, water cut 25%, GOR 1149 scf/bbl.
- Deferred oil against baseline: about 480 bbl for the day.
- No related events on this well in the previous 30 days.

## Status at note date
The incident is closed. The leak was fixed and the alarm cleared. The well is shut in as of the register status today, which is recorded separately from this incident.

## Next actions
- The Integrity Engineer will review the other needle valves at the W-36 wellhead at the next routine inspection.
- The HSE Advisor will file the alarm record under SOP-HSE-003.
- Operators will report any further detector alarms on this well at once.
