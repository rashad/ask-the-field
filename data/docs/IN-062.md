# Incident note: Power outage on W-20

**Document ID:** IN-062
**Note date:** 2026-09-07
**Event ID:** EV-0165
**Well:** W-20 (Field B, deviated, gas lift)
**Severity:** Medium
**Status at note date:** Closed
**Prepared by:** Production Engineer

## What happened
On 2026-09-07 a field power dip tripped W-20. The well went down on the trip and was restarted once power was restored to the field.

## Immediate actions
- Lead Operator confirmed the loss of power was field-wide and not a fault local to the well.
- After power was restored, the wellsite control panel was checked and the trip was reset.
- The well was restarted and brought back to stable flow. Gas lift injection was verified before handover to the next shift.

## Findings
- The trip was caused by the power dip. No equipment damage was found on the wellsite.
- Downtime was 4 hours, giving 20 hours of uptime on the day.
- Water cut on the day was 39.9 percent, in line with the previous baseline.

## Impact
| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Downtime | 4 hours |
| Oil on the day | 495.3 bbl |
| Gas on the day | 507.7 Mscf |
| Prior 7-day average oil (to 2026-09-06) | 597.5 bbl/d |
| Prior 7-day average gas | 595.8 Mscf/d |
| Prior water cut | 39.6 percent |
| Prior GOR | 997 scf/bbl |

Oil on the day was about 102 bbl below the prior average, consistent with the lost run time.

## Related events
EV-0154 on 2026-08-13 was a similar power outage on W-20 (6 hours downtime, medium severity, closed). Two power-related trips on this well within 30 days suggest the cause sits with the field supply, not the well.

## Status
Closed. W-20 is producing.

## Next actions
- Production Engineer to ask the facilities team for the power quality log covering both dates.
- Lead Operator to keep W-20 on the restart watch list for the next two weeks.
- Review whether the control panel setpoints for ride-through on short dips need adjusting.

## Applicable procedure
No specific procedure applies to this event.
