# Incident note: Flowline leak on W-39

**Document ID:** IN-062
**Event ID:** EV-0167
**Note date:** 2026-09-26
**Author:** Production Engineer
**Well:** W-39 (Salt Flat Field, vertical, gas lift)
**Severity:** Medium
**Status at note date:** Closed

## What happened
A small leak was found on a flowline flange on W-39 on 2026-09-26. The well was taken off line so the leak could be isolated and repaired.

## Immediate actions
- The well was shut in and the flowline isolated and depressurised.
- The area was barricaded and the HSE Advisor was informed.
- The Lead Operator checked the surrounding area for any spilled fluid.
- The flange was repaired and the joint was leak tested before the line was returned to service.

## Findings
The leak was small and confined to the flange. No injuries occurred. The response followed SOP-HSE-006 Flowline Leak Response.

## Impact
- Downtime: 18 hours as of the note date.
- Production on 2026-09-26: 245.5 bbl of oil and 326.4 Mscf of gas, water cut 22.8%, with 6 hours of uptime.
- Baseline for the 7 days to 2026-09-25: 1046.4 bbl/d oil, 1388.4 Mscf/d gas, water cut 21.5%, GOR 1327 scf/bbl.
- Oil on the day was therefore well below the recent average, because of the 18 hours lost.

## Status
The repair is complete and the incident is closed. W-39 is back on production. No related events were recorded in the previous 30 days.

## Next actions
- The Lead Operator will continue to monitor the repaired flange during routine rounds.
- The Production Engineer will confirm that the closure records are complete under SOP-HSE-006.
