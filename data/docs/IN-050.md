# Incident note: Water breakthrough on W-23

**Document ID:** IN-050
**Note date:** 2026-06-15
**Event ID:** EV-0128
**Well:** W-23 (Wadi Field, horizontal, ESP)
**Severity:** medium
**Status at note date:** closed

## What happened
Over recent weeks the water cut on W-23 rose sharply from about 30 percent to over 55 percent. The pattern is consistent with injector water breakthrough from the north flank of Wadi Field. The event was logged from 2026-06-14 and ended on 2026-06-15.

## Immediate actions
- The Lead Operator reviewed the trend and confirmed the rise was real, not a metering fault, by checking the separator readings at CPF-1.
- The Production Supervisor and the reservoir surveillance team were informed.
- The well was taken offline briefly to review the ESP operating parameters and re-check the settings.

## Production data
- Seven-day average to 2026-06-13: oil 890.1 bbl/d, gas 585.9 Mscf/d, water cut 34.4 percent, GOR 658 scf/bbl.
- On 2026-06-14: oil 751.3 bbl, gas 501.5 Mscf, water cut 36.3 percent, uptime 20 hours.

## Impact
- Downtime as of the note date: 8 hours.
- Oil on 2026-06-14 was about 139 bbl below the previous seven-day average rate, partly from the reduced uptime.

## Findings so far
- The breakthrough is attributed to the nearby water flood injection on the north flank.
- No equipment failure was identified on the ESP.
- No related events were recorded on this well in the previous 30 days.

## Status
The incident is closed. W-23 is back in production.

## Next actions
- The Production Engineer will keep W-23 on the frequent water cut sampling list.
- Review injection allocation on the north flank with the reservoir team.
- Check ESP loading as water cut continues to change.

## Procedure reference
No specific procedure applies to this event.
