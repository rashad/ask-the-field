# Incident note: High separator level trip on W-26

**Document id:** IN-050
**Note date:** 2026-06-28
**Event reference:** EV-0132
**Well:** W-26 (Field B, vertical, gas lift)
**Severity:** Low
**Status at note date:** Closed
**Prepared by:** Production Engineer

## What happened
On 2026-06-28 the test separator high-level trip shut in W-26. The well was down for 2 hours, which matches the 22 hours of uptime recorded for the day.

## Immediate actions
- The Lead Operator confirmed the trip was a level alarm and not a process upset on the well.
- Field Engineer checked the level controller and found it reading out of calibration.
- The controller was recalibrated and checked against the sight glass.
- The well was restarted and returned to stable flow. The Production Supervisor was informed.

## Findings
The trip came from the level controller calibration. No leak, spill or injury occurred. No other wells were affected.

## Impact
- Downtime: 2 hours.
- Production on 2026-06-28: oil 1198.4 bbl, gas 619.8 Mscf, water cut 29.3%.
- Previous 7-day average to 2026-06-27: oil 1297.6 bbl/d, gas 667.4 Mscf/d, water cut 29.8%, GOR 514 scf/bbl.
- Oil was about 99 bbl below the previous average, consistent with the short outage. Water cut is essentially unchanged.

## Status
Closed. W-26 is producing normally.

## Related events
- EV-0129: flowline pigging on 2026-06-19 (5 hours downtime, closed). No link to this trip was identified.
- EV-0138: subsurface safety valve function and leak test, planned for 2026-07-09.

## Next actions
- Operators to compare the level controller against the sight glass at each round for the next week.
- Add the controller to the next instrument calibration check list.

## Applicable procedure
No specific procedure applies to this event.
