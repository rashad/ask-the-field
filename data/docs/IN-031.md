# Incident note: Power outage on W-35

**Document ID:** IN-031
**Note date:** 2026-02-26
**Event ID:** EV-0090
**Well:** W-35 (Salt Flat Field, vertical, natural flow)
**Severity:** medium
**Status:** closed

## What happened
A field power dip on 2026-02-26 tripped W-35. The well was restarted after power was restored. The trip was caused by the supply, not by the well or its surface equipment.

## Immediate actions
- The Lead Operator confirmed the trip on the wellhead panel and the flowline pressure trend.
- Power was confirmed stable before any restart was attempted.
- Wellhead valves and the flowline to CPF-1 were checked visually, with no leaks or damage found.
- The well was restarted and returned to normal flow. The Production Supervisor was informed.

## Findings
- The trip came from the power dip alone. No equipment fault was found on the well.
- Downtime as of this note is 8 hours. Uptime on the day was 16 hours.
- Water cut on the day was 17%, in line with the previous baseline.

## Impact
| Item | Previous 7 days (to 2026-02-25) | 2026-02-26 |
|---|---|---|
| Oil | 642.4 bbl/d | 423.1 bbl |
| Gas | 864.3 Mscf/d | 563.1 Mscf |
| Water cut | 16.6% | 17% |
| GOR | 1345 scf/bbl | n/a |

Oil on the day was about 219 bbl below the 7-day average, which is the deferred oil from the 8-hour outage. Gas was about 301 Mscf below average.

## Status at note date
Closed. W-35 is producing, and no follow-up work is open on the well.

## Next actions
- The Production Engineer will monitor W-35 rates over the next few days to confirm they return to the pre-event baseline.
- The outage will be passed to the Maintenance Planner for review of field power supply reliability.

## Applicable procedure
No specific procedure applies to this event.
