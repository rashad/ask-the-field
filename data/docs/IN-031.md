# Incident note: Flowline leak on W-38

**Document ID:** IN-031
**Note date:** 2026-02-15
**Event ID:** EV-0086
**Well:** W-38 (Bayda, deviated, gas lift)
**Severity:** Low
**Status at note date:** Closed
**Applicable procedure:** SOP-HSE-006 Flowline Leak Response

## What happened
A small leak was found on a flowline flange at W-38 on 2026-02-15. The well was producing at the time and the leak was reported by the operator on round.

## Immediate actions
- The well was shut in and the flowline isolated.
- The area was barricaded and the Production Supervisor and HSE Advisor were informed.
- The flange was depressurised and the leak area checked for spilled fluid. No significant release to ground was noted.

## Findings
- The leak was limited to the flange joint. The flange was repaired and returned to service the same day.
- The flowline was pressure checked after the repair, with no further leakage seen.
- No injuries and no impact on other wells or on CPF-1.

## Impact
- Downtime: 20 hours on 2026-02-15 (uptime 4 hours).
- Production on the day: oil 234.3 bbl, gas 339.9 Mscf, water cut 29.3%.
- Prior 7-day average to 2026-02-14: oil 1394.2 bbl/d, gas 2077.4 Mscf/d, water cut 28.7%, GOR 1490 scf/bbl.
- Deferred oil is roughly the difference between the prior average and the day's volume, about 1160 bbl.

## Related events
EV-0075 (2026-01-19): subsurface safety valve function and leak test on W-38, closed, 4 hours downtime. It is a separate activity and no link to this leak has been identified.

## Status
Closed. W-38 is producing and the flowline is back in normal service.

## Next actions
- The Production Engineer to confirm the well returned to its previous rate in the next daily report.
- Operators to include the repaired flange in routine round checks for the next week.
- The incident to be reviewed against SOP-HSE-006 Flowline Leak Response and filed.
