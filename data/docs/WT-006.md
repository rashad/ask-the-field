# Well test: W-09, 2025-11-10

**Document ID:** WT-006
**Well:** W-09 (Dune Field, vertical, ESP)
**Test date:** 2025-11-10
**Procedure:** SOP-OPS-004 Well Test Procedure with the Mobile Test Separator

## Test conditions
- Test duration: 24 hours
- Choke: 28/64 in
- Flowing wellhead pressure: 260 psi
- Lift: ESP, Varenta VL-538, pump serial 4D-0733-115 (batch 4D-0733), installed 2025-11-02
- Context: the well returned to production on 2025-11-03 after the ESP replacement workover (EV-0046). The previous pump (serial 7Q-0419-027) was pulled following the ground fault failure (EV-0042). This is the first test after the workover.

| Parameter | Result |

| Gas | 934.2 Mscf/d |

## Comparison with previous baseline
The baseline is the 7-day period ending 2025-09-11.

| Parameter | Baseline | Current |
|---|---|---|
| Oil (bbl/d) | 1479.7 | 1412.5 |
| Gas (Mscf/d) | 974 | 934.2 |
| Water cut (%) | 9.9 | 11.6 |
| GOR (scf/bbl) | 658 | 661 |

Oil is about 67 bbl/d below baseline and water cut is 1.7 points higher. GOR is essentially unchanged.

## Observations
- Sand is negligible at 2 ppm.
- The pump is in its first week of running, so the trend is not yet established.
- Rates were stable through the test period.

## Recommendation
- Keep the well on the current choke and ESP settings.
- Repeat the test in about 30 days to confirm rate and water cut trends on the new pump.
- Continue routine sand monitoring.
