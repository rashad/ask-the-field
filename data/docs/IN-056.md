# Incident note: Wellhead leak on W-14

**Document ID:** IN-056
**Event ID:** EV-0149
**Note date:** 2026-08-18
**Well:** W-14 (Dune Field, deviated, ESP)
**Severity:** Low
**Status:** Closed

## What happened
On 2026-08-18 a leak was found on the wellhead valve stem packing of W-14. The well was brought down so the leak could be isolated and repaired.

## Immediate actions
- Well shut in and the wellhead area made safe and barricaded.
- Leak source confirmed as the valve stem packing, with no other wellhead leak points found.
- Production Supervisor and HSE Advisor informed.
- Packing replaced and the valve function-tested before the well was returned to service.

## Findings so far
The leak was limited to the valve stem packing. The packing was replaced and no further seepage was seen after restart.

## Impact
- Downtime: 13 hours.
- Production on the day: 362.6 bbl oil and 329.7 Mscf gas, with uptime of 11 hours and water cut of 22.3%.
- Baseline for the 7 days to 2026-08-17: 802.9 bbl/d oil, 721 Mscf/d gas, water cut 21.9%, GOR 898 scf/bbl.
- Water cut on the day is in line with the baseline. The lower daily volumes reflect the downtime.

## Status at note date
Closed. The repair is complete and W-14 is back on production.

## Next actions
- Lead Operator to check the valve stem during routine rounds for any return of the leak.
- Choke bean replacement (EV-0154) is planned from 2026-08-20.
- A further choke bean replacement (EV-0161) is planned from 2026-09-01.

## Procedure reference
No specific procedure is applicable to this event.
