# Incident note: Power outage on W-20

**Document ID:** IN-056
**Note date:** 2026-08-13
**Event ID:** EV-0154
**Well:** W-20 (Field B, deviated, gas lift)
**Severity:** medium
**Status at note date:** closed

## What happened
A field power dip on 2026-08-13 tripped W-20. The well stayed down until power was restored, then it was restarted.

## Immediate actions
- Lead Operator confirmed the trip was due to the field supply dip and not a well or lift gas fault.
- After power was restored, the gas lift injection line and wellhead pressures were checked before restart.
- W-20 was restarted and brought back to stable flow. CPF-1 control room was informed.

## Findings so far
- No equipment damage was found on the wellhead or the lift gas skid.
- The cause was the external power dip only. No well-specific cause was identified.

## Impact
- Downtime: 6 hours (uptime 18 hours on the day).
- Production on 2026-08-13: oil 487.5 bbl, gas 479 Mscf, water cut 38.1%.
- Previous 7-day baseline to 2026-08-12: oil 609.8 bbl/d, gas 610.3 Mscf/d, water cut 38.5%, GOR 1001 scf/bbl.
- Oil was about 122 bbl below the baseline rate for the day, consistent with the lost hours. Water cut was in line with the baseline.

## Status
Closed. The well is back on production and no restrictions remain.

## Next actions
- Production Engineer to keep W-20 on routine rate review for the next few days to confirm it returns to the baseline.
- Maintenance Planner to pass the trip record to the power supply team for their review of the dip.
- A further power outage on W-20 is currently listed as planned for 2026-09-07 (EV-0165). Operations should prepare restart readiness for that date.

## Applicable procedure
No specific procedure applies to this event.
