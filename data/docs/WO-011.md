## Scope of work
Subsurface safety valve function and leak test on W-02. Close the valve from the control panel, confirm closure, monitor downstream pressure for leak-off, then reopen and return the well to production.

## Safety and permits
- Work permit and isolation certificate issued by the Production Supervisor before start.
- Toolbox talk held with the operations crew at the wellhead.
- Gas detection in use at the wellsite for the duration of the test.
- Control panel pressure bled off and locked out on completion.

## Materials and equipment
- Hydraulic hand pump and calibrated test gauges
- Test record sheet
- Spare control line fittings (none used)

## Execution notes
- The well was shut in on the morning of 2025-07-13 for the test.
- The valve function test was completed and the valve closed on command.
- The leak test was held within acceptance limits, with no abnormal pressure build-up recorded.
- The valve was reopened, the panel was restored and the well was restarted.
- Total downtime: 5 hours.
