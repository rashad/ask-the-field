# Work order: Wellhead maintenance, W-09

**WO number:** WO-011
**Well:** W-09 (Dune Field, vertical, ESP)
**Category:** Maintenance, wellhead
**Priority:** Routine
**Raised:** 2025-07-17
**Planned start:** 2025-07-19
**Planned finish:** 2025-07-19
**Actual start / finish:** 2025-07-19 / 2025-07-19
**Status:** Closed
**Reference event:** EV-0014

## Scope of work
- Grease all wellhead valves (master, wing and swab) at the grease nipples.
- Function test each valve through full open and close travel and record the turns and any stiffness.
- Check the wellhead for leaks and confirm the gauges read normally.

## Safety and permits
- Work permit and isolation certificate issued before the shutdown.
- Well shut in and the flowline isolated at the wellhead before greasing.
- Toolbox talk held with the crew. Gas detector carried, and the area was barricaded during the function test.
- No hot work, no lifting operations.

## Materials
- Valve grease, approved grade, one cartridge set.
- Grease gun, torque wrench and leak detection fluid.

## Execution notes and close-out remarks
- The well was shut in for the work and returned to production the same day after the final function test. Total downtime was 6 hours.
- All valves moved through full travel and no sticking was found. No leaks were seen at the flanges or stem packings.
- The well restarted without issues. The Lead Operator confirmed normal ESP running parameters after restart.
- Production before the work (14 days to 2025-07-18): 1538.6 bbl/d oil, 1009.6 Mscf/d gas, water cut 8.8 %, GOR 656 scf/bbl.
- Production after restart (6 days to 2025-07-26): 1596.7 bbl/d oil, 1040.9 Mscf/d gas, water cut 8.3 %, GOR 652 scf/bbl.
- No follow-up actions are required. Valves return to the normal preventive maintenance schedule.

**Close-out:** Work complete, permit closed, area cleared. Work order closed.
