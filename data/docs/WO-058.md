# Work order: ESP replacement, W-21

**WO number:** WO-058
**Event reference:** EV-0176
**Well:** W-21 (Field B, horizontal, ESP)
**Category:** Maintenance
**Priority:** Medium
**Raised:** 2026-10-12
**Planned start:** 2026-10-14
**Actual completion:** 2026-10-18
**Status:** Closed

## Scope of work
Pull the existing ESP completion and replace the pump and motor assembly. The pump suffered gradual wear, which reduced the rate through Q3 2026. Run the new ESP, reconnect the surface equipment, test and return the well to production.

## Safety and permits
- Permit to Work raised and controlled under SOP-HSE-005 Permit to Work for Workover and Rig Operations.
- Well killed and barriers verified before the wellhead was opened.
- Lifting plan and toolbox talk completed by the Workover Supervisor.
- Electrical isolation and lock-out applied to the ESP switchboard.

## Materials
- ESP assembly (pump, intake, seal section, motor) supplied by the ESP vendor
- Motor lead extension and cable splices
- Wellhead penetrator and gaskets
- Completion fluid

## Execution notes
- Work started 2026-10-14 and finished 2026-10-18.
- Total downtime was 120 hours.
- The old unit was laid out for teardown inspection. The wear pattern is consistent with the gradual decline seen in Q3.
- The new ESP was run, and the cable was tested before and after landing the hanger.

## Close-out remarks
Well restarted without issues and handed back to the Lead Operator.

| 7 days to 2026-10-13 (before) | 481.9 | 369 | 49.3 | 766 |

Oil rate improved after the replacement, with GOR essentially unchanged. No related events in the previous 30 days. The well is producing today. Work order closed.
