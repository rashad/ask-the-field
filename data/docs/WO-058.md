# Work order: ESP replacement, W-21

**WO number:** WO-058
**Event reference:** EV-0174
**Well:** W-21 (Wadi Field, horizontal, ESP)
**Category:** Maintenance
**Priority:** Medium
**Raised:** 2026-10-12
**Planned start:** 2026-10-14
**Completion date:** 2026-10-18
**Status:** Closed

## Scope of work
Replace the ESP on W-21. Rate declined gradually through Q3 2026 as the pump wore. Pull the installed Varenta VL-538 pump, serial 1H-0956-071 (batch 1H-0956), after 31 months in service, and install a new Varenta VL-538, serial 5C-0822-034 (batch 5C-0822). Restart the well and confirm stable operation.

## Safety and permits
- Work performed under a permit to work per SOP-HSE-005 Permit to Work for Workover and Rig Operations.
- Well killed and barriers verified before pulling the completion.
- Isolation of the ESP power supply confirmed and locked out.
- Toolbox talk held with the rig crew and the ESP vendor technician before each lifting operation.

## Materials
- Varenta VL-538 pump, serial 5C-0822-034
- Motor and cable connections as supplied in the ESP vendor kit
- Replacement gaskets and wellhead seals

## Execution notes
- 2026-10-14: Rig up started, well secured, tubing pulled.
- Old pump recovered and tagged for vendor inspection, with wear consistent with the gradual rate decline.
- New pump run and tested.
- 2026-10-18: Work completed and handed back to Operations.

## Close-out remarks
Total downtime was 120 hours. The well was restarted and is producing.

| Period | Days | Oil (bbl/d) | Gas (Mscf/d) | Water cut (%) | GOR (scf/bbl) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Before (to 2026-10-13) | 14 | 243.6 | 255.9 | 44.2 | 1050 |
| After restart (to 2026-10-25) | 6 | 353.6 | 377.2 | 44.9 | 1067 |

Oil rate improved after the replacement, and water cut and GOR stayed essentially unchanged. No further actions are required, and the work order is closed.
