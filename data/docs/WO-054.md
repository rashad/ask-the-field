# Work order: Flowline pigging, W-08

**WO number:** WO-054
**Event reference:** EV-0157
**Well:** W-08 (Field A, vertical, ESP)
**Category:** Maintenance
**Priority:** Medium
**Raised:** 2026-08-25
**Planned start:** 2026-08-27
**Completion date:** 2026-08-27
**Status:** Closed

## Scope of work
Pig the W-08 flowline to CPF-1 to remove wax and sand deposits. Launch, run and receive the pig, record the debris volume recovered, and return the well to production after receiver checks.

This work follows the repair of the flowline flange leak on W-08 (EV-0156, 2026-08-25 to 2026-08-26). Pigging was scheduled once the line was back in service so deposits could be cleared before normal flow resumed.

## Safety and permits
- Hot work not required. Cold work permit and line-breaking permit issued.
- Pig launcher and receiver pressure checked and bled to zero before opening.
- Operators in the receiver area wore gas monitors and standard PPE.
- HSE Advisor briefed the crew at the toolbox talk before launch.

## Materials
- Foam-bodied pig and one spare
- Launcher and receiver closure seals
- Debris drums for recovered wax and sand

## Execution and close-out remarks
- Well shut in for the pigging run on 2026-08-27. Total downtime was 6 hours.
- Pig was received at CPF-1 with wax and sand deposits as expected. No stuck pig, no leaks at the repaired flange.
- Launcher and receiver closed out, seals replaced, area cleaned and permits closed.
- The well was restarted the same day and is stable.
