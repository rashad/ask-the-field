# Work order: Workover - ESP replacement, W-27

**WO number:** WO-054
**Related event:** EV-0156 (follow-up to EV-0150, ESP failure)
**Well:** W-27, Wadi Field, horizontal, ESP
**Category:** Maintenance
**Priority:** High
**Raised:** 2026-08-21
**Planned start:** 2026-08-23
**Completion date:** 2026-08-29
**Status:** Closed

## Scope of work
- Kill the well and pull the failed ESP completion (pump serial 7Q-0419-086, batch 7Q-0419, run life 11 months).
- Carry out teardown inspection of motor, pump stages and cable.
- Run a new Varenta VL-538 ESP, serial 5C-0822-019 (batch 5C-0822).
- Restart and hand the well back to production.

## Safety and permits
- Permit to Work issued under SOP-HSE-005 Permit to Work for Workover and Rig Operations.
- Pre-job meeting held with the rig crew and the ESP vendor representative.
- Well barriers verified before the BOP was nippled up.

## Materials
- New Varenta VL-538 pump assembly, serial 5C-0822-019.
- Completion running tools and consumables supplied by the rig contractor.

## Execution notes and close-out remarks
- Work ran from 2026-08-23 to 2026-08-29. Total downtime for the workover event was 168 hours (EV-0150 had already accounted for 120 hours).
- Teardown found motor winding insulation breakdown. Pump stages and cable were in good condition, so only the pump and motor assembly was replaced.
- The new ESP was installed and tested. The well returned to production on 2026-08-30.
- Production before the failure (9 days to 2026-08-22): 554 bbl/d oil, 604.3 Mscf/d gas, 40.8% water cut, GOR 1091 scf/bbl.
- Production after restart (6 days to 2026-09-05): 545.9 bbl/d oil, 594.1 Mscf/d gas, 41.7% water cut, GOR 1088 scf/bbl.
- Rates are in line with pre-failure performance. Continue routine ESP surveillance.
- Work order closed. Well status today: producing.
