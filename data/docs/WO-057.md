## Scope of work
Carry out a scale inhibitor squeeze on W-08 after carbonate scale build-up reduced ESP intake pressure and rate through Q3 2026. Shut in the well, pump the pre-flush, inhibitor and overflush stages, hold the shut-in soak, then restart the ESP and monitor.

Applicable procedure: SOP-OPS-008 Scale Inhibitor Squeeze Treatment.

## Safety and permits
- Hot work excluded. Cold work permit and chemical handling permit issued before start.
- Pre-job safety meeting held with the pumping crew and the Lead Operator.
- Pressure test of surface lines completed before pumping.
- Chemical SDS available at the wellsite. HSE Advisor reviewed spill containment.

## Materials
- Scale inhibitor and the associated pre-flush and overflush fluids
- Pumping unit with the chemical vendor crew
- Temporary surface lines and fittings

## Execution notes and close-out
- Well shut in on 2026-10-05 and treatment pumped as per SOP.
- Soak completed and well returned to service after 72 hours of downtime in total. Work finished 2026-10-07.
- No incidents, no spills and no lost-time injuries.
- Production before (7 days to 2026-10-04): oil 635.6 bbl/d, gas 466.9 Mscf/d, water cut 28.5%, GOR 735 scf/bbl.
- Production after restart (6 days to 2026-10-14): oil 977.5 bbl/d, gas 726 Mscf/d, water cut 27.6%, GOR 743 scf/bbl.
- Oil rate improved by about 342 bbl/d over the pre-job average.

## Follow-up
Production Engineer to continue monitoring intake pressure and rate and to schedule a residual inhibitor check in the produced water. Work order closed.
