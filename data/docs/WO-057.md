# Work order: Scale squeeze treatment, W-08

**WO number:** WO-057
**Event reference:** EV-0170
**Well:** W-08 (Dune Field, vertical, ESP)
**Category:** Maintenance
**Priority:** Medium
**Raised:** 2026-10-03
**Planned start / actual start:** 2026-10-05
**Completion date:** 2026-10-07
**Status:** Closed
**Applicable procedure:** SOP-OPS-008 Scale Inhibitor Squeeze Treatment

## Scope of work
Carry out a scale inhibitor squeeze on W-08 after carbonate scale build-up reduced ESP intake pressure and rate through Q3 2026. Shut in the well, pump the pre-flush, inhibitor and overflush stages per SOP-OPS-008, hold the shut-in soak period, then restart the ESP and return the well to production.

## Safety and permits
- Hot work not required. Work permit and pumping unit job safety analysis issued before mobilisation.
- Pressure test of surface lines completed before pumping.
- Chemical handling by trained crew only, with HSE Advisor sign-off on the spill containment set-up.
- ESP electrically isolated and locked out during pumping.

## Materials
- Scale inhibitor and the associated pre-flush and overflush fluids
- Pumping unit and temporary surface lines
- Chemical transfer and containment equipment

## Execution notes
- Well shut in on 2026-10-05 and treated per SOP-OPS-008.
- Total downtime was 72 hours (2026-10-05 to 2026-10-07).
- No incidents or leaks recorded during pumping.

## Close-out remarks
Production before the job (14 days to 2026-10-04) averaged 643.1 bbl/d oil, 473.6 Mscf/d gas, 28.3% water cut and GOR 736 scf/bbl. After restart (6 days to 2026-10-14) the well averaged 963.8 bbl/d oil, 706.3 Mscf/d gas, 28.3% water cut and GOR 733 scf/bbl. Water cut and GOR are unchanged, and oil rate is up by about 320 bbl/d. W-08 is producing. No related events within 30 days. Work order closed by the Production Engineer.
