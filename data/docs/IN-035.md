# Incident note: H2S alarm on W-34

**Document id:** IN-035
**Note date:** 2026-03-26
**Event id:** EV-0095
**Well:** W-34 (Bayda, horizontal, gas lift)
**Severity:** High
**Status:** Closed
**Author:** HSE Advisor
**Applicable procedure:** SOP-HSE-003 H2S Alarm Response

## What happened
On 2026-03-26 a fixed H2S detector near the W-34 wellhead went into alarm. The alarm was treated as a confirmed release until proven otherwise.

## Immediate actions
- The area around the wellhead was cleared and the muster point headcount was completed.
- The Lead Operator informed the Production Supervisor and the CPF-1 control room.
- The well was shut in and the area was entered only by personnel wearing breathing apparatus and carrying portable gas monitors.
- Access stayed barred until the portable readings confirmed a safe atmosphere.

## Findings
The inspection team traced the source to a leak on a needle valve at the wellhead. The valve was repaired. The area was re-tested after the repair and the detector returned to normal. No injuries were reported and no exposure was recorded.

## Impact
- Downtime: 3 hours on 2026-03-26 (uptime 21 hours).
- Production on the day: 1,124.4 bbl oil, 2,116.7 Mscf gas, water cut 26.7%.
- Previous 7-day average to 2026-03-25: 1,311.5 bbl/d oil, 2,338.1 Mscf/d gas, water cut 27.1%, GOR 1,783 scf/bbl.
- The day's oil was about 187 bbl below the recent average, mostly attributable to the stoppage.

## Status at note date
Closed. W-34 is back on production. No related events were recorded in the previous 30 days.

## Next actions
1. The Integrity Engineer to review the condition of the other needle valves on the Bayda wellheads at the next routine round.
2. The Maintenance Planner to add a detector function test to the next scheduled inspection.
3. The response was carried out in line with SOP-HSE-003. The Lead Operator to brief all crews on the lessons from this event at the next shift handover.
