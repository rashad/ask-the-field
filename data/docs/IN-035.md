# Incident note: H2S alarm on W-20

**Document ID:** IN-035
**Event ID:** EV-0098
**Note date:** 2026-03-20
**Well:** W-20 (Wadi Field, vertical, ESP)
**Severity:** Medium
**Status:** Closed
**Applicable procedure:** SOP-HSE-003 H2S Alarm Response

## What happened
On 2026-03-20 the fixed H2S detector near the W-20 wellhead went into alarm. The alarm was treated as a real release until proven otherwise.

## Immediate actions
- Area around the wellhead cleared and access barred.
- Personnel accounted for at the muster point; no injuries or exposures reported.
- Well taken offline while the area was checked, following SOP-HSE-003.
- Control room and Production Supervisor informed.
- Re-entry made only by a trained team with portable gas detectors and breathing apparatus.

## Findings
A small leak was found on a needle valve at the wellhead instrument connection. The valve was repaired and leak-tested. Portable detector readings around the wellhead returned to clear before the area was reopened and the well restarted.

## Impact
- Downtime: 2 hours (uptime 22 hours on the day).
- Production on 2026-03-20: oil 1207.7 bbl, gas 1056.2 Mscf, water cut 35%.
- Previous 7-day average to 2026-03-19: oil 1312.2 bbl/d, gas 1125.4 Mscf/d, water cut 34%, GOR 858 scf/bbl.
- The lower oil volume on the day is consistent with the 2 hours of downtime. Water cut is within one point of the prior average.

## Status at note date
Closed. W-20 is producing. No related events in the previous 30 days.

## Next actions
- Include the needle valve connection in the next routine wellhead leak check.
- Lead Operator to confirm the detector has been tested and reset after the event.
- HSE Advisor to file the alarm record against EV-0098.

*Production Engineer / HSE Advisor*
