# Incident note: H2S alarm on W-21

**Document ID:** IN-045
**Event ID:** EV-0118
**Note date:** 2026-05-13
**Well:** W-21 (Wadi Field, horizontal, ESP)
**Severity:** Medium
**Status:** Closed
**Author:** HSE Advisor

## What happened
On 2026-05-13 the fixed H2S detector near the W-21 wellhead went into alarm. The well was shut in and the area was cleared in line with the alarm response procedure.

## Immediate actions
- Personnel moved to the muster point and a headcount was completed.
- Access to the wellsite was barred until the area was checked.
- Operators wearing breathing apparatus and portable gas detectors approached from upwind and located the source.
- The Lead Operator and Production Supervisor were informed.

## Findings
The leak was on a needle valve at the wellhead. It was repaired, the area was re-tested with portable detectors and readings returned to normal before re-entry was permitted. No injuries were reported.

## Impact
- Downtime: 5 hours on 2026-05-13 (uptime 19 hours).
- Production on the day: oil 314.2 bbl, gas 315 Mscf, water cut 38.9%.
- Previous 7 days to 2026-05-12: oil 412.5 bbl/d, gas 444.1 Mscf/d, water cut 38.8%, GOR 1077 scf/bbl.
- Water cut is unchanged. The oil shortfall against the 7-day average is about 98 bbl, consistent with the lost hours.

## Status at note date
Closed. The well is producing and the alarm has been reset. The detector is operating normally.

## Next actions
- Production Engineer to confirm W-21 returns to its pre-event rate over the next few days.
- Maintenance Planner to include the other needle valves on the wellhead in the next routine leak check.
- HSE Advisor to review the alarm response with the shift crews at the next toolbox talk.
