# Incident note: Power outage on W-19

**Document ID:** IN-045
**Event ID:** EV-0114
**Note date:** 2026-05-02
**Well:** W-19 (Field B, horizontal, ESP)
**Severity:** medium
**Status at note date:** closed

## What happened
A field power dip on 2026-05-02 tripped the ESP on W-19. The well was restarted once power was restored and returned to production the same day.

## Immediate actions
- Lead Operator confirmed the trip was caused by the supply dip and not by a downhole or drive fault.
- Checked the variable speed drive for fault codes before restart and reviewed motor current and intake pressure trends after start-up.
- Production Supervisor was informed of the trip and the restart.

## Findings
- No equipment damage was found on the wellhead or the drive.
- The well came back on line and stable after restart.
- Water cut on the day was 39.8 percent, in line with the previous baseline of 40.1 percent.

## Impact
- Downtime: 9 hours (uptime 15 hours on the day).
- Production on 2026-05-02: oil 621.4 bbl, gas 645.2 Mscf.
- Baseline for the previous 7 days to 2026-05-01: oil 989 bbl/d, gas 975.7 Mscf/d, GOR 987 scf/bbl.
- The shortfall against baseline is about 368 bbl of oil, consistent with the lost run time.

## Related events
EV-0102 (H2S alarm on W-19, 2026-04-11, closed, 5 hours downtime) was a separate cause, a leaking needle valve at the wellhead. No link to this power event has been identified.

## Status and next actions
The incident is closed. The Production Engineer will keep watching W-19 rates and ESP motor current over the next few days to confirm they return to baseline. The power dip itself has been passed to the facilities team for review of supply stability. No specific procedure applies to this event.
