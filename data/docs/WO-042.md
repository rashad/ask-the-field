# Work order: Wellhead maintenance, W-27

**WO number:** WO-042
**Event reference:** EV-0058
**Well:** W-27 (Wadi Field, horizontal, ESP)
**Category:** Maintenance
**Priority:** Routine
**Raised:** 2025-11-30
**Planned start:** 2025-12-02
**Planned finish:** 2025-12-02
**Status:** Closed

## Scope of work
- Grease all wellhead valves (master, wing and swab) at the grease points.
- Function test each valve through full travel and check for smooth operation and correct position indication.
- Inspect the wellhead for leaks, corrosion and loose fittings.

## Safety and permits
- Work was done under a standard work permit with a toolbox talk before starting.
- The well was shut in for the work and the isolation was verified.
- Pressure was bled off the work area before the grease fittings were opened.
- No hot work and no confined space entry.

## Materials
- Valve grease (approved grade for the wellhead valves)
- Grease gun and replacement grease fittings (none needed)
- Function test log sheet

## Execution notes and close-out
Work was carried out on 2025-12-02 and finished the same day. The well was shut in for 4 hours in total. All valves were greased and function tested through full travel with no sticking found. No leaks were seen at the wellhead and no fittings needed replacing. The well was returned to service after the work.

**Production before (14 days to 2025-12-01):** oil 728.9 bbl/d, gas 797 Mscf/d, water cut 32.7%, GOR 1093 scf/bbl.

**Production after restart (6 days to 2025-12-09):** oil 700.1 bbl/d, gas 754.4 Mscf/d, water cut 33.7%, GOR 1078 scf/bbl.

The Production Engineer will keep watching the rates in the routine surveillance. No follow-up work is required.

**Closed:** 2025-12-02
