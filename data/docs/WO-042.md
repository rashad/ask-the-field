# Work order: Wellhead maintenance, W-39

**WO number:** WO-042
**Event reference:** EV-0065
**Well:** W-39 (Bayda, horizontal, ESP)
**Category:** Maintenance, wellhead
**Priority:** Routine
**Raised:** 2025-12-26
**Planned start:** 2025-12-28
**Actual start / completion:** 2025-12-28 / 2025-12-28
**Status:** Closed

## Scope of work
- Grease all wellhead valves (master, wing and swab) at the designated grease points.
- Function test each valve through full travel and confirm smooth operation and correct position indication.
- Visual check of wellhead flanges, gauges and connections for leaks or corrosion.

## Safety and permits
- Work permit and isolation certificate issued before the well was shut in.
- Toolbox talk held with the maintenance crew and the Lead Operator.
- Area barricaded, gas detector checked, wellhead pressure confirmed stable before work.
- No hot work involved.

## Materials
- Valve grease (approved grade), grease gun, calibrated torque wrench, cleaning rags, spare ring gaskets on standby (not used).

## Execution notes
The well was shut in briefly to allow the valve function test and restarted after the checks were complete. All valves moved through full travel and no stiffness was found. No leaks were seen at flanges or connections.

## Close-out remarks
- Total downtime: 2 hours.
- Work completed on 2025-12-28 as planned, no deferred items and no follow-up actions required.
- Production in the 7 days before the job (to 2025-12-27) averaged 804.6 bbl/d oil, 1304.8 Mscf/d gas, 15.7% water cut and GOR 1622 scf/bbl.
- After restart, the 6 days to 2026-01-04 averaged 808.3 bbl/d oil, 1353.4 Mscf/d gas, 15.9% water cut and GOR 1674 scf/bbl. No adverse effect on the well from the work was observed.
- Well W-39 is producing. Work order closed by the Maintenance Planner.
