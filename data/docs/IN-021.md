# Incident note: Wellhead leak on W-29

**Document ID:** IN-021
**Event ID:** EV-0072
**Note date:** 2026-01-09
**Author:** Production Engineer
**Well:** W-29 (Wadi Field, vertical, gas lift)
**Severity:** Low
**Status at note date:** Closed

## What happened
On 2026-01-09 a leak was found on the stem packing of a wellhead valve on W-29. The leak was small and contained at the wellhead. There was no release beyond the wellhead area.

## Immediate actions
- The well was shut in and the wellhead area isolated.
- The Lead Operator informed the Production Supervisor and the HSE Advisor.
- The area was barricaded and checked for residual hydrocarbons before work started.

## Findings
The leak came from worn packing on the valve stem. The packing was replaced, the valve was function tested and the wellhead was leak checked at operating pressure with no seepage observed. The well was then returned to production.

## Impact
- Downtime: 7 hours (uptime on the day was 17 hours).
- Production on 2026-01-09: 665.5 bbl oil and 533.9 Mscf gas, water cut 32.4%.
- Prior 7-day average to 2026-01-08: 987.5 bbl/d oil, 809.5 Mscf/d gas, water cut 32.6%, GOR 820 scf/bbl.
- Oil lost against the prior average was about 322 bbl, from the shorter run time on the day.

## Status
Closed on 2026-01-09. The repair is complete and the well was producing normally after restart. No related events were recorded on this well in the previous 30 days.

## Next actions
- Operators to include the W-29 wellhead valves in routine walk-round checks for stem packing seepage.
- Maintenance Planner to review the packing condition of similar valves at the next scheduled wellhead inspection.

## Procedure reference
No specific procedure applies to this event.
