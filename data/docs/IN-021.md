# Incident note: H2S alarm on W-38

**Document ID:** IN-021
**Event ID:** EV-0058
**Note date:** 2025-12-07
**Author:** HSE Advisor
**Well:** W-38 (Bayda, deviated, gas lift)
**Severity:** High
**Status at note date:** Closed

## What happened
On 2025-12-07 the fixed H2S detector near the W-38 wellhead went into alarm. The alarm was acknowledged in the control room and the response under SOP-HSE-003 H2S Alarm Response was started.

## Immediate actions
- The wellhead area was cleared and personnel were mustered at the upwind assembly point.
- The well was shut in and isolated while the area was made safe.
- A portable gas detector check was done by a trained team wearing breathing apparatus before re-entry.
- The Production Supervisor was informed.

## Findings
The leak source was a needle valve at the wellhead. The valve was repaired and a leak check after the repair showed no gas. Readings returned to normal and the area was declared safe. No injuries were reported.

## Impact
- Downtime: 3 hours (uptime on the day was 21 hours).
- Production on 2025-12-07: oil 1266.4 bbl, gas 1846.6 Mscf, water cut 27.1%.
- Baseline for the 7 days to 2025-12-06: oil 1456 bbl/d, gas 2147.2 Mscf/d, water cut 26.9%, GOR 1475 scf/bbl.
- Oil on the day was about 190 bbl below the baseline rate, consistent with the shut-in period. Water cut was essentially unchanged.

## Status
Closed. W-38 is back on production and is producing today. There are no related events on this well in the last 30 days.

## Next actions
- The Lead Operator will include the needle valve in the next round of wellhead leak checks.
- The Maintenance Planner will review other needle valves of the same type at Bayda wellheads at the next routine inspection.
- Detector response and the clearance log will be filed with the incident record.
