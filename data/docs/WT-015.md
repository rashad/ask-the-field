# Well test: W-17, 2026-02-10

**Document ID:** WT-015
**Well:** W-17 (Field B, deviated, ESP)
**Test date:** 2026-02-10
**Procedure:** SOP-OPS-004 Well Test Procedure with the Mobile Test Separator

## Test conditions
- Duration: 8 hours on the mobile test separator, after line-up and stabilisation
- Choke: 48/64 in
- Flowing wellhead pressure: 393 psi
- ESP running normally, no trips or restarts during the test
- No events recorded on the well in the last 45 days

## Measured results
| Parameter | Test 2026-02-10 | Baseline 2025-12-12 |
|---|---|---|
| Oil (bbl/d) | 1269.4 | 1494.1 |
| Gas (Mscf/d) | 702.1 | 811 |
| Water cut (%) | 29.8 | 28.1 |
| GOR (scf/bbl) | 553 | 543 |

Measured values are based on 3 days of data ending 2026-02-10. The baseline is based on 7 days of data ending 2025-12-12.

## Comparison
- Oil is down 224.7 bbl/d against the baseline, about 15 percent.
- Gas is down 108.9 Mscf/d.
- Water cut is up 1.7 points.
- GOR is almost unchanged (553 against 543 scf/bbl), so the gas decline follows the oil decline.

## Sand
Sand measured 2 ppm, which is negligible.

## Observations
- Separator readings were stable through the final hours of the test.
- The oil decline is not explained by any logged event, choke change or sand production.

## Recommendation
- Accept the test as valid for allocation.
- The Production Engineer to review the ESP performance trend (motor current, intake and discharge pressure) to look for pump wear or declining inflow.
- Repeat the test in the next scheduled cycle to confirm the lower rate.
