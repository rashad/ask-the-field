# Well test: W-38, 2026-08-05

**Document ID:** WT-028
**Well:** W-38 (Bayda, deviated, gas lift)
**Test date:** 2026-08-05
**Procedure:** SOP-OPS-004 Well Test Procedure with the Mobile Test Separator

## Test conditions
- Test duration: 8 hours on the mobile test separator
- Choke: 28/64 in
- Flowing wellhead pressure: 527 psi
- Well status today: producing

## Measured results
Rates are the 3-day measured period ending 2026-08-05.

| Parameter | Measured | Previous baseline (7 days to 2026-06-06) |
|---|---|---|
| Oil (bbl/d) | 643.7 | 1282 |
| Gas (Mscf/d) | 983.9 | 1924.1 |
| Water cut (%) | 31.5 | 30.9 |
| GOR (scf/bbl) | 1529 | 1501 |

- Sand: 17 ppm

## Comparison with baseline
Oil fell from 1282 to 643.7 bbl/d, a drop of about 50%. Gas fell in proportion, so GOR is essentially unchanged (1529 against 1501 scf/bbl). Water cut moved only slightly, from 30.9% to 31.5%. The loss is therefore a loss of liquid rate, not a change in fluid character.

## Observations
- Event EV-0143 (gas lift valve failure, 2026-07-20 to 2026-07-21, 20 hours downtime, closed) is the relevant recent event. An unloading valve stuck open and lift gas was injecting shallow.
- Sand at 17 ppm is low for Bayda and shows no sand concern.
- Separator readings were stable through the test.

## Recommendation
- The reduced rate remains well below baseline after the valve event was closed. Review the gas lift design and injection depth with the Production Engineer.
- Check the gas lift injection rate and the valve status by survey.
- Repeat the test after any adjustment, following SOP-OPS-004.
