# Work order: Flowline pigging, W-02

**WO number:** WO-009
**Related event:** EV-0015
**Well:** W-02 (Field A, deviated, gas lift)
**Category:** Maintenance
**Priority:** Routine
**Raised:** 2025-06-30
**Planned start:** 2025-07-02
**Actual start:** 2025-07-02
**Completion date:** 2025-07-02
**Status:** Closed

## Scope of work

Flowline pigging to remove wax and sand deposits from the W-02 flowline to CPF-1.

1. Isolate the well and depressurize the flowline to the pig launcher.
2. Load the pig and launch it with the lift gas and flowline supply.
3. Monitor pig progress and confirm receipt at the CPF-1 receiver.
4. Drain and clean the receiver, and record the volume and type of debris.
5. Restore the line and restart the well.

## Safety and permits

- Hot work not required. A cold work permit and a line-breaking permit were issued.
- Toolbox talk held before launch, with the CPF-1 control room informed of the shutdown.
- Pressure in the launcher confirmed at zero before opening the closure door.
- Debris handled with gloves and a containment tray.

## Materials

- Foam-bodied cleaning pig, spare pig held in reserve
- Replacement closure door seal
- Waste drum for wax and sand

## Close-out remarks

The pig run was completed on 2025-07-02 and the well was shut in for 9 hours in total. The receiver returned wax and sand deposits, consistent with the purpose of the job. The well was restarted and returned to stable flow.
