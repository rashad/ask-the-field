# Work order: Safety valve test, W-18

**WO number:** WO-009
**Event reference:** EV-0011
**Well:** W-18 (Wadi Field, deviated, ESP)
**Category:** Maintenance
**Priority:** Routine
**Raised:** 2025-07-06
**Planned start:** 2025-07-08
**Actual start:** 2025-07-08
**Completion date:** 2025-07-08
**Status:** Closed

## Scope of work
Subsurface safety valve function and leak test on W-18. Shut in the well, close the valve from the control panel, confirm closure, run the leak test and reopen. Record the results on the test sheet.

## Safety and permits
- Work permit and isolation certificate issued before start.
- Toolbox talk held with the slickline unit crew and the operators on shift.
- Wellhead area barricaded during pressure testing.
- HSE Advisor informed of the shut-in window.
- No applicable procedure is referenced on this work order. The task was run under the routine permit-to-work process.

## Materials
- Test pump and calibrated gauges
- Control line fittings (none consumed)
- Test sheet and valve record

## Execution notes
Well shut in on 2025-07-08 and the safety valve cycled from the panel. Valve closed and held the leak test. It was reopened and the well returned to service the same day. Total downtime was 5 hours.

## Close-out remarks
- Test passed, no defects found and no repairs needed.
- Production before the work (14 days to 2025-07-07): 775.6 bbl/d oil, 596.8 Mscf/d gas, 27.4% water cut, GOR 769 scf/bbl.
- Production after restart (6 days to 2025-07-15): 775.4 bbl/d oil, 592.2 Mscf/d gas, 27.7% water cut, GOR 764 scf/bbl.
- No change in well performance following the test.
- Test sheet filed in the well record. Work order closed by the Lead Operator and the Maintenance Planner.
