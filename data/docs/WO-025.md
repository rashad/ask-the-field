## Scope of work
Preventive scale inhibitor squeeze on W-08. Shut in the well, bullhead inhibitor and overflush per SOP-OPS-008, hold the shut-in soak period, then restart and bring the ESP back to its previous operating point.

## Safety and permits
- Hot work not required. Permit to work and chemical handling permit issued before mobilisation.
- Pre-job toolbox talk held with the pumping crew and operators.
- Chemical safety data sheet reviewed, eyewash and spill kit positioned at the wellhead.
- Pressure test of surface lines completed before pumping.

## Materials
- Scale inhibitor and overflush brine, quantities as per the treatment programme.
- Pumping unit and temporary lines supplied by the service contractor.

## Execution notes
Work started on 2025-09-17 and finished on 2025-09-18. The well was restarted after the soak and stabilised without issue. Total downtime was 47 hours.
