# Work order: Gas lift valve change-out, W-41

**WO number:** WO-025
**Event reference:** EV-0038
**Well:** W-41 (Bayda, vertical, gas lift)
**Category:** Maintenance
**Priority:** Routine
**Raised:** 2025-09-29
**Planned start:** 2025-10-01
**Planned completion:** 2025-10-01
**Actual start / completion:** 2025-10-01 / 2025-10-01
**Status:** Closed

## Scope of work
- Slickline gas lift valve change-out on W-41.
- Pull the existing valve, run the replacement valve and confirm seating.
- Return the well to production and monitor lift gas injection after restart.

## Safety and permits
- Work permit and slickline lifting plan issued by the Production Supervisor.
- Pressure test of the lubricator and wellhead barrier check before opening the well.
- Toolbox talk held with the slickline crew and the Lead Operator. HSE Advisor reviewed the permit.
- Lift gas isolated and bled down per the permit conditions.

## Materials
- Replacement gas lift valve with new seals and latch.
- Slickline unit with standard toolstring.
- Spare packing and O-rings for the lubricator.

## Execution notes
- Well shut in for the job, total downtime 14 hours.
- Valve change-out completed on 2025-10-01 with no incidents and no lost-time events.
- Well restarted and lift gas injection stabilized. Troubleshooting references followed SOP-OPS-007 Gas Lift Troubleshooting.
