# Incident note: Flowline leak on W-24

**Document ID:** IN-036
**Note date:** 2026-03-26
**Event ID:** EV-0099
**Well:** W-24 (Wadi Field, deviated, ESP)
**Severity:** Medium
**Status at note date:** Closed
**Author:** Production Engineer

## What happened
A small leak was found on a flowline flange at W-24 on 2026-03-26. The well was shut down, the flowline isolated and the flange repaired the same day.

## Immediate actions
- W-24 shut in and the flowline isolated and depressurised.
- Area barricaded and the Lead Operator and HSE Advisor informed.
- Spilled fluid contained and cleaned up.
- Flange joint remade and checked for leaks on restart.

## Findings so far
The leak was limited to the flange joint. No injuries were reported and no other equipment was affected. The well was returned to production after the repair and is producing today.

## Impact
- Downtime: 21 hours.
- Production on the day: 151.4 bbl oil, 75.7 Mscf gas, water cut 27.6 percent, with 3 hours of uptime.
- Previous 7-day average to 2026-03-25: 1256.1 bbl/d oil, 679 Mscf/d gas, water cut 26 percent, GOR 541 scf/bbl.
- The deferred oil is the difference between the previous average and the rate achieved on the day.

## Status
Closed as of 2026-03-26. The repair was completed and the well is back online.

## Next actions
- Production Engineer to confirm the well returns to its pre-event rate over the next few days.
- Operators to include the repaired flange in routine walk-down checks.
- A routine ESP surface equipment inspection and VSD parameter check is planned for 2026-04-11 (EV-0107).

## Applicable procedure
SOP-HSE-006 Flowline Leak Response was followed for isolation, containment and repair.
