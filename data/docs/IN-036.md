# Incident note: Wellhead leak on W-02

**Document ID:** IN-036
**Event ID:** EV-0096
**Note date:** 2026-03-27
**Well:** W-02 (Field A, deviated, gas lift)
**Severity:** medium
**Status at note date:** closed

## What happened
On 2026-03-27 a leak was detected on the wellhead valve stem packing of W-02. The well was taken offline to make the area safe and repair the leak.

## Immediate actions
- Well shut in and the wellhead area isolated and barricaded.
- Lead Operator informed the Production Supervisor and the HSE Advisor.
- Leaked fluid contained and cleaned up, with no release beyond the wellhead pad.
- Valve stem packing replaced by the maintenance crew under a work permit.
- Wellhead pressure-tested and checked for leaks before the well was returned to service.

## Findings
The leak came from worn packing on the wellhead valve stem. No damage to the valve body or other wellhead components was seen during the repair.

## Impact
- Downtime: 13 hours.
- Uptime recorded on 2026-03-27: 11 hours.
- Production on the day: 580.5 bbl oil and 244 Mscf gas, water cut 22.1%.
- Baseline for the 7 days to 2026-03-26: 1361.6 bbl/d oil, 658.7 Mscf/d gas, water cut 22.4%, GOR 484 scf/bbl.
- Deferred oil on the day was about 781 bbl against the baseline rate (1361.6 minus 580.5).

## Status
Closed. The packing was replaced, the well was restarted and it is producing today.

## Next actions
- Production Engineer to confirm the well has returned to its baseline rate over the next few days.
- Maintenance Planner to add W-02 wellhead valve packing to the next routine inspection round.
- Operators to include a visual check for stem leaks in daily wellhead rounds.

## Procedure reference
No specific procedure applies to this event.
