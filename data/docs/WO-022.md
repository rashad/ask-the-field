## Scope of work
Flowline pigging to remove wax and sand deposits from the W-23 production flowline to CPF-1. Launch the pig at the wellsite launcher, receive it at the CPF-1 receiver, and recover and record the debris returned.

## Safety and permits
- Hot work not required. Non-routine pressure work permit issued for launcher and receiver operations.
- Toolbox talk held with the operators and the maintenance crew before the start.
- Launcher and receiver depressured and drained before opening. Personnel kept clear of the barrel door line of fire.
- HSE Advisor informed of the planned shutdown of the well.

## Materials
- Foam-body cleaning pig, with a spare
- Launcher and receiver door seals
- Waste containers for wax and sand returns

## Execution and close-out remarks
- The well was shut in on 2025-09-05 for the pigging run, with total downtime of 5 hours as of close-out. The well was restarted after the pig was received.
- The pig arrived at the CPF-1 receiver with wax and sand deposits. Debris was bagged and sent for disposal.
- No leaks were seen at the launcher or receiver connections after the system was repressurised.
- The ESP was restarted without issue.
