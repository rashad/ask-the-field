# Work order: Choke replacement, W-04

**WO number:** WO-022
**Event reference:** EV-0034
**Well:** W-04 (Field A, deviated, ESP)
**Category:** Maintenance
**Priority:** Routine
**Raised:** 2025-09-24
**Planned start / finish:** 2025-09-26 / 2025-09-26
**Actual start / finish:** 2025-09-26 / 2025-09-26
**Status:** Closed

## Scope of work
- Shut in W-04 at the wellhead and isolate the flowline at the choke manifold.
- Depressurize and verify zero pressure on the choke body.
- Remove the eroded choke bean, inspect the seat and body, and install a new bean.
- Pressure test the assembly, then return the well to production on the ESP.

## Safety and permits
- Hot work not required. Cold work permit and isolation certificate issued by the Lead Operator.
- Toolbox talk held before starting. Line breaking carried out with a bleed-off check and gas monitor in use.

## Materials
- 1 x replacement choke bean (same size as the eroded unit).
- 1 x seal kit for the choke body.
- Standard hand tools and torque wrench.

## Execution notes and close-out remarks
- Work was carried out on 2025-09-26 and completed the same day.
- The bean showed clear erosion. The seat was in acceptable condition and was cleaned and reused.
- The pressure test was held with no leaks and the well was restarted without issue.
- Total downtime was 7 hours.
- Rates before the work (7 days to 2025-09-25): oil 1423.7 bbl/d, gas 830.9 Mscf/d, water cut 13.4 percent, GOR 584 scf/bbl.
- Rates after restart (6 days to 2025-10-03): oil 1425.1 bbl/d, gas 830.9 Mscf/d, water cut 12.9 percent, GOR 583 scf/bbl.
- Performance is in line with the pre-work baseline. No further action on the choke is required.
- Separate from this order, a preventive scale inhibitor squeeze on W-04 (EV-0045) is planned for 2025-10-22.

**Close-out:** Production Engineer informed. Work order closed.
