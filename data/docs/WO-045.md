# Work order: Well suspension, W-40

**WO number:** WO-045
**Event reference:** EV-0071
**Well:** W-40 (Bayda, horizontal, natural flow)
**Category:** Maintenance
**Priority:** Routine
**Date raised:** 2026-01-10
**Planned start:** 2026-01-12 (work started on this date)
**Completion date:** not yet completed
**Status:** Open

## Scope of work

Suspend W-40 after water cut passed 90 percent and rate fell below the economic limit. The work covers:

1. Close the choke and isolate the well at the wing valve and the master valve.
2. Depressurise and isolate the flowline at the wellhead and at the CPF-1 inlet manifold, and tag the isolation points.
3. Record the final wellhead pressures and close the well status in the production database as suspended.
4. Carry out a visual check of the wellhead and the Christmas tree for leaks and record the valve positions.
5. Apply suspension tags and update the well status board.

## Baseline before suspension

Seven days to 2026-01-11: oil 99.8 bbl/d, gas 126.6 Mscf/d, water cut 88.2 percent, GOR 1269 scf/bbl.

## Safety and permits

- Work permit and isolation certificate required before valve operation.
- Lock-out and tag-out applied to all isolation points.
- Gas detector carried by the crew during flowline depressurisation.
- HSE Advisor to confirm the permit before work starts.

## Materials

- Isolation tags and padlocks
- Valve position record sheet
- Bleed hose and containment tray

## Execution notes

The work is not finished as of the document date. The well remains suspended, and downtime stands at 7560 hours. The Production Engineer will review a reactivation or abandonment case. The work order stays open until the final isolation checks and the status update are signed off by the Production Supervisor.
