## Scope of work

Suspend W-40 after the water cut passed 90 percent and the rate fell below the economic limit. The scope covers:

1. Stop gas lift injection and shut in the well at the wellhead.
2. Close the master and wing valves and confirm they hold.
3. Isolate the flowline at the manifold and depressurise the local section.
4. Record the final wellhead pressures and fit isolation tags.
5. Update the well status to suspended in the production system.

## Pre-suspension baseline

Last 14 days to 2026-01-11:

- Oil: 89.5 bbl/d
- Gas: 138.7 Mscf/d
- Water cut: 88.7 percent
- GOR: 1550 scf/bbl

## Safety and permits

- Work permit and lock-out/tag-out required for valve isolation.
- Check for trapped pressure before breaking any connection.
- HSE Advisor to review the isolation plan before start.

## Materials

- Isolation tags and padlocks
- Blind flange for the manifold tie-in, if required
- Pressure gauges for the verification checks

## Execution notes

Work started on 2026-01-12. As of today the work order remains open and the well is suspended. Downtime recorded to date is 7560 hours. No completion date has been set, and the work order stays open until the Production Supervisor confirms the final status and the isolation checks are signed off.
