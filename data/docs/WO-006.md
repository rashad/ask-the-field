# Work order: Choke replacement, W-02

**WO number:** WO-006
**Well:** W-02 (Dune Field, deviated, gas lift)
**Event reference:** EV-0008
**Category:** Maintenance
**Priority:** Medium
**Raised:** 2025-06-19
**Planned start / end:** 2025-06-21 / 2025-06-21
**Actual start / end:** 2025-06-21 / 2025-06-21
**Status:** Closed

## Scope of work
Replace the eroded choke bean on the W-02 wellhead flow line. Inspect the choke body and seat for erosion damage, fit a new bean, and return the well to production at the previous choke setting.

## Safety and permits
- Hot work not required; cold work permit issued.
- Well shut in and flow line isolated and depressurised before breaking containment.
- Gas detection in use at the wellhead during the job.
- Toolbox talk held with the maintenance crew and the Lead Operator before starting.

## Materials
- 1 x replacement choke bean (same size as removed bean)
- 1 x gasket set for the choke bonnet
- Standard hand tools and torque wrench

## Execution notes
The well was shut in on the morning of 2025-06-21. The eroded bean was removed and the choke body and seat were inspected; no further damage was found. The new bean and gaskets were installed, the bonnet was torqued to specification, and a pressure test showed no leaks. The well was returned to production the same day.

## Close-out remarks
- Total downtime: 4 hours.
- Production before the job (14 days to 2025-06-20): oil 1566.2 bbl/d, gas 765.6 Mscf/d, water cut 17.4 %, GOR 489 scf/bbl.
- Production after restart (6 days to 2025-06-28): oil 1577.9 bbl/d, gas 753 Mscf/d, water cut 17.4 %, GOR 477 scf/bbl.
- Rates are stable after the replacement and water cut is unchanged.
- No follow-up work is required. Work order closed.
