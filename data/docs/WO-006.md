# Work order: Wellhead maintenance, W-15

**WO number:** WO-006
**Event reference:** EV-0012
**Well:** W-15 (Field A, vertical, gas lift)
**Priority:** Routine

**Actual start:** 2025-06-27
**Completion date:** 2025-06-27
**Status:** Closed

## Scope of work

Wellhead valve greasing and function test on W-15.

1. Shut in the well and isolate it from the flowline.
2. Grease the wellhead valves.
3. Function test each valve through its full travel and record the result.
4. Restore the well to service and confirm stable flow.

## Safety and permits

- Work permit issued before start.
- Lockout and tagout applied to the flowline isolation.
- Pressure confirmed bled down to zero before any greasing point was opened.
- Gas detection in use at the wellhead.
- Toolbox talk held with the crew before work began.

## Materials

- Valve grease (approved grade) and grease gun
- Hand tools and gauges
- Function test record sheet

## Execution notes

Work was carried out on 2025-06-27. The well was down for 4 hours as of the document date. All valves were greased and function tested, with no sticking or leakage observed. The well was returned to production and flow stabilised.

A related work record, EV-0011, covers the same wellhead valve greasing and function test on W-15 on the same date. It shows 5 hours of downtime. The two records overlap in scope and date, so the planner should review them for duplication.
