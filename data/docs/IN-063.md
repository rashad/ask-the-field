# Incident note: Wellhead leak on W-41

**Document id:** IN-063
**Event id:** EV-0173
**Note date:** 2026-10-11
**Well:** W-41 (Bayda, vertical, gas lift)
**Category:** Incident
**Severity:** Medium
**Status at note date:** Closed

## What happened
On 2026-10-11 a leak was found on the wellhead valve stem packing of W-41. The Lead Operator noticed fluid at the stem during a routine round and reported it to the Production Engineer.

## Immediate actions
- The well was shut in and the wellhead area isolated and barricaded.
- The HSE Advisor was informed and the area was checked for residual hydrocarbon before work started.
- The valve stem packing was replaced by the maintenance crew.
- The valve was function tested and a leak check was done at operating pressure. No leak was seen.
- The well was returned to production the same day.

## Findings
The leak came from worn stem packing only. No damage to the valve body, bonnet or flanges was found. The small spill was contained at the wellhead and cleaned up.

## Impact
- Downtime: 4 hours, with uptime of 20 hours on the day.
- Production on 2026-10-11: 804.4 bbl of oil, 980.5 Mscf of gas, water cut 28.7%.
- Baseline for the 7 days to 2026-10-10: 974.1 bbl/d oil, 1231 Mscf/d gas, water cut 26.3%, GOR 1264 scf/bbl.
- Oil on the day was about 170 bbl below the baseline rate, mostly from the 4 hours of downtime.

## Status
Closed. The packing was replaced, the leak check passed and W-41 is producing normally. No related events were recorded on this well in the previous 30 days.

## Next actions
- The Lead Operator will include the W-41 wellhead valves in the next rounds to confirm there is no recurrence.
- The Maintenance Planner will review packing condition on the other Bayda wellhead valves at the next scheduled inspection.

## Procedure reference
No specific procedure applies to this event.
