# Incident note: Power outage on W-27

**Document ID:** IN-063
**Event ID:** EV-0173
**Note date:** 2026-10-13
**Well:** W-27 (Wadi Field, horizontal, ESP)
**Severity:** Low
**Status:** Closed

## What happened
A field power dip on 2026-10-13 tripped the ESP on W-27. The well was restarted after power was restored to the field.

## Immediate actions
- The Lead Operator confirmed the trip at the motor controller and checked that no other fault alarms were active.
- Incoming supply was verified stable before any restart was attempted.
- The ESP was restarted and the well was brought back to its normal operating point.
- Intake pressure, motor current and wellhead pressure were observed during the first hours after restart and showed normal behaviour.

## Findings
- The trip was caused by the external power dip, not by an equipment fault on the well.
- No abnormal motor current or vibration alarms were recorded after restart.
- No related events on W-27 in the previous 30 days.

## Impact
- Downtime: 10 hours on 2026-10-13 (uptime 14 hours for the day).
- Production on the day: 294.8 bbl of oil and 312.7 Mscf of gas, water cut 42.4%.
- Baseline for the 7 days to 2026-10-12: 521.3 bbl/d oil, 555.6 Mscf/d gas, water cut 43.4%, GOR 1066 scf/bbl.
- The day's oil volume sits below the baseline rate, consistent with the 10 hours of lost run time. Water cut was in line with the baseline.

## Status at note date
Closed. W-27 is producing normally.

## Next actions
- The Production Engineer will check the following day's rates against the baseline to confirm that the well has returned to its previous level.
- The outage is to be passed to the facilities team so the cause of the field power dip can be reviewed.

## Procedure reference
No specific procedure applies to this event.
