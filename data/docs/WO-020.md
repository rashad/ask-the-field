# Work order: Flowline pigging, W-11

**WO number:** WO-020
**Event reference:** EV-0031
**Well:** W-11 (Field A, deviated, gas lift)
**Category:** Maintenance
**Priority:** Routine
**Raised:** 2025-09-19
**Planned start:** 2025-09-21
**Actual start:** 2025-09-21
**Completion date:** 2025-09-21
**Status:** Closed

## Scope of work
Flowline pigging to remove wax and sand deposits from the W-11 flowline to CPF-1. Launch the pig at the wellsite launcher, receive it at the CPF-1 receiver, and record the volume and nature of the debris recovered.

## Safety and permits
- Hot work not required. Cold work permit and line-breaking permit issued for the launcher and receiver.
- Pressure bled to zero and verified before opening the launcher door.
- HSE Advisor briefed the crew on pig trap handling, residual hydrocarbon and pyrophoric scale precautions.
- Drain and containment in place at the receiver for waxy debris.

## Materials
- One foam-core pig and one spare
- Launcher and receiver door seals (replaced)
- Waste drums for wax and sand

## Execution notes
- The well was shut in for the job, giving 10 hours of downtime on 2025-09-21.
- The pig ran through and was received at CPF-1 without sticking. Wax and sand were recovered in the receiver.
- The launcher and receiver were isolated, depressurised and returned to service after the run. W-11 was restarted and lined up normally.

## Close-out remarks
- Production averaged 1036.7 bbl/d oil, 1070.7 Mscf/d gas, 15.6% water cut and 1033 scf/bbl GOR over the 7 days to 2025-09-20.
- After restart, the 6 days to 2025-09-28 averaged 1065.1 bbl/d oil, 1041.2 Mscf/d gas, 15.4% water cut and 978 scf/bbl GOR.
- Work complete and the work order is closed. A further flowline pigging of W-11 (EV-0042) is planned from 2025-10-13.

**Signed off:** Lead Operator (K.M.), Maintenance Planner
