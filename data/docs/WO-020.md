## Scope of work
Routine ESP surface equipment inspection and VSD parameter check on W-04.
1. Inspect the VSD cabinet, cooling fans, filters and cable terminations.
2. Check the transformer and junction box for heat marks and loose connections.
3. Compare VSD parameters (frequency, current limits, trip settings) with the approved settings sheet.
4. Check the wellhead power cable gland and surface sensor readings.

## Safety and permits
- Electrical isolation and lock-out/tag-out applied before cabinet access.
- Work permit issued by the Production Supervisor.
- Arc-flash rated PPE worn. Toolbox talk held before the job.

## Materials
- Replacement cabinet filter on hand. No other spares required.

## Execution notes and close-out remarks
The well was shut down for the inspection and restarted the same day after the checks. Total downtime was 3 hours. The inspection found no abnormalities, and the VSD parameters matched the approved settings, so no changes were made. Cabinet filters were cleaned and the connections were found tight.

Production before the job (14 days to 2025-09-03) averaged 1446.5 bbl/d oil, 847.3 Mscf/d gas, 12.3 % water cut and GOR 586 scf/bbl. After restart (6 days to 2025-09-11) the well averaged 1449.4 bbl/d oil, 851.1 Mscf/d gas, 12.3 % water cut and GOR 587 scf/bbl. Performance is unchanged within normal variation.

No related events in the previous 30 days. No follow-up actions are required. Work order closed.
