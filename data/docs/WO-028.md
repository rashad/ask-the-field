# Work order: Safety valve test, W-28

**WO number:** WO-028
**Event reference:** EV-0036
**Well:** W-28 (Wadi Field, deviated, gas lift)
**Category:** Maintenance
**Priority:** Routine
**Raised:** 2025-09-29
**Planned start:** 2025-10-01
**Actual start / completion:** 2025-10-01 / 2025-10-01
**Status:** Closed

## Scope of work
Function and leak test of the subsurface safety valve (SSSV) on W-28.
1. Shut in the well and isolate it from the flowline to CPF-1.
2. Bleed down the tubing pressure above the valve in a controlled manner.
3. Close the SSSV from the control panel and confirm closure.
4. Monitor the pressure above the valve for the hold period and record any build-up.
5. Reopen the valve, equalise, and confirm full function.
6. Return the well to production.

## Safety and permits
- Work permit and isolation certificate issued before start.
- Toolbox talk held with the operations crew.
- Gas detection in use at the wellhead during bleed-down.
- HSE Advisor informed of the shut-in.

## Materials
- Calibrated pressure gauges and test recorder.
- Hydraulic control line pump and fittings.
- Test record sheet.

## Close-out remarks
- The SSSV function and leak test was completed on 2025-10-01 with no deviations reported.
- The valve closed on command, held pressure within the acceptance limit and reopened normally.
- Downtime for the well: 4 hours.
- Production before the job (14 days to 2025-09-30): oil 653.4 bbl/d, gas 517.6 Mscf/d, water cut 34.7%, GOR 792 scf/bbl.
- Production after restart (6 days to 2025-10-08): oil 665.2 bbl/d, gas 543.5 Mscf/d, water cut 33.4%, GOR 817 scf/bbl.
- No lasting change in well performance from the test. No follow-up work required.
- Test record filed with the well file. Next test to follow the normal surveillance schedule.

**Closed by:** Lead Operator, with Maintenance Planner informed.
