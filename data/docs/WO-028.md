## Scope of work
Flowline pigging to remove wax and sand deposits from the W-25 flowline to CPF-1. Launch the pig at the wellsite launcher, receive at the CPF-1 receiver, and record the volume and type of debris recovered.

## Safety and permits
- Hot work not required; cold work permit issued for the launcher and receiver.
- Pre-job meeting held with the Lead Operator and the pigging crew.
- Depressurize and verify zero pressure on the launcher before opening the door.
- Vent and drain lines checked clear; waste to be handled as per site procedure.

## Materials
- Foam-body pig and one spare
- Launcher and receiver door seals
- Waste drums for wax and sand

## Execution notes
The well was shut in for the job and the flowline was lined up for pigging. Total well downtime was 9 hours. The pig run was completed on 2025-10-16 and wax and sand deposits were received at CPF-1. The well was then restarted and the line returned to normal service.

## Close-out remarks
- Work completed on the planned date, with no safety incidents.
- Production before (7 days to 2025-10-15): oil 576.6 bbl/d, gas 481.6 Mscf/d, water cut 26.1%, GOR 835 scf/bbl.
- Production after restart (6 days to 2025-10-23): oil 553.3 bbl/d, gas 443.8 Mscf/d, water cut 27.1%, GOR 802 scf/bbl.
- Oil rate is lower than before the job; the Production Engineer to keep watching the trend.
- Related earlier event: EV-0039, high separator level trip on 2025-10-03 (6 hours downtime, closed).
- No applicable procedure is referenced for this job.
- Work order closed.
