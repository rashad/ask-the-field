# Incident note: High separator level trip on W-07

**Document ID:** IN-054
**Note date:** 2026-07-28
**Event ID:** EV-0145
**Well:** W-07 (Field A, vertical, ESP)
**Severity:** Low
**Status at note date:** Closed
**Prepared by:** Production Engineer

## What happened
On 2026-07-28 a high-level trip on the test separator shut in W-07. The well was down for 3 hours and then returned to production the same day.

## Immediate actions
- The Lead Operator confirmed the trip on the CPF-1 panel and checked that no other wells on the test header were affected.
- The level was verified locally against the gauge glass. The transmitter reading did not agree with the glass, which pointed to an instrument problem rather than a real liquid carry-over.
- The level controller was recalibrated by the instrument technician and the setpoints were checked.
- W-07 was restarted after the level returned to normal and the ESP was confirmed stable.

## Findings so far
- The cause was controller drift that gave a false high-level reading. The recalibration cleared it.
- No spill, no release and no injury. No damage to the ESP was found on restart.
- The flowline on W-07 was pigged on 2026-07-24 (EV-0144, 6 hours downtime). No link to the trip has been established, but the pigging is noted as a nearby event.

## Impact
- Downtime: 3 hours. Uptime on the day was 21 hours.
- Production on 2026-07-28: oil 524.9 bbl, gas 596.4 Mscf, water cut 27.8%.
- The previous 7-day average to 2026-07-27 was 576.8 bbl/d oil, 645 Mscf/d gas, water cut 28.4%, GOR 1118 scf/bbl. Oil on the day was about 52 bbl below that average, which is consistent with the lost hours.

## Status
Closed. W-07 is producing normally.

## Next actions
- The Maintenance Planner to add the test separator level controller to the next routine calibration check list.
- The Lead Operator to watch the level trend on the next two shifts.

## Applicable procedure
No specific procedure applies to this event.
