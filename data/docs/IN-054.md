# Incident note: Gas lift valve failure on W-38

**Document ID:** IN-054
**Event ID:** EV-0139
**Note date:** 2026-07-21
**Author:** Production Engineer
**Well:** W-38 (Salt Flat Field, deviated, gas lift)
**Severity:** Medium
**Status at note date:** Closed

## What happened
On 2026-07-20 the unloading valve on W-38 stuck open, so lift gas was injecting at a shallow point instead of the design depth. Rate fell by almost half. The well was on the 7-day baseline to 2026-07-19 at 1217 bbl/d oil, 1755.3 Mscf/d gas, 18.4% water cut and GOR 1442 scf/bbl.

## Immediate actions
- Lead Operator noted the drop in oil rate and the change in injection response and informed the Production Engineer.
- Injection rate was checked against the lift gas allocation and the wellhead and casing pressure trends were reviewed.
- The well was taken offline for the slickline change-out of the failed valve, following SOP-OPS-007 Gas Lift Troubleshooting.

## Findings
- Diagnosis: unloading valve stuck open with shallow injection.
- Production on 2026-07-20: 393.8 bbl oil, 588.2 Mscf gas, 17.9% water cut, with only 14 hours of uptime.
- Water cut was in line with the baseline, so there is no sign of a reservoir change. The loss is attributed to the valve.

## Impact
- Downtime: 20 hours as of the note date.
- Oil rate on 2026-07-20 was well below the 1217 bbl/d baseline, with the deferred volume driven by both the downtime and the reduced rate before shut-in.

## Status and next actions
The slickline change-out was completed and the incident is closed as of 2026-07-21. W-38 is producing today.
- Production Engineer to review the injection response over the next several days and confirm the rate returns toward baseline.
- Lead Operator to log any unstable injection pressure for follow-up under SOP-OPS-007.

**Reference procedure:** SOP-OPS-007 Gas Lift Troubleshooting
