## Scope of work
Preventive scale inhibitor squeeze on W-05. Shut in the well, bullhead inhibitor and overflush per the approved program, hold the shut-in soak period, then restart and confirm stable ESP operation.

## Safety and permits
- Hot work not required. Chemical handling permit and pressure test permit issued.
- Toolbox talk held before rig-up, covering chemical PPE, line pressure test and eyewash locations.
- HSE Advisor reviewed the chemical safety data sheet with the crew.

## Materials
- Scale inhibitor and overflush fluid per program
- Pumping unit supplied by the stimulation contractor
- Temporary lines and pressure gauges

## Production baseline
Before the job (14 days to 2025-06-18): oil 980.5 bbl/d, gas 891.7 Mscf/d, water cut 21.7 %, GOR 909 scf/bbl.

## Close-out remarks
- Work started 2025-06-19 and finished 2025-06-20. Total downtime was 42 hours.
- Lines pressure tested before pumping. Treatment pumped as planned with no deviations or safety incidents reported.
- Well restarted and the ESP ran stably.
- After restart (6 days to 2025-06-27): oil 981.8 bbl/d, gas 892.4 Mscf/d, water cut 22 %, GOR 909 scf/bbl. Rates are in line with the pre-job baseline.
- No related events within 30 days.
- Work order closed by the Production Engineer. Well W-05 is producing.
