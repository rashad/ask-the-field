# Work order: Wellhead maintenance, W-15

**WO number:** WO-005
**Well:** W-15 (Field A, vertical, gas lift)
**Event reference:** EV-0011
**Category:** Maintenance
**Priority:** Routine
**Raised:** 2025-06-25
**Planned start:** 2025-06-27
**Actual start:** 2025-06-27
**Completion date:** 2025-06-27
**Status:** Closed

## Scope of work
- Grease all wellhead valves (master, wing and swab) on W-15.
- Function test each valve through full open and close travel and record torque feel and any stiffness.
- Check the wellhead for leaks and visible corrosion after the work.

## Safety and permits
- Work permit and isolation certificate issued by the Production Supervisor before the well was shut in.
- Well shut in and wellhead pressure bled down to zero before greasing started.
- Gas detector check at the wellhead before work. Handover to the Lead Operator at the end of the job.
- No applicable procedure was referenced for this routine task.

## Materials
- Valve grease (approved grade), grease gun, cleaning rags, calibrated gauge for the pressure check.

## Execution notes and close-out remarks
- Well shut in for the work, with total downtime of 5 hours as of the close-out date.
- All valves greased and function tested. Valves moved freely and sealed correctly. No leaks seen at the flanges or stem packings.
- Well returned to production the same day with no issues on restart.
- A second wellhead maintenance record for the same job (EV-0012) is logged for W-15 on 2025-06-27 with 4 hours of downtime. The Maintenance Planner should reconcile the two entries so the work is counted once.
