## Scope of work
Routine ESP surface equipment inspection and VSD parameter check on W-09.
1. Inspect the VSD panel, cooling fans, filters and cable terminations.
2. Check the junction box and wellhead feed-through for signs of heat or moisture.
3. Record VSD parameters (frequency, motor current, voltage, drive temperature) and compare with the last saved set.
4. Restore the well to the previous settings and confirm stable running.

## Safety and permits
- General work permit and electrical isolation permit issued for the VSD work.
- Lock-out/tag-out applied at the VSD incomer and removed at hand-back.
- Arc-flash PPE required for panel opening.
- Toolbox talk held with the electrical technician and Lead Operator before starting.

## Materials
- No spares consumed.
- Cleaning supplies and replacement panel filter on standby (not required).

## Close-out remarks
- Inspection completed on 2025-06-14 with the well down for 3 hours in total.
- Surface equipment found in good condition. VSD parameters checked and left at the previous operating values.
- Well restarted and running stable after the work.
- Average production for the 7 days to 2025-06-13: 971.8 bbl/d oil, 935.2 Mscf/d gas, water cut 22.6%, GOR 962 scf/bbl.
- Average production for the 6 days after restart, to 2025-06-21: 952.5 bbl/d oil, 891.9 Mscf/d gas, water cut 22.8%, GOR 936 scf/bbl.
- Production Engineer to keep W-09 under normal monitoring.
- Separate item, not part of this work order: a low-severity H2S detector alarm near the W-09 wellhead is recorded as planned for 2025-06-18 (EV-0009) and will be handled under its own record.

**Work order closed.** Signed off by Lead Operator and Maintenance Planner.
