# Work order: Safety valve test, W-28

**WO number:** WO-002
**Well:** W-28 (Wadi Field, deviated, gas lift)
**Event reference:** EV-0003
**Category:** Maintenance
**Priority:** Routine
**Date raised:** 2025-06-02

**Planned finish:** 2025-06-04
**Actual start / finish:** 2025-06-04 / 2025-06-04
**Status:** Closed

## Scope of work
Subsurface safety valve function and leak test on W-28.
1. Shut in the well and isolate it from the flowline.
2. Bleed down tubing pressure above the valve to the test point.
3. Function test the valve, then run the leak test and record the hold period.
4. Re-open the valve, return the well to service and record restart parameters.

## Safety and permits
- Hot work: none. Work permit and isolation certificate issued for the well.
- Toolbox talk held with the Lead Operator and the slickline crew before starting.
- Pressure test lines checked and tagged before use.
- HSE Advisor informed of the shut-in. CPF-1 control room notified of the expected loss of production.

## Materials
- Calibrated test pump and gauges
- Valve test log sheets
- No spares consumed

## Close-out remarks
- Test completed on 2025-06-04 as planned. Valve operated correctly and held the leak test within the acceptance criteria.
- Total downtime was 6 hours.
- Well restarted and stabilised with no issues. No follow-up work required.
- No applicable procedure is referenced for this order.

**Production before (12 days to 2025-06-03):** oil 732.7 bbl/d, gas 575.5 Mscf/d, water cut 31.1 %, GOR 785 scf/bbl.
**Production after restart (6 days to 2025-06-11):** oil 709 bbl/d, gas 549.7 Mscf/d, water cut 32.3 %, GOR 775 scf/bbl.

Rates after restart are slightly lower, in line with the gradual rise in water cut on this field. Monitor routinely.
