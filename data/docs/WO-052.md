# Work order: Wellhead maintenance, W-21

**WO number:** WO-052
**Event reference:** EV-0151
**Well:** W-21 (Field B, horizontal, ESP)
**Category:** Maintenance
**Priority:** Routine
**Raised:** 2026-08-08
**Planned start:** 2026-08-10
**Actual start:** 2026-08-10
**Completion date:** 2026-08-10
**Status:** Closed

## Scope of work

- Grease all wellhead valves (master, wing and swab) at the designated grease points.
- Function test each valve through full travel and record any stiffness or leakage.
- Visually check the wellhead for leaks, corrosion and loose fittings.
- Return the well to production after the test.

## Safety and permits

- Work permit issued for the wellhead area.
- Toolbox talk held before the job with the Lead Operator and the maintenance crew.
- Well shut in and the wellhead depressurised before greasing, then returned to service in line with normal startup checks.
- No applicable procedure is referenced for this job.

## Materials

- Valve grease (approved grade) and grease gun
- Standard hand tools
- Leak detection fluid

## Execution notes

The well was shut in on 2026-08-10 for the work. All valves were greased and function tested. Valves moved through full travel with no leakage seen at stem packing or flanges. No defects were found and no follow-up work is required.

## Close-out remarks

- Downtime: 3 hours.
- Well restarted the same day and is producing.
- Seven-day average before the job (to 2026-08-09): oil 653.2 bbl/d, gas 495.4 Mscf/d, water cut 47.2%, GOR 758 scf/bbl.
- Six-day average after restart (to 2026-08-17): oil 639.3 bbl/d, gas 476.3 Mscf/d, water cut 48.1%, GOR 745 scf/bbl.
- The small change in rates and water cut is within normal variation and is not linked to the maintenance work.
- Work order closed.
