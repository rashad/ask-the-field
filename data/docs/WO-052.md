# Work order: Gas lift valve change-out, W-29

**WO number:** WO-052
**Event reference:** EV-0141
**Well:** W-29 (Wadi Field, vertical, gas lift)
**Category:** Maintenance
**Priority:** Routine
**Raised:** 2026-07-22
**Planned start:** 2026-07-24
**Planned finish:** 2026-07-24
**Actual start / finish:** 2026-07-24 / 2026-07-24
**Status:** Closed

## Scope of work
Slickline gas lift valve change-out on W-29. Pull the existing valve, run the replacement valve, and confirm gas lift injection after restart. Applicable procedure: SOP-OPS-007 Gas Lift Troubleshooting.

## Safety and permits
- Work permit and slickline lifting plan issued by the Production Supervisor.
- Pressure test of the lubricator and wellhead barrier check before opening the well.
- Gas injection isolated and bled down before rig-up.
- HSE Advisor attended the pre-job meeting.

## Materials
- Replacement gas lift valve with new seals
- Slickline unit and pressure control equipment
- Lubricator, tool string and running tools

## Execution notes and close-out remarks
- Well shut in and made safe on 2026-07-24. The valve change-out was completed the same day.
- Total downtime on the job: 13 hours.
- Gas injection was re-established and the well returned to production without issues. No leaks were found at the wellhead connections.
- Rates before the job (14 days to 2026-07-23): oil 760.2 bbl/d, gas 613.3 Mscf/d, water cut 39.7 %, GOR 807 scf/bbl.
- Rates after restart (6 days to 2026-07-31): oil 763.7 bbl/d, gas 609.4 Mscf/d, water cut 39.8 %, GOR 798 scf/bbl.
- Rates are essentially unchanged. The job restored the well to its earlier performance and no further follow-up is required.
- Lead Operator informed at handover. Work order closed.
