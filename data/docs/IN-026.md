# Incident note: Power outage on W-18

**Document ID:** IN-026
**Event ID:** EV-0081
**Note date:** 2026-02-11
**Author:** Production Engineer
**Well:** W-18 (Wadi Field, deviated, ESP)
**Severity:** medium
**Status at note date:** closed

## What happened
A field power dip on 2026-02-11 tripped the W-18 ESP. The well was restarted once power was restored.

## Immediate actions
- The Lead Operator confirmed the trip was caused by the supply dip and not by a downhole or wellhead fault.
- Power was confirmed stable before the restart.
- The ESP was restarted and the well was brought back to its prior operating point.
- Motor current, discharge pressure and wellhead pressure were checked after restart and looked normal.

## Findings so far
The trip was linked to the field power dip. No equipment damage was found. No other fault was identified on the well.

## Impact
- Downtime: 3 hours (uptime 21 hours on the day).
- Production on 2026-02-11: oil 575.1 bbl, gas 428.3 Mscf, water cut 35%.
- Prior 7-day average to 2026-02-10: oil 649.4 bbl/d, gas 490.3 Mscf/d, water cut 35.8%, GOR 755 scf/bbl.
- Oil on the day was about 74 bbl below the prior average, consistent with the lost run time.

## Status
Closed as of 2026-02-11. The well is producing and no restrictions remain. No related events were recorded on this well within the previous 30 days.

## Next actions
- The Production Supervisor to be informed of the power dip for follow-up with the site power team.
- Operators to keep watching W-18 trends on the next shifts to confirm stable performance.

## Procedure reference
No specific procedure applies to this event.
