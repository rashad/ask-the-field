# Incident note: Sand production alarm on W-14

**Note date:** 2026-01-16
**Event ID:** EV-0072
**Well:** W-14 (Field A, deviated, ESP)
**Severity:** medium
**Status at note date:** closed
**Applicable procedure:** SOP-OPS-002 Sand Management and Sand Production Alarms

## What happened
On 2026-01-16 the sand monitor on W-14 raised a high sand alarm. The alarm was confirmed against the monitor trend and the well was put on restricted flow. The choke was reduced until the readings returned to normal.

## Immediate actions
- Lead Operator acknowledged the alarm and checked the monitor trend and the instrument for faults.
- Choke reduced in steps, following SOP-OPS-002, and the readings were observed after each step.
- Production Supervisor informed.
- Sand readings were monitored at the wellhead and at CPF-1 inlet until stable.

## Findings so far
The readings normalised after the choke reduction, which points to a drawdown-related sand release and not an instrument fault. No equipment damage was reported.

## Impact
- Downtime as of the note date: 19 hours.
- Uptime on 2026-01-16: 5 hours.
- Production on the day: oil 224.7 bbl, gas 234.1 Mscf, water cut 17.9%.
- For comparison, the previous 7 days to 2026-01-15 averaged 1047 bbl/d oil, 1098.2 Mscf/d gas, water cut 18.6% and GOR 1049 scf/bbl.

The shortfall against that baseline is the deferred oil from this event.

## Status
Closed as of 2026-01-16. W-14 is producing.

## Next actions
- Production Engineer to review the sand trend and the choke setting after the return to service.
- Keep sand monitoring at the normal frequency in line with SOP-OPS-002.
- A separate event, a wellhead valve stem packing leak (EV-0076), is planned for 2026-01-23 on this well. It is not related to this alarm and will be handled under its own record.
