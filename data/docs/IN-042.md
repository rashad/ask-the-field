# Incident note: High separator level trip on W-23

**Document ID:** IN-042
**Note date:** 2026-05-01
**Event ID:** EV-0114
**Well:** W-23 (Wadi Field, horizontal, ESP)
**Severity:** High
**Status at note date:** Closed

## What happened
On 2026-05-01 the test separator high-level trip activated and shut in W-23. The well was down for 4 hours, and the day's uptime was 20 hours.

## Immediate actions
- The Lead Operator confirmed the trip on the CPF-1 control panel and kept the well shut in until the level reading was verified.
- The Field Engineer checked the level controller against the local gauge glass and found the controller reading out of agreement with it.
- The level controller was recalibrated and the reading checked against the gauge glass.
- The well was restarted once the level was stable and the ESP showed normal start-up behaviour.
- The Production Supervisor was informed of the shut-in and the restart.

## Findings so far
The trip was caused by a level controller that needed recalibration. No further faults were found on the test separator during the checks. No related events on W-23 were recorded in the previous 30 days.

## Impact
| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Downtime | 4 hours |
| Production on 2026-05-01 | 799.1 bbl oil, 515.2 Mscf gas |
| Water cut on the day | 32.7 % |
| Previous 7-day average (to 2026-04-30) | 907.1 bbl/d oil, 601.6 Mscf/d gas |
| Previous 7-day water cut | 33.9 % |
| Previous 7-day GOR | 663 scf/bbl |

The day's oil was about 108 bbl below the previous 7-day average, which is consistent with the 4 hours of downtime.

## Status
Closed. W-23 is producing and the level controller is reading correctly.

## Next actions
- The Lead Operator will watch the test separator level trend on the next few shifts.
- The Maintenance Planner will add the level controller to the next routine calibration round.

No specific procedure applies to this event.
