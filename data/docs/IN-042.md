# Incident note: High separator level trip on W-12

**Document ID:** IN-042
**Note date:** 2026-04-15
**Event ID:** EV-0107
**Well:** W-12 (Field A, vertical, natural flow)
**Category / severity:** Incident / medium
**Status at note date:** Closed
**Prepared by:** Production Engineer

## What happened
On 2026-04-15 the test separator high-level trip activated and shut in W-12. The well was down for 3 hours before being returned to service the same day.

## Immediate actions
- Operators confirmed the trip was genuine at the separator and not a spurious signal from the well.
- The well was kept shut in until the separator level was brought back within the normal operating band.
- The Lead Operator informed the Production Supervisor and the CPF-1 control room.
- The instrument technician checked the level controller.

## Findings
- The level controller was reading out of calibration, which allowed the level to rise to the high-level trip setpoint.
- The level controller was recalibrated and its response checked against the local gauge glass.
- No fluid carry-over to the gas line was observed after restart.

## Impact
- Downtime: 3 hours (uptime on the day 21 hours).
- Production on 2026-04-15: 669.6 bbl oil, 311.1 Mscf gas, water cut 18.9 percent.
- Previous 7-day average to 2026-04-14: 742.2 bbl/d oil, 357.7 Mscf/d gas, water cut 19.2 percent, GOR 482 scf/bbl.
- Estimated deferred oil is modest, roughly the difference between the prior average and the day's volume, about 73 bbl.

## Status
Closed as of 2026-04-15. The well was restored and the controller is operating normally.

## Next actions
- Operators to compare the level controller reading against the gauge glass at each shift check for the next week.
- A choke replacement on W-12 (EV-0107 is unrelated) is planned from 2026-04-20 (EV-0111, eroded choke bean). This is only planned and has not started.

## Procedure reference
No specific procedure applies to this event.
