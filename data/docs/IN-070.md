# Incident note: ESP high vibration on W-25

**Document ID:** IN-070
**Note date:** 2026-11-20
**Event ID:** EV-0184
**Well:** W-25 (Field B, deviated, ESP)
**Severity:** medium
**Status at note date:** open
**Prepared by:** Production Engineer

## What happened
On 2026-11-20 the downhole sensor on W-25 showed ESP vibration rising above the alarm level. The well stayed on line, but the unit was brought down for a short period and the frequency was then reduced from 55 to 50 Hz.

## Immediate actions
- The Lead Operator confirmed the alarm on the controller and the surface readings.
- Drive frequency reduced from 55 to 50 Hz and the response was watched.
- The Production Supervisor was informed.
- Vibration, motor current and intake pressure trends are now logged at a higher frequency.

## Findings so far
The vibration trend is still under review. No root cause is confirmed. The cause is not yet known, and the trend after the frequency reduction needs more hours of data before conclusions are drawn.

## Impact
- Downtime as of this note: 2 hours.
- Uptime on the day: 22 hours.
- Production on 2026-11-20: 327.5 bbl oil, 268.4 Mscf gas, water cut 41.5%.
- Baseline for the 7 days to 2026-11-19: 407.7 bbl/d oil, 333 Mscf/d gas, water cut 39%, GOR 817 scf/bbl.
- Oil on the day was about 80 bbl below the baseline rate, partly from the 2 hours of downtime and partly from the lower frequency.

## Status
Open. The well is producing at 50 Hz under close watch. No related events were recorded on this well in the last 30 days.

## Next actions
1. Review the vibration trend over the next shifts at 50 Hz.
2. Compare the vibration signature with earlier data from this pump.
3. Hold the frequency at 50 Hz until the Production Engineer approves a change.
4. Escalate to the ESP vendor if vibration rises again or stays above the alarm level.
5. Update this note when the trend is understood.

## Procedure reference
SOP-OPS-001 ESP Trip Response and Restart applies. Follow it for any trip, and do not restart after a vibration trip until the engineer has reviewed the data.
