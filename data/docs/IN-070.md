# Incident note: ESP high vibration on W-25

**Document ID:** IN-070
**Note date:** 2026-11-20
**Event ID:** EV-0184
**Well:** W-25 (Wadi Field, deviated, ESP)
**Severity:** Medium
**Status at note date:** Open

## What happened
On 2026-11-20 the downhole sensor on W-25 showed ESP vibration rising above the alarm level. The installed pump is a Varenta VL-538, serial 3K-1102-058 (batch 3K-1102), with a run life of 22 months.

## Immediate actions
- Drive frequency reduced from 55 to 50 Hz to lower mechanical loading.
- The well was kept on production at the reduced frequency, and the Lead Operator was asked to log vibration, motor current and intake pressure at regular intervals.
- Production Supervisor informed.

## Findings so far
- The vibration trend is under review by the Production Engineer. No root cause has been identified yet.
- No related events on this well in the previous 30 days.

## Impact
- Downtime as of this note: 2 hours.
- Uptime on 2026-11-20 was 22 hours. Production for the day was 359.1 bbl of oil and 203.6 Mscf of gas, with water cut at 35.4%.
- The 7-day average to 2026-11-19 was 409.3 bbl/d oil, 253.2 Mscf/d gas, water cut 33.9% and GOR 619 scf/bbl. Oil for the day is therefore below the recent average, reflecting the lost hours and the lower frequency.

## Status
The incident remains open. W-25 is producing at 50 Hz under close monitoring.

## Next actions
1. Continue trending vibration and electrical parameters, and review at the next daily production meeting.
2. Production Engineer to decide whether the frequency should be held, reduced further or restored.
3. If vibration keeps rising or the unit trips, follow SOP-OPS-001 ESP Trip Response and Restart, and do not attempt repeated restarts outside its limits.
4. Raise a workover request if the trend indicates pump deterioration.

**Applicable procedure:** SOP-OPS-001 ESP Trip Response and Restart
