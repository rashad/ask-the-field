## Scope of work
Pig the W-25 flowline to CPF-1 to remove wax and sand deposits. Launch the pig at the wellhead launcher, monitor differential pressure during the run, and receive the pig at the CPF-1 receiver. Record the debris recovered.

## Safety and permits
- Hot work not required. Cold work permit and line-breaking permit issued.
- Toolbox talk held with the operations crew before launch.
- Receiver depressurised and drained before opening. Gas test completed prior to door opening.
- HSE Advisor reviewed the permit. No applicable standalone procedure was referenced for this job.

## Materials
- Foam-body cleaning pig, one unit
- Spare seals for launcher and receiver closures
- Waste drum for wax and sand recovery

## Execution notes
The well was shut in for the pigging run and restarted after the pig was received. Total downtime was 6 hours. The pig arrived at the receiver intact, with wax and sand debris recovered and drummed for disposal. The launcher and receiver closures were boxed up and leak-tested with no leaks found.

## Close-out remarks
