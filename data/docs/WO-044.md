## Scope of work
Preventive scale inhibitor squeeze on W-21 to protect the near-wellbore area and ESP intake from scale as water cut rises under the Wadi Field water flood. Shut in the well, pump preflush, inhibitor and overflush per the approved program, hold the shut-in soak, then restart.

## Safety and permits
- Hot work excluded; cold work permit and pumping permit issued.
- Chemical handling toolbox talk held with the pumping crew, HSE Advisor present.
- Pressure test of surface lines before pumping, in line with SOP-OPS-008.
- ESP isolated and locked out before the wellhead was connected.

## Materials
- Scale inhibitor and displacement fluids as per program
- Pumping unit and temporary lines
- Sample bottles for residual inhibitor monitoring

## Execution and close-out remarks
- Work started 2025-12-19 and finished 2025-12-20.
- Total downtime was 44 hours, including the soak period.
- Treatment was pumped as planned with no incidents or leaks reported.
- The well was restarted and handed back to the Lead Operator, with the ESP running normally.
