# Work order: Flowline pigging, W-19

**WO number:** WO-039
**Event reference:** EV-0061
**Well:** W-19 (Field B, horizontal, ESP)
**Category:** Maintenance
**Priority:** Routine
**Raised:** 2025-12-17
**Planned start:** 2025-12-19
**Actual start / completion:** 2025-12-19 / 2025-12-19
**Status:** Closed

## Scope of work
Pig the W-19 flowline to remove wax and sand deposits. Well shut in for the launch and receipt, restarted after the pig was received at CPF-1.

## Safety and permits
- Hot work not required. Cold work permit and line-break permit issued.
- Pressure bleed-down confirmed before opening the launcher.
- Barriers and exclusion zone set at the receiver. HSE Advisor briefed the crew at the toolbox talk.

## Materials
- Foam-body cleaning pig, one spare
- Launcher and receiver seals
- Waste containers for the debris

## Execution notes
- The Lead Operator shut in W-19 and isolated the flowline.
- The pig was launched and received at CPF-1 without sticking.
- Wax and sand debris was collected at the receiver and disposed of per site waste practice.
- Well restarted the same day. Total downtime 5 hours.
