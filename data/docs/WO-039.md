## Scope of work
Subsurface safety valve function and leak test on W-20. Close the valve, confirm closure, run the leak test against the acceptance limit, reopen and confirm the valve is fully open before returning the well to service.

## Safety and permits
- Work permit and isolation certificate issued by the Production Supervisor before start.
- Wellhead area barricaded, toolbox talk held with the slickline crew and operations.
- Control room informed of the planned shut-in and restart.
- No hydrocarbon release expected. Gas detection checked before work.

## Materials and equipment
- Hydraulic control line pump and calibrated gauges
- Slickline unit on standby
- Standard seals and fittings (none consumed)

## Execution notes and close-out remarks
- The well was shut in for the test on 2025-11-20. Total downtime was 5 hours.
- Valve function test passed. The leak test was within the acceptance limit.
- The valve was reopened and confirmed fully open, then the well was restarted and returned to normal operation. W-20 is producing.
- No defects found and no follow-up work required.
