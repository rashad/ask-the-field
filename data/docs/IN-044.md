# Incident note: Flowline leak on W-41

**Document ID:** IN-044
**Note date:** 2026-05-13
**Event ID:** EV-0117
**Well:** W-41 (Salt Flat Field, vertical, natural flow)
**Severity:** Medium
**Status at note date:** Closed
**Applicable procedure:** SOP-HSE-006 Flowline Leak Response

## What happened
On 2026-05-12 a small leak was found on a flowline flange on W-41. The well was isolated and the flange was repaired. The event ran from 2026-05-12 to 2026-05-13.

## Immediate actions
- Well shut in and the flowline section isolated and depressured.
- Area barricaded and the Lead Operator and HSE Advisor informed.
- Gas readings checked around the flange before work started.
- Spilled fluid cleaned up and the area inspected.

## Findings
The leak came from the flange joint on the flowline. It was small and contained, with no injuries and no reported release beyond the immediate pad area. The repair was completed and the line was checked for tightness before the well was returned to service.

## Impact
- Downtime: 31 hours as of this note.
- On 2026-05-12 the well made 560.8 bbl of oil and 805.7 Mscf of gas at 21.4% water cut, with 8.5 hours of uptime.
- Over the 7 days to 2026-05-11 the well averaged 1542.3 bbl/d oil, 2337.9 Mscf/d gas, 18.6% water cut and a GOR of 1516 scf/bbl.
- Deferred oil is roughly the difference between that baseline and the 560.8 bbl produced on 2026-05-12, plus the downtime into 2026-05-13.

## Related event
EV-0116, a wellhead valve stem packing leak on W-41 (2026-05-09, high severity, 9 hours downtime, packing replaced), is closed. It was a separate component from the flowline flange involved here.

## Status and next actions
The incident is closed and W-41 is producing. Next actions:
1. Production Engineer to confirm the rate returns to the pre-event baseline over the next few days.
2. Lead Operator to include the repaired flange in routine leak checks on rounds.
3. Complete reporting and close-out under SOP-HSE-006 Flowline Leak Response.
