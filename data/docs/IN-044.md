# Incident note: Sand production alarm on W-37

**Document ID:** IN-044
**Event ID:** EV-0113
**Note date:** 2026-05-01
**Well:** W-37 (Bayda, vertical, natural flow)
**Severity:** Low
**Status at note date:** Closed
**Applicable procedure:** SOP-OPS-002 Sand Management and Sand Production Alarms

## What happened
On 2026-05-01 the sand monitor on W-37 raised a sand production alarm. The Lead Operator on shift acknowledged it and checked the instrument reading against the local indication before acting.

## Immediate actions
- Choke reduced on W-37 until sand readings returned to normal, as per SOP-OPS-002.
- Production Engineer informed of the alarm and the choke change.
- Wellhead and flowline walked down. No leak or visible damage found.

## Findings
- Readings normalised after the choke reduction and stayed stable.
- Water cut on the day was 27.7%, close to the 27.2% seven-day average to 2026-04-30. No sign of a change in water behaviour.
- No sand-related damage to the choke or downstream equipment was identified during the walkdown.

## Impact
- Downtime: 17 hours. The well was online for 7 hours on 2026-05-01.
- Production on the day: 388.6 bbl oil and 586.8 Mscf gas.
- Baseline for the previous 7 days to 2026-04-30: 1293.9 bbl/d oil, 1825.3 Mscf/d gas, GOR 1411 scf/bbl.
- Deferred oil is the difference between the baseline and the day's output, roughly 905 bbl.

## Status
Closed on 2026-05-01. W-37 is producing today.

## Next actions
- Production Engineer to keep watching sand readings on W-37 through routine surveillance.
- Any repeat alarm to be handled under SOP-OPS-002.
- A preventive scale inhibitor squeeze (EV-0120) is planned for 2026-05-30. It is a planned job only and has not started.
