# Work order: Workover - ESP replacement, W-09

**WO number:** WO-035
**Well:** W-09 (Dune Field, vertical, ESP)
**Related event:** EV-0046 (maintenance); follows EV-0042 (ESP failure)
**Priority:** High
**Raised:** 2025-10-25
**Planned start:** 2025-10-27
**Completion date:** 2025-11-02
**Status:** Closed

## Scope of work
- Pull the failed ESP and tubing string.
- Carry out a teardown inspection of the pump, seal section, motor and cable.
- Install a new Varenta VL-538 ESP and return the well to production.

Background: on 2025-10-21 the ESP shut down on a ground fault alarm and would not restart. A megger test showed near-zero insulation resistance phase to ground. The well was shut in pending workover (144 h downtime to 2025-10-26).

## Safety and permits
- Permit to Work issued under SOP-HSE-005 Permit to Work for Workover and Rig Operations.
- Well killed and barriers verified before the BOP was nippled up.
- Electrical isolation and lock-out of the ESP power feed confirmed by the Field Engineer.

## Materials
- Varenta VL-538 ESP, serial 4D-0733-115 (batch 4D-0733).
- New cable splice kit and clamps.

## Execution notes
- Removed pump serial 7Q-0419-027 (batch 7Q-0419).
- Teardown found motor winding insulation breakdown. No sand, scale or cable damage was seen.
- New pump installed and tested electrically before running in.

## Close-out remarks
- Work ran from 2025-10-27 to 2025-11-02, total downtime 168 h.
- The well returned to production on 2025-11-03 and is producing.
- Rates before the failure (8 days to 2025-10-26): oil 1472.4 bbl/d, gas 965 Mscf/d, water cut 10.2 %, GOR 655 scf/bbl.
- Rates after restart (6 days to 2025-11-09): oil 1417.5 bbl/d, gas 932.5 Mscf/d, water cut 11 %, GOR 658 scf/bbl.
- Continue routine ESP monitoring. Work order closed.
