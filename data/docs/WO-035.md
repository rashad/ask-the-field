# Work order: Wellhead maintenance, W-30

**WO number:** WO-035
**Event reference:** EV-0052
**Well:** W-30 (Field B, deviated, gas lift)
**Category:** Maintenance, wellhead
**Priority:** Routine
**Raised:** 2025-11-12
**Planned start:** 2025-11-14
**Actual start / completion:** 2025-11-14 / 2025-11-14
**Status:** Closed

## Scope of work
- Grease all wellhead valves on W-30.
- Function test each valve, checking full travel and smooth operation.
- Record any valve with excess torque, leakage or stem packing weep for follow-up.

## Safety and permits
- Cold work permit issued for the wellhead area.
- Well shut in and isolated for the duration of the job, then returned to service after the tests.
- Toolbox talk held before starting. Standard PPE, gas detector carried by the crew.
- No hot work and no line breaking in scope.

## Materials
- Valve grease (approved wellhead grade), grease gun, torque wrench, rags and spill tray.

## Execution notes and close-out
- The well was shut in for the work, and the downtime recorded was 3 hours.
- All valves were greased and function tested. No leaks were seen at the wellhead and no further repair was identified.
- Area cleaned and the permit closed out. The well was restarted the same day.
- Production check, 7 days to 2025-11-13 (before): oil 1563.3 bbl/d, gas 1517.1 Mscf/d, water cut 28.4%, GOR 970 scf/bbl.
- Production check, 6 days to 2025-11-21 (after restart): oil 1552.8 bbl/d, gas 1544.1 Mscf/d, water cut 28.4%, GOR 994 scf/bbl.
- Rates are in line with the pre-job baseline and water cut is unchanged. No follow-up action is required.

**Work order closed.** Next greasing to be scheduled by the Maintenance Planner under the routine wellhead program.
