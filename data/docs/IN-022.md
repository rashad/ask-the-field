# Incident note: Sand production alarm on W-24

**Document ID:** IN-022
**Note date:** 2026-01-11
**Event ID:** EV-0073
**Well:** W-24 (Wadi Field, deviated, ESP)
**Severity:** Medium
**Status at note date:** Closed
**Prepared by:** Production Engineer

## What happened
On 2026-01-11 the sand monitor on W-24 raised a sand production alarm. The Lead Operator confirmed the alarm at the wellhead and reduced the choke until the readings returned to normal.

## Immediate actions
- Choke reduced in steps and sand monitor readings watched after each change.
- Wellhead and flowline checked for leaks and abnormal noise, none found.
- Production Supervisor informed.
- Production resumed once the readings were stable and normal.

## Impact
- Downtime: 14 hours as of this note.
- Uptime on the day: 10 hours.
- Production on 2026-01-11: oil 529.4 bbl, gas 295.6 Mscf, water cut 25.6%.
- Baseline for the 7 days to 2026-01-10: oil 1300.8 bbl/d, gas 710 Mscf/d, water cut 25.5%, GOR 546 scf/bbl.
- Water cut is unchanged against the baseline. The lower oil volume reflects the downtime and the restricted choke.

## Findings so far
The alarm cleared after the choke reduction. No equipment damage was found during the checks. A previous sand alarm on this well was logged on 2025-12-15 (EV-0064, low severity, 13 hours downtime), handled the same way.

## Status
The incident is closed. The well is producing.

## Next actions
- Production Engineer to review sand monitor trends on W-24 over the coming days.
- Operators to keep the choke within the agreed limits and log any further alarm.
- Follow the actions in the applicable procedure, SOP-OPS-002 Sand Management and Sand Production Alarms.
