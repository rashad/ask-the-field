# Work order: Choke replacement, W-29

**WO number:** WO-031
**Well:** W-29 (Wadi Field, vertical, gas lift)
**Event reference:** EV-0039
**Category:** Maintenance
**Priority:** Routine
**Raised:** 2025-10-05
**Planned start:** 2025-10-07
**Planned finish:** 2025-10-07
**Actual start / finish:** 2025-10-07 / 2025-10-07
**Status:** Closed

## Scope of work
Replace the eroded choke bean on the W-29 production wing valve choke body. Fit a new bean of the same size, then check the seat and return the well to production.

## Safety and permits
- Work permit and isolation certificate issued before the job.
- Well shut in and flowline section isolated, pressure bled down and verified at zero before the choke body was opened.
- Standard PPE, gas detector carried by the crew, toolbox talk held at the wellhead.

## Materials
- 1 choke bean (replacement)
- 1 set of body seals and gasket
- Standard hand tools and torque wrench

## Execution notes and close-out remarks
- The removed bean showed clear erosion. The seat was inspected and found serviceable.
- The new bean and seals were fitted and the body torqued to specification. A leak check at line pressure showed no seepage.
- The well was shut in for a total of 3 hours and restarted the same day.
- Production before the job (14 days to 2025-10-06): 1069 bbl/d oil, 853 Mscf/d gas, water cut 30.1 %, GOR 798 scf/bbl.
- Production after restart (6 days to 2025-10-14): 1053.5 bbl/d oil, 863.2 Mscf/d gas, water cut 30.7 %, GOR 819 scf/bbl.
- Rates are stable and in line with the pre-job baseline. No further action is needed on the choke.

**Close-out:** Work complete, permit closed, area left clean. Lead Operator informed and the choke change recorded in the well file. The work order is closed.
