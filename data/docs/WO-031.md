# Work order: ESP inspection, W-22

**WO number:** WO-031
**Event reference:** EV-0047
**Well:** W-22 (Field B, horizontal, ESP)
**Category:** Maintenance
**Priority:** Routine
**Raised:** 2025-10-20
**Planned start:** 2025-10-22
**Actual start / completion:** 2025-10-22 / 2025-10-22
**Status:** Closed

## Scope of work
- Routine inspection of ESP surface equipment: VSD, transformer, junction box and wellhead power cable entry.
- VSD parameter check against the commissioned settings (frequency, current limits, under/overload trips, restart timer).
- Visual check of cooling fans, enclosure seals and cable terminations.

## Safety and permits
- Work permit and electrical isolation (lock-out/tag-out) in place before opening panels.
- Well shut in from surface for the duration of the work.
- Toolbox talk held with the electrician and the Lead Operator before start.

## Materials
- No spares consumed. Standard electrical tools, thermal camera and contact cleaner.

## Execution notes and close-out
- Well stopped and isolated on 2025-10-22, inspection completed the same day, then restarted.
- Total downtime: 6 hours.
- Surface equipment found in good condition. VSD parameters checked and left as per the commissioned settings. No defects raised.
- Production comparison (7 days before vs 6 days after restart):
  - Before (to 2025-10-21): oil 816.1 bbl/d, gas 786.9 Mscf/d, water cut 30.6%, GOR 964 scf/bbl.
  - After (to 2025-10-29): oil 803.1 bbl/d, gas 761.4 Mscf/d, water cut 31.1%, GOR 948 scf/bbl.
  - Changes are small and within normal variation. No impact attributed to the inspection.
- Area cleaned, permit closed out and the well handed back to Operations.

## Follow-up
- Planned, not yet executed: scale inhibitor squeeze treatment (EV-0050) from 2025-11-11 and wellhead valve greasing and function test (EV-0053) from 2025-11-16.

**Close-out:** Work order closed.
