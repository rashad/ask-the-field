# Incident note: H2S alarm on W-27

**Document ID:** IN-059
**Event ID:** EV-0158
**Note date:** 2026-08-27
**Well:** W-27 (Field B, vertical, ESP)
**Severity:** High
**Status at note date:** Closed
**Prepared by:** HSE Advisor

## What happened
On 2026-08-27 the fixed H2S detector near the W-27 wellhead went into alarm. The alarm was treated as a real release until proven otherwise.

## Immediate actions
- The area around the wellhead was cleared and access was barred.
- The well was shut in and the Lead Operator and Production Supervisor were informed.
- Personnel were accounted for at the muster point. No exposure or injury was reported.
- Re-entry was made only by a trained team using portable gas detectors and breathing apparatus, following SOP-HSE-003 H2S Alarm Response.

## Findings
The team found a leak on a needle valve at the wellhead. The valve was repaired. After the repair, portable detector readings in the area returned to zero and the fixed detector cleared. The well was then restarted.

## Impact
- Downtime: 6 hours (uptime on the day was 18 hours).
- Production on 2026-08-27: oil 280.9 bbl, gas 271.1 Mscf, water cut 36%.
- The prior 7-day average to 2026-08-26 was oil 396.4 bbl/d, gas 383 Mscf/d, water cut 35%, GOR 966 scf/bbl.
- The oil shortfall against the prior average is about 115.5 bbl for the day, mainly due to the shutdown. The water cut change of one point is within normal variation.

## Status
Closed. W-27 is producing. No related events were recorded on this well in the previous 30 days.

## Next actions
- Production Engineer to confirm the well returns to its prior rate over the next few days.
- Maintenance Planner to add the needle valve and neighbouring wellhead fittings to the next leak-check round.
- HSE Advisor to review the alarm response at the next safety meeting and confirm the detector calibration is current.

**Applicable procedure:** SOP-HSE-003 H2S Alarm Response
