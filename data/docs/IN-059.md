# Incident note: Wellhead leak on W-22

**Document ID:** IN-059
**Event ID:** EV-0160
**Note date:** 2026-08-31
**Author:** Production Engineer
**Well:** W-22 (Wadi Field, deviated, ESP)
**Severity:** Low
**Status:** Closed

## What happened
On 2026-08-31 a leak was found on the wellhead valve stem packing of W-22. The well was shut in to make the repair. The ESP surface equipment inspection and VSD parameter check had been done the previous day (EV-0159, 4 hours downtime, 2026-08-30) and was not linked to the leak.

## Immediate actions
- The well was shut in and the wellhead area isolated and made safe.
- Leaked fluid was cleaned up and the area was checked for residual hydrocarbon.
- The Lead Operator and Production Supervisor were informed.
- The valve stem packing was replaced and the valve was leak tested at operating pressure before restart.

## Findings
The leak came from worn packing on the valve stem. No other wellhead components showed leakage after the repair. No injuries and no environmental release beyond the immediate wellhead pad area.

## Impact
- Downtime: 12 hours (uptime on the day: 12 hours).
- Production on 2026-08-31: 210.8 bbl oil, 187.3 Mscf gas, water cut 37%.
- Baseline for the 7 days to 2026-08-30: 388.4 bbl/d oil, 355.9 Mscf/d gas, water cut 39.2%, GOR 916 scf/bbl.
- Deferred oil for the day is about 177.6 bbl against the baseline rate.

## Status at note date
Closed. The well is back on production and stable. No leak was seen at the final check.

## Next actions
- Operators to include the W-22 wellhead valves in the next routine round and confirm no weeping at the stem.
- Maintenance Planner to review the packing condition on other wellhead valves at the next scheduled inspection.

## Procedure reference
No specific procedure applies to this event.
