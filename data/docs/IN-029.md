# Incident note: Wellhead leak on W-14

**Document ID:** IN-029
**Event ID:** EV-0076
**Note date:** 2026-01-23
**Well:** W-14 (Field A, deviated, ESP)
**Severity:** Low
**Status:** Closed

## What happened
On 2026-01-23 a leak was found on the wellhead valve stem packing of W-14. The leak was seen during the routine wellhead round and was confined to the valve body. No fluid reached the cellar or the surrounding ground.

## Immediate actions
- The well was shut in and the wellhead isolated.
- The area was barricaded and checked for gas before work began.
- The Production Supervisor and HSE Advisor were informed.
- The packing was replaced and the valve stem was function tested.
- A leak check at operating pressure showed no seepage, and the well was returned to production.

## Findings
The cause was a worn stem packing. No damage to the valve body or stem was found. No other wellhead valves showed signs of leakage.

## Impact
- Downtime: 12 hours (uptime on the day was 12 hours).
- Production on 2026-01-23: 554.8 bbl of oil, 574 Mscf of gas, water cut 17.8%.
- The previous 7-day average to 2026-01-22 was 965.2 bbl/d oil, 991.4 Mscf/d gas, water cut 17.3%, GOR 1027 scf/bbl.
- The shortfall against the baseline is about 410 bbl of deferred oil, caused by the shut-in period only. No lasting change in water cut or GOR is indicated.

## Related events
EV-0072 (2026-01-16): sand monitor alarm on W-14, choke reduced until readings normalised, 19 hours downtime, closed. The two events are unrelated in cause: one was a sand alarm, the other a packing wear failure.

## Status
Closed as of 2026-01-23. W-14 is producing.

## Next actions
- The Lead Operator will include W-14 wellhead valves in the next weekly leak inspection.
- The Maintenance Planner will review the packing condition on similar valves at Field A during the next planned round.

## Procedure reference
No specific procedure applies to this event.
