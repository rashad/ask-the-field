# Incident note: H2S alarm on W-24

**Document ID:** IN-029
**Event ID:** EV-0086
**Note date:** 2026-02-17
**Author:** HSE Advisor
**Well:** W-24 (Wadi Field, deviated, ESP)
**Severity:** Medium
**Status:** Closed

## What happened
On 2026-02-17 the fixed H2S detector near the W-24 wellhead went into alarm. The alarm was treated as genuine until proven otherwise.

## Immediate actions
- The wellhead area was cleared and access barriered off.
- The well was shut in and the Lead Operator and Production Supervisor were informed.
- Personnel were mustered and accounted for. No injuries and no exposures were reported.
- Re-entry was made by a trained team in breathing apparatus using portable gas detectors.

## Findings
A leak was found on a needle valve at the wellhead instrument connection. The valve was repaired and leak-tested. Portable readings around the wellhead returned to normal after the repair, and the fixed detector cleared.

## Impact
- Downtime: 6 hours on 2026-02-17 (uptime 18 hours).
- Production on the day: 1,021.3 bbl oil, 535 Mscf gas, water cut 24.8%.
- Before the event (7 days to 2026-02-16): 1,294.1 bbl/d oil, 691.4 Mscf/d gas, water cut 25.8%, GOR 534 scf/bbl.
- Oil lost against the previous 7-day average was about 273 bbl, consistent with the 6 hours of downtime.
- No related events on this well in the previous 30 days.

## Status at note date
Closed. The well is producing and the area has been returned to normal access.

## Next actions
- The Field Engineer will check the other instrument connections on the W-24 wellhead at the next routine round.
- The Lead Operator will record the repair in the shift log.

## Procedure reference
SOP-HSE-003 H2S Alarm Response was followed for the response, clearance and re-entry.
