# Work order: Gas lift valve change-out, W-31

**WO number:** WO-008
**Event reference:** EV-0014
**Well:** W-31 (Bayda, deviated, gas lift)
**Category:** Maintenance
**Priority:** Routine
**Raised:** 2025-06-28
**Planned start:** 2025-06-30
**Planned finish:** 2025-06-30
**Actual start / finish:** 2025-06-30 / 2025-06-30
**Status:** Closed

## Scope of work
- Slickline gas lift valve change-out on W-31.
- Pull the existing valve, run the replacement valve and confirm seating.
- Restart the well and confirm stable lift gas injection.
- Applicable procedure: SOP-OPS-007 Gas Lift Troubleshooting.

## Safety and permits
- Hot work not required. Work permit and slickline lifting permit issued by the Production Supervisor.
- Pressure test of the lubricator before opening the wellhead.
- Toolbox talk held with the slickline crew and the Lead Operator before rigging up.
- Well shut in and isolated for the duration of the job.

## Materials
- One gas lift valve with latch and spare seals, from the Bayda warehouse.
- Slickline unit with standard toolstring.

## Pre-job baseline
Seven-day average to 2025-06-29: oil 1284.5 bbl/d, gas 2162.1 Mscf/d, water cut 19.5%, GOR 1683 scf/bbl.

## Close-out remarks
- Job completed on 2025-06-30 with no safety incidents or leaks reported.
- Total well downtime was 19 hours.
- Well was restarted and brought back to stable lift after the valve change.
- Six-day average to 2025-07-07: oil 1296.7 bbl/d, gas 2313.3 Mscf/d, water cut 19.8%, GOR 1784 scf/bbl.
- Oil rate is in line with the pre-job baseline. Gas rate and GOR are higher, consistent with the expected lift gas response after the valve change. Water cut is essentially unchanged.
- No related events in the following 30 days. Work order closed.

**Closed by:** Production Engineer, with Lead Operator (K.M.) confirming restart.
