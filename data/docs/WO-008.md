## Scope of work
Pig the W-27 flowline to CPF-1 to remove wax and sand deposits. Launch, track and receive the pig, record the debris volume at the receiver, and restart the well once the line is confirmed clear.

## Safety and permits
- Hot work not required. Pressure-related work covered by a cold work permit and a pig launcher/receiver isolation checklist.
- Pre-job toolbox talk held with the operations crew. Pressure bled to zero before opening the launcher and receiver doors.
- Spill containment placed at the receiver, and the HSE Advisor was informed of the pigging window.

## Materials
- Foam-body pig (standard flowline size)
- Spare seals for launcher and receiver doors
- Waste drums for wax and sand returns

## Execution notes and close-out
The well was shut in for the job and the pigging was completed on 2025-06-29. Total downtime was 9 hours. The pig arrived at the receiver without sticking, and wax and sand were recovered and bagged for disposal. Doors were boxed up, leak-tested and the well was returned to production.

Production comparison:
- Before (14 days to 2025-06-28): oil 847.5 bbl/d, gas 916.1 Mscf/d, water cut 28.4%, GOR 1081 scf/bbl.
- After restart (6 days to 2025-07-06): oil 857.8 bbl/d, gas 906.5 Mscf/d, water cut 28.7%, GOR 1057 scf/bbl.

Oil rate is slightly higher after the job, with water cut essentially unchanged.

## Follow-up
Another flowline pigging run on W-27 (EV-0013) is planned for 2025-07-13. Work order WO-008 is closed.

Lead Operator, Wadi Field
