# Work order: ESP inspection, W-17

**WO number:** WO-056

**Priority:** Routine

## Scope of work
1. Routine inspection of ESP surface equipment at W-17 (wellhead power cable entry, junction box, transformer, VSD cabinet).
2. VSD parameter check: compare running frequency, motor current, and protection settings against the commissioning record.
3. Visual check of cabinet filters, cooling fans and cable terminations.
4. Restart the well and confirm stable operation.

## Safety and permits
- Electrical isolation and lock-out/tag-out applied before cabinet work.
- Work permit and isolation certificate issued by the Production Supervisor.
- Standard PPE plus arc-rated clothing for VSD work.
- No applicable procedure reference for this task; the job followed the standard electrical isolation practice.

## Materials
- Replacement cabinet filter (on hand, not required).
- Contact cleaner and torque tools.

## Execution notes and close-out
- The well was shut down for the inspection and isolated. Total downtime was 5 hours.
- Surface equipment found in good condition. Terminations tight, no signs of overheating, filters acceptable.
- VSD parameters matched the commissioning record. No setpoints changed.
- Well restarted on 2026-10-04 and ran stably. No alarms or trips recorded after restart.
