# Work order: Choke replacement, W-17

**WO number:** WO-056
**Event reference:** EV-0169
**Well:** W-17 (Wadi Field, vertical, ESP)
**Category:** Maintenance
**Priority:** Medium
**Raised:** 2026-10-01
**Planned start:** 2026-10-03
**Actual start / completion:** 2026-10-03 / 2026-10-03
**Status:** Closed

## Scope of work
Replace the eroded choke bean on the W-17 wellhead flowline choke. Isolate the choke, remove the old bean, inspect the choke body and seat, install a new bean of the same size, pressure test and return the well to production.

## Safety and permits
- Hot work not required. Cold work permit and isolation certificate issued by the Lead Operator.
- Line depressurised and bled down before breaking containment, with gas check at the choke body.
- Toolbox talk held with the maintenance crew. HSE Advisor informed of the job scope.
- Standard PPE, with a barrier around the wellhead area.

## Materials
- 1 x replacement choke bean, same trim as removed.
- 1 x set of gaskets and seals for the choke body.
- Leak detection fluid.

## Execution notes
- The old bean showed clear erosion on the bore, consistent with the reported loss of choke control.
- The choke body and seat were inspected and found serviceable.
- New bean fitted, seals replaced, and a leak test on the joint was passed before opening the well.
- Total downtime on the well was 4 hours.

## Close-out remarks
Work completed on 2026-10-03 and the well was returned to production the same day. Rates before the job (14 days to 2026-10-02) averaged 1039.1 bbl/d oil, 820.7 Mscf/d gas, 39.7% water cut and GOR 790 scf/bbl. After restart (6 days to 2026-10-10) the well averaged 1013.9 bbl/d oil, 816.8 Mscf/d gas, 40.4% water cut and GOR 806 scf/bbl. The Production Engineer will keep watching the trend as part of normal Wadi Field surveillance. No further work is required under this order.
