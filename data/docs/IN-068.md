# Incident note: Wellhead pressure anomaly on W-05

**Document ID:** IN-068
**Event ID:** EV-0181
**Note date:** 2026-11-15
**Author:** Production Engineer
**Well:** W-05 (Dune Field, vertical, ESP)
**Severity:** Medium
**Status at note date:** Open

## What happened
On 2026-11-15 the flowing wellhead pressure on W-05 began swinging by plus or minus 60 psi with no choke change made by operations. The well stayed on production, with 3 hours of downtime logged as of this note.

## Immediate actions
- Lead Operator checked the wellhead pressure gauge against a reference gauge. The gauge reads correctly.
- Choke setting confirmed unchanged at the wellhead.
- Production Supervisor informed.
- Well kept online under closer monitoring.

## Findings so far
The swings are not a gauge fault. The working suspicion is partial choke plugging. No other cause has been confirmed yet.

## Impact
- Uptime on 2026-11-15: 21 hours.
- Oil on the day: 620.6 bbl, against a 7-day average of 749.8 bbl/d to 2026-11-14, a shortfall of about 129 bbl.
- Gas on the day: 556.4 Mscf, against 680.1 Mscf/d before.
- Water cut unchanged at 30.5 percent, so the loss comes from downtime and restriction, not from a change in fluid.
- Gas-oil ratio before the event was 907 scf/bbl.

## Status at note date
Open. The well is producing and under monitoring. No related events on this well in the last 30 days.

## Next actions
1. Operators to log wellhead pressure at short intervals each shift and record any further swings.
2. Production Engineer to review ESP motor current and intake data for any matching fluctuation.
3. If swings continue or grow, plan a choke inspection and clean-out, with a short shut-in agreed with the Production Supervisor.
4. Update this note when the cause is confirmed.

**Applicable procedure:** none identified for this event type.
