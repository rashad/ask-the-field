# Incident note: Wellhead pressure anomaly on W-05

**Document ID:** IN-068
**Note date:** 2026-11-15
**Event ID:** EV-0181
**Well:** W-05 (Field A, vertical, ESP)
**Severity:** medium
**Status at note date:** open

## What happened
On 2026-11-15 the flowing wellhead pressure on W-05 began swinging by plus or minus 60 psi with no choke change. The well remains on production.

## Immediate actions
- The Lead Operator confirmed the choke position had not been changed.
- The wellhead gauge was checked against a reference gauge and read correctly, so an instrument fault is considered unlikely.
- The Production Supervisor was informed.
- The well was kept online under closer monitoring, with pressure readings taken at shorter intervals.

## Findings so far
The working suspicion is partial plugging of the choke. This is not confirmed. No ESP trip or alarm was reported in connection with the swings.

## Impact
- Downtime as of the note date: 3 hours (uptime on the day 21 hours).
- Production on 2026-11-15: oil 685.6 bbl, gas 631.5 Mscf, water cut 28.5%.
- Baseline for the 7 days to 2026-11-14: oil 784.3 bbl/d, gas 700.4 Mscf/d, water cut 29.7%, GOR 893 scf/bbl.
- Oil on the day was about 98.7 bbl below the baseline daily rate, consistent with the lost hours and the unstable flow. Water cut is essentially unchanged, so a change in reservoir fluid is not indicated at this point.

## Status
Open. The well is producing and under monitoring. No related events were recorded in the last 30 days. No specific procedure applies to this event.

## Next actions
1. The Production Engineer will review the pressure trend over the next shifts to see whether the swings persist or grow.
2. If the swings continue, plan a choke inspection or change-out, with the downtime scheduled to limit deferred oil.
3. Check ESP motor current and intake data for any sign of a downhole cause.
4. Update this note once the cause is confirmed or the swings stop.
