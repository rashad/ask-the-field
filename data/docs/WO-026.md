## Scope of work
Subsurface safety valve function and leak test. Close the valve, confirm closure, then monitor downstream pressure for the leak test hold period and record the result.

## Safety and permits
- Work permit and isolation certificate issued by the Lead Operator before the well was shut in.
- Toolbox talk held with the slickline crew and the control room.
- Wellhead area barricaded, gas detection checked before work started.
- Control room informed of the planned shut-in and restart.

## Materials and equipment
- Test pump and calibrated gauges
- Hydraulic control line fittings
- Valve test record sheet

## Execution notes
- Well shut in on 2025-10-11 and the safety valve function tested from the control panel.
- The valve closed fully and held during the leak test. No leak-off outside the acceptance limit was observed.
- The valve was reopened, the well was restarted and the wellhead was checked for leaks after restart.
- Total downtime for the job: 5 hours.

## Close-out remarks
Test passed and the valve was returned to service. The test record was filed and the Maintenance Planner has scheduled the next test per the routine program. No outstanding actions. Work order closed.
