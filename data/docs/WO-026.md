## Scope of work
Preventive scale inhibitor squeeze on W-17 to protect the completion and ESP as water cut rises in Wadi Field. Well shut in, treatment bullheaded, overflushed and held for the inhibitor soak, then the well was restarted.

## Safety and permits
- Hot work not required. Pumping permit and pressure-test permit issued by the Production Supervisor.
- Toolbox talk held before rigging up. Chemical handling PPE in use and the HSE Advisor checked the bunding at the pump skid.
- Lines pressure tested before pumping, as required by the SOP.

## Materials
- Scale inhibitor and overflush fluid supplied by the chemical vendor.
- Pumping unit and transfer tank from the service contractor.

## Execution and close-out remarks
- Work ran from 2025-09-18 to 2025-09-19 as planned. Total downtime was 34 hours.
- Squeeze pumped and soaked per SOP-OPS-008, no leaks or pressure excursions reported. Site cleared and the well handed back to the Lead Operator.
- Production, 14 days before shutdown (to 2025-09-17): oil 1644.7 bbl/d, gas 1307.5 Mscf/d, water cut 26.3 %, GOR 795 scf/bbl.
- Production, 6 days after restart (to 2025-09-26): oil 1671.3 bbl/d, gas 1333.8 Mscf/d, water cut 26 %, GOR 798 scf/bbl.
- No adverse change after restart. Well is producing.
- Follow-up: Production Engineer to schedule produced-water residual inhibitor sampling to set the next squeeze interval.

**Work order closed.**
