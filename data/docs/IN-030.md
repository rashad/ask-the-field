# Incident note: H2S alarm on W-13

**Document ID:** IN-030
**Note date:** 2026-02-24
**Event ID:** EV-0089
**Well:** W-13 (Dune Field, horizontal, gas lift)
**Category / severity:** Incident, low
**Prepared by:** HSE Advisor
**Status at note date:** Closed

## What happened
On 2026-02-24 the fixed H2S detector near the W-13 wellhead went into alarm. The area was cleared and the well was shut in under the alarm response.

## Immediate actions
- Personnel mustered at the designated point and a headcount was completed.
- The Lead Operator informed the Production Supervisor and the control room.
- The well was shut in and the wellhead area was kept isolated.
- A portable gas detector survey was carried out by trained personnel wearing breathing apparatus.

## Findings
The survey located a leak on a needle valve at the wellhead. The valve was repaired, the connection was leak tested, and a follow-up survey showed no H2S above alarm level. The detector was checked and reset before the area was reopened. No injuries were reported.

## Impact
- Downtime: 8 hours on 2026-02-24 (uptime 16 hours).
- Production on the day: 662.4 bbl oil, 426.5 Mscf gas, water cut 15.4%.
- Prior 7 days to 2026-02-23: 970.5 bbl/d oil, 570.4 Mscf/d gas, water cut 15.2%, GOR 588 scf/bbl.
- The shortfall against the prior 7-day average is about 308 bbl of oil, from the 8 hour shutdown.

## Status
Closed. The well was returned to production and is producing today. No related events were recorded in the last 30 days.

## Next actions
- Production Engineer to confirm that W-13 rates return to the prior baseline over the next few days.
- Maintenance Planner to add the wellhead needle valves to the next routine leak check round.
- Lead Operator to review the response with the shift crews at the next toolbox talk.

## Applicable procedure
SOP-HSE-003 H2S Alarm Response.
