# Incident note: H2S alarm on W-24

**Document ID:** IN-030
**Event ID:** EV-0079
**Note date:** 2026-01-30
**Well:** W-24 (Field B, vertical, ESP)
**Severity:** Medium
**Status at note date:** Closed

## What happened
On 2026-01-30 the fixed H2S detector near the W-24 wellhead went into alarm. The area was cleared in line with SOP-HSE-003 H2S Alarm Response and the well was shut in while the source was investigated.

## Immediate actions
- Personnel mustered at the upwind assembly point and a headcount was completed.
- Access to the wellsite was barred and the Production Supervisor was informed.
- Trained responders wearing breathing apparatus entered and checked the area with portable gas detectors.
- The well was isolated and the area was kept restricted until readings were clear.

## Findings
The leak came from a needle valve at the wellhead. The valve was repaired, and the area was rechecked with portable detectors and found clear before re-entry was allowed. No injuries were reported.

## Impact
- Downtime: 5 hours on 2026-01-30 (uptime 19 hours).
- Production on the day: 477.3 bbl oil, 228 Mscf gas, water cut 32.3 percent.
- Baseline for the 7 days to 2026-01-29: 645.2 bbl/d oil, 324.2 Mscf/d gas, water cut 29.9 percent, GOR 502 scf/bbl.
- The shortfall against baseline is about 168 bbl of oil for the day, mostly from the shutdown.
- No related events in the previous 30 days.

## Status
Closed. W-24 is back on production.

## Next actions
- Production Engineer to check W-24 rates over the next few days against the baseline above.
- Integrity Engineer to include the other wellhead needle valves in the next routine leak check.
- HSE Advisor to review the response with the shift crew at the next safety meeting.
