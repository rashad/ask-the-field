## Comparison with previous baseline

The previous baseline covers the 7 days to 2026-09-12: oil 752 bbl/d, gas 1419.5 Mscf/d, water cut 30.7 %, GOR 1888 scf/bbl.

- Oil is down 250 bbl/d (about 33 %).
- Gas is down 495.7 Mscf/d.
- Water cut is up 0.8 points, a small change.
- GOR is slightly lower (1840 against 1888 scf/bbl), so the gas decline follows the oil decline and does not point to a gas breakthrough.

## Observations

- Sand alarm EV-0180 has been open since 2026-11-09 (acoustic sand monitor above threshold, erosion probe on the flowline showing metal loss). Downtime to date is 6 hours. The choke has since been reduced again to 24/64 and sand is sampled every shift.
- Measured sand of 12 ppm was taken at the 48/64 test choke, before the further reduction.
- The rate loss is partly due to the sand-related restrictions, so this test should not be treated as a clean reservoir baseline.

## Recommendation

1. Keep the well at 24/64 until the sand investigation is complete.
2. Continue shift sand sampling and track erosion probe readings.
3. Repeat the test at the restricted choke once sand is stable, following SOP-OPS-004.
4. The Production Engineer to review the flowline integrity result with the Integrity Engineer before any choke increase.
