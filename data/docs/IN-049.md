# Incident note: Water breakthrough on W-23

**Document id:** IN-049
**Note date:** 2026-06-15
**Event id:** EV-0126
**Well:** W-23 (Field B, vertical, ESP)
**Severity:** medium
**Status at note date:** closed

## What happened
A sharp rise in water cut was observed on W-23, from about 30 percent to over 55 percent within weeks. The trend is consistent with injector water breakthrough from the north flank. The event ran from 2026-06-14 to 2026-06-15.

## Immediate actions
- Well kept on production under close watch, with additional water cut sampling at the CPF-1 test separator.
- ESP motor current and intake pressure checked, with no abnormal readings noted.
- Production Supervisor and Field Engineer informed.

## Findings so far
- Production for the 7 days to 2026-06-13 averaged 934.7 bbl/d oil, 486.5 Mscf/d gas, water cut 34.2 percent and GOR 520 scf/bbl.
- On 2026-06-14 the well made 743.8 bbl of oil and 418.8 Mscf of gas at a water cut of 36.2 percent, with 20 hours of uptime.
- The water source is attributed to the north flank injector. No equipment failure was identified.

## Impact
- Downtime as of the note date: 8 hours.
- Oil rate on 2026-06-14 was below the prior 7-day average, partly from the lost run time.
- No related events on this well in the last 30 days.

## Status
Closed as of 2026-06-15. W-23 is producing.

## Next actions
- Production Engineer to keep W-23 on the routine well test schedule and track water cut against the north flank injection rates.
- Field Engineer to review injection allocation with the reservoir team.
- Consider whether the water cut trend warrants a change in ESP operating settings if it continues.

## Applicable procedure
No specific procedure applies to this event.
