# Incident note: ESP trip on W-21

**Document ID:** IN-049
**Event ID:** EV-0127
**Note date:** 2026-06-14
**Author:** Production Engineer
**Well:** W-21 (Wadi Field, horizontal, ESP)
**Severity:** Low
**Status at note date:** Closed

## What happened
On 2026-06-14 the ESP on W-21 tripped on underload. The well was shut in by the trip and stayed down while checks were carried out, then the pump was restarted.

## Immediate actions
- The Lead Operator confirmed the trip on the drive and logged the fault code.
- Field Engineer checked the surface power supply, drive settings and wellhead pressures before any restart attempt.
- The well was restarted following SOP-OPS-001 ESP Trip Response and Restart, with motor current and intake readings monitored after start-up.
- The Production Supervisor was informed of the trip and the restart.

## Findings
- The trip cause was recorded as underload. No surface electrical fault was found during the checks.
- Motor current and flowing parameters were stable after the restart.
- No related events on W-21 in the previous 30 days.

## Impact
- Downtime: 21 hours on 2026-06-14 (uptime on the day was 3 hours).
- Production on the day: 47.8 bbl of oil and 49 Mscf of gas, water cut 40.9%.
- Baseline for the 7 days to 2026-06-13: 402.4 bbl/d oil, 428.1 Mscf/d gas, water cut 40.1%, GOR 1064 scf/bbl.
- Deferred oil on the day is therefore roughly 355 bbl against the baseline rate.

## Status
The well is producing and the incident is closed as of 2026-06-14.

## Next actions
- Operators to keep watching W-21 motor current and intake pressure on each round for the next few days.
- Production Engineer to review the drive trend data at the next weekly well review.
- Any repeat underload trip is to be reported as a new event and handled under SOP-OPS-001.
