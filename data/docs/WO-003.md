# Work order: Choke replacement, W-23

**WO number:** WO-003
**Event reference:** EV-0007
**Well:** W-23 (Field B, vertical, ESP)
**Category:** Maintenance
**Priority:** Routine
**Raised:** 2025-06-12
**Planned start:** 2025-06-14
**Actual start:** 2025-06-14
**Completion date:** 2025-06-14
**Status:** Closed

## Scope of work
Replace the eroded choke bean on the W-23 wellhead flow line. Inspect the choke body and seat for erosion damage, fit a new bean, and return the well to production at the previous choke setting.

## Safety and permits
- Hot work not required; cold work permit issued.
- Flow line isolated, depressurised and verified at zero pressure before breaking containment.
- Lock-out applied to the wing valve for the duration of the job.
- Standard PPE plus gas monitor carried by the crew.

## Materials
- 1 choke bean, same size as the removed bean
- 1 set of gaskets and seals
- Torque tools and a spare seat, held on standby

## Execution notes and close-out remarks
- The bean removed showed clear erosion. The choke body and seat were inspected and found serviceable.
- New bean fitted, joints torqued, and a leak test completed with no leaks.
- Total downtime for the well was 7 hours.
- A field power dip tripped W-23 on the same day (EV-0006, 11 hours downtime, low severity). The well was restarted once power was restored. The two events overlapped, so the downtime figures are recorded separately against each event.
- Production, 7 days before the job (to 2025-06-13): 1209.9 bbl/d oil, 671.1 Mscf/d gas, water cut 25%, GOR 555 scf/bbl.
- Production, 6 days after restart (to 2025-06-21): 1247.2 bbl/d oil, 684.4 Mscf/d gas, water cut 24.7%, GOR 549 scf/bbl.
- Oil rate is up after the job, with water cut and GOR essentially unchanged.
- No follow-up work required. The work order is closed.
