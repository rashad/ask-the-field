# Work order: Wellhead maintenance, W-19

**WO number:** WO-003
**Event reference:** EV-0004
**Well:** W-19 (Wadi Field, deviated, ESP)
**Category:** Maintenance
**Priority:** Routine
**Raised:** 2025-06-05
**Planned start:** 2025-06-07
**Actual start:** 2025-06-07
**Completion date:** 2025-06-07
**Status:** Closed

## Scope of work
Wellhead valve greasing and function test on W-19. Grease all wellhead valves, cycle each valve fully open and closed, and record any stiffness, leakage or gland weeping.

## Safety and permits
- Work permit and isolation certificate issued before start.
- Well shut in for the duration of the work, with the ESP stopped and the electrical supply locked out.
- Toolbox talk held with the crew. Gas detection carried and wellhead area barricaded.
- No hot work was required.

## Materials
- Valve grease (grade per maintenance plan)
- Grease gun and fittings
- Torque wrench and gland packing kit (on standby, not used)

## Execution notes
The well was shut in and the valves greased in sequence. Each valve was then function tested through a full cycle. All valves moved freely, with no leakage at stems or flanges. No parts were replaced. The well was restarted after the function test and returned to normal operation.

## Impact
Downtime was 4 hours as of the close-out date.

## Close-out remarks
Work completed as planned on 2025-06-07 with no findings requiring follow-up. Post-restart rates were slightly below the pre-work average, and the Production Engineer will keep watching the trend in routine surveillance. Valve greasing returns to the normal maintenance schedule. Work order closed.
