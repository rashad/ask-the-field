# Incident note: Flowline leak on W-25

**Document ID:** IN-014
**Note date:** 2025-11-24
**Event ID:** EV-0053
**Prepared by:** Production Engineer
**Well:** W-25 (Wadi Field, deviated, ESP)
**Severity:** Medium
**Status at note date:** Closed

## What happened
On 2025-11-23 a small leak was found on a flowline flange on W-25. The well was shut down so the line could be isolated and repaired.

## Immediate actions
- Well shut down and flowline isolated and depressurised.
- Area barricaded and the spill cleaned up.
- Production Supervisor and HSE Advisor informed.
- Flange joint repaired and the line returned to service on 2025-11-24.

## Findings
The leak was limited to the flange joint. No injuries were reported and no other part of the flowline showed signs of leakage during the follow-up walkdown. The well is producing normally today.

## Impact
- Downtime: 28 hours as of the note date.
- On 2025-11-23 the well ran for only 10 hours and produced 241.5 bbl of oil and 142.5 Mscf of gas, with water cut at 24.1%.
- For the 7 days to 2025-11-22 the well averaged 602.7 bbl/d oil, 365.7 Mscf/d gas, 24.3% water cut and a GOR of 607 scf/bbl, so the lost production can be estimated against that baseline.
- No related events on this well in the previous 30 days.

## Status
Closed. The repair was completed, the line was checked at operating pressure and no further leakage was seen.

## Next actions
- Lead Operator to include the flange in the routine flowline round for the coming week.
- Integrity Engineer to record the repair in the flowline register.
- Response followed SOP-HSE-006 Flowline Leak Response.
