# Incident note: High separator level trip on W-25

**Document ID:** IN-014
**Event ID:** EV-0039
**Note date:** 2025-10-03
**Prepared by:** Production Engineer
**Well:** W-25 (Field B, deviated, ESP)
**Severity:** Low
**Status:** Closed

## What happened
On 2025-10-03 the test separator at CPF-1 tripped on high level. The trip shut in W-25, which was lined up to the test separator at the time. The well was down for 6 hours and uptime for the day was 18 hours.

## Immediate actions
- The Lead Operator confirmed the trip was a level alarm and not a downstream process upset.
- The separator level was checked locally against the gauge glass and found to disagree with the transmitter reading.
- The level controller was recalibrated by the instrument technician.
- The well was restarted after the level reading was verified as stable.

## Findings
The cause was the level controller, which was reading out of calibration. No fluid carry-over, leak or release was observed. No injuries occurred.

## Impact
- Downtime: 6 hours.
- Production on 2025-10-03: 456.4 bbl oil, 376.8 Mscf gas, water cut 25%.
- Baseline for the 7 days to 2025-10-02: 564.2 bbl/d oil, 466.6 Mscf/d gas, water cut 25.1%, GOR 827 scf/bbl.
- Deferred oil against baseline is roughly 108 bbl for the day. Water cut was unchanged, so the loss is attributed to the downtime.

## Status at note date
Closed. W-25 is producing normally after the restart and the level controller is reading correctly.

## Next actions
- Production Engineer to add the test separator level controller to the next instrument calibration round.
- Operators to compare transmitter and gauge glass readings once per shift for the next week.
- Flowline pigging on W-25 is planned from 2025-10-16 to remove wax and sand deposits. It is not related to this trip.

## Procedure reference
No specific procedure applies to this event.
