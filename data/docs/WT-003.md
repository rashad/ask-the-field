# Well test: W-10, 2025-09-01

**Document ID:** WT-003
**Well:** W-10 (Dune Field, horizontal, ESP)
**Test date:** 2025-09-01
**Procedure:** SOP-OPS-004 Well Test Procedure with the Mobile Test Separator

## Test conditions

- Test duration: 8 hours on the mobile test separator
- Choke: 40/64 in
- Flowing wellhead pressure: 335 psi
- Well was stable before the test and lined up per SOP-OPS-004.
- Recent activity: the eroded choke bean was replaced on 2025-08-23 (EV-0019, closed, 6 hours downtime). This test is the first one since that work and reflects the new bean.

## Measured results

| Parameter | Test 2025-09-01 (3 days) | Baseline 2025-07-03 (7 days) |
|---|---|---|
| Oil (bbl/d) | 791 | 822.2 |
| Gas (Mscf/d) | 720.6 | 747.9 |
| Water cut (%) | 10.4 | 10.4 |
| GOR (scf/bbl) | 911 | 910 |

Sand: 1 ppm.

## Comparison with baseline

- Oil is about 31 bbl/d lower than the baseline (roughly 4 percent), and gas is about 27 Mscf/d lower.
- Water cut is unchanged at 10.4 percent and GOR is essentially flat (911 vs 910 scf/bbl), so the fluid composition has not changed.
- Sand is negligible.

## Observations

- Separator readings were steady through the test, with no slugging noted.
- The small rate difference may relate to the new choke bean, since the previous bean was eroded, and to the short averaging window compared with the baseline.

## Recommendation

- Keep W-10 on the current 40/64 choke.
- Repeat the test at the next scheduled interval to confirm whether the rate settles.
- Continue routine sand monitoring.
