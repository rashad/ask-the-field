## Scope of work
- Grease all wellhead valves (master, wing and swab) on W-38.
- Function test each valve through full travel and record the number of turns and any stiffness.
- Check for leaks at stem packing and flange connections after greasing.
- Return the well to production after the test.

## Safety and permits
- Hot work: not required.
- Cold work permit issued by the Production Supervisor.
- Well shut in and isolated before work. Pressure around the wellhead checked as zero on the bleed point before opening grease fittings.
- Toolbox talk held with the maintenance crew. Standard PPE and gas detector worn.

## Materials
- Valve grease (approved wellhead grade), grease gun, lint-free rags, leak detection fluid.
- No spare parts consumed.

## Close-out remarks
- Work completed on 2025-09-23 within one shift.
- All valves greased and function tested. Valves operated smoothly with no sticking and no leaks seen at packing or flanges.
- Total downtime on W-38: 4 hours.
- Well restarted and stable. Wellhead area cleaned and permit closed.
