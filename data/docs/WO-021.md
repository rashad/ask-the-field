# Work order: Wellhead maintenance, W-27

**WO number:** WO-021
**Event reference:** EV-0027
**Well:** W-27 (Wadi Field, horizontal, ESP)
**Category / type:** Maintenance / Wellhead maintenance
**Priority:** Routine
**Raised:** 2025-09-03
**Planned start:** 2025-09-05
**Actual start / completion:** 2025-09-05 / 2025-09-05
**Status:** Closed

## Scope of work
- Grease all wellhead valves (master, wing and swab) per the maintenance schedule.
- Function test each valve, recording the number of turns and any stiffness.
- Check the wellhead for leaks and confirm that gauges are readable.

## Safety and permits
- Work permit issued before start. Lock-out of the well was applied after shut-in.
- Pressure on the wellhead was confirmed bled to zero before greasing.
- Toolbox talk held with the crew. HSE Advisor was informed of the shut-in window.

## Materials
- Valve grease (approved grade), grease gun, calibrated torque tools, cleaning rags.
- No spares were required.

## Execution notes
The well was shut in for the work and restarted after the function tests. Total downtime was 6 hours. All valves moved freely through full travel and no leaks were seen at the wellhead after restart.

## Close-out remarks
Wellhead valve greasing and function test completed as planned. No defects were found and no follow-up work is raised.

Production averaged over 14 days to 2025-09-04 (before):
- Oil 807.1 bbl/d, gas 885.2 Mscf/d, water cut 29.4%, GOR 1097 scf/bbl.

Production averaged over 6 days to 2025-09-12 (after restart):
- Oil 770.3 bbl/d, gas 838.1 Mscf/d, water cut 30.8%, GOR 1088 scf/bbl.

Rates after restart are slightly lower, with a small rise in water cut. The Production Engineer will keep watching the trend in routine surveillance.

Related recent work on this well: EV-0016 (ESP surface inspection, 2025-08-10) and EV-0018 (power outage trip, 2025-08-16). Both are closed.

**Closed by:** Lead Operator, with Maintenance Planner sign-off.
