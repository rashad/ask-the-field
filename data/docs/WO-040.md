# Work order: Flowline pigging, W-19

**WO number:** WO-040
**Event reference:** EV-0054
**Well:** W-19 (Wadi Field, deviated, ESP)
**Category:** Maintenance
**Priority:** Routine
**Raised:** 2025-11-23
**Planned start:** 2025-11-25
**Actual start:** 2025-11-25
**Completion:** 2025-11-25
**Status:** Closed

## Scope of work

Pig the W-19 flowline to CPF-1 to remove wax and sand deposits. Launch the pig at the wellsite launcher, receive it at the CPF-1 receiver, and record the debris volume recovered.

## Safety and permits

- Hot work not required. Permit to work issued for line breaking and pressure isolation.
- Well shut in for the duration of the job and the flowline depressurised before opening the launcher.
- Pig receiver area barricaded. Operators wore gas detectors and the HSE Advisor was informed before launch.
- Drain and flare routing checked with the CPF-1 control room.

## Materials

- Foam-bodied cleaning pig, one unit, plus one spare
- Replacement launcher door seal
- Waste containers for wax and sand

## Execution notes

W-19 was shut in and the flowline isolated on 2025-11-25. The pig was launched and received at CPF-1 without sticking. Wax and sand solids were recovered at the receiver and disposed of in the waste containers. The launcher door seal was replaced. The well was returned to production the same day. Total downtime was 7 hours.

## Close-out remarks

- Work completed on the planned date with no safety incidents or leaks.
- Production for the 14 days to 2025-11-24: oil 984.5 bbl/d, gas 954.5 Mscf/d, water cut 35.4 percent, GOR 970 scf/bbl.
- Production for the 6 days after restart to 2025-12-02: oil 932.9 bbl/d, gas 921.7 Mscf/d, water cut 35.1 percent, GOR 988 scf/bbl.
- Oil rate is lower than before the job. The Production Engineer will keep monitoring the rate and report any further trend.
- No related events in the 30 days around the job. Work order closed.
