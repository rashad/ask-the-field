# Work order: Wellhead maintenance, W-09

**WO number:** WO-040
**Event reference:** EV-0062
**Well:** W-09 (Field A, vertical, ESP)
**Category:** Maintenance
**Priority:** Routine
**Raised:** 2025-12-19
**Planned start:** 2025-12-21
**Actual start:** 2025-12-21
**Completion date:** 2025-12-21
**Status:** Closed

## Scope of work
- Grease all wellhead valves (master, wing and swab) using the approved grease type.
- Function test each valve through its full travel and record the number of turns and any stiffness.
- Check the wellhead for leaks at flanges and stem packings after the test.

## Safety and permits
- Work performed under a general work permit with a job safety analysis reviewed at the toolbox talk.
- The well was shut in for the work and the wellhead pressure was verified at isolation before any greasing began.
- Barriers were confirmed and the area was kept clear of non-essential personnel.
- No hot work and no line breaking were required.

## Materials
- Valve grease (approved grade) and grease gun fittings
- Cleaning rags and leak detection fluid
- Valve operation log sheet

## Execution notes and close-out remarks
- The well was shut in on the morning of 2025-12-21 and the full job was completed the same day.
- All valves greased and function tested with smooth travel. No leaks were seen at flanges or stem packings.
- Well restarted after the work. Total downtime was 3 hours.
- Tools were removed, the area was cleaned and the permit was closed out with the Lead Operator.
