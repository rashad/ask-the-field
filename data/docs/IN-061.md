# Incident note: H2S alarm on W-28

**Document ID:** IN-061
**Note date:** 2026-09-03
**Event ID:** EV-0163
**Well:** W-28 (Field B, vertical, ESP)
**Severity:** Low
**Status at note date:** Closed
**Applicable procedure:** SOP-HSE-003 H2S Alarm Response

## What happened
On 2026-09-03 the fixed H2S detector near the W-28 wellhead went into alarm. The alarm was acknowledged by the control room and the Lead Operator was dispatched.

## Immediate actions
- The area around the wellhead was cleared and access was barred.
- The well was shut in as a precaution, in line with SOP-HSE-003.
- Personnel were accounted for at the muster point. No exposure or injury was reported.
- Responders with portable gas detectors and breathing apparatus approached from upwind to check the wellhead.

## Findings
The leak came from a needle valve on the wellhead instrument tubing. The valve was repaired, and a follow-up check with portable detectors showed no readings above background. The fixed detector returned to normal and the area was declared safe.

## Impact
- Downtime: 7 hours (uptime on the day was 17 hours).
- Production on 2026-09-03: oil 698.2 bbl, gas 708.1 Mscf, water cut 41.5%.
- Prior 7-day average to 2026-09-02: oil 994.2 bbl/d, gas 990.8 Mscf/d, water cut 41.8%, GOR 997 scf/bbl.
- The shortfall on the day is consistent with the shut-in period. Water cut and GOR showed no meaningful change.
- No related events were recorded on this well in the previous 30 days.

## Status
Closed. W-28 is back on production and the well is currently producing.

## Next actions
- The Production Engineer will confirm that the replaced needle valve fitting is recorded in the wellhead maintenance history.
- The HSE Advisor will review the alarm log and response times against SOP-HSE-003.
- The Lead Operator will include the wellhead in the next routine leak-check round.
