# Incident note: Sand production alarm on W-25

**Document ID:** IN-061
**Event ID:** EV-0165
**Note date:** 2026-09-23
**Author:** Production Engineer
**Well:** W-25 (Wadi Field, deviated, ESP)
**Severity:** Low
**Status:** Closed

## What happened
On 2026-09-23 the sand monitor on the W-25 flowline raised an alarm. The Lead Operator confirmed the alarm on the local panel and reduced the choke until the sand readings returned to normal.

## Immediate actions
- Choke reduced in steps while the sand monitor was watched.
- Readings stabilised at normal levels after the reduction.
- Production Supervisor informed.
- Visual check of the wellhead and flowline done, with no leaks or abnormal noise found.

## Impact
- Downtime on the day: 10 hours (uptime 14 hours).
- Production on 2026-09-23: 244.9 bbl oil, 147.7 Mscf gas, water cut 33.1%.
- Baseline for the 7 days to 2026-09-22: 440.3 bbl/d oil, 264.4 Mscf/d gas, water cut 32.9%, GOR 600 scf/bbl.
- Water cut and GOR are in line with the baseline. The oil loss on the day comes from the downtime and the choke restriction.

## Findings so far
No other abnormal parameters were seen with the alarm. No related events on this well in the previous 30 days.

## Status at note date
Closed. The alarm cleared, the well is stable and producing, and it is producing today.

## Next actions
- Production Engineer to review the sand monitor trend over the next several days as routine follow-up.
- Operators to keep recording sand readings each shift.
- Any repeat alarm to be handled under the same procedure.

## Reference
SOP-OPS-002 Sand Management and Sand Production Alarms.
