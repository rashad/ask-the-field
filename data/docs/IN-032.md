# Incident note: ESP trip on W-17

**Document ID:** IN-032
**Note date:** 2026-03-09
**Event ID:** EV-0092
**Well:** W-17 (Wadi Field, vertical, ESP)
**Severity:** High
**Status at note date:** Closed
**Prepared by:** Production Engineer

## What happened
On 2026-03-04 the ESP on W-17 tripped on motor overload with phase current imbalance. The installed pump is a Varenta VL-538, serial 7Q-0419-112 (batch 7Q-0419), with a run life of 13 months.

## Immediate actions
- Restart attempts were made by the operations team and all failed.
- A megger test on the motor showed low insulation resistance.
- The well was shut in and held shut in pending workover.
- The Production Supervisor and Workover Supervisor were informed.

## Findings so far
The electrical readings point to a motor fault. Downhole inspection is only possible once the pump is pulled, so the root cause is not yet confirmed.

## Impact
- Event period: 2026-03-04 to 2026-03-09.
- Downtime as of the note date: 144 hours.
- Production on 2026-03-04 was 0 bbl oil, 0 Mscf gas and 0 hours uptime.
- Rates in the 7 days to 2026-03-03 averaged 1198.5 bbl/d oil, 925.4 Mscf/d gas, 32.6% water cut and GOR 772 scf/bbl. Deferred oil is estimated from this baseline, about 1198.5 bbl for each day of downtime.

## Status
The incident record is closed at the note date. The well remains shut in. A workover to replace the ESP is planned to start on 2026-03-10 (EV-0094) and is tracked under its own maintenance event. Return-to-production timing will be confirmed after the workover.

## Next actions
1. Workover Supervisor to confirm the rig and the ESP vendor crew for 2026-03-10.
2. Field Engineer to request a teardown inspection of the pulled equipment.
3. Production Engineer to review the restart conditions and set the post-workover startup plan.
4. Lead Operator to keep the wellhead isolated and log checks until the rig arrives.
