# Incident note: ESP trip on W-17

**Document id:** IN-032
**Note date:** 2026-03-10
**Event id:** EV-0090
**Well:** W-17 (Field B, deviated, ESP)
**Severity:** high
**Status at note date:** closed

## What happened
On 2026-03-04 the ESP on W-17 tripped on motor overload with phase current imbalance. Restart attempts failed. A megger test showed low insulation resistance on the motor lead. The well was shut in pending workover.

## Immediate actions
- Well isolated and the surface controller locked out.
- Restart attempts made in line with SOP-OPS-001 ESP Trip Response and Restart, with no success.
- Megger test run on the motor lead, confirming the insulation fault.
- Production Supervisor and CPF-1 control room informed of the loss of the well.

## Findings so far
- The failure is consistent with a motor lead insulation fault rather than a surface or process cause.
- No further restarts are advised, since repeated attempts risk more damage to the downhole equipment.

## Impact
- Downtime: 168 hours from 2026-03-04 to 2026-03-10.
- Production on 2026-03-04 was 0 bbl oil, 0 Mscf gas and 0 hours uptime.
- Baseline for the 7 days to 2026-03-03: 1175.5 bbl/d oil, 646.2 Mscf/d gas, water cut 29.9 percent, GOR 550 scf/bbl. The deferred oil is estimated against this baseline at roughly 1175.5 bbl for each day of outage.

## Status at note date
The incident record is closed as of 2026-03-10. The failure is diagnosed and the remedy is a downhole equipment change. Well W-17 stays shut in until the workover.

## Next actions
1. Workover (EV-0093) is planned to start on 2026-03-11, subject to rig mobilization.
2. Inspect the pulled completion and the motor lead on recovery, and record findings for the failure analysis.
3. Production Engineer to review the ESP run life and electrical trends for W-17 after the workover.
4. Review the recorded trip data against SOP-OPS-001 and confirm the shutdown steps were followed.
