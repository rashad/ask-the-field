# Incident note: Power outage on W-33

**Document ID:** IN-006
**Event ID:** EV-0028
**Note date:** 2025-09-07
**Author:** Production Engineer
**Well:** W-33 (Salt Flat Field, vertical, gas lift)
**Severity:** High
**Status:** Closed

## What happened
A field power dip on 2025-09-07 tripped W-33. The well was restarted once power was restored. The event started and ended on the same day.

## Immediate actions
- The Lead Operator confirmed the trip at the wellhead panel and checked that the field supply was stable before restart.
- Wellhead pressures and the gas lift injection line were checked before the well was brought back online.
- The well was restarted and observed through its return to stable flow.
- The Production Supervisor was informed of the trip and the restart.

## Findings
The trip was caused by the field power dip and not by a fault on the well or its gas lift equipment. No equipment damage was reported after the restart.

## Impact
- Downtime: 6 hours (uptime on the day was 18 hours).
- Production on 2025-09-07: 1030.5 bbl of oil and 1310.7 Mscf of gas, water cut 19.1%.
- Baseline for the 7 days to 2025-09-06: 1476.1 bbl/d oil, 1849.9 Mscf/d gas, water cut 18.3%, GOR 1253 scf/bbl.
- Oil on the day was about 445.6 bbl below the baseline rate, which is the deferred oil attributable to the outage.

## Status at note date
Closed. W-33 is back on production and the well is producing today. No related events were recorded in the preceding 30 days.

## Next actions
- Production Engineer to confirm the rate returns to the pre-event baseline in the next daily allocation.
- Maintenance Planner to pass the outage on to the power supply team for their own review of the dip.

## Applicable procedure
No specific procedure applies to this event.
