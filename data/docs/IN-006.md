# Incident note: Wellhead leak on W-11

**Document ID:** IN-006
**Event ID:** EV-0010
**Note date:** 2025-06-18
**Well:** W-11 (Field A, deviated, gas lift)
**Severity:** Low
**Status at note date:** Closed
**Prepared by:** Production Engineer

## What happened
On 2025-06-18 a leak was found on the wellhead valve stem packing of W-11. The well was taken off line so the leak could be isolated and repaired.

## Immediate actions
- The well was shut in and the wellhead area was barricaded.
- Pressure was bled down and the valve was isolated before any work began.
- The Production Supervisor and HSE Advisor were informed.
- The packing was replaced and the valve was leak tested before the well was returned to service.

## Findings
- The leak was limited to the valve stem packing. No other wellhead component showed signs of leakage.
- No injury and no release off the pad were reported. Spilled fluid was contained and cleaned up at the wellhead.

## Impact
- Downtime: 11 hours on 2025-06-18, which matches the 13 hours of uptime recorded for the day.
- Production on the day: 619.4 bbl of oil and 640.9 Mscf of gas, with water cut at 14.9%.
- The 7-day average before the event (to 2025-06-17) was 1105.5 bbl/d of oil, 1108.3 Mscf/d of gas, 15.7% water cut and a GOR of 1003 scf/bbl. The loss against that baseline is deferred oil caused by the shutdown.
- No change in water cut or GOR was seen that would point to a reservoir cause.

## Status
Closed. The packing was replaced, the leak test was passed and W-11 is producing normally.

## Next actions
- The Lead Operator will include the W-11 wellhead valves in routine rounds for the next week, checking for any recurrence of the leak.
- The Maintenance Planner will review the packing condition on similar valves in the next scheduled inspection.

## Applicable procedure
No specific procedure applies to this event.
