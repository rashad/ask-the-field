# Work order: Scale squeeze treatment, W-02

**WO number:** WO-037
**Event reference:** EV-0054
**Well:** W-02 (Field A, deviated, gas lift)
**Category:** Maintenance
**Priority:** Routine (preventive)
**Raised:** 2025-11-15
**Planned start:** 2025-11-17
**Completion date:** 2025-11-18
**Status:** Closed

## Scope of work
Preventive scale inhibitor squeeze on W-02 in line with SOP-OPS-008 Scale Inhibitor Squeeze Treatment. Shut in the well, pump pre-flush, inhibitor and overflush with the pumping vendor, hold for the soak period, then return the well to production.

Baseline before the job (7 days to 2025-11-16): oil 1449.5 bbl/d, gas 705.9 Mscf/d, water cut 20 %, GOR 487 scf/bbl.

## Safety and permits
- Hot work excluded; cold work permit and chemical handling permit issued.
- Pre-job toolbox talk held with the pumping crew and Lead Operator.
- Pressure test of surface lines before pumping; HSE Advisor reviewed the chemical safety data sheets.
- Area barricaded during pumping.

## Materials
- Scale inhibitor and compatible brine for pre-flush and overflush (supplied by the chemical vendor)
- Temporary pumping unit and hoses
- Sample bottles for returns

## Execution notes and close-out remarks
- Work started 2025-11-17 and finished 2025-11-18. The well was restarted after the soak and brought back on gas lift.
- Total downtime recorded: 47 hours.
- No leaks or pressure excursions during pumping. No injuries or spills reported.
- Production after restart (6 days to 2025-11-25): oil 1409 bbl/d, gas 694.9 Mscf/d, water cut 20.3 %, GOR 493 scf/bbl. Rates are slightly below the pre-job baseline, which is within normal variation after a shut-in; Production Engineer to keep monitoring.
- Residual inhibitor monitoring to follow the routine produced-water sampling schedule.
- All equipment demobilised and the location cleaned up. Work order closed.

**Closed by:** Production Engineer
