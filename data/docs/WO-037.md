# Work order: Choke replacement, W-27

**WO number:** WO-037
**Event reference:** EV-0048
**Well:** W-27 (Wadi Field, horizontal, ESP)
**Category:** Maintenance
**Work type:** Choke replacement
**Raised:** 2025-10-29
**Planned start:** 2025-10-31
**Planned finish:** 2025-10-31
**Status:** Closed

## Scope of work
Replace the eroded choke bean on the W-27 wellhead choke. Fit a new bean of the same size, record the choke setting before and after, and return the well to production.

## Safety and permits
- Work permit issued before isolation.
- Wellhead flowline isolated and depressurised before the choke body was opened.
- Lockout applied on the ESP power supply during the job.
- Standard PPE for hydrocarbon service, with gas detector carried by the crew.
- Toolbox talk held at the wellhead before work started.

## Materials
- 1 replacement choke bean
- 1 set of body seals and gaskets
- Torque tools and a leak-test fluid kit

## Execution notes
The work was done on 2025-10-31 and finished the same day. The old bean showed clear erosion on the bore. The new bean and seals were installed, the body was leak tested at line pressure with no leaks found, and the well was restarted. Total downtime was 4 hours.

## Close-out remarks
- Before the job, the 14 days to 2025-10-30 averaged 744.7 bbl/d oil, 817.5 Mscf/d gas, 32 % water cut and GOR 1098 scf/bbl.
- After restart, the 6 days to 2025-11-07 averaged 736.7 bbl/d oil, 808.6 Mscf/d gas, 32.9 % water cut and GOR 1098 scf/bbl.
- Rates and GOR are in line with the pre-job period. Water cut is slightly higher.
- The old bean was tagged and kept for the Production Engineer to review.
- No open items. Work order closed.
