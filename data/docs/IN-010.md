# Incident note: H2S alarm on W-15

**Document id:** IN-010
**Event id:** EV-0027
**Note date:** 2025-08-26
**Author:** HSE Advisor
**Well:** W-15 (Field A, vertical, gas lift)
**Severity:** High
**Status at note date:** Closed

## What happened
On 2025-08-26 the fixed H2S detector near the W-15 wellhead went into alarm. The area was cleared and the well was shut in while the source was investigated.

## Immediate actions
- Personnel mustered at the designated point and the area was cleared.
- The Lead Operator informed the Production Supervisor and the control room.
- The well was isolated and the wellhead area was kept barricaded.
- Entry was made only with personal gas monitors and breathing apparatus, following SOP-HSE-003 H2S Alarm Response.

## Findings
A small leak was found on a needle valve at the wellhead. The valve was repaired and the area was re-tested with portable monitors. Readings were clear before the barricade was removed and the well was returned to service.

## Impact
- Downtime: 5 hours.
- Uptime on the day: 19 hours.
- Production on 2025-08-26: 801.3 bbl oil, 477.5 Mscf gas, water cut 15.4%.
- Prior 7-day average to 2025-08-25: 1104.5 bbl/d oil, 671.5 Mscf/d gas, water cut 14.5%, GOR 608 scf/bbl.
- The day's oil was below the prior average, in line with the lost hours. No related events were recorded in the previous 30 days.

## Status
Closed. The leak is repaired, the well is back on production and no injuries or exposures were reported.

## Next actions
- The Production Engineer will confirm the well returns to its baseline rates over the next few days.
- The Integrity Engineer will review the condition of similar needle valves on neighbouring wellheads.
- The Lead Operator will include the event in the shift handover and keep the detector check on the routine round.

**Applicable procedure:** SOP-HSE-003 H2S Alarm Response
