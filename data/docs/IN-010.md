# Incident note: ESP failure on W-09

**Document ID:** IN-010
**Event ID:** EV-0042
**Note date:** 2025-10-26
**Well:** W-09 (Dune Field, vertical, ESP)
**Severity:** High
**Status at note date:** Closed
**Prepared by:** Production Engineer

## What happened
On 2025-10-21 the W-09 ESP shut down on a ground fault alarm and would not restart. The well was shut in pending workover.

## Immediate actions
- Operators isolated the ESP at the variable speed drive and locked out the supply.
- A megger test was run on the motor circuit. It showed near-zero insulation resistance phase to ground.
- The Production Supervisor was informed and the well was left shut in.

## Findings so far
- Installed pump: Varenta VL-538, serial 7Q-0419-027 (batch 7Q-0419), run life 8 months.
- The megger result is consistent with an electrical failure in the downhole motor or cable. The failed component can only be confirmed when the pump is pulled and inspected.

## Impact
- Production before the event (7 days to 2025-10-20): oil 1461.8 bbl/d, gas 957.5 Mscf/d, water cut 10.1 %, GOR 655 scf/bbl.
- On 2025-10-21 the well produced 0 bbl oil and 0 Mscf gas, with 0 hours uptime.
- Downtime as of this note: 144 hours (2025-10-21 to 2025-10-26).
- Deferred oil is roughly 1461.8 bbl/d for the downtime period, based on the pre-event rate.

## Status at note date
The incident record EV-0042 is closed. The well remains shut in until the replacement of the ESP.

## Next actions
- A workover to replace the ESP is planned to start on 2025-10-27 (EV-0046). It is a planned activity only, and its results are not yet known.
- The Workover Supervisor will confirm rig and ESP vendor readiness.
- The Maintenance Planner will update the deferment log once the well is back on production.

## Procedure reference
No specific procedure applies to this incident.
