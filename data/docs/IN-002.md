# Incident note: H2S alarm on W-11

**Document ID:** IN-002
**Note date:** 2025-06-14
**Event ID:** EV-0005
**Well:** W-11 (Dune Field, horizontal, gas lift)
**Severity:** Low
**Status:** Closed
**Applicable procedure:** SOP-HSE-003 H2S Alarm Response

## What happened
On 2025-06-14 the fixed H2S detector near the W-11 wellhead went into alarm. The alarm was treated as genuine until proven otherwise.

## Immediate actions
- The wellhead area was cleared and access was restricted.
- The Lead Operator informed the Production Supervisor and the HSE Advisor.
- Personnel in breathing apparatus checked the area with portable gas detectors before any work started.
- The well was shut in while the source was investigated.

## Findings
The leak was traced to a needle valve at the wellhead. The valve was repaired and the area was re-tested with portable detectors. Readings were clear, the alarm was reset and the well was returned to service the same day.

## Impact
- Downtime: 7 hours on 2025-06-14 (uptime 17 hours).
- Production on the day: 560.9 bbl oil, 531.8 Mscf gas, water cut 27.5%.
- Baseline for the 7 days to 2025-06-13: 789.7 bbl/d oil, 753.7 Mscf/d gas, water cut 26.9%, GOR 954 scf/bbl.
- The oil shortfall against baseline was about 229 bbl, which is consistent with the lost hours. No related events were recorded in the previous 30 days.

## Status at note date
Closed. The leak is repaired and W-11 is producing.

## Next actions
- Production Engineer to confirm that rates return to baseline over the next few days.
- Integrity Engineer to include the needle valve and neighbouring wellhead fittings in the next routine leak check.
- HSE Advisor to file the alarm record in line with SOP-HSE-003.
