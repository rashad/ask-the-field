# Incident note: Wellhead leak on W-19

**Document ID:** IN-002
**Event ID:** EV-0002
**Note date:** 2025-06-05
**Well:** W-19 (Field B, horizontal, ESP)
**Severity:** Low
**Status at note date:** Closed
**Prepared by:** HSE Advisor

## What happened
A leak was found on the valve stem packing of a wellhead valve on W-19 on 2025-06-05. The leak was small and contained to the wellhead cellar area.

## Immediate actions
- The well was taken offline and the wellhead isolated.
- The area was barricaded and the Production Supervisor was informed.
- Gas readings around the wellhead were checked before the maintenance crew entered.
- The leaked fluid was cleaned up and the affected area inspected.

## Findings
The leak came from worn valve stem packing. The packing was replaced, and the valve was tested and showed no further leakage. No injuries and no environmental release beyond the immediate pad area were reported.

## Impact
- Downtime: 11 hours on 2025-06-05.
- Uptime on the day: 13 hours.
- Production on the day: 846.8 bbl oil, 871 Mscf gas, water cut 27.9%.
- Baseline (7 days to 2025-06-04): 1448.8 bbl/d oil, 1486.4 Mscf/d gas, water cut 30.7%, GOR 1026 scf/bbl.
- Deferred oil is roughly 602 bbl against the baseline rate for the day.

## Status
The incident is closed. W-19 is back on production, and the ESP restarted normally after the repair. No related events were recorded on this well in the previous 30 days.

## Next actions
- The Lead Operator will check the packing on the other wellhead valves during routine rounds.
- The Maintenance Planner will consider a packing inspection at the next scheduled wellhead service.

## Procedure reference
No specific procedure applies to this incident.
