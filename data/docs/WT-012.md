# Well test: W-40, 2026-01-05

**Document id:** WT-012
**Well:** W-40 (Bayda, horizontal, natural flow)
**Test date:** 2026-01-05
**Procedure:** SOP-OPS-004 Well Test Procedure with the Mobile Test Separator

## Test conditions
- Test duration: 12 hours on the mobile test separator
- Choke: 24/64 in
- Flowing wellhead pressure: 440 psi
- No events recorded on the well in the last 45 days

## Measured results
| Parameter | Test 2026-01-05 | Baseline 2025-11-06 |
|---|---|---|
| Oil (bbl/d) | 102.7 | 200.5 |
| Gas (Mscf/d) | 137.8 | 270.2 |
| Water cut (%) | 88.1 | 77.1 |
| GOR (scf/bbl) | 1342 | 1348 |

The test result is based on a 3-day period ending 2026-01-05. The baseline is a 7-day period ending 2025-11-06.

Sand: 13 ppm.

## Observations
- Oil rate is about 49% lower than the baseline (102.7 vs 200.5 bbl/d), and gas fell in similar proportion (137.8 vs 270.2 Mscf/d).
- Water cut rose by 11.0 points, from 77.1% to 88.1%.
- GOR is essentially unchanged (1342 vs 1348 scf/bbl), so the gas decline follows the oil decline.
- Sand production was low and stable during the test, with no separator carry-over noted.

## Recommendation
- The decline is consistent with rising water production rather than a change in gas behavior. The well is currently suspended, and the result does not support a restart at the present choke.
- The Production Engineer should review the result against the water-handling limits at CPF-1 before any decision on the well status.
- Repeat the test per SOP-OPS-004 if the well is returned to service.
