# Work order: Safety valve test, W-02

**WO number:** WO-016
**Event reference:** EV-0023
**Well:** W-02 (Field A, deviated, gas lift)
**Category:** Maintenance
**Priority:** Routine (planned)
**Date raised:** 2025-08-13
**Planned start:** 2025-08-15
**Actual start:** 2025-08-15
**Completion date:** 2025-08-15
**Status:** Closed

## Scope of work
Subsurface safety valve function and leak test on W-02.
1. Shut in the well and isolate the flowline at the wellhead.
2. Close the valve from the control panel and confirm closure.
3. Bleed down above the valve and monitor for pressure build-up.
4. Record the leak rate and compare against the acceptance criterion.
5. Reopen the valve, confirm full function, return the well to production.

## Safety and permits
- Work permit and isolation certificate issued by the Production Supervisor.
- Toolbox talk held before work with the Lead Operator and the slickline/control crew.
- Pressure-test barriers checked, area barricaded, gas detection on.
- HSE Advisor informed of the shut-in.

## Materials
- Hydraulic control line test pump
- Calibrated gauges and test recorder
- Standard consumables (seals, fittings), none used

## Execution and close-out remarks
- Valve closed and opened on command. No sticking observed.
- Leak test passed within the acceptance limit. No repair or replacement required.
- Well shut in for 4 hours in total and restarted the same day.
- Test results filed in the well integrity record.
