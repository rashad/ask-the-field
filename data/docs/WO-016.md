## Scope of work
Replace the eroded choke bean on the W-10 wellhead choke. Isolate the flowline, remove the worn bean, fit a new bean of the same size, pressure test the choke body and return the well to production.

## Safety and permits
- Hot work not required; cold work permit and isolation certificate issued.
- Lockout of the choke manifold valves, line depressurised and verified at zero before breaking containment.
- Standard PPE plus a gas detector at the wellhead during the job.
- No applicable procedure is referenced for this task; the job followed the routine isolation practice for the asset.

## Materials
- 1 x replacement choke bean
- 1 x seal and gasket set for the choke body
- Torque wrench and leak test fluid

## Execution notes and close-out remarks
- W-10 was shut in for the job and the work was completed on 2025-08-23. Total downtime was 6 hours.
- The removed bean showed clear erosion on the bore. The choke body seat was inspected and found serviceable.
- The new bean was fitted, the seals were replaced and the choke body passed a leak check at operating pressure. No leaks were seen at restart.
- The well was returned to production and the Lead Operator confirmed stable flow after startup.
