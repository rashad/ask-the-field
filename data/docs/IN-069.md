# Incident note: Flowline leak on W-36

**Document ID:** IN-069
**Event ID:** EV-0183
**Note date:** 2026-11-18
**Well:** W-36 (Bayda, horizontal, natural flow)
**Severity:** High
**Status at note date:** Open
**Applicable procedure:** SOP-HSE-006 Flowline Leak Response

## What happened
During a routine patrol on 2026-11-18, the Lead Operator found a pinhole leak on the W-36 flowline near the manifold. The leak was a small, steady weep with no spray.

## Immediate actions
- W-36 was shut in.
- The affected section was isolated and depressurised.
- The area was barricaded and signed, and the HSE Advisor was informed.
- Spill containment was placed under the leak point and the small volume of released fluid was cleaned up.

## Findings so far
- The leak is at a single pinhole on the flowline pipe body close to the manifold tie-in.
- The cause is not yet confirmed. The Integrity Engineer will review external and internal corrosion and erosion as possible contributors, given the sand-prone nature of Bayda.
- No related events on this well in the last 30 days.

## Impact
- Downtime as of the note date: 24 hours.
- Production on 2026-11-18: 0 bbl oil, 0 Mscf gas, uptime 0 hours.
- The 7-day average before the event (ending 2026-11-17) was 1832.5 bbl/d oil, 2506.7 Mscf/d gas, 13.1 % water cut and GOR 1368 scf/bbl. Deferred oil is therefore building at roughly that oil rate each day while the well stays shut in.

## Status
Open. The well remains shut in and the area stays isolated. A clamp repair is pending.

## Next actions
1. Maintenance Planner to schedule the clamp repair and raise the work order.
2. Integrity Engineer to perform thickness checks on the flowline around the leak and along the adjacent sections.
3. HSE Advisor to confirm the permit to work and gas testing requirements before the repair starts.
4. Following the repair, carry out a pressure test and a leak check before restart.
5. Production Engineer to plan a controlled restart and a follow-up well test to confirm rates against the baseline.

Response steps follow SOP-HSE-006 Flowline Leak Response. This note will be updated when the repair is completed and the incident is closed.
