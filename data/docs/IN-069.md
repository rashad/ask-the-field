# Incident note: Flowline leak on W-36

**Document ID:** IN-069
**Event ID:** EV-0183
**Note date:** 2026-11-18
**Author:** Production Engineer
**Well:** W-36 (Salt Flat Field, deviated, ESP)
**Severity:** High
**Status at note date:** Open

## What happened
During the routine patrol on 2026-11-18, the operator found a pinhole leak on the W-36 flowline near the manifold. The leak was reported to the control room immediately.

## Immediate actions
- W-36 was shut in and the ESP stopped.
- The area around the leak was isolated and barricaded, and access was restricted.
- The Lead Operator and HSE Advisor were informed.
- The leaking section was depressurised and checked for residual hydrocarbons before any further inspection.

## Findings so far
- The leak is a single pinhole on the flowline section close to the manifold.
- No spread of fluid beyond the isolated area has been reported.
- The cause of the pinhole has not yet been determined. Wall thickness readings around the leak point are to be taken by the Integrity Engineer.

## Impact
- Downtime as of this note: 24 hours.
- Production on 2026-11-18 was 0 bbl oil and 0 Mscf gas, with 0 hours of uptime.
- Before the event, W-36 averaged 1,165.4 bbl/d oil and 1,333.1 Mscf/d gas over the 7 days to 2026-11-17, with 27% water cut and a GOR of 1,144 scf/bbl. This is the rate being deferred while the well is shut in.
- No related events on this well in the last 30 days.

## Status
Open. The well remains shut in and the area stays isolated. A clamp repair is pending.

## Next actions
1. Maintenance Planner to raise the work order and schedule the clamp repair.
2. Integrity Engineer to measure wall thickness on the adjacent flowline section and assess the need for further repair.
3. HSE Advisor to confirm permits and gas testing before repair work starts.
4. After the repair, carry out a pressure test and a controlled restart of W-36, with the ESP started under Production Engineer supervision.
5. Update this note when the repair is complete and the well is back online.

## Procedure
The response follows SOP-HSE-006 Flowline Leak Response.
