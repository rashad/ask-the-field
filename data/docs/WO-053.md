# Work order: Choke bean-down for sand control, W-34

**WO number:** WO-053
**Event reference:** EV-0147
**Well:** W-34 (Salt Flat Field, vertical, gas lift)
**Category:** Maintenance
**Priority:** Medium
**Raised:** 2026-08-10
**Planned start / end:** 2026-08-12 / 2026-08-12
**Actual start / end:** 2026-08-12 / 2026-08-12
**Status:** Closed

## Scope of work
Reduce the choke from 48/64 to 32/64 after sand was detected in the separator. The aim is to restrict the rate so the well stays below the sand-free drawdown limit. Work follows SOP-OPS-002 Sand Management and Sand Production Alarms.

## Safety and permits
- Routine work permit issued for choke change on the wellhead.
- Toolbox talk held with the Lead Operator and the operator crew before isolation.
- Line depressurised and isolation confirmed before the bean was changed.
- HSE Advisor informed of the sand-related restriction.

## Materials
- 32/64 choke bean
- Replacement gasket set
- Standard hand tools

## Execution notes
The well was shut in, the choke changed to 32/64 and the well returned to production on 2026-08-12. Total downtime was 3 hours. Wellhead connections were leak-checked on restart with no leaks found.

## Production context
| Period | Days | Oil (bbl/d) | Gas (Mscf/d) | Water cut (%) | GOR (scf/bbl) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Before (to 2026-08-11) | 14 | 1526.2 | 2549.7 | 15 | 1671 |
| After restart (to 2026-08-19) | 6 | 930.7 | 1533.1 | 14.2 | 1647 |

The rate is lower after the bean-down, which is the expected effect of the restriction.

## Close-out remarks
Work completed as planned on 2026-08-12 and the work order is closed. The well is producing on the restricted choke. The Production Engineer will keep monitoring separator sand readings under SOP-OPS-002 before any choke increase is considered.
