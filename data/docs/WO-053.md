# Work order: Choke bean-down for sand control, W-34

**WO number:** WO-053
**Event reference:** EV-0152
**Well:** W-34 (Bayda, horizontal, gas lift)
**Category:** Maintenance
**Priority:** Medium
**Raised:** 2026-08-10
**Planned start:** 2026-08-12
**Actual start / completion:** 2026-08-12 / 2026-08-12
**Status:** Closed

## Scope of work

Reduce the choke on W-34 from 48/64 to 32/64 after sand was detected in the separator. Hold the rate below the sand-free drawdown limit. Confirm sand returns have dropped after the change and record stabilised rates.

## Safety and permits

- Routine work permit issued for wellhead choke manifold access.
- Applicable procedure: SOP-OPS-002 Sand Management and Sand Production Alarms.
- Pressure isolation checked before the choke bean was changed.
- Sand-prone service: erosion check of the downstream spool carried out by the Lead Operator.

## Materials

- 32/64 choke bean and seal kit from field stores.
- Sand detector log sheet.

## Execution notes

- Well shut in briefly for the bean change. Total downtime: 3 hours.
- Choke set to 32/64 and well returned to production on 2026-08-12.
- Production Engineer informed of the new setting for the next surveillance cycle.

## Close-out remarks

- Seven days before the work (to 2026-08-11): oil 1236.2 bbl/d, gas 2264.7 Mscf/d, water cut 30.3 percent, GOR 1832 scf/bbl.
- Six days after restart (to 2026-08-19): oil 742.9 bbl/d, gas 1314.4 Mscf/d, water cut 29.9 percent, GOR 1769 scf/bbl.
- The lower rate is the expected cost of staying inside the sand-free drawdown limit. Water cut was essentially unchanged.
- No related events in the previous 30 days.
- Work completed as planned. Work order closed. The Production Engineer will review the choke setting at the next well test.
