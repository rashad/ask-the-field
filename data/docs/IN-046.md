# Incident note: ESP trip on W-08

**Document ID:** IN-046
**Note date:** 2026-05-26
**Event ID:** EV-0120
**Well:** W-08 (Dune Field, vertical, ESP)
**Severity:** Low
**Status at note date:** Closed
**Prepared by:** Production Engineer

## What happened
On 2026-05-26 the ESP on W-08 tripped on underload. The well was down for the full day, with 0 hours of uptime recorded and no oil or gas produced.

## Immediate actions
- The Lead Operator confirmed the trip at the local panel and isolated the well in line with the applicable procedure.
- Motor current, wellhead pressure and the drive fault log were checked before any restart attempt.
- The Production Supervisor was informed of the trip.
- After the checks showed no electrical or surface fault, the pump was restarted.

## Findings so far
- The trip was an underload shutdown, and the checks found no damage or abnormal readings on the surface equipment.
- The well returned to service after the restart and is producing as of today.
- No related events were logged on this well in the previous 30 days.

## Impact
- Downtime: 24 hours as of the note date.
- Rates in the 7 days to 2026-05-25 were 1159.8 bbl/d oil, 874.7 Mscf/d gas, water cut 26.6 percent and GOR 754 scf/bbl.
- Deferred oil is therefore about 1160 bbl, based on the pre-trip average, for the day of the trip.

## Status
Closed. The cause was recorded as underload, and the well was restarted after checks.

## Next actions
- The Field Engineer will review the first days of post-restart rates and motor current against the pre-trip baseline.
- The Lead Operator will report any further underload alarms on W-08 to the Production Engineer.

## Procedure reference
SOP-OPS-001 ESP Trip Response and Restart.
