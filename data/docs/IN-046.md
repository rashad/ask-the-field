# Incident note: High separator level trip on W-41

**Document id:** IN-046
**Note date:** 2026-05-19
**Event id:** EV-0116
**Well:** W-41 (Bayda, vertical, gas lift)
**Severity:** high
**Status at note date:** closed
**Prepared by:** Production Engineer

## What happened
On 2026-05-19 the test separator high-level trip shut in W-41. The well was returned to service the same day after the level controller was recalibrated.

## Immediate actions
- Lead Operator confirmed the trip on the CPF-1 panel and kept the well shut in while the separator level was brought down.
- Instrument technician checked the level controller and found it out of calibration, then recalibrated it.
- Level response was verified against the local gauge before restart.
- Production Supervisor was informed of the shut-in and the restart.

## Findings
The trip was caused by a level controller reading that had drifted out of calibration. No damage to the separator or the downstream lines was reported. After recalibration the controller tracked the local gauge correctly.

## Impact
- Downtime: 3 hours on 2026-05-19 (uptime 21 hours).
- Production on the day: 928.2 bbl oil, 1087.3 Mscf gas, water cut 24.6%.
- Baseline for the 7 days to 2026-05-18: 1037.9 bbl/d oil, 1276.2 Mscf/d gas, water cut 25.3%, GOR 1230 scf/bbl.
- Oil shortfall against baseline was about 110 bbl for the day. This matches the lost hours and is not a sign of any change in well performance.

## Status
Closed. W-41 is producing normally and no restrictions remain in place.

## Next actions
- Add the level controller to the next routine instrument calibration round so the drift can be tracked.
- The subsurface safety valve function and leak test on W-41 (EV-0127) is planned for 2026-06-16 and is not affected by this event.

## Procedure reference
No specific procedure applies to this event.
