# Work order: Scale squeeze treatment, W-04

**WO number:** WO-029
**Event reference:** EV-0045
**Well:** W-04 (Field A, deviated, ESP)
**Category:** Maintenance
**Priority:** Routine (preventive)
**Raised:** 2025-10-20
**Planned start:** 2025-10-22
**Planned finish:** 2025-10-23
**Status:** Closed

## Scope of work
Preventive scale inhibitor squeeze on W-04. Pump the inhibitor package and overflush per SOP-OPS-008 Scale Inhibitor Squeeze Treatment, shut in the well for the soak period, then restart and confirm stable ESP operation.

## Safety and permits
- Hot work not required. Cold work permit and pressure-related work permit issued.
- Toolbox talk held before chemical handling. Chemical-resistant PPE and eyewash station in place at the pumping spread.
- Surface lines pressure tested before pumping. Spill containment placed under the pump unit and chemical tanks.

## Materials
- Scale inhibitor concentrate and overflush fluid, supplied by the chemical vendor.
- Temporary pumping unit with hoses and fittings.
- Spare gaskets and ring joints for the wellhead connections.

## Execution notes
- Work started 2025-10-22 and finished 2025-10-23.
- Total well downtime was 31 hours, covering shut-in, pumping, soak and restart.
- Treatment completed as planned with no leaks or safety observations.
- Related history: choke replacement on W-04 on 2025-09-26 (EV-0034, 7 hours downtime). No link to this job.

## Close-out remarks
Production before the job (7 days to 2025-10-21): oil 1419.8 bbl/d, gas 847.1 Mscf/d, water cut 13.7%, GOR 597 scf/bbl.

Production after restart (6 days to 2025-10-30): oil 1390.7 bbl/d, gas 809.9 Mscf/d, water cut 14%, GOR 582 scf/bbl.

Rates are close to the pre-job level, with a small decline in oil of about 29 bbl/d. Water cut is essentially unchanged. The Production Engineer will keep monitoring ESP parameters and water chemistry to judge treatment effectiveness. Work order closed.
