# Work order: Scale squeeze treatment, W-16

**WO number:** WO-029
**Event reference:** EV-0037
**Well:** W-16 (Dune Field, horizontal, ESP)
**Category:** Maintenance, preventive
**Priority:** Routine
**Date raised:** 2025-10-02
**Planned/actual start:** 2025-10-04
**Completion date:** 2025-10-05
**Status:** Closed
**Applicable procedure:** SOP-OPS-008 Scale Inhibitor Squeeze Treatment

## Scope of work
Preventive scale inhibitor squeeze on W-16. Well shut in for the treatment, inhibitor pumped and displaced per SOP-OPS-008, then shut-in soak and controlled restart of the ESP.

## Safety and permits
- Hot work not required; work permit and pumping unit job safety analysis completed before start.
- Pressure test of surface lines carried out before pumping.
- HSE Advisor briefed the crew on chemical handling and spill containment.

## Materials
- Scale inhibitor and displacement fluid as per the treatment programme.
- Chemical pumping unit and associated hoses.

## Baseline before work
Average over 14 days to 2025-10-03: oil 1062.8 bbl/d, gas 865.3 Mscf/d, water cut 13.8%, GOR 814 scf/bbl.

## Execution notes and close-out remarks
- Treatment started 2025-10-04 and finished 2025-10-05.
- Total downtime 25 hours, including shut-in soak and restart.
- Well restarted without issues and was handed back to the Lead Operator.
- Average over 6 days to 2025-10-12: oil 1067.8 bbl/d, gas 876.9 Mscf/d, water cut 13.9%, GOR 821 scf/bbl. Rates are in line with the pre-job baseline.
- No deviations from the procedure recorded.
- A field power dip affecting W-16 is planned or expected for 2025-10-22 (EV-0043); the Production Engineer will check the restart afterwards.

**Close-out:** Work complete, WO closed. Well producing.
