# Work order: Flowline pigging, W-38

**WO number:** WO-055
**Event reference:** EV-0168
**Well:** W-38 (Salt Flat Field, deviated, gas lift)
**Category:** Maintenance
**Priority:** Routine
**Raised:** 2026-09-27
**Planned start:** 2026-09-29
**Actual start / completion:** 2026-09-29 / 2026-09-29
**Status:** Closed

## Scope of work
Flowline pigging to remove wax and sand deposits between the W-38 wellsite and CPF-1. Launch the pig at the wellsite launcher, receive it at the CPF-1 receiver, and record the volume and type of debris recovered.

## Safety and permits
- Hot work not required. Permit to work and isolation certificate issued for the launcher and receiver.
- Flowline depressurised and gas freed before opening the launcher door.
- HSE Advisor briefed the crew on pig handling, trapped pressure and handling of waxy, sand-laden debris.
- Receiver area barricaded during pig arrival.

## Materials
- Foam-body cleaning pig and spare pig
- Launcher and receiver door seals
- Debris containment drums

## Execution notes
- Well shut in at start of job on 2026-09-29 for the pigging window.
- Pig launched and received at CPF-1 without sticking. Wax and sand debris recovered in the receiver and drummed for disposal.
- Launcher and receiver closed, leak tested and returned to service.
- Well restarted and flowline lined up to CPF-1 with no leaks observed.

## Close-out remarks
- Total downtime: 10 hours.
- Average rates for the 14 days to 2026-09-28: oil 631.9 bbl/d, gas 909.5 Mscf/d, water cut 19.7%, GOR 1439 scf/bbl.
- Average rates for the 6 days after restart, to 2026-10-06: oil 611.2 bbl/d, gas 897 Mscf/d, water cut 20.3%, GOR 1468 scf/bbl.
- Rates are slightly below the pre-job average. Production Engineer to keep monitoring the well; no further action raised on this work order.
- Work order closed by Lead Operator and acknowledged by Maintenance Planner.
