# Work order: Wellhead maintenance, W-29

**WO number:** WO-055
**Event reference:** EV-0167
**Well:** W-29 (Field B, vertical, ESP)
**Category:** Maintenance, wellhead
**Priority:** Routine
**Date raised:** 2026-09-30
**Planned start:** 2026-10-02
**Actual start:** 2026-10-02
**Completion date:** 2026-10-02
**Status:** Closed

## Scope of work
- Grease all wellhead valves (master, wing and swab) at the designated grease points.
- Function test each wellhead valve through its full stroke and check for smooth operation.
- Check the wellhead for leaks and verify gauge readings.

## Safety and permits
- Work permit issued by the Production Supervisor before the well was shut in.
- Well isolated and pressure checked at the wellhead before work began.
- Gas detection in use, area barricaded, standard PPE required.
- Toolbox talk held with the maintenance crew at the wellhead.

## Materials
- Valve grease (high-temperature grade), one cartridge set.
- Grease gun, torque wrench, leak detection fluid.
- No spares consumed.

## Execution notes
The well was shut in on 2026-10-02 for the job. All valves were greased and function tested, and each moved through full stroke without sticking. No leaks were seen at the flanges or stem packings during the leak check. The well was returned to service the same day. Total downtime was 2 hours.

## Close-out remarks
- No defects found and no follow-up work required.
- Production, 7 days to 2026-10-01 (before): oil 641.4 bbl/d, gas 408.6 Mscf/d, water cut 48.5 %, GOR 637 scf/bbl.
- Production, 6 days to 2026-10-09 (after restart): oil 626.8 bbl/d, gas 414.4 Mscf/d, water cut 48.7 %, GOR 661 scf/bbl.
- Rates are broadly consistent with the pre-job baseline. The Lead Operator informed of completion and the permit closed out.
