# Incident note: High separator level trip on W-14

**Document ID:** IN-028
**Event ID:** EV-0084
**Note date:** 2026-02-13
**Author:** Production Engineer
**Well:** W-14 (Dune Field, deviated, ESP)
**Severity:** Low
**Status:** Closed

## What happened
On 2026-02-13 the test separator high-level trip shut in W-14. The well was down for 6 hours before it was returned to service. Uptime for the day was 18 hours.

## Immediate actions
- The Lead Operator confirmed the trip on the control panel and isolated the well from the test separator line.
- Field Engineer inspected the level controller and found the reading out of calibration.
- The level controller was recalibrated and checked against the sight glass at several levels.
- The well was restarted after the separator level returned to normal and the readings were stable.

## Findings so far
The trip came from the level controller calibration. No ESP fault, no wellhead damage and no release of fluids were reported.

## Impact
- Downtime: 6 hours.
- Production on 2026-02-13: oil 704 bbl, gas 633.9 Mscf, water cut 18.5% (18 hours uptime).
- Previous 7 days to 2026-02-12: oil 915.6 bbl/d, gas 833.7 Mscf/d, water cut 19.4%, GOR 911 scf/bbl.
- Oil was below the previous 7-day average because of the shut-in. Water cut and GOR showed no abnormal change.

## Status at note date
Closed. W-14 is producing normally.

## Next actions
- Operators to compare the level controller reading against the sight glass at each shift check for the next several days.
- Maintenance Planner to add the controller to the next instrument calibration round.
- A subsurface safety valve function and leak test on W-14 is planned from 2026-02-22 (EV-0088). It is not yet done.

## Procedure reference
No specific procedure applies to this event.
