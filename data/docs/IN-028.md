# Incident note: Power outage on W-12

**Document ID:** IN-028
**Event ID:** EV-0074
**Note date:** 2026-01-19
**Well:** W-12 (Field A, vertical, natural flow)
**Severity:** Low
**Status at note date:** Closed

## What happened
A field power dip on 2026-01-19 tripped W-12. The well was restarted once power was restored and returned to normal operation the same day.

## Immediate actions
- The Lead Operator confirmed the trip was caused by the power dip and not by a well or wellhead fault.
- Wellhead pressures and the flowline were checked before restart, with no abnormal readings.
- The well was restarted and the choke setting was left unchanged.
- The Production Supervisor was informed.

## Findings
- The trip was caused by the field power dip alone.
- No equipment damage was found.
- No HSE impact and no release.

## Impact
- Downtime: 9 hours (uptime on the day was 15 hours).
- Production on the day: 459.7 bbl of oil and 216.8 Mscf of gas, water cut 17.7 percent.
- Baseline for the 7 days to 2026-01-18: 764.2 bbl/d oil, 377.7 Mscf/d gas, water cut 19.8 percent, GOR 494 scf/bbl.
- Deferred oil is roughly 300 bbl against the baseline rate, which is consistent with the lost hours.

## Status
Closed. The well was back on stream after power was restored and there is no outstanding work. No related events were recorded on this well in the previous 30 days.

## Next actions
- The Production Engineer will check W-12 rates over the next few days to confirm they return to the pre-trip baseline.
- The Maintenance Planner will pass the power dip to the utilities team so they can review supply stability.

## Procedure reference
No specific procedure applies to this event.
