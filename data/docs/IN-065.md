# Incident note: Wellhead leak on W-35

**Document ID:** IN-065
**Event ID:** EV-0176
**Note date:** 2026-10-15
**Author:** Production Engineer
**Well:** W-35 (Salt Flat Field, vertical, natural flow)
**Severity:** High
**Status at note date:** Closed

## What happened
On 2026-10-15 a leak was found on the wellhead valve stem packing of W-35. The Lead Operator spotted it during the routine round and reported it to the Production Supervisor.

## Immediate actions
- The well was shut in and the wellhead area was isolated and barricaded.
- The HSE Advisor checked the area for gas and confirmed no personnel exposure.
- Pressure was bled off and the valve was isolated before any work started.
- Maintenance replaced the stem packing under a permit to work.

## Findings
The leak came from the valve stem packing only. No other wellhead components showed a leak. The packing was replaced and the valve was leak tested at working pressure with no leakage observed. The well was returned to production the same day.

## Impact
- Downtime: 14 hours as of the note date (uptime 10 hours on the day).
- Production on 2026-10-15: 215.9 bbl oil, 270.6 Mscf gas, water cut 19.8%.
- Baseline for the 7 days to 2026-10-14: 536.5 bbl/d oil, 692.3 Mscf/d gas, water cut 20.3%, GOR 1290 scf/bbl.
- Deferred oil is estimated from the gap between the baseline rate and the oil produced on the day, roughly 320 bbl.

## Status
Closed. The well is flowing normally and no related events were recorded in the previous 30 days.

## Next actions
- The Field Engineer will check the stem packing on this valve during the next scheduled wellhead inspection.
- The Maintenance Planner will add the valve to the packing inspection list.
- No specific procedure applies to this event, so general wellhead leak response practice was followed.
