# Incident note: H2S alarm on W-19

**Document ID:** IN-065
**Note date:** 2026-10-21
**Event ID:** EV-0177
**Well:** W-19 (Field B, horizontal, ESP)
**Severity:** medium
**Status at note date:** closed

## What happened
On 2026-10-21 the fixed H2S detector near the W-19 wellhead went into alarm. The area was cleared and the well was shut down while the source was checked.

## Immediate actions
- Personnel moved to the upwind muster point and the Lead Operator confirmed headcount.
- Access to the wellhead area was barred until readings were clear.
- The well was taken offline and the response followed SOP-HSE-003 H2S Alarm Response.
- The Production Supervisor and HSE Advisor were informed.

## Findings
A small leak was found on a needle valve at the wellhead. The valve was repaired, the area was re-tested with portable gas detectors and readings returned to normal before re-entry was allowed.

## Impact
- Downtime: 3 hours.
- Uptime on the day: 21 hours.
- Production on 2026-10-21: oil 727.5 bbl, gas 682 Mscf, water cut 47.6%.
- Baseline for the previous 7 days (to 2026-10-20): oil 806.8 bbl/d, gas 773.2 Mscf/d, water cut 46.2%, GOR 958 scf/bbl.
- The lower oil and gas on the day are consistent with the 3 hours of downtime.

## Status
The incident is closed. The leak is fixed, the detector is clear and W-19 is back on production.

## Next actions
- Wellhead valve greasing and function test (EV-0178) is planned from 2026-10-22.
- The Production Engineer will watch W-19 rates and water cut over the next few days against the baseline.
- The HSE Advisor will include this event in the weekly safety review.

**Applicable procedure:** SOP-HSE-003 H2S Alarm Response
