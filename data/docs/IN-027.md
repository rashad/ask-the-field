# Incident note: H2S alarm on W-09

**Document ID:** IN-027
**Note date:** 2026-02-13
**Event ID:** EV-0083
**Author:** Production Engineer
**Well:** W-09 (Dune Field, vertical, ESP)
**Severity:** Medium
**Status at note date:** Closed

## What happened
On 2026-02-13 the fixed H2S detector near the W-09 wellhead went into alarm. The alarm was treated as genuine and the response followed SOP-HSE-003 H2S Alarm Response.

## Immediate actions
- The area around the wellhead was cleared and access was barred.
- The well was shut in and the Lead Operator informed the Production Supervisor and the HSE Advisor.
- Personnel with breathing apparatus and portable gas monitors approached the wellhead to trace the source.

## Findings
A leak was found on a needle valve at the wellhead. The valve was repaired. Portable monitor readings in the area were checked afterwards and the fixed detector returned to normal. No injuries were reported.

## Impact
- Downtime: 6 hours on 2026-02-13.
- Uptime on the day: 18 hours.
- Production on the day: oil 1068.9 bbl, gas 727.3 Mscf, water cut 11.3%.
- Baseline for the 7 days to 2026-02-12: oil 1365.3 bbl/d, gas 884.5 Mscf/d, water cut 12.2%, GOR 648 scf/bbl.
- Oil on the day was about 296 bbl below the baseline daily rate, consistent with the shut-in period.

## Status
The leak is repaired, the well is back on production and the incident is closed. No related events were logged on this well in the previous 30 days.

## Next actions
1. The Production Engineer will confirm the needle valve repair is recorded in the maintenance system.
2. The HSE Advisor will file the alarm record and review the response against SOP-HSE-003.
3. The Lead Operator will include the wellhead valve in the next routine leak check round.

**Reference:** SOP-HSE-003 H2S Alarm Response
