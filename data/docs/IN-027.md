# Incident note: Wellhead leak on W-18

**Document ID:** IN-027
**Event ID:** EV-0073
**Note date:** 2026-01-16
**Well:** W-18 (Field B, horizontal, ESP)
**Category:** Incident
**Severity:** Medium
**Status at note date:** Closed
**Author:** Production Engineer

## What happened
On 2026-01-16 a leak was found on the wellhead valve stem packing of W-18. The well was taken off line so the leak could be isolated and repaired.

## Immediate actions
- The well was shut in and the wellhead area isolated and barricaded.
- The Lead Operator informed the Production Supervisor and the HSE Advisor.
- The area was checked for hydrocarbon vapour before work started, and the spill was contained and cleaned up.
- Maintenance replaced the valve stem packing under a work permit.
- A pressure and leak check was done after the repair, then the well was returned to service.

## Findings so far
The leak came from worn stem packing on the wellhead valve. No injuries occurred. Other wellhead valves were checked visually and showed no leaks.

## Impact
- Downtime on 2026-01-16: 10 hours (uptime 14 hours).
- Production on the day: 846.7 bbl oil, 806.1 Mscf gas, water cut 36.4%.
- Baseline for the 7 days to 2026-01-15: 1408.8 bbl/d oil, 1313.6 Mscf/d gas, water cut 38.9%, GOR 932 scf/bbl.
- Deferred oil is roughly 560 bbl against the baseline rate. The day's production is consistent with the 14 hours of uptime.

## Status
Closed. W-18 is producing. The incident started and ended on 2026-01-16. No related events were recorded in the previous 30 days.

## Next actions
- Add the W-18 wellhead valves to the next routine round for a packing and leak check.
- Production Engineer to confirm that rates return to baseline over the next few days.
- No specific procedure applies to this incident type, so the repair followed the standard work permit practice.
