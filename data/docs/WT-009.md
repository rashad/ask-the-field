# Well test: W-08, 2025-12-21

**Document ID:** WT-009
**Well:** W-08 (Dune Field, vertical, ESP)
**Test date:** 2025-12-21
**Procedure:** SOP-OPS-004 Well Test Procedure with the Mobile Test Separator

## Test conditions
- Test duration: 8 hours on the mobile test separator
- Choke: 36/64 in
- Flowing wellhead pressure: 453 psi
- Well status: producing, stable throughout the test. No events in the last 45 days.

## Measured results
Rates are averaged over the 3-day period ending 2025-12-21.

| Parameter | Result |
|---|---|
| Oil | 1288.2 bbl/d |
| Gas | 979.7 Mscf/d |
| Water cut | 24.6 % |
| GOR | 761 scf/bbl |
| Sand | 3 ppm |

## Comparison with previous baseline
Baseline period ended 2025-10-22 (7 days):

| Parameter | Baseline | This test |
|---|---|---|
| Oil (bbl/d) | 1286.3 | 1288.2 |
| Gas (Mscf/d) | 925.6 | 979.7 |
| Water cut (%) | 24.9 | 24.6 |
| GOR (scf/bbl) | 720 | 761 |

Oil rate and water cut are essentially unchanged. Gas rate and GOR are slightly higher than the baseline, but still moderate for this well.

## Observations
- Separator conditions were steady and the readings were consistent through the test.
- Sand production was negligible at 3 ppm.
- No abnormal ESP behaviour was noted by the Lead Operator during the test.

## Recommendation
- Keep W-08 on its current choke and operating settings.
- Track the GOR at the next scheduled test to confirm whether the small rise continues.
- Update the well test record with these results.
