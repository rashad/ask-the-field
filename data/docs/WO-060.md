# Work order: Gas lift valve change-out, W-38

**WO number:** WO-060
**Event reference:** EV-0185
**Well:** W-38 (Salt Flat Field, deviated, gas lift)
**Category:** Maintenance
**Priority:** Medium
**Date raised:** 2026-11-12
**Planned start:** 2026-12-03
**Planned completion:** Not yet set (open)
**Status:** Planned, not started. Downtime to date: 0 hours.

## Scope of work

Slickline change-out of the stuck unloading valve in the W-38 gas lift completion. The scope is to:

1. Rig up the slickline unit and pressure test the lubricator.
2. Retrieve the stuck unloading valve and record its condition.
3. Run and set the replacement valve, then confirm it is seated.
4. Return the well to gas lift and monitor injection response.

Troubleshooting before and after the job follows SOP-OPS-007 Gas Lift Troubleshooting.

## Baseline before work

Four-day average to 2026-12-02 (to be confirmed at handover): oil 612.3 bbl/d, gas 896.5 Mscf/d, water cut 20.6 %, GOR 1464 scf/bbl. The well is currently producing and will stay on production until the job starts.

## Safety and permits

- Hot work-free job, but a work permit and a job safety analysis are required.
- Wellhead pressure check and barrier verification before rig-up.
- Gas lift injection isolated and bled down safely before the lubricator is opened.
- HSE Advisor to review the sand and high-GOR conditions typical of Salt Flat Field before the job.
- Exclusion zone set around the wellhead and lubricator.

## Materials

- Replacement unloading valve (to be confirmed against the completion schematic)
- Slickline unit and standard toolstring
- Spare seals and latch

## Execution notes

Not yet executed. The Production Engineer will update this section with valve condition, run depth, injection rates after restart and the final downtime. Work order stays open until the close-out is entered.
