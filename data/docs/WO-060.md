# Work order: Gas lift valve change-out, W-38

**WO number:** WO-060
**Event reference:** EV-0185
**Well:** W-38 (Bayda, deviated, gas lift)
**Category:** Maintenance
**Priority:** Medium
**Date raised:** 2026-11-12
**Planned start:** 2026-12-03
**Planned completion:** To be confirmed after job
**Status:** Open, planned (work not started)

## Scope of work

Slickline change-out of the stuck unloading valve in W-38. The well is currently producing, and downtime to date is 0 hours.

1. Rig up the slickline unit and pressure test the lubricator.
2. Run in and latch the stuck unloading valve.
3. Retrieve the valve and inspect it, then record the condition and depth.
4. Run and set the replacement valve.
5. Confirm the valve is seated, then rig down.
6. Return the well to gas lift and monitor injection response.

## Safety and permits

- Hot work is not expected. A work permit and a lifting plan for the slickline unit are required.
- Hold a toolbox talk before rig-up with the slickline crew and the Lead Operator.
- Because Bayda is sand-prone, check for sand fill and plan for a possible tag before running tools.
- Isolate and depressurize per site practice, and verify the barriers.
- HSE Advisor to review the job safety analysis before start.

## Materials

- Replacement gas lift valve, with its latch and test certificate
- Slickline toolstring, including a pulling tool and a gauge cutter
- Replacement seals and lubricator O-rings

## Reference

Apply SOP-OPS-007 Gas Lift Troubleshooting for injection checks before and after the change-out.
