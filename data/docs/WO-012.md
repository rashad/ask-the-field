# Work order: Choke replacement, W-24

**WO number:** WO-012
**Related event:** EV-0018
**Well:** W-24 (Field B, vertical, ESP)
**Category:** Maintenance
**Priority:** Routine
**Raised:** 2025-07-22
**Planned start:** 2025-07-24
**Planned finish:** 2025-07-24
**Actual start / finish:** 2025-07-24 / 2025-07-24
**Status:** Closed

## Scope of work
Replace the eroded choke bean on the W-24 wellhead choke. Isolate the flowline, remove the old bean, inspect the choke body and seat, fit a new bean of the same size, pressure test and return the well to production.

## Safety and permits
- Hot work not required; cold work permit issued.
- Isolation and lockout of the wellhead choke and flowline valves confirmed by the Lead Operator.
- Pressure bled to zero and verified before breaking containment.
- Standard gas detection and PPE for the wellhead area. HSE Advisor informed.

## Materials
- 1 x replacement choke bean (same trim as removed)
- 1 x seal and gasket set for the choke bonnet
- Torque tools and leak test fluid

## Execution notes and close-out remarks
- The old bean showed clear erosion of the bore, consistent with the reason for the job.
- Choke body and seat inspected, no further damage found. Bonnet reassembled with new seals and leak tested with no leaks.
- Well restarted the same day. Total downtime was 7 hours.
- Production before the job (7 days to 2025-07-23): oil 757.7 bbl/d, gas 372.3 Mscf/d, water cut 22.7%, GOR 491 scf/bbl.
- Production after restart (6 days to 2025-07-31): oil 723.8 bbl/d, gas 351.3 Mscf/d, water cut 23.1%, GOR 485 scf/bbl.
- Oil is lower by about 34 bbl/d after restart. Production Engineer to keep W-24 on watch in the routine well review.
- No applicable procedure is referenced for this work. No related events within 30 days.

**Work order closed.** Well W-24 is producing.
