## Scope of work
Routine inspection of ESP surface equipment and check of VSD parameters.
1. Visual inspection of the wellhead power cable entry, junction box and surface cable terminations.
2. Inspection of the VSD cabinet, cooling fans and filters.
3. Check of VSD parameters (frequency setpoint, current limits, under/overload trip settings) against the approved settings sheet.
4. Record motor current, voltage and wellhead readings before shutdown and after restart.

## Safety and permits
- Electrical isolation and lock-out/tag-out applied on the VSD before cabinet work.
- Work permit issued by the Lead Operator; toolbox talk held with the Field Engineer and electrician.
- Well shut down and restarted by operations under the standard start-up checklist.

## Materials
- Replacement cabinet filter on hand (used if required).
- Calibrated multimeter and thermal camera.

## Execution notes and close-out remarks
- Work carried out on 2025-08-02 and completed the same day. Well downtime was 5 hours.
- Surface equipment found in good condition. VSD parameters checked and left as per the settings sheet, with no changes needed.
- Well restarted without problems and returned to normal operation.
- Production for the 14 days to 2025-08-01 averaged 665.1 bbl/d oil, 388.5 Mscf/d gas, 21 % water cut, GOR 584 scf/bbl. For the 6 days to 2025-08-09 after restart: 663.8 bbl/d oil, 394.3 Mscf/d gas, 21.5 % water cut, GOR 594 scf/bbl. No significant change.
- No follow-up actions raised. Work order closed.
