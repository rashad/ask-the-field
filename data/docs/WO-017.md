# Work order: Wellhead maintenance, W-06

**WO number:** WO-017
**Event reference:** EV-0026
**Well:** W-06 (Field A, deviated, natural flow)
**Work type:** Wellhead maintenance (maintenance)
**Priority:** Routine
**Raised:** 2025-08-23
**Planned start:** 2025-08-25
**Actual start:** 2025-08-25
**Completion date:** 2025-08-25
**Status:** Closed

## Scope of work
- Grease all wellhead valves (master, wing and swab) at the designated fittings.
- Function test each valve through its full travel and record the operating condition.
- Check for leaks at the flanges and valve stems after greasing.

## Safety and permits
- Work permit issued for the well pad and reviewed at the toolbox talk.
- Well shut in and isolated for the duration of the job, with the flowline pressure bled off before work started.
- Gas detector carried by the crew; the HSE Advisor was informed of the isolation.

## Materials
- Valve grease (approved wellhead grade) and a grease gun
- Cleaning rags
- Spare stem packing, on standby only (not used)

## Execution notes and close-out remarks
The crew completed the greasing and function test in one day on 2025-08-25. All valves moved freely through full travel and no leaks were seen at the flanges or stems. No defects were raised and no follow-up work is required. The well was returned to production after the test. Total downtime was 5 hours.
