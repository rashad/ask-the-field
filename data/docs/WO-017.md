# Work order: Choke replacement, W-07

**WO number:** WO-017
**Event reference:** EV-0021
**Well:** W-07 (Dune Field, vertical, ESP)
**Category:** Maintenance
**Priority:** Routine
**Raised:** 2025-08-22
**Planned start:** 2025-08-24
**Planned finish:** 2025-08-24
**Status:** Closed

## Scope of work
- Shut in W-07 at the wellhead and isolate the flowline.
- Remove the eroded choke bean from the choke body.
- Inspect the choke seat and body for erosion damage.
- Install a new choke bean and reassemble.
- Pressure test the choke body, then restart the well and confirm stable flow.

## Safety and permits
- Cold work permit issued for the wellhead area.
- Isolation and lock-out of the flowline confirmed by the Lead Operator before breaking containment.
- Pressure bled to zero and verified before the choke bonnet was opened.
- Toolbox talk held with the maintenance crew. No incidents.

## Materials
- 1 x replacement choke bean
- 1 x bonnet gasket set
- Standard hand tools and torque wrench

## Execution notes and close-out remarks
Work started and finished on 2025-08-24. The eroded choke bean was replaced. The seat showed no damage requiring further repair. The well was returned to service after a total downtime of 5 hours. The well is currently producing.

**Production comparison**
- Before (14 days to 2025-08-23): oil 733.5 bbl/d, gas 798.4 Mscf/d, water cut 26 %, GOR 1088 scf/bbl.
- After restart (6 days to 2025-08-31): oil 741.2 bbl/d, gas 808.1 Mscf/d, water cut 26.6 %, GOR 1090 scf/bbl.

Rates are in line with the pre-work baseline. No follow-up actions are required and no related events were recorded within 30 days. Work order closed by the Production Engineer.
