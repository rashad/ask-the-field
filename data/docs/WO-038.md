## Scope of work
Subsurface safety valve function and leak test on W-23. Shut in the well, close the valve, check it holds against the downstream bleed-off, reopen, and record results. Return the well to production after the test.

## Safety and permits
- Work permit and isolation certificate issued before the well was shut in.
- Toolbox talk held with the operator crew and the slickline unit crew.
- Wellhead area barricaded during pressure testing. Gas detection in service.
- No applicable procedure is referenced for this order; the job followed the standard valve test checklist.

## Materials
- Test pump and calibrated gauges.
- Standard seal kit held on site as contingency (not used).

## Execution notes
- Well shut in and the ESP stopped in a controlled manner on 2025-11-17.
- Valve function test completed, and the leak test held within the acceptance criteria.
- Well restarted and stabilised the same day.
- Total downtime: 6 hours.

- Test passed with no defects found and no repairs required.
