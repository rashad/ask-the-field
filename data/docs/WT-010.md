# Well test: W-40, 2026-01-05

**Document:** WT-010
**Field:** Salt Flat Field
**Well:** W-40 (vertical, gas lift)
**Test date:** 2026-01-05
**Procedure:** SOP-OPS-004 Well Test Procedure with the Mobile Test Separator

## Test conditions

- Test duration: 24 hours on the mobile test separator
- Choke: 48/64 in
- Flowing wellhead pressure: 430 psi
- Gas lift system lined up and stable during the test
- No events recorded on the well in the last 45 days

## Measured results

Measurement period ended 2026-01-05 (3 days).

| Parameter | Current test | Previous baseline (period end 2025-11-06, 7 days) |
|---|---|---|
| Oil (bbl/d) | 84.6 | 177.4 |
| Gas (Mscf/d) | 132.8 | 278 |
| Water cut (%) | 89.2 | 78.4 |
| GOR (scf/bbl) | 1570 | 1567 |

Sand: 19 ppm at the sand probe.

## Observations

- Oil rate is down by about 92.8 bbl/d against the baseline, roughly half of the earlier rate.
- Water cut has risen by 10.8 points.
- Gas rate has fallen in step with oil, and GOR is essentially unchanged (1570 against 1567 scf/bbl), so the decline comes from lower liquid oil rather than a change in gas behaviour.
- Sand production is low and does not limit the choke setting.
- Separator readings were steady through the 24-hour period.

## Recommendation

- Record this test as the new baseline for W-40.
- Review the well's economics at the current oil rate and water cut, given the current status of the well (suspended).
- Repeat the test with the same choke and lift conditions before any decision on the well's future status.
