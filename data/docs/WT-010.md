The previous baseline (7-day period ending 2025-10-16) gave 1115 bbl/d oil, 1150.4 Mscf/d gas, 16.5 % water cut and GOR 1032 scf/bbl.

- Oil: up 18.5 bbl/d (about 1.7 %)

Changes are small and within normal test scatter. The slight rise in water cut should be watched.

## Observations

- Separator conditions were stable through the test.
- Sand at 1 ppm is negligible, with no erosion concern.
- No abnormal ESP behaviour was noted by the operator during the test.

## Recommendation

- Accept the test as the new baseline for W-14.
- Keep the choke at 36/64 in.
- Repeat the test at the next scheduled interval and track water cut trend.
