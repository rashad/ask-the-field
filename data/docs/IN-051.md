# Incident note: Flowline leak on W-39

**Document ID:** IN-051
**Note date:** 2026-07-10
**Event ID:** EV-0139
**Well:** W-39 (Bayda, horizontal, ESP)
**Severity:** Low
**Status at note date:** Closed
**Prepared by:** Production Engineer

## What happened
On 2026-07-09 a small leak was found on a flowline flange at W-39. The well was producing normally before the event. The leak was contained to the flange area, with no spread beyond the immediate pad surface.

## Immediate actions
- The well was shut in and the flowline isolated.
- The area was barricaded and the Lead Operator informed the Production Supervisor and the HSE Advisor.
- Residual pressure was bled off and the line confirmed isolated before work started.
- Spilled fluid was cleaned up and the affected pad surface checked.

## Findings
The leak came from the flange joint. The flange was repaired and the line returned to service on 2026-07-10. No injuries and no other equipment damage were reported. A check of the adjacent flanges found no further weeping.

## Impact
- Downtime as of the note date: 32 hours.
- On 2026-07-09 the well made 246.9 bbl of oil and 413.2 Mscf of gas, at 14.7% water cut, with 8 hours of uptime.
- Production over the 7 days to 2026-07-08 averaged 700.4 bbl/d of oil, 1154 Mscf/d of gas, 17.2% water cut and a GOR of 1648 scf/bbl.
- Deferred oil is roughly the gap between the pre-event rate and the rate actually produced on the day of the leak, plus the further downtime on 2026-07-10.

## Status
Closed. W-39 is producing. No related events were recorded in the preceding 30 days.

## Next actions
- Production Engineer to confirm the well is back at its pre-event rate over the next few days.
- Integrity Engineer to add the repaired flange to the routine flange inspection round.
- Operators to keep a visual check on the repaired joint during rounds.

## Applicable procedure
SOP-HSE-006 Flowline Leak Response.
