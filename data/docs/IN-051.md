# Incident note: H2S alarm on W-09

**Document ID:** IN-051
**Event ID:** EV-0129
**Note date:** 2026-06-23
**Author:** HSE Advisor
**Well:** W-09 (Dune Field, vertical, ESP)
**Severity:** Medium
**Status:** Closed

## What happened
On 2026-06-23 the fixed H2S detector near the W-09 wellhead went into alarm. The alarm was treated as genuine until proven otherwise.

## Immediate actions
- The wellhead area was cleared and access was restricted.
- The Lead Operator and Production Supervisor were informed.
- The well was shut in as a precaution, in line with SOP-HSE-003 H2S Alarm Response.
- Trained personnel wearing breathing apparatus and a portable gas detector checked the area before anyone re-entered.

## Findings
The leak was traced to a needle valve at the wellhead. The valve was repaired, and a follow-up check with the portable detector showed no H2S at the wellhead. The fixed detector was reset and functioned normally afterwards.

## Impact
- Downtime: 5 hours (uptime on the day was 19 hours).
- Production on 2026-06-23: 1043.4 bbl oil, 687 Mscf gas, water cut 13.3%.
- Seven-day average to 2026-06-22: 1325.5 bbl/d oil, 853.9 Mscf/d gas, water cut 13.6%, GOR 644 scf/bbl.
- Oil lost against that average is about 282 bbl for the day.

## Status
Closed as of 2026-06-23. The well is back on production with no restrictions.

## Next actions
- The Production Engineer will watch W-09 rates over the coming days to confirm they return to the pre-event level.
- A routine ESP surface equipment inspection and VSD parameter check (EV-0136) is planned from 2026-07-18.
- Operators should keep to the normal H2S checks during rounds.

**Reference procedure:** SOP-HSE-003 H2S Alarm Response
