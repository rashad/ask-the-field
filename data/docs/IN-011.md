# Incident note: H2S alarm on W-34

**Document ID:** IN-011
**Note date:** 2025-09-06
**Event ID:** EV-0030
**Well:** W-34 (Bayda, horizontal, gas lift)
**Category / severity:** Incident / medium
**Status at note date:** Closed
**Applicable procedure:** SOP-HSE-003 H2S Alarm Response

## What happened
On 2025-09-06 the fixed H2S detector near the W-34 wellhead went into alarm. The area was cleared and the well was taken offline while the source was investigated.

## Immediate actions
- Personnel mustered at the upstream assembly point and a headcount was completed.
- The Lead Operator informed the Production Supervisor and the CPF-1 control room.
- The area was kept isolated until portable gas monitors confirmed safe readings.
- The well was shut in and the wellhead area was inspected by trained personnel wearing breathing apparatus.

## Findings
The leak was traced to a needle valve at the wellhead. The valve was repaired and a leak check was done before restart. Follow-up readings with portable monitors showed no H2S at the wellhead, and the fixed detector returned to normal.

## Impact
- Downtime: 6 hours on 2025-09-06.
- Production on the day: 1,118.4 bbl oil, 2,161 Mscf gas, water cut 27.2 percent, uptime 18 hours.
- Baseline for the previous 7 days (to 2025-09-05): 1,486.5 bbl/d oil, 2,819.4 Mscf/d gas, water cut 24.5 percent, GOR 1,897 scf/bbl.
- Deferred oil against baseline is about 368 bbl for the day.
- No injuries and no related events in the previous 30 days.

## Status
Closed. W-34 is back on production and is currently producing.

## Next actions
- Production Engineer to review W-34 production over the next few days to confirm that rates return to baseline.
- Integrity Engineer to check similar needle valves on neighbouring Bayda wellheads at the next routine round.
- HSE Advisor to review the alarm response at the next toolbox talk, in line with SOP-HSE-003.
