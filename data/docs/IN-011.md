# Incident note: Power outage on W-16

**Document ID:** IN-011
**Event ID:** EV-0043
**Note date:** 2025-10-22
**Well:** W-16 (Dune Field, horizontal, ESP)
**Severity:** medium
**Status:** closed

## What happened
A field power dip on 2025-10-22 tripped the W-16 ESP. The well was restarted once power was restored and returned to production the same day.

## Immediate actions
- Operator confirmed the trip on the CPF-1 control panel and checked that the field supply had stabilised.
- Restart was done after power was restored, with motor current and wellhead pressure watched through the start-up.
- Production Supervisor was informed of the trip and the restart.

## Findings
- The cause was the external field power dip, not a fault on the well.
- No abnormal readings were noted on the ESP after restart.
- Water cut on the day was 13.5%, against a 7-day baseline of 13% to 2025-10-21.

## Impact
- Downtime: 11 hours (uptime on the day was 13 hours).
- Production on 2025-10-22: 557.9 bbl of oil and 443 Mscf of gas.
- Baseline before the event (7 days to 2025-10-21): 1072.4 bbl/d oil, 880.9 Mscf/d gas, GOR 821 scf/bbl.
- Deferred oil against baseline is roughly 515 bbl for the day.

## Related events
EV-0037, a preventive scale inhibitor squeeze on W-16 (2025-10-04 to 2025-10-05, 25 hours downtime), is closed and was completed earlier in the month. The squeeze is not linked to this trip, which was caused by the power dip.

## Status at note date
Closed. W-16 is producing.

## Next actions
- Production Engineer to confirm the rate has returned to the pre-event baseline over the next few days.
- Operations to log the power dip for the field electrical review.
- No applicable procedure is referenced for this event.
