# Work order: Wellhead maintenance, W-22

**WO number:** WO-036
**Event reference:** EV-0053
**Well:** W-22 (Field B, horizontal, ESP)
**Category:** Maintenance
**Priority:** Routine
**Date raised:** 2025-11-14
**Planned start:** 2025-11-16
**Actual start:** 2025-11-16
**Completion date:** 2025-11-16
**Status:** Closed

## Scope of work
- Grease all wellhead valves (master, wing and swab) at the designated grease points.
- Function test each valve through full travel and record the operation.
- Check for external leaks at flanges and the stem packing after testing.

## Safety and permits
- Work permit and isolation certificate issued by the Production Supervisor before the start.
- Well shut in and the wellhead area barricaded for the duration of the work.
- Toolbox talk held with the crew, covering pinch points and pressure checks before valve operation.
- No applicable procedure is referenced for this job. Standard wellhead maintenance practice was followed.

## Materials
- Valve grease (approved grade), grease gun, calibrated torque wrench, leak detection fluid, and a function test record sheet.

## Execution and close-out remarks
- Work was carried out on 2025-11-16 and completed the same day.
- Total downtime was 5 hours.
- All valves greased and cycled through full travel. No sticking or abnormal torque was found.
- No leaks were seen at flanges or stem packing after the function test.
- The well was restarted and returned to normal operation, and the Lead Operator was informed.
- Recent work on this well: ESP surface inspection (EV-0047, 2025-10-22) and scale squeeze treatment (EV-0050, 2025-11-11 to 2025-11-12). Both are closed.
