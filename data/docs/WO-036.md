# Work order: Wellhead maintenance, W-30

**WO number:** WO-036
**Event reference:** EV-0047
**Well:** W-30 (Wadi Field, vertical, ESP)
**Category:** Maintenance, Wellhead maintenance
**Priority:** Routine
**Raised:** 2025-10-25
**Planned start:** 2025-10-27
**Actual start / completion:** 2025-10-27 / 2025-10-27
**Status:** Closed

## Scope of work
- Grease all wellhead valves (master, wing and swab) per the routine greasing schedule.
- Function test each valve through full travel and record the operation.
- Check the wellhead for leaks and visible corrosion after greasing.

## Safety and permits
- Work permit issued for the shutdown and wellhead intervention.
- Well shut in and isolated for the job, with pressure checked at zero on the gauges before valve work.
- Lockout applied at the ESP switchboard.
- Toolbox talk held with the crew before starting.

## Materials
- Valve grease (approved grade) and grease gun.
- Replacement grease fittings (spare, none required).
- Leak detection fluid.

## Execution notes and close-out remarks
The well was shut in and the job completed on 2025-10-27. Total downtime was 3 hours. All valves were greased and function tested through full travel. No leaks were found and no defects were raised. The well was restarted and returned to normal operation, and it remains producing.
