# Incident note: High separator level trip on W-27

**Document id:** IN-064
**Event id:** EV-0175
**Note date:** 2026-10-13
**Well:** W-27 (Field B, vertical, ESP)
**Severity:** high
**Status at note date:** closed

## What happened
On 2026-10-13 the test separator at CPF-1 tripped on high level while W-27 was lined up to it. The trip shut in W-27. The well was down for 6 hours and returned to production the same day.

## Immediate actions
- The Lead Operator confirmed the high-level trip and isolated the well from the test separator.
- The level controller and its transmitter were checked against the local sight glass. The reading was found out of calibration.
- The Field Engineer recalibrated the level controller. The trip setpoints were verified and left unchanged.
- The separator level was brought back to normal, the trip was reset, and W-27 was restarted and monitored through stabilisation.
- The Production Supervisor was informed.

## Findings so far
The trip came from the level controller, which was reading incorrectly. After recalibration the level held steady. No release of fluid or injury was reported.

## Impact
- Downtime: 6 hours (uptime 18 hours on the day).
- Production on 2026-10-13: oil 286.7 bbl, gas 253.5 Mscf, water cut 35.3%.
- Baseline for the 7 days to 2026-10-12: oil 378.1 bbl/d, gas 377.1 Mscf/d, water cut 36.4%, GOR 997 scf/bbl.
- Deferred oil is about 91 bbl against the baseline rate (378.1 minus 286.7). The water cut is in line with the baseline.

## Status
Closed. W-27 is producing normally and no related events were recorded in the previous 30 days.

## Next actions
- The Production Engineer will check the W-27 rates over the next few days against the baseline.
- Maintenance Planner to add the separator level controller to the next routine calibration round.
- No specific procedure applies to this event type, so no procedure reference is cited.
