# Incident note: Flowline leak on W-07

**Document ID:** IN-064
**Event ID:** EV-0175
**Note date:** 2026-10-15
**Author:** Production Engineer
**Well:** W-07 (Dune Field, vertical, ESP)
**Severity:** High
**Status at note date:** Closed

## What happened
A small leak was found on a flowline flange on W-07 on 2026-10-15. The leak was isolated and the flange repaired the same day.

## Immediate actions
- The Lead Operator shut in W-07 and isolated the flowline upstream and downstream of the leaking flange.
- The area was barricaded and the HSE Advisor was informed.
- The released fluid was cleaned up and the area was checked for gas before repair work started.
- The flange was repaired and the line was checked before restart.

## Findings
- The leak was limited to the flange connection.
- No injuries were reported.
- No other flowline leaks were found on the W-07 line during the walk-down.

## Impact
- Downtime: 24 hours as of this note (uptime on 2026-10-15 was 0 hours).
- Production on the day: 0 bbl oil and 0 Mscf gas.
- Deferred oil: about 560 bbl, based on the 7-day average before the event (period ending 2026-10-14): 560.2 bbl/d oil, 619.8 Mscf/d gas, 29.1 percent water cut, GOR 1106 scf/bbl.

## Status
Closed. The flange has been repaired and W-07 is producing today. There are no related events on this well in the previous 30 days.

## Next actions
- The Production Engineer will confirm that the well returns to its previous rates over the next few days.
- The Lead Operator will include the repaired flange in the routine daily walk-down for the next week.
- The HSE Advisor will file the incident record.

## Applicable procedure
SOP-HSE-006 Flowline Leak Response.
