# Work order: Safety valve test, W-09

**WO number:** WO-024
**Event reference:** EV-0030
**Well:** W-09 (Dune Field, vertical, ESP)
**Category:** Maintenance
**Priority:** Routine
**Raised:** 2025-09-10
**Planned start:** 2025-09-12
**Planned completion:** 2025-09-12
**Status:** Closed

## Scope of work
Subsurface safety valve function and leak test on W-09.
1. Shut in the well and isolate it from the flowline to CPF-1.
2. Function test the valve from the control panel.
3. Carry out the leak test and record the result.
4. Return the valve to service and restart the well.

## Safety and permits
- Work permit and isolation certificate issued by the Production Supervisor.
- Toolbox talk held before the job. HSE Advisor reviewed the pressure test limits.
- Wellhead area barricaded during the test. No applicable procedure is linked to this work order.

## Materials
- Hydraulic test pump and calibrated gauges.
- Replacement seals and fittings held on standby (none used).

## Execution notes and close-out
- Work started and finished on 2026-09-12 as planned.

Correction: work started and finished on 2025-09-12 as planned. The well was down for 5 hours in total.
- The valve passed both the function test and the leak test. No defects found and no repairs needed.
- The well was restarted the same day and is producing.
- Production check before the job (14 days to 2025-09-11): oil 1502 bbl/d, gas 975.1 Mscf/d, water cut 9.5 %, GOR 649 scf/bbl.
- Production check after restart (6 days to 2025-09-19): oil 1490.6 bbl/d, gas 959.2 Mscf/d, water cut 9 %, GOR 643 scf/bbl.
- Rates are in line with the pre-job level. No follow-up action raised.

**Closed by:** Production Engineer, with the Lead Operator confirming the restart.
