## Scope of work
Preventive scale inhibitor squeeze on W-29. Shut in the well, pump the pre-flush, inhibitor and overflush stages, hold for the shut-in soak, then restart the ESP and bring the well back to its previous rate. Follow SOP-OPS-008 Scale Inhibitor Squeeze Treatment.

## Safety and permits
- Work permit and pumping permit issued by the Production Supervisor before mobilisation.
- Pre-job safety meeting held with the pumping crew and operations.
- Pressure test of surface lines before pumping, lockout of the ESP drive during the treatment.
- HSE Advisor reviewed chemical handling and spill containment.

## Materials
- Scale inhibitor and the pre-flush and overflush fluids as per program.
- Pumping unit and temporary surface lines.

## Execution and close-out remarks
- Treatment started 2025-09-29 and was completed 2025-09-30, with all stages pumped as programmed.
- Total downtime was 43 hours.
- Production before the job (7 days to 2025-09-28): oil 1005.4 bbl/d, gas 652.9 Mscf/d, water cut 35.9 percent, GOR 649 scf/bbl.
- Production after restart (6 days to 2025-10-07): oil 998.1 bbl/d, gas 662.6 Mscf/d, water cut 35.9 percent, GOR 664 scf/bbl.
- Oil rate is within about 1 percent of the pre-job level and water cut is unchanged. No operational issues recorded during execution.
- Follow-up: the Production Engineer will monitor the well and schedule produced water sampling for inhibitor residual. A field power dip trip on this well is expected as a planned item on 2025-10-18 (EV-0044, low severity); restart after power is restored is anticipated.

Work order closed by the Maintenance Planner.
