# Incident note: Power outage on W-23

**Document id:** IN-004
**Note date:** 2025-06-14
**Event id:** EV-0006
**Well:** W-23 (Field B, vertical, ESP)
**Severity:** low
**Status at note date:** closed
**Prepared by:** Production Engineer

## What happened
A field power dip on 2025-06-14 tripped the ESP on W-23. The well was restarted once power was restored and returned to production the same day.

## Immediate actions
- The Lead Operator confirmed the trip was caused by the supply dip and not by a downhole or surface fault on the well.
- Incoming supply was checked as stable before restart.
- The ESP was restarted and the Lead Operator watched motor current and wellhead pressure through the start-up. Readings were normal.
- The Production Supervisor was informed.

## Findings
- Cause: field power dip, with no sign of equipment damage on W-23.
- Downtime recorded for the outage: 11 hours as of this note.
- The day's uptime was 6 hours.
- The choke replacement (EV-0007) on the same well on the same day was a separate maintenance job. It accounted for 7 hours of downtime. The eroded choke bean was replaced.

## Impact
- Baseline for the 7 days to 2025-06-13: oil 1209.9 bbl/d, gas 671.1 Mscf/d, water cut 25 %, GOR 555 scf/bbl.
- Production on 2025-06-14: oil 320.3 bbl, gas 172.2 Mscf, water cut 23.5 %.
- The shortfall against baseline is about 890 bbl of oil for the day. This is deferred, not lost, and is due to the outage together with the choke job.

## Status
Closed. W-23 is producing and no follow-up is open on this event.

## Next actions
- The Production Engineer will check the W-23 rate and water cut over the next few days against the baseline.
- The Maintenance Planner will pass the power dip to the power supply team for review of the cause.

## Applicable procedure
None is assigned to this event.
