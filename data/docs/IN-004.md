# Incident note: Power outage on W-27

**Document ID:** IN-004
**Note date:** 2025-08-16
**Event ID:** EV-0018
**Well:** W-27 (Wadi Field, horizontal, ESP)
**Severity:** medium
**Status at note date:** closed
**Prepared by:** Production Engineer

## What happened
A field power dip on 2025-08-16 tripped W-27. The ESP stopped on the supply disturbance. The well was restarted once power was restored.

## Immediate actions
- Operations confirmed the trip was caused by the supply dip and not by a downhole or wellhead fault.
- The VSD was checked for fault codes after power returned, then the ESP was restarted.
- The Lead Operator monitored motor current and discharge pressure after the restart until the readings were stable.
- The Production Supervisor was informed.

## Findings
The trip was linked to the field power dip only. No equipment damage was found on the surface equipment. The well returned to normal operation after the restart.

## Impact
- Downtime: 6 hours (uptime on the day: 18 hours).
- Oil produced on 2025-08-16: 574 bbl, against a 7-day average of 788.4 bbl/d to 2025-08-15.
- Gas on the day: 637.2 Mscf, against 834.6 Mscf/d before the event.
- Water cut on the day: 30.5 percent (previous 7-day average 29.8 percent).
- The 7-day average GOR before the event was 1059 scf/bbl.

The shortfall reflects the 6 hours of lost run time. The small water cut change is within normal variation.

## Status at note date
Closed. W-27 is producing.

## Related work
- EV-0016: routine ESP surface inspection and VSD parameter check on 2025-08-10 (4 hours downtime, closed).
- EV-0027: wellhead valve greasing and function test, planned for 2025-09-05.

## Next actions
- Production Engineer to keep watching W-27 rates for the next few days to confirm they return to the pre-event level.
- Maintenance Planner to pass the power dip event to the facility electrical team for a review of supply stability.

## Applicable procedure
No specific procedure applies to this event.
