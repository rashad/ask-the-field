# Incident note: Sand production alarm on W-39

**Document ID:** IN-008
**Event ID:** EV-0024
**Note date:** 2025-08-17
**Author:** Production Engineer
**Well:** W-39 (Bayda, horizontal, ESP)
**Severity:** High
**Status at note date:** Closed

## What happened
On 2025-08-17 the sand monitor on W-39 raised a sand production alarm. The well is in Bayda, which is sand-prone, and it is on ESP lift.

## Immediate actions
- The Lead Operator acknowledged the alarm and reduced the choke in steps until the sand readings returned to normal.
- The field team checked the wellhead and flowline for visible leaks or abnormal vibration. Nothing was found.
- The Production Supervisor was informed.
- The well was brought back online once the readings were stable.

## Findings so far
- The alarm cleared after the choke reduction, and readings stayed normal afterwards.
- Water cut on the day was 14.6%, close to the 7-day baseline of 14.1%, so there is no sign of a water-related change.
- No related events were logged on this well in the previous 30 days.

## Impact
- Downtime: 5 hours (uptime on the day was 19 hours).
- Production on 2025-08-17: oil 663.6 bbl, gas 1007.5 Mscf.
- Baseline for the 7 days to 2025-08-16: oil 895.1 bbl/d, gas 1473.3 Mscf/d, GOR 1646 scf/bbl.
- Oil on the day was about 231.5 bbl below the baseline rate, which reflects the downtime and the choke restriction.

## Status
The event started and ended on 2025-08-17. Sand readings are normal and the incident is closed. W-39 is producing.

## Next actions
- The Production Engineer will review the sand trend on W-39 over the coming days to confirm the choke setting is sustainable.
- Operators will keep recording sand readings at each round.
- Any repeat alarm will be handled the same way and reported as a new event.

## Applicable procedure
SOP-OPS-002 Sand Management and Sand Production Alarms.
