# Incident note: ESP trip on W-03

**Document ID:** IN-008
**Note date:** 2025-09-28
**Event ID:** EV-0035
**Author:** Production Engineer
**Well:** W-03 (Dune Field, horizontal, ESP)
**Severity:** Low
**Status at note date:** Closed

## What happened
On 2025-09-28 the ESP on W-03 tripped on underload. The well was down for 21 hours on the day, with only 3 hours of uptime recorded.

## Immediate actions
- The Lead Operator confirmed the trip on the VSD and logged the underload alarm.
- Wellhead pressures, choke position and flowline valve line-up were checked and found normal.
- Motor and cable insulation readings were checked before the restart and were within acceptable limits.
- The ESP was restarted after the checks and the well returned to production.

## Findings so far
No mechanical or electrical fault was found during the checks. The trip is recorded as an underload event that cleared after the restart. No related events on this well were recorded in the previous 30 days.

## Impact
- Downtime: 21 hours.
- Production on 2025-09-28: 232.8 bbl oil and 225.1 Mscf gas, with water cut at 6.7%.
- Baseline for the 7 days to 2025-09-27: 1953.1 bbl/d oil, 1943.3 Mscf/d gas, water cut 6.9%, GOR 995 scf/bbl.
- Deferred oil is roughly 1,720 bbl against the baseline rate (1953.1 minus 232.8 bbl).

## Status at note date
Closed. W-03 is producing normally and no further restrictions apply. HSE impact: none, no release or injury.

## Next actions
- The Production Engineer will review the drawdown and the VSD current trend over the next few days to confirm stable operation.
- The Night Lead Operator will report any repeat underload alarm at handover.

## Procedure reference
SOP-OPS-001 ESP Trip Response and Restart was followed for the checks and the restart.
