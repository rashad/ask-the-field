# Work order: Safety valve test, W-12

**WO number:** WO-041
**Event reference:** EV-0055
**Well:** W-12 (Dune Field, deviated, ESP)
**Category:** Maintenance
**Priority:** Routine
**Date raised:** 2025-11-24
**Planned start:** 2025-11-26
**Planned finish:** 2025-11-26
**Actual start / finish:** 2025-11-26 / 2025-11-26
**Status:** Closed

## Scope of work
Function and leak test of the subsurface safety valve (SSSV) on W-12. Close the valve, confirm it holds, record the leak-off and reopen. Log the result in the well safety valve register.

## Safety and permits
- Work permit and isolation certificate issued before start.
- Toolbox talk held with the slickline crew and the Lead Operator.
- Wellhead area barricaded, gas detection checked and the CPF-1 control room informed.
- Well shut in under controlled conditions for the test.

## Materials
- Test gauge and pressure recorder
- Hydraulic hand pump for the control line
- Standard wellhead seals (not consumed)

## Execution notes and close-out
- Pre-shutdown baseline (14 days to 2025-11-25): oil 1188.5 bbl/d, gas 1004.9 Mscf/d, water cut 29.5%, GOR 846 scf/bbl.
- The well was shut in for the test and the valve function test was completed on 2025-11-26.
- The leak test was completed on the same day and the valve was reopened.
- Total downtime was 4 hours.
- The well was restarted without issues and no follow-up work is required.
- Post-restart performance (6 days to 2025-12-03): oil 1163.1 bbl/d, gas 980.8 Mscf/d, water cut 28.8%, GOR 843 scf/bbl.
- Rates are slightly below the pre-test baseline. Production Engineer to keep watching the trend in the routine surveillance review.

**Close-out:** Work complete, permits closed and the site left clean. The Production Supervisor was informed. Work order closed.
