# Work order: Scale squeeze treatment, W-23

**WO number:** WO-041
**Event reference:** EV-0063
**Well:** W-23 (Field B, vertical, ESP)
**Category:** Maintenance, preventive
**Priority:** Routine
**Raised:** 2025-12-22
**Planned start:** 2025-12-24
**Completion date:** 2025-12-25
**Status:** Closed
**Applicable procedure:** SOP-OPS-008 Scale Inhibitor Squeeze Treatment

## Scope of work
Preventive scale inhibitor squeeze on W-23. Shut in the well, pump the pre-flush, inhibitor and overflush stages per SOP-OPS-008, hold the shut-in soak period, then restart the ESP and return the well to CPF-1 production.

## Safety and permits
- Work permit and pumping permit issued by the Production Supervisor.
- Pre-job safety meeting held with the pumping crew and operators.
- Pressure test of surface lines completed before pumping.
- Chemical handling controls in place (PPE, spill containment, safety data sheet at the site).

## Materials
- Scale inhibitor and the associated pre-flush and overflush fluids.
- Pumping unit and temporary surface lines supplied by the service vendor.

## Execution and close-out remarks
- Treatment started 2025-12-24 and finished 2025-12-25. Well restarted after the soak period.
- Total downtime was 33 hours.
- Rates for the 7 days to 2025-12-23: oil 1039.3 bbl/d, gas 569.5 Mscf/d, water cut 30%, GOR 548 scf/bbl.
- Rates for the 6 days to 2026-01-01 after restart: oil 1015.4 bbl/d, gas 548.5 Mscf/d, water cut 30%, GOR 540 scf/bbl.
- Water cut was unchanged. Oil is slightly lower than before the job, and the Production Engineer will keep monitoring.
- No leaks or abnormal conditions were reported during the job. Site left clean and equipment demobilised.
- Related item: a fixed H2S detector alarm near the W-23 wellhead (EV-0069) is planned for 2026-01-10. It is a separate matter and not part of this work order.

**Close-out:** Work complete, WO closed. Well W-23 is producing.
