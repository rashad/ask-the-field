# Incident note: H2S alarm on W-34

**Document ID:** IN-001
**Note date:** 2025-06-01
**Event ID:** EV-0001
**Author:** HSE Advisor
**Well:** W-34 (Salt Flat Field, vertical, gas lift)
**Severity:** Low
**Status at note date:** Closed
**Applicable procedure:** SOP-HSE-003 H2S Alarm Response

## What happened
On 2025-06-01 the fixed H2S detector near the W-34 wellhead went into alarm. The alarm was acknowledged at the CPF-1 control room and the response was started under SOP-HSE-003.

## Immediate actions
- The area around the wellhead was cleared and access was barricaded.
- The well was shut in as a precaution while the source was investigated.
- Personnel were accounted for at the muster point. No exposure or injury was reported.
- The Production Supervisor was informed.

## Findings
A portable gas detector survey of the wellhead located a leak on a needle valve. The valve was repaired, and the area was re-checked and confirmed clear before the well was restarted.

## Impact
- Downtime: 7 hours on 2025-06-01 (uptime 17 hours).
- Production on the day: 1,237.6 bbl oil and 1,950.7 Mscf gas, water cut 10 %.
- Reference: the 7-day average to 2025-05-31 was 1,776 bbl/d oil, 2,911.5 Mscf/d gas, water cut 10.6 % and GOR 1,639 scf/bbl.
- Deferred oil is therefore about 538 bbl against that average.

## Status
Closed. The leak is repaired, the well is back on production, and no related events were recorded on W-34 in the previous 30 days.

## Next actions
- The Lead Operator will include the repaired needle valve in the next routine leak check round.
- The HSE Advisor will file the alarm record and review the response against SOP-HSE-003 at the next safety meeting.
