# Incident note: High separator level trip on W-04

**Document ID:** IN-001
**Event ID:** EV-0001
**Note date:** 2025-05-28
**Author:** Production Engineer
**Well:** W-04 (Field A, deviated, ESP)
**Severity:** Medium
**Status at note date:** Closed

## What happened
On 2025-05-28 the test separator at CPF-1 tripped on high level, which shut in W-04. The well was down for 2 hours before it was restarted the same day.

## Immediate actions
- The Lead Operator confirmed the trip was a high-level shutdown and not a downstream restriction.
- The separator level was checked locally against the transmitter reading, and a deviation between the two was found.
- The level controller was recalibrated and its response was checked against a manual dump.
- W-04 was restarted after the level returned to normal, and the ESP was monitored through startup.

## Findings
The trip was caused by the level controller on the test separator reading incorrectly. After recalibration the level control behaved normally. No damage to the ESP or surface equipment was observed.

## Impact
- Downtime: 2 hours (uptime 22 hours on the day).
- Production on 2025-05-28: oil 1,478.2 bbl, gas 920.2 Mscf, water cut 13.5%.
- Baseline for the 5 days to 2025-05-27: oil 1,526.2 bbl/d, gas 882.8 Mscf/d, water cut 11.9%, GOR 578 scf/bbl.
- Oil on the day was about 48 bbl below the baseline rate, consistent with the short shut-in and ramp-up.
- No other W-04 events occurred in the previous 30 days.

## Status
Closed. W-04 is back on production and the controller is performing within tolerance.

## Next actions
- The Production Engineer will review the water cut on the next routine sample, since the day's value of 13.5% is above the 11.9% baseline.
- Maintenance Planner to add the test separator level transmitter to the next calibration round.
- Operators to compare local and remote level readings once per shift for the next week.

## Procedure reference
No specific procedure applies to this event.
