# Incident note: Wellhead leak on W-01

**Document ID:** IN-005
**Note date:** 2025-08-30
**Event ID:** EV-0023
**Well:** W-01 (Dune Field, vertical, ESP)
**Severity:** medium
**Status at note date:** closed
**Author:** Production Engineer

## What happened
A leak was found on the wellhead valve stem packing of W-01 on 2025-08-30. The well was taken offline so the leak could be repaired safely.

## Immediate actions
- The well was shut in and the wellhead area was isolated and barricaded.
- The Lead Operator informed the Production Supervisor and the HSE Advisor.
- The leaked fluid was cleaned up and the area was checked for gas before work started.
- The valve stem packing was replaced.

## Findings
The leak came from worn stem packing on the wellhead valve. No other leak points were found on the wellhead during the post-repair check. The valve was function tested and a leak check at operating pressure showed no seepage.

## Impact
- Downtime: 8 hours (uptime on the day was 16 hours).
- Production on 2025-08-30: 564.1 bbl oil, 356.7 Mscf gas, water cut 27.6 %.
- Baseline for the 7 days to 2025-08-29: 845.9 bbl/d oil, 521.1 Mscf/d gas, water cut 27.9 %, GOR 616 scf/bbl.
- Deferred oil is about 282 bbl against the baseline rate, roughly the output of the 8 lost hours.

## Status
Closed on 2025-08-30. W-01 is back on production, and its water cut and GOR are in line with the pre-event baseline.

## Next actions
- The Lead Operator will keep the wellhead on the routine leak-check round for the next week.
- The Maintenance Planner will add the other wellhead valves on W-01 to the next preventive inspection.
- No specific procedure applies to this event, so none is referenced.
