# Incident note: H2S alarm on W-09

**Document ID:** IN-005
**Note date:** 2025-06-18
**Event ID:** EV-0009
**Well:** W-09 (Field A, vertical, ESP)
**Severity:** Low
**Status at note date:** Closed
**Applicable procedure:** SOP-HSE-003 H2S Alarm Response

## What happened
On 2025-06-18 the fixed H2S detector near the W-09 wellhead went into alarm. The area was cleared in line with the response procedure.

## Immediate actions
- Personnel moved to the muster point and the area was isolated.
- The Production Supervisor and HSE Advisor were informed.
- After the area was declared safe to re-enter, a trained team with portable detectors checked the wellhead and surface piping.

## Findings
- The source was a leak on a needle valve at the wellhead.
- The leak was fixed the same day. Follow-up readings showed no further H2S at the detector, and the alarm was reset.
- The ESP surface equipment inspection and VSD parameter check on 2025-06-14 (3 hours, closed) is the only related event in the last 30 days. No link to the alarm has been identified.

## Impact
- Downtime: 3 hours.
- Uptime on the day was 21 hours. Output was 848.3 bbl oil and 739.3 Mscf gas, with water cut at 21.7%.
- The previous 7 days to 2025-06-17 averaged 963.9 bbl/d oil, 908.1 Mscf/d gas, 22% water cut and a GOR of 942 scf/bbl.
- The shortfall in oil against that average is about 115.6 bbl, mostly due to the lost hours.
- The well is producing today. No injuries or further releases.

## Status
Closed as of 2025-06-18. The leak has been repaired and the area returned to normal operation.

## Next actions
- The Lead Operator to include the repaired needle valve in the next round of wellhead leak checks.
- The HSE Advisor to confirm the alarm response was logged per SOP-HSE-003.
- The Integrity Engineer to review the valve fitting for any repeat issues.
