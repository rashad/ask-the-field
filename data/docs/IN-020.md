# Incident note: High separator level trip on W-37

**Document ID:** IN-020
**Note date:** 2026-01-04
**Event ID:** EV-0071
**Author:** Production Engineer
**Well:** W-37 (Salt Flat Field, horizontal, gas lift)
**Severity:** Low
**Status at note date:** Closed

## What happened
On 2026-01-04 the test separator high-level trip shut in W-37. The well was flowing through the test separator at the time. The trip stopped production until the level was brought back under control.

## Immediate actions
- The Lead Operator confirmed the trip on the CPF-1 control panel and checked that the well had shut in safely.
- Operators inspected the test separator and the level controller. No leak, overpressure or carry-over was observed.
- The level controller was recalibrated and checked against the local gauge glass.
- The well was restarted once the level was stable and the readings agreed.

## Findings so far
The cause was the level controller, which was out of calibration. After recalibration the level held steady. No other equipment fault was found.

## Impact
- Downtime: 4 hours (uptime for the day was 20 hours).
- Production on 2026-01-04: oil 1091.8 bbl, gas 1267.9 Mscf, water cut 20.7%.
- Baseline for the 7 days to 2026-01-03: oil 1273.2 bbl/d, gas 1410.8 Mscf/d, water cut 18.6%, GOR 1108 scf/bbl.
- The lower oil volume for the day is consistent with the 4 hours of downtime. The slightly higher water cut will be watched on the next routine readings.

## Status
Closed on 2026-01-04. W-37 is producing. No related events were recorded for this well in the last 30 days.

## Next actions
- The Lead Operator will check the level controller reading against the gauge glass during the next few shifts.
- The Production Engineer will review the water cut on W-37 at the next scheduled well test.
- No applicable procedure is referenced for this event.
