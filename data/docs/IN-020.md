# Incident note: Power outage on W-11

**Document ID:** IN-020
**Note date:** 2025-12-06
**Event ID:** EV-0057
**Well:** W-11 (Field A, deviated, gas lift)
**Severity:** low
**Status at note date:** closed
**Applicable procedure:** none identified for this event type

## What happened
A field power dip on 2025-12-06 tripped W-11. The well was restarted once power was restored and returned to production the same day.

## Immediate actions
- The Lead Operator confirmed the trip on the control system and checked that the wellhead and gas lift injection line showed no abnormal pressure.
- Power supply to the field was verified as stable before the restart.
- The well was brought back online and monitored through the stabilisation period.
- The Production Supervisor was informed.

## Findings
- The trip was caused by the field power dip. No equipment fault on the well was found.
- No related events on W-11 in the previous 30 days.

## Impact
- Downtime: 10 hours (uptime on the day: 14 hours).
- Production on 2025-12-06: 566.7 bbl oil, 598.2 Mscf gas, water cut 15.6%.
- Baseline for the 7 days to 2025-12-05: 960.4 bbl/d oil, 974.9 Mscf/d gas, water cut 17%, GOR 1015 scf/bbl.
- Oil lost against the baseline rate is about 394 bbl for the day. The water cut on the day was slightly below the baseline and shows no sign of a change in well behaviour.

## Status
Closed. W-11 is producing normally.

## Next actions
- The Production Engineer will check W-11 rates over the next few days to confirm they return to the baseline.
- The Maintenance Planner has been asked to review the cause of the field power dip with the facility electrical team.
- No further action is required on the well.
