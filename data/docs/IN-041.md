# Incident note: ESP trip on W-24

**Document ID:** IN-041
**Note date:** 2026-04-25
**Event ID:** EV-0113
**Well:** W-24 (Wadi Field, deviated, ESP)
**Severity:** medium
**Status at note date:** closed
**Author:** Production Engineer

## What happened
On 2026-04-24 the ESP on W-24 tripped on underload. The well was down until restart on 2026-04-25.

## Immediate actions
- The Lead Operator confirmed the trip alarm at the VSD and kept the well shut in.
- Surface checks were completed: VSD parameters, motor current history, wellhead and flowline pressures, and choke position.
- After the checks, the unit was restarted following SOP-OPS-001 ESP Trip Response and Restart.

## Findings so far
- The trip was an underload trip. The checks found no further fault, and the unit ran normally after restart.
- Recent W-24 events are noted for context only: flowline flange leak (EV-0099, 2026-03-26), routine ESP surface inspection and VSD parameter check (EV-0107, 2026-04-11), and wellhead valve stem packing leak (EV-0109, 2026-04-14). All three are closed.

## Impact
- Total downtime: 25 hours.
- Production on 2026-04-24: 565.7 bbl oil and 311 Mscf gas, water cut 27.1 %, uptime 11.5 hours.
- Baseline for the 7 days to 2026-04-23: 1231 bbl/d oil, 655.8 Mscf/d gas, water cut 27.6 %, GOR 533 scf/bbl.
- Oil lost against baseline on the day: about 665 bbl (1231 minus 565.7).

## Status
Closed. W-24 is producing.

## Next actions
- The Production Engineer will review the VSD trend for the first days after restart.
- The Lead Operator will record any further underload alarms in the shift report.
- Reference procedure: SOP-OPS-001 ESP Trip Response and Restart.
