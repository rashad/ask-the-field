# Incident note: Flowline leak on W-06

**Document id:** IN-041
**Note date:** 2026-04-12
**Event id:** EV-0104
**Well:** W-06 (Field A, deviated, natural flow)
**Severity:** medium
**Status at note date:** closed
**Prepared by:** Production Engineer

## What happened
A small leak was found on a flowline flange on W-06 on 2026-04-12. The well was shut in and the line isolated. The flange was repaired the same day.

## Immediate actions
- Well shut in and flowline isolated and depressurised.
- Area barricaded and the Production Supervisor and HSE Advisor informed.
- Spilled fluid contained and cleaned up, with no reported release beyond the immediate flange area.
- Flange joint remade by the maintenance crew and leak-tested before the line was returned to service.

## Findings
- The leak came from the flowline flange joint only. No other damage was found on the adjacent pipework.
- Visual checks of nearby flanges found no further weeping.
- Detailed cause review of the gasket and bolting is to be recorded in the integrity file.

## Impact
- Downtime: 20 hours on 2026-04-12.
- Uptime on the day: 4 hours.
- Production on the day: 110 bbl of oil and 95.8 Mscf of gas, with water cut at 20.1 percent.
- Baseline for the prior 7 days to 2026-04-11: 693.2 bbl/d oil, 595.8 Mscf/d gas, water cut 17.6 percent, GOR 859 scf/bbl.
- Deferred oil was therefore roughly 580 bbl against baseline.

## Status
Closed. The repair is complete, the line is back in service and W-06 is producing. No related events were recorded in the preceding 30 days.

## Next actions
1. Integrity Engineer to review flange gasket and bolt torque records for similar joints on Field A flowlines.
2. Lead Operator to include the W-06 flange in the next round of walk-down checks.
3. Production Engineer to confirm rates and water cut return to baseline over the following days.
