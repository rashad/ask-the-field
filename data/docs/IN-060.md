# Incident note: Wellhead leak on W-12

**Document id:** IN-060
**Event id:** EV-0162
**Note date:** 2026-09-03
**Author:** Production Engineer
**Well:** W-12 (Field A, vertical, natural flow)
**Severity:** Low
**Status at note date:** Closed

## What happened
On 2026-09-03 a leak was found on the wellhead valve stem packing of W-12. The well was taken offline to make the repair. The leak was contained at the wellhead and did not spread beyond the cellar area.

## Immediate actions
- The Lead Operator shut in the well and isolated the affected valve.
- The area was barricaded and the HSE Advisor was informed.
- The stem packing was replaced and the valve was pressure checked before the well was returned to service.
- No further seepage was seen at the stem after restart.

## Findings so far
The leak came from worn valve stem packing. No damage to the valve body or to other wellhead components was seen during the check. No other wells were affected.

## Impact
- Downtime: 9 hours on 2026-09-03.
- Production on the day: 443.2 bbl of oil and 195.6 Mscf of gas, with water cut at 21.8 percent and uptime of 15 hours.
- Baseline for the 7 days to 2026-09-02: 671.8 bbl/d oil, 328.8 Mscf/d gas, water cut 20.9 percent, GOR 489 scf/bbl.
- Oil lost against baseline on the day was about 228.6 bbl, consistent with the downtime.

## Status at note date
Closed. The packing was replaced, the well was back on production the same day, and the leak is not recurring.

## Next actions
- The Maintenance Planner to add the stem packing condition of the other wellhead valves on W-12 to the next routine inspection round.
- The Lead Operator to include a visual check of the W-12 wellhead in the next shift rounds.

## Applicable procedure
No specific procedure applies to this event.
