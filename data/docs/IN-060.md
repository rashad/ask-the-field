# Incident note: ESP trip on W-03

**Document ID:** IN-060
**Note date:** 2026-09-07
**Event ID:** EV-0163
**Well:** W-03 (Dune Field, horizontal, ESP)
**Severity:** Low
**Status:** Closed

## What happened
On 2026-09-07 the ESP on W-03 tripped on underload. The well was down for 6 hours before it was restarted after checks. Uptime for the day was 18 hours.

## Immediate actions
- The Lead Operator confirmed the trip on the drive and logged the fault code.
- The surface checks followed SOP-OPS-001 ESP Trip Response and Restart: motor current and voltage trends, wellhead pressure, flowline valve positions and choke setting.
- No abnormal readings were found on the surface equipment. The Production Engineer was informed and approved the restart.
- The ESP was restarted and the well was brought back to stable operation.

## Findings so far
- The trip was an underload event. No damage to the drive or cable was observed during the checks.
- After the restart the well returned to normal operating parameters.
- The pump is Varenta VL-538. No further follow-up on the pump is planned.

## Impact
- Downtime: 6 hours.
- Production on 2026-09-07: oil 1218.7 bbl, gas 1193.4 Mscf, water cut 9.7%.
- Previous 7-day average to 2026-09-06: oil 1588.6 bbl/d, gas 1559.9 Mscf/d, water cut 10.2%, GOR 982 scf/bbl.
- The day's oil is about 370 bbl below the previous average, consistent with the lost run time.
- No related events were recorded on this well in the last 30 days.

## Status at note date
Closed. W-03 is producing and no restrictions remain.

## Next actions
- The Lead Operator will keep watching the drive current trend on the next shifts as routine.
- The Production Engineer will file the trip record against the well history.
- If another underload trip occurs, follow SOP-OPS-001 again and escalate for a review of pump performance.

**Procedure reference:** SOP-OPS-001 ESP Trip Response and Restart
