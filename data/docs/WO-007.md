## Scope of work
Flowline pigging from the W-39 wellsite to CPF-1 to remove wax and sand deposits. Launch the pig at the wellsite launcher, monitor the run, and receive it at the CPF-1 receiver. Record the debris volume recovered.

## Safety and permits
- Hot work not required. Cold work permit and line-break permit issued for launcher and receiver operations.
- Pressure bleed-down and isolation verified by the Lead Operator before opening the launcher.
- HSE Advisor briefed the crew on the risk of trapped pressure and handling of pyrophoric scale at the receiver.
- Gas detectors worn at both ends. Radio check between wellsite and CPF-1 before launch.

## Materials
- Standard foam-core cleaning pig
- Replacement launcher door seal
- Debris containment drums and absorbent pads

## Execution notes
- Well W-39 was shut in on 2025-06-29 for the pigging run and isolated from the flowline.
- Pig launched, received at CPF-1 without sticking, and the differential pressure stayed within the expected band.
- Wax and sand debris recovered at the receiver and bagged for disposal.
- Launcher door seal replaced and leak-tested before return to service.

## Close-out remarks
- Total downtime: 9 hours. Well restarted the same day, work completed 2025-06-29.
- Production before (7 days to 2025-06-28): 959 bbl/d oil, 1537.1 Mscf/d gas, 13% water cut, GOR 1603 scf/bbl.
- Production after restart (6 days to 2025-07-06): 943.6 bbl/d oil, 1529.1 Mscf/d gas, 13% water cut, GOR 1620 scf/bbl.
- Water cut unchanged. Oil rate is slightly lower than before, within normal variation. Production Engineer to keep watching the rate trend.
- No related events in the last 30 days. Work order closed.
