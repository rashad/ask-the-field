# Work order: Scale squeeze treatment, W-15

**WO number:** WO-007
**Event reference:** EV-0009
**Well:** W-15 (Dune Field, deviated, ESP)
**Category:** Maintenance, preventive
**Priority:** Routine
**Raised:** 2025-06-26
**Planned start:** 2025-06-28
**Completion date:** 2025-06-29
**Status:** Closed

## Scope of work
Preventive scale inhibitor squeeze on W-15. Shut in the well, bullhead the inhibitor package and overflush, hold for the soak period, then restart and return the ESP to normal operation. Follow SOP-OPS-008 Scale Inhibitor Squeeze Treatment.

## Safety and permits
- Hot work not required. Pumping operation covered by a work permit and a pressure-test permit.
- Pre-job safety meeting held with the pumping crew and the Lead Operator.
- Chemical handling per the material safety data sheet, HSE Advisor to confirm PPE and spill containment.
- Lines pressure tested before pumping.

## Materials
- Scale inhibitor and overflush fluids supplied by the chemical vendor
- Pumping unit and temporary lines
- Spill containment and sampling kit

## Execution notes
Well shut in for the treatment on 2025-06-28. Treatment pumped and soaked as planned, no abnormal pressures recorded during injection. Well restarted and brought back to normal operation on 2025-06-29. Total downtime 47 hours.

Context: flowline pigging on W-15 was completed on 2025-06-18 (EV-0006, 6 hours downtime), so the flowline was clean ahead of this job.
