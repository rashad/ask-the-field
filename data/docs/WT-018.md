# Well test: W-17, 2026-03-23

**Document ID:** WT-018
**Well:** W-17 (Field B, deviated, ESP)
**Test date:** 2026-03-23
**Procedure:** SOP-OPS-004 Well Test Procedure with the Mobile Test Separator

## Test conditions
- Test duration: 12 hours
- Choke: 40/64 in
- Flowing wellhead pressure: 581 psi
- Well returned to production on 2026-03-21 after ESP replacement, so the test covers the third day of flow (3 days of data).

## Measured rates
| Parameter | Result |
|---|---|
| Oil | 1188 bbl/d |
| Gas | 623.9 Mscf/d |
| Water cut | 32.5 % |
| GOR | 525 scf/bbl |
| Sand | 1 ppm |

## Comparison with previous baseline (period ending 2026-01-22, 7 days)
- Oil: 1448.4 to 1188 bbl/d (down 260.4 bbl/d)
- Gas: 771.8 to 623.9 Mscf/d
- Water cut: 28.6 to 32.5 % (up 3.9 points)
- GOR: 533 to 525 scf/bbl, essentially unchanged

## Observations
- The well was down from 2026-03-04 to 2026-03-20 (EV-0090 ESP trip on motor overload, then EV-0093 workover replacing motor, pump and cable). Both events are closed.
- Sand is negligible at 1 ppm.
- Rates are lower and water cut higher than the January baseline. The well is only a few days into flow after the workover, so cleanup and ESP settling may still be affecting the result.
- Stable GOR suggests no change in gas behaviour.

## Recommendation
- Treat this test as an early post-workover result, not a new baseline.
- Repeat the test after the well has stabilised, and monitor ESP current and intake pressure in the meantime.
- Review the drawdown and frequency settings if oil rate stays below baseline on the repeat test.
