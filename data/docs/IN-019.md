# Incident note: High separator level trip on W-26

**Document ID:** IN-019
**Note date:** 2025-12-24
**Event ID:** EV-0068
**Well:** W-26 (Wadi Field, horizontal, gas lift)
**Category / severity:** Incident / medium
**Status at note date:** Closed
**Prepared by:** Production Engineer

## What happened
On 2025-12-24 the test separator high-level trip shut in W-26. The well was down for 5 hours and returned to production the same day.

## Immediate actions
- The Lead Operator confirmed the trip on the CPF-1 control panel and kept the well shut in while the level was checked.
- The level controller on the test separator was inspected and recalibrated.
- The well was restarted after the level returned to normal and held stable. The Production Supervisor was informed.

## Findings so far
The trip was caused by the test separator level controller, which was reading out of calibration. Recalibration cleared the condition. No damage to the well or surface equipment was reported, and no other wells were affected.

## Impact
- Downtime: 5 hours (uptime on the day: 19 hours).
- Production on 2025-12-24: oil 909 bbl, gas 712.6 Mscf, water cut 32.8%.
- Prior 7-day average to 2025-12-23: oil 1136.8 bbl/d, gas 900.9 Mscf/d, water cut 33.2%, GOR 792 scf/bbl.
- Deferred oil for the day is about 228 bbl against the prior average. Water cut is in line with the baseline.

## Status
Closed as of 2025-12-24. W-26 is producing. No related events were logged on this well in the previous 30 days.

## Next actions
- Production Engineer to review rates over the next few days to confirm the well returns to the prior baseline.
- Field Engineer to add the test separator level controller to the next routine instrument check list.

## Applicable procedure
No specific procedure is referenced for this event.
