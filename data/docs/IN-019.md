# Incident note: Flowline leak on W-18

**Document ID:** IN-019
**Event ID:** EV-0056
**Note date:** 2025-12-05
**Author:** Production Engineer
**Well:** W-18 (Field B, horizontal, ESP)
**Severity:** Medium
**Status at note date:** Closed

## What happened
A small leak was found on a flowline flange on W-18 on 2025-12-05. The well was taken off line so the leak could be isolated and repaired.

## Immediate actions
- The well was shut in and the flowline isolated and depressurised.
- The area was barricaded and the Production Supervisor and HSE Advisor were informed.
- Spilled fluid was contained and cleaned up. No injuries were reported.
- The flange was repaired and checked for tightness before the well was returned to service.

## Findings
The leak came from the flowline flange joint and was small. The flange was repaired and is holding. Wider inspection of the flowline is not part of this note.

## Impact
- Downtime: 19 hours. The well ran for only 5 hours on the day.
- Production on 2025-12-05: 320 bbl oil, 297.7 Mscf gas, water cut 37.1%.
- Seven-day average to 2025-12-04: 1511.5 bbl/d oil, 1385.9 Mscf/d gas, water cut 36.6%, GOR 917 scf/bbl.
- Oil deferred against the baseline is roughly 1190 bbl (1511.5 minus 320).
- No related events on this well in the previous 30 days.

## Status
Closed as of 2025-12-05. W-18 is producing.

## Next actions
- Lead Operator to check the repaired flange at each round over the coming days.
- Integrity Engineer to review the flange gasket and bolting records for Field B flowlines.
- Production Engineer to confirm W-18 rates return to baseline.
