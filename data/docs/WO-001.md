# Work order: Flowline pigging, W-03

**WO number:** WO-001

**Category:** Maintenance

## Scope of work
Pig the W-03 flowline to CPF-1 to remove wax and sand deposits. Launch the pig at the wellsite launcher, receive at the CPF-1 receiver, and record the debris volume recovered.

## Safety and permits
- Hot work not required. Cold work permit issued for the launcher and receiver.
- Pressure bleed-down confirmed before opening the launcher and receiver doors.
- Toolbox talk held with the operations crew covering line-of-fire and residue handling.
- Wax and sand residue collected in drums for disposal.

## Materials and equipment
- Foam-bodied cleaning pig, one spare on site
- Launcher and receiver door seals (replaced as a precaution)
- Waste drums and absorbent material

## Execution notes
W-03 was shut in on 2025-06-06 for the pigging run. The pig was launched, received at CPF-1 without sticking, and returned wax and sand debris. The well was restarted the same day. Total downtime was 5 hours.
