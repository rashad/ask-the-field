## Scope of work
Routine ESP surface equipment inspection and VSD parameter check on W-10.
1. Inspect the VSD cabinet, cooling fans, filters and terminations.
2. Inspect the junction box, wellhead power penetrator and surface cable.
3. Record VSD frequency, motor current, voltage and protection settings, and compare against the commissioning sheet.
4. Check the trip and alarm set points and the data logging.
5. Restore the well and confirm stable operation.

## Safety and permits
- Electrical isolation and lock-out/tag-out applied for the inspection.
- Work permit issued by the Production Supervisor.
- The well was shut in for the job. Total downtime: 6 hours.
- Toolbox talk held before the start.

## Materials
- Replacement cabinet filter on hand (not required).
- Contact cleaner and torque tools.

## Close-out remarks
- Inspection completed on 2025-06-02 with no defects found on surface equipment.
- VSD parameters checked and left unchanged.
- The well was restarted and ran normally.
- Production before the job (10 days to 2025-06-01): 853.9 bbl/d oil, 771.2 Mscf/d gas, 9.7% water cut, GOR 903 scf/bbl.
- Production after restart (6 days to 2025-06-09): 842.5 bbl/d oil, 747.6 Mscf/d gas, 9.5% water cut, GOR 887 scf/bbl.
- Rates after restart are close to the pre-job baseline. No follow-up work required.
