# Incident note: High separator level trip on W-28

**Document id:** IN-015
**Note date:** 2025-10-05
**Event id:** EV-0040
**Well:** W-28 (Field B, vertical, ESP)
**Severity:** high
**Status at note date:** closed

## What happened
On 2025-10-05 the test separator high-level trip activated and shut in W-28. The well was down for 4 hours before being returned to service the same day.

## Immediate actions
- The Lead Operator confirmed the trip on the CPF-1 control panel and isolated the well from the test separator line.
- The field team checked the separator level visually against the gauge glass and found a mismatch with the transmitter reading.
- The Production Engineer was informed and the level controller was recalibrated.
- The well was restarted after the level reading was verified against the gauge glass. ESP motor current and discharge pressure were observed after restart and were stable.

## Findings
The cause was a level controller reading out of calibration, which gave a false high-level signal. No fluid carry-over was reported and no release occurred. No injuries or environmental impact were recorded.

## Impact
- Downtime: 4 hours (uptime 20 hours on the day).
- Production on 2025-10-05: 1271.3 bbl oil and 1141.8 Mscf gas, water cut 31.6%.
- Baseline for the 7 days to 2025-10-04: 1450.8 bbl/d oil, 1410.9 Mscf/d gas, water cut 32%, GOR 972 scf/bbl.
- Oil shortfall against baseline for the day: about 179.5 bbl.

## Status
Closed. The well is producing and no other events were recorded on W-28 in the previous 30 days.

## Next actions
- Instrument technician to add the transmitter to the next routine calibration round to confirm the setting holds.
- Lead Operator to log level readings against the gauge glass on each shift for the coming week.

## Procedure reference
No specific procedure applies to this event.
