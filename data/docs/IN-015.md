# Incident note: Wellhead leak on W-13

**Document ID:** IN-015
**Event ID:** EV-0057
**Note date:** 2025-12-01
**Author:** Production Engineer
**Well:** W-13 (Dune Field, horizontal, gas lift)
**Severity:** Low
**Status at note date:** Closed

## What happened
On 2025-12-01 a leak was found on the stem packing of a wellhead valve on W-13. The leak was small and contained at the wellhead. No injuries and no release beyond the wellhead cellar area were reported.

## Immediate actions
- The well was shut in and the wellhead area isolated and barricaded.
- The Lead Operator informed the Production Supervisor and the HSE Advisor.
- The valve was depressurised and the stem packing was replaced.
- After the repair the valve was leak tested at the wellhead and the well was returned to production the same day.

## Findings
The leak came from worn valve stem packing. No damage to the valve body or to other wellhead components was found. No related events were recorded on this well in the previous 30 days.

## Impact
- Downtime: 8 hours (uptime on the day was 16 hours).
- Production on 2025-12-01: 691.6 bbl of oil and 412.6 Mscf of gas, water cut 15.5%.
- Baseline for the 7 days to 2025-11-30: 991 bbl/d oil, 585.8 Mscf/d gas, water cut 14.8%, GOR 591 scf/bbl.
- Oil deferred against baseline: about 299 bbl for the day.

## Status
The repair is complete and the well is producing. The incident is closed.

## Next actions
- Operators to check the repaired valve for seepage during routine rounds over the next week.
- Maintenance Planner to include the remaining wellhead valves of the same type in the next routine packing inspection.

## Procedure reference
No specific procedure applies to this incident.
