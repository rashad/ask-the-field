# Work order: Workover - ESP replacement, W-17

**WO number:** WO-048
**Event reference:** EV-0093
**Well:** W-17 (Field B, deviated, ESP)
**Category:** Maintenance
**Priority:** High
**Raised:** 2026-03-09
**Planned start:** 2026-03-11
**Actual completion:** 2026-03-20
**Status:** Closed

## Scope of work
- Mobilize the workover rig to W-17.
- Pull the completion and inspect the ESP string.
- Replace ESP motor, pump and cable.
- Run the new completion and return the well to production.

Background: EV-0090 (ESP trip, 2026-03-04 to 2026-03-10) recorded motor overload with phase current imbalance. The megger test showed low insulation resistance on the motor lead, and the well was shut in pending workover.

## Safety and permits
- Work performed under SOP-HSE-005 Permit to Work for Workover and Rig Operations.
- Well control equipment checked before pulling the completion.
- Pre-job safety meeting held with rig crew and the ESP vendor.

## Materials
- ESP motor, pump and power cable (new).
- Motor lead extension and associated connectors.

## Execution notes and close-out remarks
- Rig mobilized and work started 2026-03-11.
- Completion pulled. Inspection found a burnt motor lead extension and scaled pump stages.
- Motor, pump and cable replaced as per scope.
- Work finished 2026-03-20. Well returned to production on 2026-03-21.
- Total downtime recorded: 240 hours.
- Production for the 6 days to 2026-03-27 after restart: oil 1254.3 bbl/d, gas 657.4 Mscf/d, water cut 31 %, GOR 524 scf/bbl.
- Current status of W-17: producing.
- Recommendation: the Production Engineer to review scale control for Field B ESP wells given the scaled pump stages found.

**Closed by:** Workover Supervisor
