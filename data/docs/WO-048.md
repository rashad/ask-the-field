## Scope of work
Replace the failed ESP on W-17 following the ESP trip on 2026-03-04 (EV-0092: motor overload, phase current imbalance, low insulation resistance on megger test). Mobilize workover rig, pull the existing completion, replace pump, motor and cable, run back and restore production.

## Safety and permits
- Permit to Work issued under SOP-HSE-005 Permit to Work for Workover and Rig Operations.
- Well killed and barriers verified before pulling the string.
- Pre-job safety meeting held with rig crew, ESP vendor and Lead Operator.

## Materials
- Varenta VL-538 pump, serial 2M-1187-044 (batch 2M-1187)
- New ESP power cable
- Standard consumables and cable clamps

## Execution notes and close-out remarks
- Workover rig mobilized and work started 2026-03-10.
- Pulled ESP serial 7Q-0419-112 (batch 7Q-0419), which had run 13 months.
- Teardown found motor winding insulation breakdown and light scale on the pump stages.
- Installed new Varenta VL-538, serial 2M-1187-044 (batch 2M-1187), with new cable.
- Work completed 2026-03-17. Well returned to production on 2026-03-18.
- Total downtime recorded: 192 hours.
