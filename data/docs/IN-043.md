# Incident note: Wellhead leak on W-41

**Document ID:** IN-043
**Event ID:** EV-0116
**Note date:** 2026-05-09
**Well:** W-41 (Salt Flat Field, vertical, natural flow)
**Severity:** High
**Status at note date:** Closed
**Prepared by:** Production Engineer

## What happened
On 2026-05-09 a leak was found on the wellhead valve stem packing of W-41. The leak was on the valve stem packing only. The well was shut in to make the repair.

## Immediate actions
- Well shut in and the wellhead area isolated and barricaded.
- Area checked for hydrocarbon presence before work started.
- Lead Operator and Production Supervisor informed.
- HSE Advisor attended the site during the repair.

## Findings
The leak came from worn stem packing on the wellhead valve. The packing was replaced and the valve was leak-tested before the well was returned to service. No further leakage was seen on the stem after restart.

## Impact
- Downtime on 2026-05-09: 9 hours (uptime 15 hours).
- Production on the day: 970.3 bbl oil, 1492.1 Mscf gas, water cut 20.1%.
- Previous 7-day average to 2026-05-08: 1622.4 bbl/d oil, 2451.8 Mscf/d gas, water cut 18.1%, GOR 1511 scf/bbl.
- Deferred oil on the day is roughly 650 bbl against the previous 7-day average.

## Status
Closed as of 2026-05-09. W-41 is back on production and no restrictions are in place.

## Next actions
- Operators to check the W-41 wellhead valve stems for seepage on each round for the next week.
- Maintenance Planner to review the packing condition of the other valves on this wellhead at the next planned visit.
- A flowline flange leak on W-41 (EV-0117) is planned for 2026-05-12 and is tracked separately.

## Procedure reference
No specific procedure applies to this incident.
