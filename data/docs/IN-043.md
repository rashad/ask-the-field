# Incident note: Sand production alarm on W-03

**Note ID:** IN-043
**Note date:** 2026-04-18
**Event ID:** EV-0109
**Author:** Production Engineer
**Well:** W-03 (Field A, horizontal, ESP)
**Severity:** Medium
**Status at note date:** Closed

## What happened
On 2026-04-18 the sand monitor on the W-03 flowline raised a sand production alarm. The well was producing normally at the time. The alarm was handled under SOP-OPS-002 Sand Management and Sand Production Alarms.

## Immediate actions
- Lead Operator confirmed the alarm at the local panel and checked the monitor for a false trigger (no instrument fault found).
- Choke was reduced to cut drawdown and sand rate.
- Production Supervisor and Production Engineer were informed.
- Choke was held at the reduced setting until sand readings returned to normal.

## Findings
- Readings normalised after the choke reduction, and the alarm cleared.
- No further alarms were recorded on the well after the readings stabilised.
- No related events on W-03 in the previous 30 days.

## Impact
- Downtime: 4 hours (uptime 20 hours on the day).
- Production on 2026-04-18: oil 1473.5 bbl, gas 1411 Mscf, water cut 10%.
- Previous 7 days to 2026-04-17: oil 1695.9 bbl/d, gas 1708.4 Mscf/d, water cut 9.2%, GOR 1007 scf/bbl.
- The day's oil is about 222 bbl below the previous 7-day average rate, reflecting the 4 hours of downtime and the restricted choke.

## Status
Closed. W-03 is producing.

## Next actions
- Production Engineer to review sand trend and water cut on W-03 over the coming week, as water cut was slightly higher than the baseline.
- Operators to continue routine sand monitor checks per SOP-OPS-002 and report any repeat alarm promptly.
- Any later choke increase to be made in small steps while watching sand readings.

**Reference procedure:** SOP-OPS-002 Sand Management and Sand Production Alarms
