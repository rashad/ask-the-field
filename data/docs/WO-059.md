# Work order: Workover - tubing replacement, W-29

**WO number:** WO-059
**Event reference:** EV-0182
**Well:** W-29 (Wadi Field, vertical, gas lift)
**Category:** Maintenance
**Priority:** High
**Raised:** 2026-11-14
**Planned start / actual start:** 2026-11-16
**Completion date:** not yet completed
**Status:** Open
**Applicable procedure:** SOP-HSE-005 Permit to Work for Workover and Rig Operations

## Scope of work
- Rig on location to replace corroded tubing found during the caliper log.
- Kill the well, pull the existing tubing string and inspect it.
- Run the new tubing string and gas lift completion, then pressure test.
- Hand the well back to production for restart and clean-up.

## Production before workover
Average over the 14 days to 2026-11-15:
- Oil: 675.8 bbl/d
- Gas: 544 Mscf/d
- Water cut: 43.3 %
- GOR: 805 scf/bbl

## Safety and permits
- Permit to Work issued under SOP-HSE-005 before rig-up, with daily revalidation by the Workover Supervisor.
- Well control equipment tested before pulling tubing.
- Pre-job safety meeting held with the rig crew and the Lead Operator.
- Exclusion zone around the wellhead maintained for the duration of the job.

## Materials
- Replacement tubing string and couplings
- Gas lift mandrels and valves
- Wellhead seals and kill fluid

## Execution notes (as of 2026-11-22)
- Rig on location since 2026-11-16 and the job is ongoing.
- Downtime to date: 168 hours.
- Well status: workover. No restart has taken place, so there are no post-workover rates yet.
- Next steps: complete the tubing run, pressure test, return the well to production and record restart rates.
