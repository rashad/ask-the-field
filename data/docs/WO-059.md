## Scope of work

Rig on location to replace corroded tubing found during caliper log. Scope: pull the existing completion including the ESP, lay down corroded tubing joints, run new tubing, re-run or replace the ESP as per condition inspection, and hand the well back to production after pressure testing.

## Safety and permits

- Permit to Work under SOP-HSE-005 Permit to Work for Workover and Rig Operations, issued and kept current for each shift.
- Well killed and barriers verified before pulling the completion.
- Daily toolbox talk led by the Workover Supervisor.
- HSE Advisor to review lifting plan and simultaneous operations around the wellsite.

## Materials

- Replacement tubing string, corrosion-resistant grade
- ESP cable, clamps and protectors
- Wellhead seals and test fittings
- Kill fluid (brine), volume as per program
