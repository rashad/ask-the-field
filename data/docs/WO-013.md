# Work order: ESP inspection, W-27

**WO number:** WO-013
**Event reference:** EV-0016
**Well:** W-27 (Wadi Field, horizontal, ESP)
**Category:** Maintenance, routine ESP inspection
**Priority:** Routine
**Raised:** 2025-08-08
**Planned start:** 2025-08-10
**Actual start / completion:** 2025-08-10 / 2025-08-10
**Status:** Closed

## Scope of work
- Routine inspection of ESP surface equipment.
- Check of VSD parameters against the commissioning set points.
- Visual check of junction box, cable entry and wellhead power connections.

## Safety and permits
- Electrical isolation permit and lockout/tagout applied before opening the VSD cabinet.
- Toolbox talk held with the Lead Operator and Field Engineer.
- No hot work. Area barriered during the job.

## Materials
- Standard electrical inspection kit, thermal camera, calibrated multimeter.
- No spares consumed.

## Execution notes
The well was shut down in a controlled manner for the inspection and restarted once the VSD checks were complete. Total downtime was 4 hours. Surface equipment was found in good condition, and the VSD parameters were verified and left unchanged. No defects were raised.
