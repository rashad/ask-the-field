# Work order: Flowline pigging, W-21

**WO number:** WO-013
**Related event:** EV-0019
**Well:** W-21 (Field B, horizontal, ESP)
**Category:** Maintenance
**Priority:** Routine
**Date raised:** 2025-07-25
**Planned start:** 2025-07-27
**Planned finish:** 2025-07-27
**Status:** Closed

## Scope of work
Flowline pigging to remove wax and sand deposits. Launch the pig at the W-21 wellsite launcher and receive it at the CPF-1 receiver. Collect and weigh the debris recovered, and record the pig condition on arrival.

## Safety and permits
- Hot work not required; cold work permit issued.
- Pressure-containing equipment isolation per site practice. Launcher and receiver depressurised and vented before opening.
- Spill kit and drip trays at both ends. HSE Advisor briefed the crew at the toolbox talk.
- Operations at CPF-1 informed before the well was shut in.

## Materials
- Foam pig (spare on site)
- Launcher and receiver door seals
- Waste containers for wax and sand debris

## Execution notes
- Work carried out on 2025-07-27 as planned.
- Well W-21 was shut in for the pigging run and restarted after receipt of the pig.
- Total downtime: 5 hours.
- Pig received at CPF-1 intact. Wax and sand debris recovered and bagged for disposal.

## Close-out remarks
Work completed on 2025-07-27 and the work order is closed. No leaks found at the launcher or receiver after reinstatement. The well was returned to production without issues.

Production comparison (7 days before vs 6 days after restart):
