# Incident note: H2S alarm on W-26

**Document ID:** IN-009
**Event ID:** EV-0025
**Note date:** 2025-08-24
**Author:** HSE Advisor
**Well:** W-26 (Field B, vertical, gas lift)
**Severity:** Medium
**Status at note date:** Closed

## What happened
On 2025-08-24 the fixed H2S detector near the W-26 wellhead went into alarm. The Lead Operator confirmed the alarm on the local panel and the area was cleared in line with the site muster arrangements.

## Immediate actions
- Personnel moved upwind of the wellhead and a headcount was completed.
- The well was shut in as a precaution and the Production Supervisor was informed.
- Trained personnel wearing breathing apparatus and carrying portable gas monitors approached the wellhead to locate the source.
- A leak was found on a needle valve at the wellhead. It was repaired, and the area was re-tested with portable monitors until readings were clear.
- The area was released for normal access and the well was returned to production.

## Findings so far
The source was a leaking needle valve. No injuries were reported and there was no exposure above alarm limits among personnel who had been cleared from the area. The detector responded as designed.

## Impact
- Downtime: 2 hours.
- Production on the day: 1545.3 bbl oil and 758.5 Mscf gas, water cut 23 percent, uptime 22 hours.
- Baseline for the previous 7 days (to 2025-08-23): 1675.5 bbl/d oil, 863.9 Mscf/d gas, water cut 22.4 percent, GOR 516 scf/bbl.
- The oil shortfall against the baseline is about 130 bbl for the day, which is consistent with the 2 hours lost.

## Status
Closed. The leak is fixed, the well is producing and no related events were recorded in the previous 30 days.

## Next actions
- Maintenance Planner to add a check of needle valve and fitting condition at the W-26 wellhead to the next routine round.
- Lead Operator to confirm the detector reads normal at the next shift handover.

## Applicable procedure
SOP-HSE-003 H2S Alarm Response.
