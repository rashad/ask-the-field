# Incident note: Wellhead leak on W-06

**Document ID:** IN-009
**Event ID:** EV-0041
**Note date:** 2025-10-19
**Author:** Production Engineer
**Well:** W-06 (Dune Field, deviated, natural flow)
**Severity:** Medium
**Status at note date:** Closed

## What happened
On 2025-10-19 a leak was found on the wellhead valve stem packing of W-06. The well was taken off line so the leak could be isolated and repaired.

## Immediate actions
- The well was shut in and the wellhead area isolated and barricaded.
- The Lead Operator informed the Production Supervisor and the HSE Advisor.
- The area was checked for residual hydrocarbons before work began.
- The stem packing was replaced and the valve was function-tested and leak-checked at operating pressure.
- The well was returned to production the same day.

## Findings
The leak came from worn packing on the valve stem. No other wellhead component showed signs of leakage during the post-repair inspection. No injuries and no environmental release beyond the immediate wellhead pad were reported.

## Impact
- Downtime: 4 hours on 2025-10-19 (uptime 20 hours).
- Production on the day: oil 609.2 bbl, gas 480.9 Mscf, water cut 17.2 percent.
- Baseline for the 7 days to 2025-10-18: oil 736.7 bbl/d, gas 624.8 Mscf/d, water cut 16.2 percent, GOR 848 scf/bbl.
- Oil on the day was about 127.5 bbl below the baseline rate. This reflects the 4-hour stop and the restart period.

## Status
Closed as of 2025-10-19. W-06 is producing. No related events were recorded on this well in the previous 30 days.

## Next actions
- The Production Engineer will check W-06 rates over the next few days to confirm they return to the pre-event baseline.
- Maintenance Planner to add the W-06 wellhead valve stem packing to the next routine wellhead inspection round.
- No specific procedure applies to this event.
