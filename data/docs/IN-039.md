# Incident note: H2S alarm on W-22

**Document ID:** IN-039
**Note date:** 2026-03-31
**Event ID:** EV-0101
**Well:** W-22 (Field B, horizontal, ESP)
**Severity:** Medium
**Status at note date:** Closed
**Prepared by:** HSE Advisor, with input from Production Engineer

## What happened
On 2026-03-31 the fixed H2S detector near the W-22 wellhead went into alarm. The area was cleared in line with the alarm response requirements and the well was taken offline while the source was located.

## Immediate actions
- Personnel mustered at the designated point and a head count was completed.
- Access to the wellsite was barred and the Lead Operator informed the Production Supervisor.
- A trained team using portable gas detectors and breathing apparatus entered, checked the wellhead and located the source.
- The leak was traced to a needle valve. It was repaired and the area was rechecked with portable detectors before re-entry was permitted.

## Findings
The source was a leaking needle valve at the wellhead. No injuries were reported. Fixed and portable detector readings returned to normal after the repair, and the well was returned to service the same day.

## Impact
- Downtime: 8 hours on 2026-03-31 (uptime 16 hours).
- Production on the day: 473.6 bbl oil, 443.1 Mscf gas, water cut 36.9%.
- Baseline for the 7 days to 2026-03-30: 695.3 bbl/d oil, 671.2 Mscf/d gas, water cut 35.8%, GOR 965 scf/bbl.
- Deferred oil is about 221.7 bbl against the baseline rate (695.3 minus 473.6).
- No related events on this well in the previous 30 days.

## Status
Closed as of 2026-03-31. W-22 is producing.

## Next actions
- Production Engineer to confirm water cut and rates return to baseline over the next few days.
- Maintenance Planner to include a check of similar needle valves on neighbouring wellheads in the next routine round.
- Detector function to be verified at the next scheduled test.

## Reference
Response followed SOP-HSE-003 H2S Alarm Response.
