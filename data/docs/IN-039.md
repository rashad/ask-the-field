# Incident note: Wellhead leak on W-24

**Note ID:** IN-039
**Event ID:** EV-0109
**Note date:** 2026-04-14
**Author:** Production Engineer
**Well:** W-24 (Wadi Field, deviated, ESP)
**Severity:** Low
**Status at note date:** Closed

## What happened
On 2026-04-14 a leak was found on the wellhead valve stem packing of W-24. The well was shut in to make the repair safe.

## Immediate actions
- Well shut in and the wellhead area isolated and barricaded.
- Lead Operator informed the Production Supervisor and the HSE Advisor.
- Leaked fluid cleaned up and the area checked for residual hydrocarbon before work began.

## Findings
The leak came from worn stem packing on the wellhead valve. The packing was replaced. A leak check at operating pressure after the repair showed no seepage, and the well was returned to production the same day.

## Impact
- Downtime: 8 hours (uptime 16 hours on 2026-04-14).
- Production on the day: 796.1 bbl oil and 418.4 Mscf gas, water cut 27.9 percent.
- Previous 7-day average to 2026-04-13: 1202.5 bbl/d oil, 650.5 Mscf/d gas, water cut 26.9 percent, GOR 541 scf/bbl.
- The reduced volume on the day is consistent with the 8 hours offline. No other effect on the well was seen.

## Related history (same well, last 30 days)
- EV-0099, flowline flange leak on 2026-03-26, closed (21 hours downtime).
- EV-0107, routine ESP surface inspection and VSD parameter check on 2026-04-11, closed (6 hours downtime).
- EV-0113, an ESP trip on underload, is listed as planned for 2026-04-24. This is only an expected entry and has not occurred as of this note.

## Status and next actions
The incident is closed. The well is producing normally. The Lead Operator will include the valve in routine wellhead rounds and watch for any recurrence of seepage at the stem. No specific procedure applies to this event.
