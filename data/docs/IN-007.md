# Incident note: ESP trip on W-36

**Document ID:** IN-007
**Note date:** 2025-09-21
**Event ID:** EV-0033
**Well:** W-36 (Salt Flat Field, deviated, ESP)
**Severity:** Low
**Status at note date:** Closed
**Author:** Production Engineer

## What happened
On 2025-09-20 the ESP on W-36 tripped on underload. The well was down for part of the day and recorded 10 hours of uptime.

## Immediate actions
- The Lead Operator confirmed the trip at the surface panel and kept the well shut in.
- Electrical and wellhead checks were completed before any restart attempt, following the applicable procedure.
- The pump was restarted after the checks and the well was monitored through the restart.

## Findings so far
- No damage was found during the checks.
- Trip cause recorded as underload only. No further fault was identified.
- Water cut on the day was 25.3%, in line with the prior 7-day average of 24.8%.

## Impact
- Total downtime as of this note: 28 hours (2025-09-20 to 2025-09-21).
- Production on 2025-09-20: oil 607 bbl, gas 735.9 Mscf, water cut 25.3%.
- Baseline for the 7 days to 2025-09-19: oil 1575.7 bbl/d, gas 1819 Mscf/d, water cut 24.8%, GOR 1154 scf/bbl.
- Deferred oil for 2025-09-20 is about 969 bbl against the baseline rate (1575.7 minus 607). Additional deferment on 2025-09-21 is not included in this figure.

## Status at note date
Closed. The well was restarted after checks, and no open actions remain against this event.

## Next actions
- The Lead Operator will continue to watch motor current and intake pressure trends on routine rounds.
- Wellhead valve greasing and function test is planned from 2025-10-04 (EV-0038). It is planned work and not yet started.
