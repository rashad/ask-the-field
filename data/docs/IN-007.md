# Incident note: ESP trip on W-35

**Document ID:** IN-007
**Note date:** 2025-08-07
**Event ID:** EV-0021
**Well:** W-35 (Bayda, horizontal, ESP)
**Category / severity:** Incident / low
**Status at note date:** Closed
**Applicable procedure:** SOP-OPS-001 ESP Trip Response and Restart

## What happened
On 2025-08-06 the ESP on W-35 tripped on underload. The well was offline until the restart on 2025-08-07, after checks were completed.

## Immediate actions
- The Lead Operator confirmed the trip at the drive and logged the underload alarm.
- The well was kept isolated while the surface checks were done, following SOP-OPS-001.
- The Production Engineer reviewed the drive trend data before approving the restart.
- The Production Supervisor was informed of the trip and of the planned restart.

## Findings
- The ESP tripped on underload. The checks found no fault that required intervention.
- The unit was restarted after checks and returned to normal operation.
- No related events on this well were recorded in the previous 30 days.

## Impact
- Downtime as of the note date: 30 hours.
- On 2025-08-06 the well produced 512.8 bbl of oil and 768.7 Mscf of gas, with 9 hours of uptime and a water cut of 14.1%.
- The previous 7-day baseline to 2025-08-05 was 1359.4 bbl/d oil, 2036.4 Mscf/d gas, 15.1% water cut and a GOR of 1498 scf/bbl.
- Deferred oil is the difference between the baseline rate and the volume produced on the trip day, plus the production lost on the following day while the well was down. The 2025-08-06 shortfall alone is about 846.6 bbl.

## Status
Closed as of 2025-08-07. The well is producing.

## Next actions
- The Production Engineer will keep watching motor current and intake pressure on W-35 for the next few shifts, in line with SOP-OPS-001.
- If underload trips recur, the Production Engineer will raise a review of the pump operating range.
