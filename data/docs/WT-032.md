## Comparison with baseline
Oil is down 368.9 bbl/d (about 35 percent) and gas is down 276.3 Mscf/d against the previous test. Water cut is almost unchanged (+0.7 points) and GOR is flat at 747 against 748 scf/bbl. The loss therefore looks like a drop in total liquid rate, not a change in fluid composition. Sand is low.

## Observations
- The well had two events in the last 45 days: a flowline flange leak (EV-0156, 2026-08-25 to 2026-08-26, 35 hours downtime, closed) and flowline pigging (EV-0157, 2026-08-27, 6 hours, closed).
- Separator readings were stable through the test period.
- The lower rate could reflect ESP performance decline or flowline restriction, and neither is confirmed by this test.

## Recommendation
- Review ESP amps, frequency and intake pressure trends since 2026-07-17.
- Confirm that the flowline is clear after the repair and pigging.
- Repeat the test after any ESP adjustment to confirm the rate.
