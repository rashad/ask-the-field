# Work order: Safety valve test, W-14

**WO number:** WO-034
**Event reference:** EV-0045
**Well:** W-14 (Dune Field, deviated, ESP)
**Category:** Maintenance
**Priority:** Routine (planned)
**Raised:** 2025-10-22
**Planned start:** 2025-10-24
**Planned finish:** 2025-10-24
**Actual start / finish:** 2025-10-24 / 2025-10-24
**Status:** Closed

## Scope of work
Subsurface safety valve function and leak test on W-14.
1. Shut in the well and isolate it from the flowline.
2. Bleed down tubing pressure above the valve to the test pressure.
3. Function the valve from the control panel and confirm full closure and reopening.
4. Hold the leak test and record the pressure trend.
5. Restore the valve to service and restart the well.

## Safety and permits
- Work permit and isolation certificate issued before start.
- Toolbox talk held with the operations crew and the slickline unit crew.
- Gas detection active at the wellhead during bleed-down.
- HSE Advisor informed of the shut-in.

## Materials
- Calibrated test gauge and pump
- Standard seal kit held in reserve (not used)

## Execution notes and close-out remarks
- The valve function test and leak test were completed on 2025-10-24 within the planned day.
- No defects found. No parts replaced.
- Total well downtime was 5 hours, from shut-in to restart.
- Well restarted and returned to normal operation with no issues.
- Production before the job (14 days to 2025-10-23): oil 970.3 bbl/d, gas 860.6 Mscf/d, water cut 17.3 percent, GOR 887 scf/bbl.
- Production after restart (6 days to 2025-10-31): oil 960 bbl/d, gas 876 Mscf/d, water cut 18.7 percent, GOR 913 scf/bbl.
- Rates remain close to the pre-job levels. No further action is required.

**Close-out:** Work complete. Work permit closed and the test result filed in the well file. Work order closed by the Maintenance Planner.
