# Work order: Wellhead maintenance, W-39

**WO number:** WO-034
**Event reference:** EV-0051
**Well:** W-39 (Bayda, horizontal, ESP)
**Category:** Maintenance, wellhead
**Priority:** Routine
**Raised:** 2025-11-11
**Planned start:** 2025-11-13
**Actual start / completion:** 2025-11-13 / 2025-11-13
**Status:** Closed

## Scope of work
- Grease all wellhead valves (master, wing and swab) to the manufacturer's grease schedule.
- Function test each valve through full open and full close travel and record any stiffness or leakage.
- Visual check of flanges, studs and the ESP power penetrator for leaks or damage.

## Safety and permits
- Permit to work and isolation certificate issued before the well was shut in.
- Well shut in and flowline isolated at the wellhead. Pressure bled down and verified zero before work.
- Toolbox talk held with the maintenance crew. Gas detection carried by the crew.
- Electrical isolation of the ESP drive confirmed and locked out.

## Materials
- Valve grease, as per the wellhead schedule
- Grease gun and fittings
- Valve function test record sheet

## Execution notes and close-out remarks
- Work done on 2025-11-13 as planned. The well was down for 6 hours in total.
- All valves greased and function tested. Valves cycled fully and no leaks were seen at the stem packing or flanges.
- Well restarted and returned to normal operation. No punch-list items remain.
- Permits closed and the site left clean.
