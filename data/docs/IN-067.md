# Incident note: Sand production alarm on W-34

**Note ID:** IN-067
**Event ID:** EV-0180
**Note date:** 2026-11-09
**Author:** Production Engineer
**Well:** W-34, Salt Flat Field (vertical, gas lift)
**Severity:** High
**Status at note date:** Open

## What happened
On 2026-11-09 the acoustic sand monitor on W-34 alarmed above threshold. The erosion probe on the flowline shows metal loss. The well was already on a reduced choke from earlier sand restrictions.

## Immediate actions
- Choke reduced again to 24/64.
- Sand sampling set to every shift.
- Lead Operator and Production Supervisor informed.
- Flowline and choke area visually checked for leaks, none observed.

## Impact
- Downtime as of this note: 2 hours (uptime on the day 22 hours).
- Production on 2026-11-09: oil 630.6 bbl, gas 1040.9 Mscf, water cut 16.2%.
- Previous 7 days to 2026-11-08 averaged oil 899.1 bbl/d, gas 1482 Mscf/d, water cut 16.8%, GOR 1648 scf/bbl.
- Oil on the day was about 268 bbl below the 7-day average, which includes the downtime and the choke reduction.

## Findings so far
- Water cut is steady against the baseline, so no change in water production is indicated.
- Probe metal loss confirms that sand is eroding the flowline. The source and the rate of loss are not yet established.
- Investigation is open.

## Status
The well is producing on the 24/64 choke. The incident remains **open**.

## Next actions
1. Review the sand sample results each shift and log the sand content in ppm.
2. Integrity Engineer to assess the flowline wall thickness at the probe location and downstream bends.
3. Keep the choke at 24/64 until sand levels are stable below the alarm threshold.
4. Production Engineer to decide on any further choke change or well intervention after the sample trend is reviewed.
5. Report the outcome in the next update to this note.

SOP-OPS-002 Sand Management and Sand Production Alarms.
