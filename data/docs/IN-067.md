# Incident note: Sand production alarm on W-34

**Document id:** IN-067
**Event id:** EV-0180
**Note date:** 2026-11-09
**Well:** W-34 (Bayda, horizontal, gas lift)
**Severity:** High
**Status at note date:** Open

## What happened
On 2026-11-09 the acoustic sand monitor on W-34 alarmed above threshold. The erosion probe on the flowline shows metal loss. The well is still producing.

## Immediate actions
- Choke reduced again to 24/64.
- Sand sampling set to every shift.
- Lead Operator informed the Production Supervisor and the CPF-1 control room.
- Flowline walk-down carried out at surface; no leak or visible damage found.

## Findings so far
- The alarm and the probe metal loss point to active sand production from the well.
- Water cut on the day was 32.8%, against 30.7% in the 7 days before the event (to 2026-11-08).
- No related events on this well in the last 30 days.
- The cause is not yet confirmed. Investigation is open.

## Impact
| Item | Before (7 days to 2026-11-08) | On 2026-11-09 |
|---|---|---|
| Oil | 723 bbl/d | 493.3 bbl |
| Gas | 1380.6 Mscf/d | 837.3 Mscf |
| Water cut | 30.7% | 32.8% |
| Uptime | n/a | 22 h |

Downtime as of the note date is 2 hours. The pre-event GOR was 1910 scf/bbl. Oil on the day was about 230 bbl below the previous 7-day average, mostly from the choke reduction and the 2 hours of downtime.

## Status at note date
Open. The well is producing on the 24/64 choke under increased monitoring.

## Next actions
1. Continue sand sampling every shift and record the results against the alarm threshold.
2. Production Engineer to review the sand trend and the erosion probe readings.
3. Integrity Engineer to assess flowline wall loss and schedule thickness checks.
4. Hold the choke at 24/64 until the investigation gives direction.
5. Follow the alarm response steps in SOP-OPS-002 Sand Management and Sand Production Alarms.

**Author:** Production Engineer
