# Work order: Wellhead maintenance, W-33

**WO number:** WO-043
**Related event:** EV-0066
**Well:** W-33 (Bayda, vertical, gas lift)
**Category:** Maintenance, Wellhead maintenance
**Priority:** Routine
**Raised:** 2025-12-30
**Planned start:** 2026-01-01
**Planned completion:** 2026-01-01
**Status:** Closed

## Scope of work
- Grease all wellhead valves (master, wing and swab) at the designated grease points.
- Function test each valve through full travel and record any stiffness or leakage.
- Visual check of the wellhead for leaks, corrosion and loose fittings.

## Safety and permits
- General work permit issued by the Production Supervisor.
- Well shut in and wellhead isolated before work began.
- Toolbox talk held with the crew. Sand-prone Bayda wellhead: check for erosion on exposed surfaces.
- Gas detector carried by the Lead Operator throughout.

## Materials
- Valve grease (approved grade) and grease gun
- Cleaning rags
- Torque wrench and basic hand tools

## Execution notes and close-out
Work was carried out on 2026-01-01 and completed the same day. The well was down for 2 hours in total. All valves were greased and function tested through full travel with no sticking and no external leakage observed. No erosion or damage found on the wellhead. The well was returned to production without issues.
