# Incident note: Flowline leak on W-08

**Document ID:** IN-058
**Note date:** 2026-08-26
**Event ID:** EV-0156
**Well:** W-08 (Field A, vertical, ESP)
**Severity:** High
**Status at note date:** Closed

## What happened
On 2026-08-25 a small leak was found on a flowline flange on W-08. The well was brought down, the line isolated and the flange repaired. The leak was contained and no injuries were reported.

## Immediate actions
- Well shut down and flowline isolated and depressurised.
- Area barricaded, and the Production Supervisor and HSE Advisor informed.
- Flange joint re-made by the maintenance crew and leak-tested before return to service.
- Spill area checked and cleaned up.

## Findings so far
The leak came from the flange joint only. The pipe body showed no visible damage on inspection. Wax and sand deposits in the line are a possible contributing factor, so a pigging run has been planned (see next actions).

## Impact
- Downtime as of the note date: 35 hours (2026-08-25 to 2026-08-26).
- On 2026-08-25 the well made 206.5 bbl of oil and 155.8 Mscf of gas, at 30.5% water cut, with 6.5 hours of uptime.
- Baseline for the 7 days to 2026-08-24: 838.9 bbl/d oil, 616.1 Mscf/d gas, 28.3% water cut, GOR 734 scf/bbl. The deferred oil is therefore roughly the baseline rate for the hours lost.

## Status
Closed as of 2026-08-26. The repair passed its leak test and W-08 is back in production.

## Next actions
- Flowline pigging (EV-0157) is planned from 2026-08-27 to remove wax and sand deposits.
- The Integrity Engineer to review the flange condition and gasket record.
- Lead Operator to watch the flange during the first rounds after restart.

## Procedure
The response followed SOP-HSE-006 Flowline Leak Response.
