# Incident note: Wellhead leak on W-01

**Document ID:** IN-058
**Event ID:** EV-0152
**Note date:** 2026-08-20
**Well:** W-01 (Dune Field, vertical, ESP)
**Severity:** Medium
**Status:** Closed

## What happened
A leak was found on the wellhead valve stem packing of W-01 on 2026-08-20. The well was shut in so the leak could be isolated and repaired.

## Immediate actions
- The well was shut in and the wellhead area was made safe and barricaded.
- The Lead Operator informed the Production Supervisor and the HSE Advisor.
- Leaked fluid was cleaned up and the area was checked for residual hydrocarbons.

## Findings
The leak came from the valve stem packing. The packing was replaced and the valve was function tested. A pressure check of the wellhead showed no visible leakage after the repair. The well was returned to production the same day.

## Impact
- Downtime: 7 hours on 2026-08-20 (uptime 17 hours).
- Production on the day: 484.5 bbl oil and 307.9 Mscf gas, water cut 32.1%.
- Baseline for the 7 days to 2026-08-19: 642.5 bbl/d oil, 387.8 Mscf/d gas, water cut 33.1%, GOR 604 scf/bbl.
- The reduced volume on the day matches the lost run time. Water cut was in line with the baseline.

## Status at note date
Closed. The packing has been replaced, the well is flowing normally and no further leakage has been observed.

## Next actions
- Operators to include the W-01 wellhead valves in routine rounds and report any weeping at the stems.
- The Maintenance Planner to review the packing condition on other wellhead valves during scheduled inspections.
- A preventive scale inhibitor squeeze on W-01 (EV-0162) is planned from 2026-09-06. It is not part of this incident.

## Procedure reference
No specific procedure applies to this event.
