## Scope of work
Preventive scale inhibitor squeeze on W-34 in line with SOP-OPS-008 Scale Inhibitor Squeeze Treatment. Shut in the well, pump pre-flush, inhibitor and overflush, hold for the soak period, then return to production on gas lift.

## Safety and permits
- Work permit and chemical handling permit issued before mobilisation.
- Pre-job safety meeting held with the pumping crew and operations.
- Pressure test of surface lines before pumping.
- HSE Advisor reviewed the chemical data sheet and spill containment.

## Materials
- Scale inhibitor and pre-flush/overflush fluids as per the treatment program.
- Pumping unit and temporary surface lines.

## Pre-job baseline
Average over the 14 days to 2025-08-22: oil 1707 bbl/d, gas 2846 Mscf/d, water cut 11.8 %, GOR 1667 scf/bbl.

## Execution and close-out remarks
- Treatment completed on 2025-08-23 with no safety incidents or spills.
- Well downtime recorded: 24 hours.
- Well restarted on gas lift and stabilised without issues.
- Average over the 6 days to 2025-08-30: oil 1685.8 bbl/d, gas 2786.2 Mscf/d, water cut 12.3 %, GOR 1653 scf/bbl.
- Rates are close to the pre-job baseline. Production Engineer to keep monitoring water cut and rates as part of routine surveillance.
- No related events in the 30 days around the job.
- Work order closed. Well W-34 is producing.
