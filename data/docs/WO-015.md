# Work order: Safety valve test, W-38

**WO number:** WO-015
**Related event:** EV-0022
**Well:** W-38 (Bayda, deviated, gas lift)
**Category:** Maintenance
**Priority:** Routine
**Raised:** 2025-08-09
**Planned start:** 2025-08-11
**Actual start:** 2025-08-11
**Completion date:** 2025-08-11
**Status:** Closed

## Scope of work
Function and leak test of the subsurface safety valve (SSSV) on W-38.
1. Shut in the well and isolate it from the flowline at the wellhead.
2. Bleed down tubing pressure in a controlled manner.
3. Function test the SSSV from the control panel.
4. Leak test the valve and record the pressure hold.
5. Restore the control line, reopen the well and return it to production.

## Safety and permits
- Work permit and isolation certificate issued by the Production Supervisor.
- Toolbox talk held before the job. Gas detection checked at the wellhead.
- Bayda is a high GOR area, so no ignition sources were allowed near the wellhead during bleed-down.

## Materials
- Test pump and calibrated gauges
- Control panel hydraulic supply
- Standard seal kit (held on standby, not used)

## Execution notes and close-out
- The SSSV function and leak test was completed on 2025-08-11 and the valve met the acceptance criteria.
- Total downtime was 4 hours. The well was returned to production the same day.
- The wellhead valve greasing and function test (EV-0020) on 2025-08-05 was done earlier, with 2 hours of downtime, and nothing from that job affected this test.
- Production before the job (7 days to 2025-08-10): oil 1545.3 bbl/d, gas 2354.5 Mscf/d, water cut 25.3 %, GOR 1524 scf/bbl.
- Production after restart (6 days to 2025-08-18): oil 1543.9 bbl/d, gas 2253.4 Mscf/d, water cut 25 %, GOR 1460 scf/bbl.
- Oil rate and water cut are essentially unchanged. No defects were found and no follow-up work is required.
