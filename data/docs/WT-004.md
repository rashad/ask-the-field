# Well test: W-09, 2025-09-30

- Choke: 48/64 in

## Comparison with previous baseline
The previous baseline (period ending 2025-08-01, 7 days) gave 1530.8 bbl/d oil, 988.6 Mscf/d gas, 9.3 % water cut and GOR 646 scf/bbl.

- Oil is down 32.2 bbl/d (about 2.1 %).
- Gas is down 25.5 Mscf/d.
- Water cut is up 0.1 point, within normal test scatter.
- GOR is essentially unchanged.

## Observations
- Flow was stable during the test and the separator readings were consistent.
- Sand was negligible at 2 ppm.
- The subsurface safety valve function and leak test on 2025-09-12 (EV-0030, 5 hours downtime) is closed and had no visible effect on the test.

## Recommendation
- Keep the well on its current choke and ESP settings.
- Repeat the test at the next scheduled interval to confirm the small oil decline.
- Continue routine sand and water cut monitoring.
