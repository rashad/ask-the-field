Oil is down 44.8 bbl/d against the baseline and water cut is up 1.7 points. Wadi Field average water cut was 32.5 % in January 2026 and 41.7 % over the last 30 days, so the W-27 value is in line with the field trend.

## Observations
- This is the first test after the ESP replacement workover (EV-0156, 2026-08-23 to 2026-08-29). The previous pump failed on motor winding insulation breakdown (EV-0150). The well returned to production on 2026-08-30.
- Sand is negligible at 1 ppm.
- Gas rate fell in proportion to oil, and GOR moved only slightly, from 1045 to 1072 scf/bbl.

## Recommendation
- Treat this test as the new baseline for the replacement pump.
- Repeat the test in about 30 days to confirm the oil decline against the water cut trend.
- Keep monitoring pump motor current and intake pressure at the routine shift checks.
