## Scope of work
Replace the eroded choke bean on the W-17 wellhead flow line. Isolate the well, depressurise the choke body, remove the worn bean, fit a new bean of the same size, pressure test and return the well to production.

## Safety and permits
- Hot work not required; cold work permit and isolation certificate issued.
- Line depressurised and verified at zero before the choke body was opened.
- Toolbox talk held with the crew before starting.
- Gas detection kept on during the job.

## Materials
- Replacement choke bean (as per the well's choke schedule)
- Body gasket and seals
- Torque tools and test pump

## Execution notes
The well was shut in on 2026-05-12 and the eroded bean was removed. Visible wear on the bean confirmed the reason for replacement. The choke body was inspected and found serviceable. A new bean and seals were installed, the assembly was pressure tested with no leaks, and the well was restarted the same day. Total downtime was 5 hours.

## Close-out remarks
Work completed on 2026-05-12 and the isolation was removed. Before the job (7 days to 2026-05-11), W-17 averaged 1152.5 bbl/d oil, 631.1 Mscf/d gas, 32.4% water cut and GOR 548 scf/bbl. After restart (6 days to 2026-05-19) it averaged 1153.6 bbl/d oil, 643.8 Mscf/d gas, 32.5% water cut and GOR 558 scf/bbl. Rates are stable and in line with the pre-job baseline. No related events were logged within 30 days. Work order closed.
