# Work order: Choke replacement, W-08

**WO number:** WO-049
**Event reference:** EV-0103
**Well:** W-08 (Dune Field, vertical, ESP)
**Category:** Maintenance
**Priority:** Routine
**Raised:** 2026-04-02
**Planned start:** 2026-04-04
**Actual start / completion:** 2026-04-04 / 2026-04-04
**Status:** Closed

## Scope of work
- Isolate the flowline at the wellhead and depressurize.
- Remove the eroded choke bean and inspect the choke body and seat.
- Install a new choke bean and reassemble the choke.
- Pressure test, then restart the well and confirm stable flow.

## Safety and permits
- Hot work not required. Cold work permit and isolation certificate issued.
- Lock-out of the wellhead wing valve and the ESP power supply confirmed before breaking containment.
- Toolbox talk held with the maintenance crew. Pressure bleed-down verified at the gauge before opening.

## Materials
- 1 x replacement choke bean (same bore as the removed bean)
- 1 x gasket and seal kit for the choke body

## Execution notes
- The removed bean showed clear erosion on the bore. The seat was inspected and judged serviceable.
- Reassembly and pressure test were completed without leaks.
- Well returned to production the same day. Total downtime was 6 hours.

## Close-out remarks
Eroded choke bean replaced. Work completed on 2026-04-04 and the work order is closed.
