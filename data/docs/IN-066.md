# Incident note: Sustained casing annulus pressure on W-12

**Document ID:** IN-066
**Event ID:** EV-0179
**Note date:** 2026-10-28
**Well:** W-12 (Field A, vertical, natural flow)
**Severity:** High
**Status at note date:** Open
**Prepared by:** Production Engineer

## What happened
On 2026-10-28 the B-annulus pressure on W-12 rose above the allowed limit and did not bleed off. The pressure returned after each bleed-down attempt, which is consistent with sustained casing pressure rather than thermal build-up. The well was shut in pending integrity diagnostics.

## Immediate actions
- Well shut in and the wellhead area checked for visible leaks, with none reported.
- Annulus pressure readings logged at regular intervals after shut-in.
- Production Supervisor and Integrity Engineer informed.
- Access to the wellhead restricted to essential checks only.

## Findings so far
- The annulus pressure did not bleed off to the expected level.
- No cause has been confirmed. Possible sources, such as a tubing or packer leak or a casing issue, are not yet ruled in or out.
- Diagnostics are not yet complete.

## Impact
- Downtime as of this note: 24 hours.
- Production before the event (7 days to 2026-10-27): oil 650.7 bbl/d, gas 306.6 Mscf/d, water cut 21.8 %, GOR 471 scf/bbl.
- Production on 2026-10-28: oil 0 bbl, gas 0 Mscf, uptime 0 hours.
- Deferred oil is running at about the pre-event rate of 650.7 bbl/d for as long as the well stays shut in.

## Status at note date
Open. W-12 remains shut in. No restart is planned until the diagnostics are reviewed and the well is confirmed to be within its barrier limits.

## Next actions
1. Run the annulus bleed-down and build-up test per the procedure and record the results.
2. Review the results with the Integrity Engineer to identify the likely leak path.
3. Decide on further diagnostics or an intervention plan.
4. Update the deferred production estimate daily.
5. Keep this note open until the well is cleared or repaired.
