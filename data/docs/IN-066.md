# Incident note: Sustained casing annulus pressure on W-12

**Document ID:** IN-066
**Note date:** 2026-10-28
**Event ID:** EV-0179
**Well:** W-12 (Dune Field, deviated, ESP)
**Severity:** High
**Status at note date:** Open
**Author:** Production Engineer

## What happened
On 2026-10-28 the B-annulus pressure on W-12 built up above the allowed limit and did not bleed off. The well was shut in pending integrity diagnostics.

## Immediate actions
- Well shut in and the ESP stopped.
- Annulus pressure bleed-off attempted and repeated, with no sustained drop.
- Production Supervisor and HSE Advisor informed.
- Area around the wellhead checked and access limited to essential personnel.

## Findings so far
- No root cause has been identified. Diagnostics are not yet complete.
- A preventive scale inhibitor squeeze (EV-0172) was carried out on W-12 on 2026-10-07 to 2026-10-08, with 41 hours of downtime. It is closed and is listed here only as recent well history. No link to the current event has been established.

## Impact
- Downtime as of this note: 24 hours.
- Production on 2026-10-28: 0 bbl oil, 0 Mscf gas, uptime 0 hours.
- Rates before the event (7 days to 2026-10-27): oil 928.3 bbl/d, gas 779.9 Mscf/d, water cut 32 percent, GOR 840 scf/bbl. This is the rate being deferred while the well is down.

## Status at note date
Open. W-12 remains shut in. No restart is planned until integrity is confirmed.

## Next actions
1. Run annulus pressure build-up and bleed-off diagnostics and record the readings.
2. Review wellhead and annulus valve condition with the Integrity Engineer.
3. Decide on further intervention once diagnostics are reviewed.
4. Update this note when the cause is understood and the well is cleared to restart.
