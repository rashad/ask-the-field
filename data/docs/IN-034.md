# Incident note: High separator level trip on W-07

**Document ID:** IN-034
**Note date:** 2026-03-18
**Event ID:** EV-0097
**Well:** W-07 (Dune Field, vertical, ESP)
**Category:** Incident, severity low
**Status at note date:** Closed

## What happened
On 2026-03-18 the test separator on W-07 tripped on high level, which shut in the well. The Lead Operator saw the trip alarm at CPF-1 and confirmed that the well had shut in on the trip.

## Immediate actions
- Confirmed the well was safely shut in and that no fluid carry-over reached the gas line.
- Checked the separator level visually against the gauge glass and found a mismatch with the controller reading.
- Informed the Production Supervisor.
- Instrument technician recalibrated the level controller and verified the response with a stroke test.
- Reset the trip and restarted the well with normal ESP start-up checks.

## Findings so far
The cause was the level controller reading out of calibration, which produced a false high-level condition. No damage to the separator or to the ESP was observed, and no other wells were affected.

## Impact
- Downtime: 3 hours on 2026-03-18 (uptime 21 hours).
- Production on the day: 595.6 bbl oil, 655.8 Mscf gas, water cut 27.3%.
- Baseline for the 7 days to 2026-03-17: 654.8 bbl/d oil, 711.6 Mscf/d gas, water cut 27.9%, GOR 1087 scf/bbl.
- Deferred oil is about 59 bbl against the baseline rate. Water cut and GOR stayed in line with the baseline.

## Status
The well is producing normally and the incident is closed. No related events were recorded in the previous 30 days.

## Next actions
- Production Engineer to review the level controller calibration interval for the test separator.
- Lead Operator to check the level reading against the gauge glass at the start of each shift for the next week.

## Procedure reference
No specific procedure applies to this event.
