# Incident note: Sand production alarm on W-21

**Document ID:** IN-034
**Event ID:** EV-0094
**Note date:** 2026-03-17
**Well:** W-21 (Field B, horizontal, ESP)
**Severity:** High
**Status at note date:** Closed
**Applicable procedure:** SOP-OPS-002 Sand Management and Sand Production Alarms

## What happened
On 2026-03-17 the sand monitor on W-21 raised a sand production alarm. The Lead Operator confirmed the alarm on site and the choke was reduced until the readings returned to normal.

## Immediate actions
- Choke reduced in steps in line with SOP-OPS-002 while the sand monitor was observed.
- Wellhead and flowline walked down by the operator, with no leaks or abnormal vibration seen.
- Production Supervisor informed of the alarm and the rate restriction.
- Readings were held for a stable period before the well was returned to normal operation.

## Findings so far
The sand readings returned to normal at the reduced choke setting and stayed there. No equipment damage was reported. The 7-day average before the event (to 2026-03-16) was 937.6 bbl/d oil, 722.5 Mscf/d gas, 42% water cut and a GOR of 771 scf/bbl.

## Impact
- Downtime as of the note date: 11 hours.
- Uptime on 2026-03-17: 13 hours.
- Production on the day: 504.8 bbl oil and 391.1 Mscf gas, at 40.5% water cut.
- Compared with the 937.6 bbl/d prior average, the shortfall is about 433 bbl of oil for the day.

## Status
Closed as of 2026-03-17. W-21 is producing. No related events were logged on this well in the preceding 30 days.

## Next actions
1. The Production Engineer to review the sand trend and the choke setting for W-21 over the coming days.
2. Operators to keep to the SOP-OPS-002 monitoring frequency on the well.
3. Raise a new event if the alarm repeats.
