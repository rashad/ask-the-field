# Work order: Choke replacement, W-36

**WO number:** WO-033
**Related event:** EV-0044
**Well:** W-36 (Salt Flat Field, deviated, ESP)
**Category:** Maintenance
**Priority:** Medium
**Raised:** 2025-10-20
**Planned start / end:** 2025-10-22 / 2025-10-22
**Actual start / end:** 2025-10-22 / 2025-10-22
**Status:** Closed

## Scope of work
- Shut in W-36 and isolate the flowline at the wellhead.
- Remove the eroded choke bean and fit a new bean of the same size.
- Inspect the choke body and seat for erosion damage.
- Pressure test the assembly, then restart the well and confirm stable flow.

## Safety and permits
- Hot work not required. Cold work permit and isolation certificate issued.
- Pressure bled to zero and verified before breaking containment.
- Sand-prone service: operators wore standard PPE and checked for trapped pressure and solids on opening the choke.

## Materials
- 1 x replacement choke bean (same size as removed)
- 1 x seal kit for the choke body
- Gaskets and lubricant

## Execution notes and close-out
- The bean was eroded, consistent with sand-laden flow. The choke body and seat were checked and found serviceable.
- Work completed on 2025-10-22. Total downtime was 8 hours.
- The leak test passed and no leaks were seen at restart.
- The previous wellhead valve greasing and function test (EV-0038, 2025-10-04) was noted, and the valves operated freely during isolation.
