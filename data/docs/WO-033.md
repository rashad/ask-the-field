# Work order: Scale squeeze treatment, W-22

**WO number:** WO-033
**Event reference:** EV-0050
**Well:** W-22 (Field B, horizontal, ESP)
**Category:** Maintenance
**Priority:** Routine (preventive)
**Raised:** 2025-11-09
**Planned start:** 2025-11-11
**Completion date:** 2025-11-12
**Status:** Closed
**Applicable procedure:** SOP-OPS-008 Scale Inhibitor Squeeze Treatment

## Scope of work
Preventive scale inhibitor squeeze on W-22. Shut in the well, pump the treatment per SOP-OPS-008, allow the shut-in soak period, then restart the ESP and bring the well back to the CPF-1 line.

## Safety and permits
- Hot work not required. Permit to work and pumping unit lifting plan issued.
- Pre-job toolbox talk held with the pumping crew and operators.
- Chemical handling per the product safety data sheet, with spill containment in place around the pumping unit.
- Pressure test of surface lines completed before pumping.

## Materials
- Scale inhibitor chemical and overflush fluid
- Pumping unit and temporary lines
- Chemical sampling kit

## Execution and close-out remarks
Work started 2025-11-11 and finished 2025-11-12. Total downtime was 30 hours. The squeeze was pumped and displaced as designed, with no pressure excursions or leaks reported. The ESP was restarted after the soak and the well returned to production without trips.
