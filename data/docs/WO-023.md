# Work order: Wellhead maintenance, W-30

**WO number:** WO-023
**Event reference:** EV-0035
**Well:** W-30 (Field B, deviated, gas lift)
**Category:** Maintenance, wellhead
**Priority:** Routine
**Raised:** 2025-09-25
**Planned start:** 2025-09-27
**Planned finish:** 2025-09-27
**Actual start / completion:** 2025-09-27 / 2025-09-27
**Status:** Closed

## Scope of work
- Grease all wellhead valves (master, wing and swab) at the fittings provided.
- Function test each valve through full travel and record turns to close and open.
- Check for leaks at the stem packing and flange connections after greasing.
- Restore the well to production once testing is complete.

## Safety and permits
- General work permit issued by the Production Supervisor.
- Well shut in and wellhead pressure confirmed stable before work.
- Toolbox talk held with the operations crew. HSE Advisor reviewed the job hazard analysis.
- No hot work and no line breaking were required.

## Materials
- Valve grease (cartridges), grease gun, valve torque tools, leak detection fluid, and a logsheet for the valve checks.

## Execution notes
The well was shut in for the job on 2025-09-27. All valves were greased and cycled through full travel with no sticking noted. Stem packing and flanges showed no leaks at the leak detection check. The well was returned to production the same day. Total downtime was 3 hours.

## Close-out remarks
- Work completed as scoped, with no deferred items and no follow-up work orders raised.
- Seven-day average before the job (to 2025-09-26): oil 1649.9 bbl/d, gas 1627.4 Mscf/d, water cut 26.1%, GOR 986 scf/bbl.
- Six-day average after restart (to 2025-10-04): oil 1630 bbl/d, gas 1641 Mscf/d, water cut 26.9%, GOR 1007 scf/bbl.
- Rates are in line with pre-job levels. No procedure was applicable to this work order.
