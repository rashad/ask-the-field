# Work order: Choke replacement, W-15

**WO number:** WO-023
**Event reference:** EV-0029
**Well:** W-15 (Dune Field, deviated, ESP)
**Category:** Maintenance
**Priority:** Routine
**Raised:** 2025-09-09
**Planned start:** 2025-09-11
**Planned finish:** 2025-09-11
**Actual start / finish:** 2025-09-11 / 2025-09-11
**Status:** Closed

## Scope of work
Replace the eroded choke bean on the W-15 wellhead flow line. Inspect the choke body and seat for erosion damage, fit a new bean, and return the well to production at the previous choke setting.

## Safety and permits
- Hot work not required; cold work permit issued.
- Well shut in and flow line isolated and depressurised before breaking containment.
- Lock-out and tag-out applied to the ESP power supply and the wing valve.
- Toolbox talk held with the crew before starting.

## Materials
- 1 x replacement choke bean (matching size)
- Gasket set and ring seals for the choke body
- Standard hand tools and torque wrench

## Execution notes
The eroded bean was removed and the choke body and seat were checked, with no further damage found. The new bean was installed, the body was reassembled with new seals and a pressure check showed no leaks. The well was restarted and the flow line was lined up to CPF-1.

## Close-out remarks
- Total downtime: 3 hours, all on 2025-09-11.
- Production before the job (14 days to 2025-09-10): 1461.2 bbl/d oil, 944.1 Mscf/d gas, 12.2 % water cut, GOR 646 scf/bbl.
- Production after restart (6 days to 2025-09-18): 1420.4 bbl/d oil, 912.3 Mscf/d gas, 12.0 % water cut, GOR 642 scf/bbl.
- Well is producing normally and the work order is closed. No follow-up actions raised.
