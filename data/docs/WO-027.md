# Work order: Flowline pigging, W-14

**WO number:** WO-027
**Related event:** EV-0034
**Well:** W-14 (Dune Field, deviated, ESP)
**Category:** Maintenance
**Priority:** Routine
**Raised:** 2025-09-20
**Planned start:** 2025-09-22
**Actual start / completion:** 2025-09-22 / 2025-09-22
**Status:** Closed

## Scope of work

Pig the W-14 flowline to CPF-1 to remove wax and sand deposits. Launch the pig at the wellsite launcher, receive it at the CPF-1 receiver, and record the volume and type of debris recovered. Shut in the well for the duration of the operation and restart once the line is confirmed clear.

## Safety and permits

- Hot work excluded. Cold work permit issued for the launcher and receiver operations.
- Line depressurised and vented to the closed drain before the launcher door was opened.
- Gas detection in use at both ends. Radio check between wellsite and CPF-1 before launch.
- Exclusion zone set up around the receiver during pig arrival.
- Toolbox talk held with the pigging crew before work started.

## Materials

- Foam-bodied cleaning pig, one unit, with a spare on site
- Launcher and receiver door seals (inspected, replaced as needed)
- Waste drums for wax and sand returns

## Close-out remarks

Work was carried out on 2025-09-22 as planned. The well was shut in for 6 hours in total, covering line preparation, pig run, receipt and restart. The pig arrived at CPF-1 intact, and returns of wax and sand were collected for disposal. The flowline was confirmed clear and the well was restarted without issues.
