# Work order: Flowline pigging, W-11

**WO number:** WO-027
**Event reference:** EV-0042
**Well:** W-11 (Field A, deviated, gas lift)
**Category:** Maintenance
**Priority:** Routine
**Raised:** 2025-10-11
**Planned start:** 2025-10-13
**Planned finish:** 2025-10-13
**Status:** Closed

## Scope of work
Run a cleaning pig through the W-11 flowline to CPF-1 to remove wax and sand deposits. Launch from the wellsite launcher, receive at the CPF-1 receiver, and record the debris recovered. This is the second pigging of this line in about three weeks (previous run EV-0031 on 2025-09-21).

## Safety and permits
- Hot work not required; cold work permit issued for launcher and receiver operations.
- Pressure isolation and bleed-down confirmed before opening the launcher door.
- Gas lift supply to W-11 isolated for the duration of the job.
- Toolbox talk held with the operators at both ends. Radio check agreed between launcher and receiver.

## Materials
- Cleaning pig (foam-bodied with brushes), one spare
- Launcher door seal, replaced as standard practice
- Waste containers for wax and sand recovered at the receiver

## Execution and close-out remarks
- Work carried out on 2025-10-13 and completed the same day.
- The well was shut in for the job. Downtime was 5 hours.
- The pig was received at CPF-1 with no sticking or stalling. Wax and fine sand were recovered at the receiver and disposed of per site waste routine.
- Well restarted and gas lift re-established without issue.
- Production comparison:

| Period | Oil (bbl/d) | Gas (Mscf/d) | Water cut (%) | GOR (scf/bbl) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 7 days to 2025-10-12 (before) | 1027.4 | 1038.2 | 15.8 | 1011 |
| 6 days to 2025-10-20 (after restart) | 1015.4 | 995.8 | 16.2 | 981 |

- Rates after restart are close to the pre-job level. Deposit build-up looks to be recurring, so the Production Engineer should review the pigging interval for this line.

**Close-out:** Work complete, tools and area cleared, permit closed. Work order closed.
