# Incident note: Wellhead leak on W-17

**Document ID:** IN-023
**Event ID:** EV-0064
**Note date:** 2025-12-25
**Well:** W-17 (Field B, deviated, ESP)
**Category:** Incident
**Severity:** Low
**Status at note date:** Closed

## What happened
On 2025-12-25 a leak was found on the wellhead valve stem packing of W-17. The well was taken offline so the leak could be isolated and repaired safely.

## Immediate actions
- The well was shut in and the wellhead area was isolated and barricaded.
- The Production Supervisor and HSE Advisor were informed.
- The area was checked for residual hydrocarbon and the small amount of fluid on the pad was cleaned up.

## Findings
The leak came from the stem packing of a wellhead valve. The packing was replaced and the valve was tested for tightness before the well was returned to service. No other leak points were found at the wellhead during the walk-down.

## Impact
- Downtime: 10 hours on 2025-12-25 (uptime 14 hours).
- Production on the day: oil 873.9 bbl, gas 493.8 Mscf, water cut 29.7%.
- Baseline for the 7 days to 2025-12-24: oil 1488.3 bbl/d, gas 810.4 Mscf/d, water cut 28.5%, GOR 545 scf/bbl.
- Oil on the day was about 614 bbl below the 7-day average, which is the deferred volume attributed to this event.
- No injuries and no reportable release.

## Status
Closed on 2025-12-25. W-17 is producing today. No related events were recorded in the previous 30 days.

## Next actions
- The Lead Operator will include the W-17 wellhead valves in the next routine round and check the repaired stem packing for weeping.
- The Maintenance Planner will review the packing condition on similar wellhead valves at the next planned inspection.

## Procedure reference
No specific procedure applies to this event.
