# Incident note: High separator level trip on W-35

**Document ID:** IN-023
**Note date:** 2026-01-22
**Event ID:** EV-0075
**Well:** W-35 (Salt Flat Field, vertical, natural flow)
**Severity:** medium
**Status at note date:** closed
**Applicable procedure:** none identified for this event type

## What happened
On 2026-01-22 the test separator high-level trip shut in W-35. The well was down for 3 hours, which is consistent with the 21 hours of uptime recorded for the day.

## Immediate actions
- The Lead Operator confirmed the trip on the control panel and isolated the well at the wellhead.
- Field operations checked the test separator for a genuine liquid carry-over. No abnormal liquid surge was seen at the inlet.
- The level controller was inspected and recalibrated.
- After a stable level reading was confirmed, the well was returned to service.

## Findings
The trip was traced to the test separator level controller, which had drifted out of calibration. Recalibration restored normal level control. No damage to the separator or downstream equipment was observed.

## Impact
- Downtime: 3 hours on 2026-01-22.
- Production on the day: 599 bbl oil, 781.4 Mscf gas, water cut 16.2%.
- Previous 7 days (to 2026-01-21): 650.3 bbl/d oil, 878.9 Mscf/d gas, water cut 17%, GOR 1352 scf/bbl.
- The day's oil was about 51 bbl below the 7-day average, which is broadly the loss expected from the shut-in period. There were no related events on this well in the previous 30 days.

## Status
Closed. W-35 is producing normally and the level controller is holding setpoint.

## Next actions
- The Production Engineer will request that the Maintenance Planner add the test separator level controller to the next routine instrument calibration round.
- Operators will check the separator level trend at each shift handover for the next few days.
- No dedicated procedure applies to this event type. The Production Engineer will consider whether a short instrument-checking guideline is warranted.
