# Incident note: High separator level trip on W-35

**Document ID:** IN-013
**Note date:** 2025-09-27
**Event ID:** EV-0036
**Well:** W-35 (Bayda, horizontal, ESP)
**Severity:** medium
**Status:** closed

## What happened
On 2025-09-27 the test separator at CPF-1 reached a high-level trip, which shut in W-35. The well was back online after 4 hours of downtime. The event started and ended on the same day.

## Immediate actions
- The Lead Operator confirmed the trip was a level trip and not a process upset on the well itself.
- The level controller was checked locally and found out of calibration.
- The controller was recalibrated, the separator level was stabilised and the well was restarted.
- The Production Supervisor was informed of the shut-in and the restart.

## Findings so far
- The trip came from the test separator level controller, which was reading incorrectly.
- No other wells reported abnormal behaviour during the event.
- No injury, release or environmental impact was reported.

## Impact
- Downtime: 4 hours (uptime for the day was 20 hours).
- Production on 2025-09-27: oil 1089.6 bbl, gas 1732.3 Mscf, water cut 16%.
- Baseline for the previous 7 days to 2025-09-26: oil 1270.7 bbl/d, gas 1908.4 Mscf/d, water cut 16.1%, GOR 1502 scf/bbl.
- The shortfall against baseline is about 181 bbl of oil for the day, consistent with the lost run time. Water cut was unchanged.

## Status at note date
Closed. The well is producing and the level controller is operating normally after recalibration.

## Next actions
- The Production Engineer will review the separator level readings over the next few days to confirm the calibration holds.
- Instrumentation checks of the level controller will be added to the routine round.
- A subsurface safety valve function and leak test on W-35 is planned for 2025-10-11. It is expected to be routine and is not linked to this event.

## Procedure reference
No specific procedure applies to this event.
