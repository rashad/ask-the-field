# Incident note: Sand production alarm on W-17

**Document ID:** IN-013
**Event ID:** EV-0052
**Note date:** 2025-11-21
**Author:** Production Engineer
**Well:** W-17 (Wadi Field, vertical, ESP)
**Severity:** medium
**Status at note date:** closed

## What happened
On 2025-11-21 the sand monitor on W-17 raised a sand production alarm. The well was on ESP lift and flowing normally before the alarm.

## Immediate actions
- The Lead Operator acknowledged the alarm and reduced the choke to cut flow velocity.
- Sand monitor readings were watched at the wellhead and in the control room until they returned to normal.
- Production Supervisor informed.
- Choke was then restored in steps and readings were rechecked at each step.

## Findings so far
- The alarm was a single event. No repeat alarm was recorded after readings normalised.
- No leak or visible damage was found on the wellhead and flowline during the walk-down.
- No related events on W-17 in the previous 30 days.

## Impact
- Downtime as of the note date: 7 hours.
- Uptime on 2025-11-21: 17 hours.
- Production on the day: 1,112.9 bbl oil, 906.6 Mscf gas, water cut 27.5%.
- Baseline for the 7 days to 2025-11-20: 1,577.8 bbl/d oil, 1,233.5 Mscf/d gas, water cut 28.4%, GOR 782 scf/bbl.
- Oil on the day was about 465 bbl below the baseline daily rate, mainly from the reduced uptime and choke restriction.

## Status
Closed. W-17 is producing.

## Next actions
- Production Engineer to review sand monitor trend over the next several days after the choke restoration.
- Operators to continue routine sand monitor checks each shift.
- Escalate to a sand rate review if the alarm recurs.

## Procedure reference
SOP-OPS-002 Sand Management and Sand Production Alarms.
